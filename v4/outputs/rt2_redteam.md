Roughly 38% of guided FY27 adjusted-EBIT growth (my arithmetic: ~+$514m at the guide; client-funds interest +$195m) comes from interest on client funds, a rate-sensitive stream; ex-float, EBIT growth is closer to 5.4% and EPS growth ~6-7% including ~1.3% buyback shrink, short of the 9% EPS base case. Higher-for-longer rates cut both ways (float tailwind, but a risk-free rate of 5.17% is already in the dossier's WACC). The dossier's stale ERISA wording was corrected, so litigation is not a live bear point.

**Single assumption that breaks the valuation logic.** That ADP can still compound FCF at least ~5% a year once the 2026-27 client-funds lift fades and pays per control stays at 0-1%, i.e. that price/product/margin (+70-90 bps a year) can replace the volume and float tailwinds. Reverse DCF (reproduced): implied 4.2% at 8.6% WACC/3% terminal, ~3.9% at $259, ~5.1% at WACC 9.0%; the margin of safety is real but thin, about 1-2 points against an ex-float underlying ~5-6%.
