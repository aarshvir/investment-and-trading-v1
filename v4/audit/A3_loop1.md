## 1. Total score and verdict

**80 / 100.** This is a genuine, independently-verified rebuild, not a re-skin: every one of 9 recomputed headline numbers and every one of 11 facts I checked across 5 dossiers against primary sources (SEC filings, a DOJ press release, company releases) matched exactly. It would clear most of a top-tier IC's quantitative diligence largely unchanged. It would not yet clear a private bank's suitability sign-off unconditionally, because three delivery-layer gaps remain squarely inside this audit's brief: the investor still cannot see a dollar amount anywhere against his own $50k–$100k, the single largest position in the book (HAS, 10% weight) ships with a blank kill-criteria field in the very tables he's told to check weekly, and no technical term used in the dashboard he actually reads is glossed in plain English.
