# A1 — Loop 0 baseline audit of the v3 "conviction portfolio"

**Auditor persona:** former head of quantitative research at a systematic equity fund — point-in-time data, backtest integrity, statistics and risk models.
**Scope:** `HANDOFF_v3_US_Equity_Conviction_Research.md` (main body, not the code appendix), `equity_project/` data/code, `v4/dashboard/artifact_v3_published_backup.html`. Evidence drawn from `v4/outputs/lead_v3_audit.md`, `v4/outputs/r3_evidence_base.md` §0, and `review/agents/01–06`, cross-checked against my own Python recomputation (5 checks) and 2 fresh primary-source WebSearch spot-checks. Per the efficiency instruction for this loop, I did not re-read Appendix A/B/C of the handoff line-by-line; I read the `.py` sources directly where I needed exact formulas.

## 1. Total score and verdict

**Total: 41 / 100 — do not fund as delivered.** The company facts are genuinely sourced and the arithmetic is honestly reproducible (I re-derived five independent headline numbers to within rounding, including two of the most consequential ones exactly). But as a *portfolio* the thing is not investable: its stated weight/sector caps are mathematically infeasible for the chosen universe and are breached in the delivered weights (I verified this directly), its only true out-of-sample test contradicts the recommendation and was not acted on, it was tuned repeatedly on the same three backtest years, and its one truly decision-relevant number — the real drawdown a client would have lived through — is understated by about ten points against a client who said he tolerates 15–20%. The "90% five-year confidence" headline is, on my own re-run of the code's own bootstrap, almost entirely a restatement of the analyst's assumed 10.68% market drift: at a still-optimistic 4% drift the same model produces 66%, and at 0% it produces 44%. That is not a finding about these 14 stocks; it is an assumption wearing a probability's clothes.

## 3. Must-fix to reach ≥95 (ranked by score impact)

1. **Rebuild the backtest on point-in-time data** (criterion 2, ~+6–7 pts): historical index membership + delisting returns, as-filed (not restated) fundamentals, pre-register the model before looking at any test-window result, add realistic transaction costs, and report t-stats/CIs plus subperiod stability. Do not let a fourth redesign look at 2023–2025 again.
2. **Fix portfolio construction feasibility, not just its arithmetic** (criterion 6, ~+6 pts): check constraint feasibility against the *eligible universe* before optimizing; if infeasible (as here — 95% max under stated caps), either widen the eligible set, add a cash sleeve, or relax the cap explicitly — never clip-then-renormalize past a stated limit. Re-verify every constraint on the final, rounded weights.
3. **Replace the month-end drawdown with the daily, monthly-rebalanced figure and size the portfolio to it** (criteria 2/6, ~+4–5 pts): report −36%, not −26%, as the COVID-window reference case, and either reduce equity exposure or renegotiate the 15–20% tolerance with the client explicitly — do not let a reporting convention substitute for a risk decision.
4. **Build a data-lineage ledger** (criterion 1, ~+4 pts): source URL/accession + retrieval timestamp + fiscal period + GAAP-vs-adjusted basis for every material number; convert every silent fill (missing guidance, missing leverage, pre-IPO returns) into an explicit "unknown" state that cannot pass a quality gate by default; declare NTM vs. current/next-FY basis on every multiple shown.
5. **Re-derive and re-frame the "90%" claim** (criterion 3, ~+4 pts): show it as a sensitivity table against the assumed index drift (as I did in §4e), not a single number; replace the self-assigned 92/100 with scoring against an external, adversarial rubric before it goes to the client again.
6. **Fix valuation coherence** (criterion 5, ~+3 pts): one declared horizon across all names; proper FCFF (EBIT(1-t)+D&A−capex−ΔNWC) discounted at WACC instead of CFO−capex; drop the 70% ad hoc owner-earnings substitution or justify it per-company; make bull/bear cases driven by company-specific revenue/margin/multiple assumptions, not an algebraic identity that always nets to ±21%/−32%.
7. **Surface the adverse facts already on file** (criterion 4, ~+2 pts) directly in the client-facing dossiers (NTAP cash conversion, AME pro forma leverage, CPAY GAAP/adjusted gap, HIG one-off tax benefit) and stop routing operating companies like CPAY through an ROE-only Financials gate.
8. **Correct the tax paragraph and add operating rules** (criterion 8, ~+2 pts): graduated-rate estate tax, not "40% above $60k"; confirm citizenship/domicile before recommending the UCITS mitigation; add explicit kill-criteria, rebalance triggers, and a weekly checklist.
9. **Show ranges, not points, in every investor-facing number** (criterion 9, ~+1–2 pts).
10. **Reconcile mandate fit last, after 1–5 are fixed** (criterion 7, ~+2 pts) — the right answer may still be 14 stocks, but the risk budget behind them needs to actually match what the client said he can tolerate.
