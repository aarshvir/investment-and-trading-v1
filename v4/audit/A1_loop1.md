## 1. Total score and verdict

**Total: 85 / 100 — fund it (with the Cautious or Moderate allocation), on a genuinely rebuilt and mostly-honest process; not yet a 95.** This is a different order of work from the v3 baseline (loop 0: 41). The backtest is pre-registered, point-in-time, cost-aware, dually implemented by two independent agents that caught a real bug in each other, and passes 16/16 explicit no-look-ahead unit tests — I reproduced nine of its headline numbers myself, several to three-plus decimal places, and they matched. Portfolio caps are verified post-solve rather than clip-and-renormalize (v3's real bug), and I confirmed by direct computation that they hold to within a hundredth of a percentage point. The flagship "90%" claim is honestly deconstructed into scenario-conditioned, empirically calibrated odds, and the document repeatedly says the thing that costs it face — "no honest process can promise a basket of stocks will beat the index" — instead of hiding it. What keeps this out of the 90s: I found one genuinely decision-relevant gap squarely in my own specialty (the dashboard's per-name "risk contribution" table is, by direct arithmetic, mechanically equivalent to squared position size and not disclosed as such, even though the same team's own risk engine documented exactly this failure mode for the old v3 portfolio), one data-integrity finding that didn't make it from the internal audit into the client-facing limitations section, and several smaller presentation/completeness loose ends.
