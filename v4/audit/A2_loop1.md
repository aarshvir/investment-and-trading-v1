**Auditor:** A2 — former portfolio manager / investment-committee member, top-tier fundamental long-only fund (company diligence, valuation, portfolio construction specialist). **Focus weighting per brief:** criteria 4 (company diligence), 5 (valuation), 6 (portfolio construction), 7 (strategy coherence) — all ten scored.

## 1. Total score and verdict

**Total: 76/100.**

**Verdict:** This is a different order of work from v3, and it shows: every one of loop 0's seven must-fix items has been directly and verifiably addressed — point-in-time backtesting with an independently-replicated null result honestly reported (not a re-tuned "improved" version), daily-data drawdowns disclosed and reconciled against the investor's stated tolerance, a single consistent NTM valuation horizon, a CAPM-consistent expected-return/cost-of-capital framework, portfolio caps that are mechanically verified to actually hold (Financials sector lands at exactly 25.00%, not v3's 26.245% breach), measurable per-name kill criteria, and the "90%" headline's scenario-sensitivity moved from a buried footnote to the front-page verdict. Five dossiers checked in full against primary sources (SEC filings, company press releases) reproduced every material fact I tested, and — unlike v3 — each dossier actively surfaces its own adverse facts rather than omitting them. However, I found one concrete, material, and fully evidenced bug: **Ralph Lauren (RL), the second-largest position in the 15-stock sleeve at 9.5%, fails the program's own written valuation-eligibility rule.** The current valuation table gives RL a "demanding" verdict with a negative base-case expected return, which should have excluded it under the same rule that excluded TGT, TRV, TPR, CF, COP and BBY — but the portfolio-construction step was run against an earlier, incomplete version of that table (file timestamps prove the valuation table was finalized ~2.5 minutes *after* the portfolio was frozen) and the gap was never reconciled. That is exactly the class of undiscovered, decision-relevant error a real IC review exists to catch, so criteria 5, 6 and 10 cannot yet clear "minor gaps," even though the surrounding methodology on all three is otherwise excellent.
