## 3. Loop-3-specific judgments (per the persona-file notes and the task brief)

**a) Is the all-stock option presented clearly, with its real risks?** The *content* is clear and honest wherever it appears — crisis-replay falls (−60%/−39%/−15%), the coin-flip odds, the 92% chance of a >20% fall, concentration risk below 12–15 names, and the ~$60k estate-tax threshold are all named in the dashboard's "All-stock option" card, and I independently confirmed every one of those figures against `lead_risk_results.json`. What is not clear is that this option is *the* answer to what the owner asked this loop: the primary Verdict (FINAL_REPORT §1), the dashboard hero, and the single "next step" all still present the three-layer index+T-bill structure as the headline recommendation. The 30% dividend-withholding fact is stated generally in §8 but not repeated in the all-stock card itself, and no per-$10,000 or estate-tax table row exists for the 100%-stock case, unlike every blended mix. Net: accurate and honestly risk-disclosed, but not prominent, and not fully self-contained where it does appear.

**b) Is "beat the index for sure, by about 10 points a year" handled truthfully without being preachy?** Truthfully, yes — the 43–46%/49% "coin flip" framing, the historical base rate that a stock-picking tilt over the index realistically nets "+1%/yr (range 0 to +2%)," and the "86–93% of large-cap funds lagged the index over 10–20 years" statistic together make it impossible to read this report as endorsing a "beat by 10 points, for sure" outcome, without ever lecturing the reader about it. It is stated once, plainly, in each place it is relevant, and left alone. The one gap: nowhere does the report explicitly engage the owner's specific "~10 points a year" figure by name and say, in so many words, why that particular target is not achievable by any honest process at this portfolio's tracking error (≈11.6%, i.e. beating by 10 points a year would require a return roughly one standard deviation from the mean merely to hit once) — the refutation is implicit in the base rates rather than a direct answer to the number he actually named.

**c) Is it still research, not personal advice?** Yes throughout — "Research, not advice" appears verbatim in the all-stock card; the cover line ("research and general information, not personalised investment, tax or legal advice... no trades are placed") is unchanged; every implementation fact is still hedged with "confirm with a cross-border tax adviser" where appropriate.
