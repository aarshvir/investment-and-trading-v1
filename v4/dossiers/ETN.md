# ETN - Eaton Corporation plc (research only; not personal investment advice)

**1. Verdict: WATCH.** A best-in-class electrical-equipment franchise riding data-center and grid demand with a consistent beat-and-raise record in 2026, but at $439.98 (25 Sep 2026) the price implies about 12% a year of free-cash-flow growth for ten years (range 10.8%-13.5%) against my base of 11%, and the base-case three-year return is about 8% a year. Triage "do not advance" is confirmed on price, not on quality: this is a good business bought at a full price, so the call is WATCH with a named entry level near $400 (section 7). Horizon 24-48 months.

**2. Business in plain English.** Eaton makes the equipment that distributes and protects electrical power: switchgear, power distribution, uninterruptible power supplies, busway and modular power enclosures (Fibrebond, bought Apr 2025), now liquid cooling and thermal systems (Boyd Thermal, bought 12 Mar 2026 for $9.55bn net of cash), plus aerospace fuel and fluid components (Ultra PCS bought 23 Jan 2026 for $1.53bn). Customers are data-center builders, utilities, industrial and commercial builders and aircraft makers; it earns an equipment margin (segment margin 23.1% in Q2 2026) with a growing aftermarket. Edge: installed base, channel reach, scarce manufacturing capacity and certifications. Mobility (transmissions and clutches for trucks, segment margin 13.0%) is being separated through a Reverse Morris Trust with Dana Incorporated, expected to close in Q1 2027.
