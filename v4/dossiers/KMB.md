# KMB — Kimberly-Clark Corporation (F149, wave 8, STANDARD depth)

**1. Verdict: WATCH.** A cheap-looking staples franchise (13.2x NTM EPS, 5.2% dividend yield) whose 2026 outlook was just cut, and which is about to nearly double its share count and take on up to roughly $15bn of assumed and new gross debt to buy Kenvue, with no pro forma guidance yet and the Tylenol (acetaminophen) litigation revived on appeal in July 2026. The price is not the problem (implied growth is at or below base); the unquantifiable deal and litigation risk is. Horizon 24-36 months. Upgrade to INCLUDE-SMALL if the deal closes with pro forma net debt/EBITDA at or below about 3.0x, the first pro forma guide supports at least about $8 adjusted EPS by 2028, and the acetaminophen remand shows no path to a >$1bn exposure.

**2. Business in plain English (10-K acc. 0001628280-26-007567; 10-Q acc. 0001628280-26-052348).** Kimberly-Clark sells tissue, diapers and personal care (Kleenex, Scott, Huggies, Pull-Ups, Kotex, Depend) mainly in North America and international personal care. It just sold 51% of its International Family Care & Professional (IFP) tissue business into a joint venture (Arbex) with Suzano (closed 1 Jul 2026: cash price "approximately $1.3 billion", retained 49% stake valued about $1.2bn; 10-Q Note 11) and agreed on 2 Nov 2025 to acquire Kenvue (Tylenol, Listerine, Band-Aid, Neutrogena) for 0.14625 KMB shares plus $3.50 cash per Kenvue share. Moat: brands and scale in categories growing about 2%; pricing power is modest (price investments in 2025).

**3. Why the model likes it (b1/d4).** Quality family 73rd pct: gross-profit/assets 98th pct, OCF/assets 90th pct, ROE 98th pct (ROE 112%: an artefact of book equity of only $1.75bn after years of buybacks and AOCI losses of $3.4bn, not of unusual returns). Value 60th pct (earnings yield 6.0%, FCF yield 5.6% in b1). Momentum 23rd pct (-9.8%) and earnings surprise 15th pct (-0.61): the market is already marking down the story. Composite decile 3. Durable: brand cash generation. Artefact: ROE and the FCF figure (which includes the discontinued IFP business and a one-off insurance recovery). Triage (Q03): not advanced because of Kenvue leverage; I agree and add the guidance cut and litigation.

**9. Key risks and kill criteria (measurable).**
1. Kenvue acquisition not closed by 31 Mar 2027 or terminated (either side owes a $1.1bn fee in specified cases).
2. FY26 organic sales growth guide cut again (currently "approximately 100 basis points below its weighted average category growth") or adjusted operating profit growth guide cut below mid-single digits.
3. Pro forma consolidated net debt/adjusted EBITDA at close above 3.25x (my estimate 2.6-2.9x) or a credit-rating downgrade below BBB.
4. Dividend cut, or trailing-12-month free cash flow below dividends paid for two consecutive quarters after closing.
5. Any acetaminophen judgment, settlement or accrual above $1bn, or a trial date set in the remanded MDL.
