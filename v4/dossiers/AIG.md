# AIG — American International Group (F142, wave 8, STANDARD depth; insurance playbook metrics)

**1. Verdict: INCLUDE-SMALL (half weight), thesis horizon 24–36 months.** Reason: at $74.17 the stock trades below book value ($77.39 per share at 30 Jun 2026) and at ~9x trailing adjusted earnings, which a reverse DCF says only requires earnings to shrink ~3–4% a year; the named reservations are (a) a soft-pricing commercial market, (b) a recurring adverse-development pattern in US Excess Casualty, (c) management turnover (Executive Chair and GI CEO both leaving in September 2026), and (d) a sub-10% reported ROE. The Q05 triage ("ROE 7.2% below cost of capital; EPS jump a rebound") is partly overturned: core operating ROE is 11.1% and underwriting profit is broad-based, but 2025 EPS comparisons are flattered by a catastrophe-heavy 2025 start.

**Most recent period incorporated: Q2 2026 (10-Q filed 2026-08-07, acc. 0000005272-26-000076; earnings release 2026-08-06); events checked to 2026-09-25 (8-Ks of 2026-09-02, 09-16 and 09-24 read).**

**2. Business in plain English.** AIG sells commercial property, casualty, specialty, financial-lines and personal insurance (high net worth, travel) in the US, Lloyd's (Talbot) and internationally, and earns money from underwriting profit plus investment income on ~$92bn of investments. Since selling Corebridge (life/annuities; last shares sold 7 May 2026 for ~$710m) it is a pure property-casualty group. Position: a top global commercial insurer with large-account and excess-and-surplus franchises.

**3. Why the model likes it.** b1 rank 267, quality and value families high (P/E ~9x, ROE, EP 0.073). Durable? Partly: underwriting income is real ($686m Q2) but TTM adjusted EPS of $8.27 includes favourable prior-year reserve development and benign 2026 catastrophes; I normalise to ~$7.6.

**8. Bull / bear.** Bull: (1) a 89.0% combined ratio with AYCR 88.1% and three segments growing NPW; (2) buybacks ($641m Q2, ~8m shares) at below book value are accretive; (3) new capabilities (Convex stake, Everest retail renewal rights, Syndicate 2479) add premium. Bear: (1) management itself says the market "has transitioned from an extended phase of broad positive pricing into a more selective environment"; (2) recurring adverse Excess Casualty development and social inflation in a long-tail book, with favourable PYD (2.5 pts) propping the combined ratio; (3) leadership vacuum: Executive Chair Zaffino stepped down 15 Sep 2026 and GI CEO Hancock retires 31 Dec 2026 (8-Ks acc. 0000005272-26-000088 and -000091).
