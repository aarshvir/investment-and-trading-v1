# CLX - The Clorox Company (agent F163, standard depth, information cutoff 2026-09-25 close)

**1. Verdict: WATCH** (horizon 12-24 months). Cheap on a normalised basis (price implies about -0.5% a year of 10-year free cash flow growth) but the evidence of recovery is not there yet: consolidated leverage is 4.2x GAAP EBITDA (balance-sheet debt of the Clorox Company) after two debt-funded deals, FY26 reported free cash flow ($405m) did not cover the dividend ($602m), and the FY27 organic-sales guide of +3.5% to +4.5% includes "more than 3.5 points" from lapping the ERP drawdown, so underlying growth is about 0% to +1%. Upgrade condition: two clean quarters of positive organic sales (Q2 and Q3 FY27) with net debt/EBITDA on a path below 3.5x. The triage said "not advance" (value trap); I agree on the verdict but not the numbers (4.8x leverage is Yahoo debt including leases over a different EBITDA, section 6).

**2. Business in plain English.** Clorox sells household and consumer brands: Clorox cleaners, Glad bags, Fresh Step litter, Kingsford, Brita, Burt's Bees, Hidden Valley and, since 1 Apr 2026, Purell (GOJO Industries, bought for about $2.15bn, "primarily debt-financed", 10-K risk factors). It sells mostly through US retailers, so it competes on price against private label and larger rivals. It spent about $580m over five years on an ERP and digital overhaul, completed in Q3 FY26, and in March 2026 paid P&G $476m cash for its 20% stake in the Glad venture.

**3. Why the model likes it.** b1/d4: low NTM P/E (14.2x), high dividend yield (6.0%), value family high. Artefact risk: FY26 EPS was cut by the ERP shipment unwind (about $0.90) and FY27 gets that back, so part of the FY27 rebound is mechanical, not demand. Momentum is -17% over 12 months in b1.

**8. Bull case and bear case.** Bull: (1) FY27 lapping of the ERP drawdown and GOJO (+9.5 pts to sales) restore growth and EPS to $5.70-$6.00; (2) heavy brand investment and "nearly doubling innovation" rebuild share lost to order-fulfilment problems; (3) interest burden and capex fall as ERP spend ends, enabling debt paydown. Bear: (1) underlying organic growth is flat at best (Q2 and Q3 FY26 -1%) with private-label and value-seeking consumers; (2) leverage over 4x with dividend uncovered by reported FCF limits flexibility, and FY27 gross margin guide of about 42% assumes "higher-than-normal inflationary headwinds"; (3) GOJO integration risk and a goodwill-heavy balance sheet.

**9. Kill criteria (thesis-invalidation triggers).**
- FY27 adjusted EPS guidance low end cut below $5.70 at any quarterly release (current "between $5.70 and $6.00").
- Organic sales growth below 0% in each of Q2 FY27 and Q3 FY27 (quarters free of the ERP lap).
- Consolidated net debt (notes and loans payable + current maturities + long-term debt - cash) above 4.0x GAAP EBITDA at 30 Jun 2027 (30 Jun 2026: 4.2x).
- Full-year FY27 gross margin below 41.0% (guidance: "Gross margin is expected to be about 42%", acc. 0000021076-26-000028; FY26 42.3%).
- FY27 reported free cash flow (operating cash flow less capex) below total dividends paid ($602m in FY26), or any dividend cut.
