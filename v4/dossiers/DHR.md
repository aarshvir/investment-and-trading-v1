## 1. Verdict
**WATCH**, horizon 12–36 months. Excellent franchise, but $224.49 implies ~9.3% 10-year free-cash-flow growth against my base of ~7%: a great business priced for more than it can deliver. The triage verdict (advance=false) stands, but its stated reasons rested on general knowledge; the filings add three new facts that raise the bar: Danaher swapped $9.8bn of cash for Masimo in June 2026 (consolidated net debt about $22bn, ~2.9x EBITDA), it cut the top half of its 2026 core-growth range (3–6% to 3–4%) in July, and the CEO hands over on 1 Oct 2026. Would move to INCLUDE-SMALL near ~$190 (implied <= base), or on two quarters of core growth ex-respiratory at or above 5% with leverage falling below 2.5x.

## 2. Business in plain English
Danaher sells the tools and consumables of biotech and diagnostics: bioprocessing (Cytiva/Pall filters, bioreactors, resins for making biologic drugs), life-science instruments (SCIEX, Leica, Beckman's research lines), and diagnostics (Cepheid molecular tests, Beckman Coulter lab analyzers, Radiometer, and now Masimo patient monitoring). Revenue is mostly recurring consumables on an installed base; the Danaher Business System (continuous improvement) is its edge, and ~60,000 associates. FY25 sales were $24.6bn.

## 3. Why the model likes it, and is it durable
b1 live rank 328 (composite 0.34): stability/size and a value percentile of 0.63, but the quality family is weak (0.12; GAAP ROE 7.6%, GP/A 15.9%) because the balance sheet carries ~$68.8bn of goodwill and intangibles, 74% of total assets. That is an accounting-capital artefact of the acquisition-led history (Cytiva, Abcam, now Masimo), not poor operations: adjusted operating margin is guided at ~26.5% for Q3'26. The positive surprise score (SUE +1.8) reflects cost-led EPS beats on 2–3% core growth, which is less durable than volume-led beats.

2. Consolidated net debt (debt carrying $26,558m less cash $4,348m, Note 10 and balance sheet) / (TTM GAAP operating profit plus D&A) above 3.25x at any fiscal year-end (now ~2.9x).
3. Full-year 2026 adjusted EPS guidance cut below $8.35 (now "$8.45 to $8.60", acc. 0000313616-26-000163).
4. Biotechnology segment core sales growth below 2.0% for two consecutive quarters while bioprocessing orders growth falls below 5% (Q2'26: +2.5% and mid-teens).
5. A Masimo-related criminal charge, or cumulative Masimo litigation payments and accruals above $500m (Apple, DOJ, Kiani matters, 10-Q Note 12).
