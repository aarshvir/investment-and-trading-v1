# CL (Colgate-Palmolive Co) — F101 Diligence Dossier

## 1. Verdict
**INCLUDE-SMALL** (half weight). Reason: dominant, high-quality oral-care franchise trading at a discount to a reasonable base-case growth path, but growth itself is only mid-single-digit, negative price momentum is present, and the balance sheet's near-zero consolidated equity (structural, from decades of buybacks) is a real disclosure item even though it is not a solvency risk. Thesis horizon: 24–36 months.

## 2. Business in plain English
Colgate-Palmolive sells toothpaste, toothbrushes, personal-care and home-care products (Colgate, Palmolive, Softsoap, Hill's Pet Nutrition) in over 200 countries. It is the global #1 in toothpaste with roughly 40% volume share and has the deepest emerging-market distribution network of any Western consumer-staples company (Q03 triage). It makes money on brand-driven pricing power and scale manufacturing/distribution efficiency; Hill's pet food is now a meaningful, faster-growing profit contributor alongside the legacy oral/personal-care core.

## 6. Earnings quality & balance sheet
- **Consolidated** stockholders' equity per XBRL `StockholdersEquity`: $363m (Mar-25) → $702m (Jun-25) → $856m (Sep-25) → **$54m (Dec-25)** → $145m (Mar-26) → $236m (Jun-26). This is genuinely near zero/thin — Colgate has bought back stock in excess of retained earnings for decades, so consolidated book equity is structurally minimal (Yahoo `bookValue` per share $0.296, `priceToBook` 290x). **This is a known, long-standing feature of CL's capital structure, not a new distress signal**, but it means there is essentially no equity cushion and leverage ratios must be read off debt/EBITDA rather than debt/equity.
- **Consolidated** long-term debt (XBRL `LongTermDebt`, 10-K FY2025 table): $7.839bn (2025-12-31) vs $7.005bn (2024-12-31) — debt is rising. Net debt/EBITDA ≈ (7.86bn debt − 1.45bn cash) / 4.98bn TTM EBITDA (Yahoo) ≈ **1.3x** — moderate, investment-grade-consistent leverage.
- Cash conversion: OCF $4.456bn / FCF $3.458bn (Yahoo TTM) vs TTM net income ≈ $2.038bn to $2.77bn range (varies by GAAP vs adjusted base) — FCF comfortably covers the dividend ($2.12/share, ~82% payout ratio on Yahoo trailing EPS basis, but trailing EPS of $2.54 GAAP is depressed by one-offs; on a cleaner run-rate the payout is closer to 50–55%).
- Share count: 797.2m shares outstanding — buybacks are the primary reason equity stays near zero; this is capital-return, not distress.

## 8. Bull case / Bear case
**Bull:** (1) Input-cost and tariff pressure genuinely eases through 2027, restoring the 21%+ operating margin last seen in 2024; (2) emerging-market volume reaccelerates on Colgate's unmatched EM distribution; (3) Hill's Pet Nutrition keeps outgrowing the core and becomes a larger share of mix, lifting the growth algorithm.
**Bear:** (1) Tariffs/input costs stay elevated and opex inflation (seen in the last two quarters) persists, capping EPS growth near zero for several more quarters; (2) EM currency weakness (a recurring CL risk) offsets constant-currency volume gains; (3) rising debt ($7.0bn→$7.8bn YoY) combined with near-zero equity limits flexibility for further buybacks if a margin/FX shock hits simultaneously.

## 9. Key risks & measurable kill criteria
1. Operating margin stays below 20% (GAAP) for three consecutive quarters (currently 18.1%, 19.0% in the last two vs a 20.6–21.9% run-rate a year ago).
2. Organic/constant-currency revenue growth falls below 2% for two consecutive quarters.
3. Consolidated net debt/EBITDA rises above 2.0x (currently ≈1.3x).
4. Diluted GAAP EPS declines YoY for two consecutive quarters (it already declined YoY in Q1-2026: 0.80 vs 0.85).
5. Dividend payout ratio (on GAAP EPS) exceeds 100% for two consecutive quarters, signalling the dividend is no longer earned.
