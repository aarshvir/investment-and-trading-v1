# IT - Gartner, Inc. (NYSE: IT) - standard-depth diligence (agent F134)

**1. Verdict: INCLUDE-SMALL. Horizon 12-36 months.** The triage reject (negative revenue growth, street target below price) does not survive primary-source work: the -0.6% headline revenue change in Q2 2026 is the sale of Digital Markets in February 2026; adjusted revenue grew +2.8% (+1.8% FX neutral) and contract value (CV) is re-accelerating, from roughly flat to +1.7% YoY. Named reservation: CV growth is only about 1-2%, so per-share EPS growth is mostly buyback-funded, and the AI-substitution risk to research subscriptions is unproven either way.

**2. Business in plain English.** Gartner sells annual subscriptions (Insights: about 77% of Q2 revenue) giving technology and business leaders research, benchmarks and analyst access; it also runs conferences (Q2 revenue $244M, +15.5%) and consulting ($142M, -8.8%). Subscriptions are paid in advance (deferred revenue $2,756M at 30 Jun 2026, consolidated 10-Q balance sheet), so the model throws off cash ahead of profit. Moat: proprietary research, brand and a large analyst bench; weakness: discretionary client budgets and US federal exposure (mid-single-digit CV decline in the public sector, 10-Q MD&A).

**3. Why the model likes it.** Cheap on earnings and cash (about 13x the 2026 adjusted EPS guide ("at or above $14", Q2 call), about 10% company-defined FCF yield) plus strong margins and ROE (ROE is inflated by an equity deficit of -$167M after buybacks, consolidated 10-Q). Durable or artefact? The cheapness is real but partly reflects the market pricing AI disruption; the quality scores are flattered by the shrunken equity base (accounting artefact), not by operations.
