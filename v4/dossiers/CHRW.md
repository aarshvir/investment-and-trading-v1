# CHRW - C.H. Robinson Worldwide (Industrials / Air Freight & Logistics)
**Verdict: WATCH** - a good business with verified cost execution, but a price that needs a full freight recovery and a $604 million verdict still unresolved. Horizon 12-36 months. Revisit if the Lipe exposure is capped within the $155 million insurance tower and the price nears 17x FY2026E EPS (about $105).

## 1. Business in plain English
C.H. Robinson arranges truck, LTL, ocean and air freight for shippers using other companies' trucks and ships (asset-light). It earns the spread between what customers pay and carriers charge (adjusted gross profit). Its edge is scale, proprietary technology and a "Lean AI" automation programme (company claims productivity up over 60% since end-2022).

## 7. Bull and bear
Bull: (1) 13 straight quarters of NAST volume outgrowth vs the Cass index with headcount down 10.8%; (2) operating leverage when the truckload cycle turns, with mid-cycle margins already reached in a trough; (3) large buyback capacity.
Bear: (1) Lipe exposure up to $604M joint and several vs $155M insurance; (2) Q2 profit helped by price inflation while cash was consumed by working capital and returns were debt-funded; (3) 26x trailing earnings for an unguided cyclical.

## 8. Risks and kill criteria
1. Adjusted gross profit per NAST truckload shipment falls year-over-year for two consecutive quarters while NAST volume growth trails the Cass Freight Shipment Index (market-share thesis broken; source: quarterly earnings release NAST table).
2. Consolidated debt (10-Q debt footnote: revolver + receivables securitization + senior notes, carrying value) exceeds 2.5x trailing-twelve-month adjusted EBITDA, versus about 1.6x at 30 Jun 2026 (Yahoo-derived EBITDA, cross-check only).
3. Lipe v. Lupus Superior (Dallas County) is entered as a final judgment of $300 million or more against C.H. Robinson and is not stayed or bonded within insurance limits, or a settlement/accrual above the $155 million per-occurrence insurance tower is disclosed.
4. Quarterly adjusted diluted EPS (non-GAAP, release reconciliation) is below $1.25 in any quarter of 2027, i.e. no step-up from the Q4 2025 level of $1.23 (earnings power not rising despite claimed productivity gains).
5. Net cash from operating activities (10-Q cash flow statement, consolidated) is below $250 million for full-year 2026, meaning the working-capital build is not reversing while buybacks and dividends continue to be debt-funded.
