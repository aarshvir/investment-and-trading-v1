# AMT — American Tower Corporation (Agent F79)

1. **Verdict: INCLUDE-SMALL** (12–36mo). Quality tower REIT with a genuinely soft near-term growth patch (not just "comp noise" as Q14 triage framed it) but a valuation that no longer demands reacceleration to work.

2. **Business:** Leases space on ~40,000+ US and ~150,000 international communications towers to wireless carriers on long-term (5–10yr, escalator-linked) leases, plus a data-center business (CoreSite) for hyperscaler/enterprise interconnection. Toll-road economics: high incremental margins as carriers add tenants/equipment to existing structures.

3. **Why the model likes it / durability:** High recurring-revenue quality (34% ROE per triage) and active deleveraging/portfolio pruning (India exit) rather than growth-chasing — durable, capital-discipline-driven re-rating, not an accounting artefact.

4. **Recent results (primary source: Q2 2026 8-K/press release, filed with SEC 29 Jul 2026, and Q2 2026 earnings call, 29 Jul 2026):** Q2 2026 adjusted EPS $1.87 (vs. ~$1.55 consensus); total revenue $2.75B; property revenue cash/FX-neutral growth >7% ex-DISH churn; CoreSite data-center revenue +~12% y/y, fifth straight quarter of double-digit growth. Net leverage 4.9x, inside the 3–5x target band.

5. **Guidance track record:** Raised full-year 2026 outlook for the second time this year at Q2: property-revenue midpoint +$110M, adjusted EBITDA midpoint +$45M, Attributable AFFO/share midpoint +$0.09 (implying ~3% AFFO/share growth y/y for 2026). Management explicitly frames 2026 as the AFFO/share growth **trough**, with DISH-related churn and elevated refinancing costs rolling off into 2027; data-center growth guide raised to ~15%. This is a real, disclosed deceleration this year — the Q14 triage's "comp noise, not a demand problem" undersells it; it is a genuine, management-acknowledged trough, but one the company itself expects to exit.

6. **Balance sheet / capital:** Net debt/EBITDA 4.9x (consolidated, per Q2 2026 release), within AMT's own 3–5x target. Continues to prioritize buybacks/deleveraging over new international builds. No major near-term maturity wall disclosed in the release; treat as a monitoring item pending 10-Q detail.

7. **Valuation:** Price $169.03 (FMP live quote, ~current). Against a ~$11.00 2026 AFFO/share guide midpoint (derived from the $0.09 raise language), that is ~15.4x AFFO — below AMT's own 10-year historical band (typically 18–24x AFFO) despite the raised guide. No V1 systematic row exists for AMT in `v1_valuation_table.csv`, so v1_verdict = null. My own reverse-DCF sense: at 15.4x AFFO with a high-single-digit COE, the price implies roughly flat-to-low-single-digit AFFO/share growth in perpetuity — **below** my base case (mid-single-digit growth in 2026 reaccelerating toward high-single-digit as DISH churn and refinancing headwinds lapse in 2027, per management's own trough framing plus continuing 5G/data-center demand).

8. **Bull:** (a) Data-center (CoreSite) growth guide raised to ~15%, a structurally faster-growing sleeve inside the REIT; (b) 2026 explicitly framed by management as the trough — 2027 AFFO/share growth should reaccelerate as churn and refi costs roll off; (c) capital discipline (India exit, buybacks, sub-5x leverage) improves per-share economics even with slower top-line growth.
**Bear:** (a) "Trough" framing is a forecast, not a fact — if 5G tenant additions stay slow, the reacceleration may not show up in 2027; (b) leverage at 4.9x leaves less room to absorb a rate shock than peers; (c) international (ex-India) tower economics remain lower-return than the US book, a lingering capital-allocation drag.

9. **Kill criteria:** (i) 2027 AFFO/share growth guidance issued below +5% at the Q4 2026 call; (ii) net debt/EBITDA rises above 5.0x for two consecutive quarters; (iii) CoreSite revenue growth falls below 8% y/y for two consecutive quarters; (iv) organic tenant billings growth (ex-DISH) falls below 5% for two consecutive quarters; (v) a further guidance cut (not raise) at any 2026 quarterly release.

10. **Catalysts:** Q3 2026 earnings (est. late Oct/early Nov 2026); FY2026 guidance update at Q4 call (Feb 2027) is the key test of the "trough" thesis.

11. **Red flags:** None identified in this pass (no auditor change, no restatement, no going-concern language found in the Q2 2026 release or search results). Not independently verified against the full 10-Q; flag as a gap.
