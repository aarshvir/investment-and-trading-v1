## 1. Verdict
**INCLUDE**. Reason: durable #1/#2 door-hardware and electronic-access-control franchise, guidance raised twice in
2026 (not cut), reasonable 16.2x NTM P/E for high-single/low-double-digit EPS growth, and a healthy, *improving*
balance sheet (shareholders' equity has more than doubled since 2023 as buybacks moderated). Horizon: 12–36 months.

## 2. Business in plain English
Allegion (spun off from Ingersoll-Rand in 2013, Irish-domiciled) makes mechanical and electronic security products:
locks, door closers, exit devices, and increasingly electronic/mobile-credential access control, sold mainly to
commercial end markets (education, healthcare, offices) through distributors and installers, plus a smaller
residential business. It earns money on high-margin branded hardware (Schlage, LCN, Von Duprin, CISA) with a
growing recurring/replacement tailwind as institutions convert physical keys to electronic and mobile credentials.

## 8. Bull case / Bear case
Bull: (1) Guidance raised twice in 2026 with margin expansion (Q2'26 operating margin 22.1%, a cycle high in the
table above) — the opposite trajectory to ZTS. (2) Electronics/mobile-credential mix shift in Americas (low-teens
growth, higher-education-led) is a genuine structural tailwind, not cyclical. (3) Balance sheet has de-risked
materially (consolidated) — equity up ~57% since 2023 while debt is flat, and consolidated net debt/EBITDA (~1.6x)
is comfortable, leaving room
to resume faster buybacks or bolt-on M&A.
Bear: (1) Q1'26's margin dip (18.9%, vs 21–22% in adjacent quarters) and the reported EPS miss show quarter-to-quarter
lumpiness that could recur if the Q2 beat was partly mix/timing. (2) International segment is described as "weaker"
even in the guidance-raise quarter — a real, unquantified drag we could not size this cycle. (3) The capex/FCF
figures for FY2025–2026 are incomplete in this pull (see §6) — until confirmed, the >100% FCF/NI conversion claim is
provisional, and a lower actual capex-adjusted FCF would mean the ~3.7% FCF yield understates the multiple.

## 9. Key risks & kill criteria (measurable)
1. FY2026 adjusted EPS guidance (currently $8.85–9.00) is **cut** at the Q3'26 or Q4'26 release (reversing the
   two-for-two raise pattern).
2. Consolidated operating margin falls **below 19%** for two consecutive quarters (Q1'26's 18.9% would count as one).
3. Consolidated net debt/EBITDA rises **above 2.5x** (vs ≈1.6x now, consolidated) without a stated, offsetting M&A growth rationale.
4. Americas electronics revenue growth decelerates **below high-single-digit** YoY for two consecutive quarters
   (the structural-tailwind thesis breaking).
5. International segment organic revenue is **negative** for two consecutive quarters (the currently-unquantified
   "weaker international" comment becoming a confirmed trend).
