# KVUE - Kenvue Inc. (consumer health; pending acquisition by Kimberly-Clark)

**1. Verdict: WATCH** - the shares are a pending-merger instrument (0.5% gross spread to deal value, expected close Q4 2026); the equity thesis becomes Kimberly-Clark exposure, which this dossier does not underwrite. Horizon 3-6 months to close; 12-36 months only as a KMB holder. No V1 row exists (v1_verdict = null).

**2. Business.** Owner of Tylenol, Listerine, Band-Aid, Neutrogena, Aveeno and Johnson's, sold through retailers and e-commerce in three segments: Self Care, Skin Health and Beauty, Essential Health. Revenue is consumer-staples-like (low single digit growth, ~58% GAAP gross margin) and the brands are category leaders. Spun out of Johnson & Johnson; J&J retains US/Canada talc liabilities under the Separation Agreement (10-Q, 28 Jun 2026).
