## 1. Business in plain English
IP makes containerboard, corrugated boxes and related paper, selling mainly to consumer, food and e-commerce box buyers. After buying DS Smith (2025) it runs two segments, Packaging Solutions North America (PS NA) and EMEA (PS EMEA), and sold the Global Cellulose Fibers business (net proceeds $1.1B in Q1'26). On 29 Jan 2026 it announced a plan to spin off PS EMEA within 12-15 months. Profit depends on box volumes, containerboard prices, recovered-fibre and energy costs, and on mill reliability; IP is a scale player with an operating-cost gap to fix.

The 2027 EPS consensus ($3.02, +127% on FY26 $1.33) requires roughly +$1B of EBITDA to materialise; not durable evidence yet.
