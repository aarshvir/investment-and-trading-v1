## 2. Business in plain English
Global asset manager (AUM $1.79tn at 2026-06-30: equity $757bn, fixed income $441bn, alternatives $294bn, multi-asset $219bn, cash management $81bn) selling mutual funds, ETFs, separate accounts and private-market funds (secondary private equity, alternative credit, real estate, venture per the release) through advisers and institutions. It earns management fees on AUM (~42.7 bp on my arithmetic: annualised investment management fees $1,866.2m / average AUM $1,750.0bn, vs ~41.9 bp a year earlier) plus distribution fees largely passed through. Pivot from legacy active funds to alternatives, ETFs and custom solutions; Western Asset (fixed income) is the weak spot.
