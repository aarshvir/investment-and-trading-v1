# IBM - International Business Machines (agent F157, as of 2026-09-25 close, price $225.51)

## 1. Verdict
**INCLUDE-SMALL (half weight)** - a cash machine priced for roughly 0-3% free-cash-flow growth, but the named reservation is real: the July 2026 quarter missed badly, full-year growth guidance was cut, and the second-half recovery has not yet been shown. Horizon 12-36 months. Move to full weight only after Q3 (about 21 Oct 2026) shows Software and Infrastructure stabilising. Research only, not personal advice.

## 2. Business in plain English
IBM sells enterprise software (Red Hat/OpenShift, automation, data, mainframe transaction processing, and from 2026 HashiCorp and Confluent), IT and AI consulting, and hardware (IBM Z mainframes, Power servers, storage) with a large installed base of banks, insurers and governments. Software is about 45% of revenue and most of the profit; about 80% of Software revenue is recurring (10-Q). Moat: mainframe incumbency that pulls through high-margin software and long contracts, plus Red Hat's open-source position. Weakness: Consulting is flat and Infrastructure is cyclical around the mainframe launch cycle.

## 3. Why the model likes it and is it durable
b1 ranks IBM 410 of 496 (decile 2) overall, but the value family is strong (0.86); quality 0.25, momentum 0.19 and size/safety 0.16 are weak. The model sees a cheap multiple (17.4x NTM P/E) with decent return on equity (31%) after the stock de-rated, with high leverage (0.41). The cheapness is real on cash flow; the leverage is inflated by IBM Financing's $13.0B of debt, which is matched by client receivables, and by the $11.5B Confluent acquisition.

## 8. Bull and bear
Bull: (1) FCF of about $15-16B on a $212B equity value with a $6.76 dividend; (2) Red Hat +11%, Data +19%, Distributed Infrastructure +37% with $500M backlog, z17 program at "nearly 130 percent program-to-program" (July 14 letter); (3) management now expects "improved pre-tax income margin expansion for the full year".
Bear: (1) Software and Infrastructure missed in the last weeks of June on "numerous large deals" that slipped, and Transaction Processing fell 8%; (2) growth is partly bought: $10.5B of acquisitions this year, goodwill 49% of assets, $62.0B of debt; (3) AI could cannibalise Consulting (flat) and parts of software, and IBM has pledged more than $10B to quantum over five years plus a $5B "Lightwell" commitment.

## 9. Key risks and kill criteria
1. Full-year 2026 constant-currency revenue growth guidance cut below 4% (current: four-to-five percent).
2. Software segment revenue growth (constant currency, including acquisitions) below 3% in either Q3 or Q4 2026.
3. Free cash flow growth guidance of "about $1 billion" lowered, or FY2026 FCF (IBM definition) below $14.7B.
4. Consolidated debt excluding IBM Financing above $52B at any quarter-end without a matching rise in FCF guidance, or a downgrade to an A- equivalent or lower senior rating.
5. IBM Z plus Transaction Processing combined revenue still down more than 20% year on year in Q4 2026 (the first quarter against the z17 launch comparison is already past).
