# KDP - Keurig Dr Pepper (F159, wave 8, standard depth)

## 1. Verdict
**INCLUDE-SMALL** (half weight). Horizon 24-36 months. Named reservation: leverage after the debt-funded JDE Peet's purchase plus the 2027 split into two companies, which creates execution and valuation uncertainty. Triage rejected it on a 6.7x net-debt ratio built from partial-year EBITDA; on management's own pro-forma basis leverage is about 4.1x by year-end, and the price already discounts that risk.

## 2. Business in plain English
KDP sells Dr Pepper, Canada Dry, Mott's, Snapple, GHOST and Core to bottlers, retailers and distributors (U.S. Refreshment Beverages, 29.9% adjusted operating margin in Q2 26), plus Keurig K-Cup pods and brewers. On 1 April 2026 it bought JDE Peet's, a global coffee group (Jacobs, L'OR, Peet's). It plans to separate into Beverage Co. and Global Coffee Co.; the Q2 release says "preparing for a successful separation in early 2027".

## 3. Why the model likes it, and is that durable
b1: strong value and quality families (gross profit to assets 11%, ROE 5.7%, forward earnings yield 3.3% trailing) but leverage and asset growth percentiles are poor because of the acquisition (asset growth 61%). The attractiveness rests on U.S. Refreshment Beverages growth, which is durable, and on pre-deal balance-sheet metrics that no longer apply. The trailing data (EPS $0.99 GAAP) understate the combined company.

## 8. Bull and bear
Bull: (1) U.S. Refreshment Beverages grows double digits with 29.9% margins; (2) deleveraging from about $0.9bn H1 FCF and the Chobani $800m plus $125m; (3) the split may re-rate each part at a pure-play multiple.
Bear: (1) U.S. Coffee volume/mix -8.2% and operating income -24.7%; (2) Keurig antitrust damages claims of "more than $1.5 billion" with "not accrued for a loss contingency" (10-Q); (3) a $4.5bn preferred plus about $30bn debt leaves little room if coffee prices or integration disappoint; separation costs are unquantified.

## 9. Kill criteria (measurable)
1. Management leverage ratio (consolidated, company-defined, pro forma) above 4.5x at 31 Dec 2026 or guidance for it withdrawn.
2. Full-year 2026 constant-currency adjusted EPS growth guidance cut below "low-double-digit" (company release).
3. U.S. Refreshment Beverages volume/mix growth below 2% for two consecutive quarters.
4. Keurig antitrust: any adverse judgment or settlement above $500m (10-Q litigation note).
5. Separation timetable slips beyond 30 June 2027, or the preferred is not retired or refinanced on announced terms.
