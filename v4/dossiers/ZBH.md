# ZBH — Zimmer Biomet Holdings, Inc.

## 1. Verdict

**INCLUDE-SMALL** (half weight) — thesis horizon 12–36 months. At $91.04 (25 Sep 2026 close) the equity discounts roughly 1–2% a year of 10-year free-cash-flow growth against my evidence-based base case of 5%, and management raised 2026 adjusted-EPS guidance at both the April and August releases. The named reservations that stop it being full weight: (i) knee sales are flat (Q2 2026 organic +0.1%), (ii) the CFO seat has been held by an interim since 28 Apr 2026 and the Group President departs 30 Sep 2026, (iii) consolidated net debt is about 3.1x GAAP EBITDA with $1.17bn of notes due within eight months, and (iv) adjusted EPS was flat in Q2. Triage (Q07) said "value trap" and did not advance it; I partly overturn that: the cheapness is real and cash-backed, but it is cheap because growth is low, not mispriced.

## 2. Business in plain English

Zimmer Biomet makes hip and knee replacements, sports-medicine, extremity, trauma and dental implants, plus robots (ROSA) and software that help surgeons place them. It sells to hospitals and surgery centres through direct reps and distributors (US 57% of Q2 2026 sales, consolidated). Revenue comes from implants and instruments used per procedure, so volumes follow ageing-driven surgery demand. The moat is surgeon training, installed robots and hospital contracts; it is a top-three player in a consolidated market, but has lagged peers on growth for years (peer comparison below uses vendor data; I did not verify peer filings).

## 3. Why the model likes it — and is it durable?

About $30m of H1 2026 cost-of-sales relief came from a probable IEEPA tariff refund (10-Q, accession 0001193125-26-335044, Note 16), which will not recur.

## 8. Bull and bear

**Bull:** (1) FY26 guide ("$8.47 - $8.59") raised twice; Q2 US organic +4.6% and technology/data +21.5% (robots). (2) FCF yield about 7% funds $1bn of buybacks, shrinking the share count roughly 3% a year. (3) Restructuring ($147m of $155m incurred) targets $175m gross opex savings by 2027.
**Bear:** (1) Knees, the largest category, grew 0.1% organically in Q2 2026 and 0.9% in H1; adjusted EPS flat in Q2. (2) Leadership churn: CFO resigned (8-K, acc. 0001193125-26-183430, effective 28 Apr 2026), Group President departing (8-K, acc. 0001193125-26-385256), APAC president replaced. (3) Consolidated net debt 3.1x with refinancing needs, IRS disputes for 2013–2019, China distributor claims with unestimable loss.
