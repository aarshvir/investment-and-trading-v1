# Camden Property Trust (CPT) - Fundamental Diligence Dossier (F147, standard depth)

**Verdict: WATCH** - 12-36 month horizon. A well-run Sunbelt apartment REIT that is selling expensive California assets ($1.625B) to buy Sunbelt homes and shares at lower values, but same-property NOI is still shrinking (Q2'26 -1.4%), leverage peaked at 5.3x net debt to EBITDAre, and my base case returns about 6.8% a year against an 8.5% equity hurdle. Condition that changes it: a price near $83-89 (implied AFFO growth of 0-1%), or two quarters of positive blended lease-rate growth with post-sale leverage back under 4.5x.

**Triage reconciliation (Q14 said not advanced):** Q14 called the 69x P/E "optically inflated" and saw no catalyst until oversupply clears in 12-18 months. Adopted: the GAAP P/E is meaningless for a REIT (Yahoo forward EPS $1.09; the 2026 GAAP EPS guide of $9.76-$10.10 includes the California gain). Changed: there is a real capital-allocation catalyst (California sale, buybacks at about $104, acquisitions at about $310k per home) that triage missed; it is not enough to reach INCLUDE.

## 1. Business in plain English
Camden owns and operates about 176 multifamily communities (59,676 apartment homes at 30 Jun 2026 before the sale) in Sunbelt and other growth markets, plus a development pipeline. It earns rent; the public multiple is judged on Core FFO (funds from operations excluding one-offs) and AFFO (after maintenance capex). Competitive position: scale in Texas, Florida, Georgia, Carolinas, Arizona and Nashville, in-house development and operations, investment-grade balance sheet. The 2023-26 supply wave has pushed new-lease rates negative.

## 5. Growth drivers, capital recycling and moat
California sale: per the 10-Q (Note 5) and 8-K of 30 Jul 2026, 11 communities (3,620 homes, Los Angeles/Orange County and San Diego/Inland Empire) were classified as held for sale at 30 Jun 2026 at net book value $625.3M and "sold ... on July 29, 2026 ... for an aggregate sales price of approximately $1.625 billion" (about $449k per home; implied gain about $1.0B, reflected in the $9.27 EPS guidance change). "Approximately $0.9 billion of the proceeds will be used to retire balances outstanding under the Company's unsecured revolving credit facility and commercial paper program." Acquisitions: five operating properties for $449.3M in H1'26 (1,422 homes, about $316k per home) and two in July 2026 for $196.1M (639 homes, about $307k per home), plus $45.0M of land. Buybacks: H1'26 4,062,166 shares at an average $104.08 ($422.9M); Q2 1,429,136 shares at $100.78; $297.9M authorisation remains. Its own implied enterprise value per home is about $240-270k (my estimate including development assets), a discount to the $307-316k it is paying: this is the main value argument. Growth depends on the supply wave clearing; no catalyst dates are available from filings.

## 8. Bull and bear
Bull: (1) selling California at about $449k per home while buying Sunbelt at about $310k and repurchasing at about $104 creates NAV and deleverages; (2) same-property revenue is still +0.2% YTD and renewals +2.8%, so rent growth reaccelerates when deliveries fall; (3) 4.3% dividend covered 1.4x by AFFO with investment-grade funding.
Bear: (1) same-property NOI has worsened four quarters in a row to -1.4% and new-lease rates are -3.3%; (2) leverage of 5.3x, floating-rate and 364-day term debt plus $501M of 2026 notes refinancing keep funding risk present; (3) management is growing the asset base through acquisitions while NOI shrinks, so FFO growth is capital-driven.

## 9. Key risks and kill criteria
1. Same-property NOI growth (ex-California) worse than -2.0% YoY for two consecutive quarters (Q2'26: -1.4%; source: earnings supplement "Same Property Results").
2. Net debt to annualised Adjusted EBITDAre (consolidated, supplement) above 5.0x at both the Q3'26 and Q4'26 reports (Q2'26: 5.3x).
3. FY26 Core FFO guidance midpoint cut below $6.50 (current $6.75).
4. Blended same-property lease-rate growth below -2.0% for two consecutive quarters (Q2'26: -0.2%; Q1'26: -1.6%).
5. Same-property occupancy below 94.5% in any quarter (Q2'26: 95.7%).
