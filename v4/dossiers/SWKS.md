# SWKS - Skyworks Solutions (diligence agent F164, standard depth)

## 1. Verdict
**WATCH** (12-36 months). Execution is solid (8 of 8 recent quarters beat Street EPS per d4), but at $89.34 (25 Sep 2026 close) the price sits above the Street mean target of $68.35 (d4, 17 analysts) and discounts most of the Qorvo synergy plan. Implied 10-year growth exceeds my base case (section 7). Condition that would change it: a post-close pro forma print showing SBC-adjusted earnings per share at or above about $4.70 with Apple below 55% of combined revenue, or a lower entry price.
Triage (Q12_triage.json, "no", 2/2/2) is partly overturned: the "declining revenue / Apple share erosion" call is right on concentration, wrong on trend (four straight guidance beats) but the deal now dominates the case.

## 2. Business in plain English
Skyworks makes the radio-frequency chips that let phones, cars, Wi-Fi routers and data-centre gear talk wirelessly. Apple is the anchor customer: "Apple ... in the aggregate accounted for 67%, 69%, and 66% of the Company's net revenue" in fiscal 2025, 2024 and 2023 (FY25 10-K, acc 0000004127-25-000085). The company is about to merge with Qorvo (its closest RF peer): each Qorvo share converts into "0.960 ... of a share of Skyworks common stock and $32.50 in cash" (10-Q, acc 0000004127-26-000049). Broad Markets (auto, data centre, Wi-Fi, industrial) is the diversification story.

## 8. Bull and bear
Bull: (1) Broad Markets (auto, data centre, Wi-Fi) grew double digits and a multi-generational design win with "a leading Android OEM" is "expected to generate over $1 billion in revenue through 2030" (Q2 release); (2) all regulatory clearances received 30 Sep 2026, close expected about 5 Oct 2026 (8-K, acc 0001104659-26-112129 - post-cutoff); (3) $500M synergies on a combined base of about $7.7B revenue.
Bear: (1) Apple was 67% of FY25 revenue (Qorvo customer mix not reviewed), so single-customer risk stays high; (2) GAAP operating margin about 5% and FCF negative in two of the last three quarters; (3) deal debt takes net cash to net debt, dividend gone, and shares trade above every Street target.

## 9. Key risks and kill criteria
1. Combined (or Skyworks standalone, if no close) quarterly revenue below $1,010M for the guided September 2026 quarter, or two consecutive quarters of y/y revenue decline after close.
2. Consolidated net debt/non-GAAP EBITDA above 2.5x at the first post-close balance sheet, or no net debt reduction within four quarters of close.
3. GAAP gross margin below 38% (Q3 FY26: 40.1%) for two consecutive quarters.
4. Apple above 60% of combined net revenue in the first full post-close 10-K.
5. Cumulative restructuring plus acquisition charges above $600M in the first 12 months post-close.
