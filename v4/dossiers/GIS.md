# GIS - General Mills, Inc. (research only; not personal investment advice)

**1. Verdict: INCLUDE-SMALL.** A packaged-food and pet-food maker priced at 10.9x the midpoint ($3.10) of guided FY27 adjusted EPS ("Adjusted diluted earnings are expected to be between $3.00 and $3.20 per share", 8-K 0001628280-26-063141) with a 7.3% dividend yield on the $33.64 price; the 25 Sep 2026 price implies about -2.2% a year free-cash-flow growth for ten years, below my base of about +2.5%. Named reservation: consolidated net debt is about 4.0x TTM adjusted EBITDA (my calculation) and the $2.44 dividend takes about 79% of guided adjusted EPS, so there is little room for another earnings miss. Horizon 24-48 months. The triage rejection (cheap because declining) is overturned on price, not on quality: it is a shrinking-earnings year being priced as permanent decline.

**2. Business in plain English.** General Mills sells cereal (Cheerios, Lucky Charms), snacks, soups, baking and meals through US grocers, plus Blue Buffalo pet food, a foodservice arm and an international business. It earns a spread between brand prices and ingredient/manufacturing cost, and funds growth with large cost-saving programmes. Its edge is shelf scale and brand recognition; its weakness is that US centre-of-store volumes are falling and it spent fiscal 2026 cutting base prices to win volume back.

**3. Why the model likes it.** b1 value and yield families (7.0% yield, 10.6x forward P/E from d4); quant triage flagged negative reported margin, -2.8% revenue and -13% momentum. The negative GAAP margin is real but non-cash (FY26 goodwill/brand impairments plus the Brazil valuation loss, section 6); revenue decline is mostly the yogurt divestiture (organic net sales flat in Q1 FY27). The cheapness is therefore not an accounting artefact, but it is cheap because earnings are falling, and the FY27 guide quoted in section 5 ("Adjusted diluted earnings are expected to be between $3.00 and $3.20 per share") is below the $3.55 earned in FY26.

**5. Guidance track record (full-year adjusted EPS and organic sales; verbatim).**
- 23 Sep 2026 (Q1 FY27, 8-K 0001628280-26-063141): "General Mills reaffirmed its full-year financial targets" - "Organic net sales are expected to range between down 1.5 percent and up 0.5 percent." "Adjusted operating profit is expected to be down 13 percent to down 8 percent in constant currency." "Adjusted diluted earnings are expected to be between $3.00 and $3.20 per share." "Free cash flow conversion is expected to be approximately 95 percent of adjusted after-tax earnings." Unchanged vs 1 Jul 2026; only the reported-sales headwind moved from "approximately 2 percent" to "approximately 4 percent" (53rd-week comparison and Brazil sale).
- 1 Jul 2026 (Q4 FY26, 8-K 0001628280-26-046337): same four targets, initial FY27 guide; "$750 million" savings in FY27 and a "$3 billion" cumulative savings target by fiscal 2030 in the headline. Actual FY26 against the 18 Mar guide: adjusted EPS $3.55, "down 16 percent in constant currency" (guide was down 16 to 20), organic sales -2% (guide "down 1.5 to 2 percent"); free cash flow conversion "85 percent of adjusted after-tax earnings" against the guide "at least 95 percent" - a miss.
- 18 Mar 2026 (Q3 FY26, 8-K 0001628280-26-019020): "Organic net sales are expected to be down 1.5 to 2 percent." "Adjusted operating profit and adjusted diluted EPS are both expected to be down 16 to 20 percent in constant currency." "Free cash flow conversion is expected to be at least 95 percent of adjusted after-tax earnings." Labelled "reaffirmed" but the previous range was down 10 to 15 percent (below); I found no intervening Item 2.02 8-K, so the date and channel of that cut are not verified from filings.
- 17 Dec 2025 (Q2 FY26, 8-K 0001628280-25-057486): "Organic net sales are expected to range between down 1 percent and up 1 percent." "Adjusted operating profit and adjusted diluted EPS are both expected to be down 10 to 15 percent in constant currency." (unchanged from 17 Sep 2025, 8-K 0001193125-25-205477).
Pattern: FY26 EPS guide cut by about 6 pts at the low end between December and March, then delivered at the top of the lowered range; cash conversion missed.

**8. Bull / bear.** Bull: (1) FY27 is the trough: it laps the 53rd week, incentive-pay normalisation and divested yogurt earnings (company: headwinds "approximately 9 points on operating profit and 11 points on EPS"), and organic sales were flat in Q1 with North America Retail retail-sales growth improving 2 pts sequentially; (2) $750m FY27 cost savings and International and Foodservice growing profit +14% and +12%; (3) price investment is "completed", so volume recovery plus pet innovation could restore growth. Bear: (1) organic sales -3% in Retail and Pet profit -12% in Q1; adjusted EPS fell 16% in FY26 and is guided down 10-15% again; (2) about 4.0x net leverage, 79% payout and free cash flow that missed its own conversion guide (85% vs at least 95%); (3) CEO succession (announced 29 Sep 2026) and a restructuring-heavy plan add execution risk.

**9. Key risks and kill criteria (thesis-invalidation triggers).**
- Organic net sales below -2.0% in two consecutive quarters (FY27 guide: "Organic net sales are expected to range between down 1.5 percent and up 0.5 percent."; Q1 FY27 flat).
- Annual dividend cut below $2.44 per share, or guided adjusted EPS falling so that the $2.44 dividend exceeds 90% of the adjusted EPS midpoint of the guide "between $3.00 and $3.20 per share" (79% now).
- Consolidated net debt (current portion of long-term debt + notes payable + long-term debt less cash, condensed consolidated balance sheet) above 4.5x TTM adjusted EBITDA (about 4.0x at 30 Aug 2026).
- FY27 adjusted diluted EPS guidance cut below the current low end of $3.00 ("between $3.00 and $3.20 per share").
- Any further non-cash goodwill or brand impairment above $500 million (Blue Buffalo or Progresso flagged in the 10-Q).
