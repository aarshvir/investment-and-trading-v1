# WAT - Waters Corporation (diligence agent F166, wave 8)

**1. Verdict: WATCH** (thesis horizon 12-36 months). An excellent franchise whose guidance has been raised at every step this year, but the 25 Sep 2026 price of $433.54 (about 29.8x the FY26 adjusted EPS guide midpoint) already needs about 9.7% a year of free-cash-flow growth for ten years, versus about 8% that organic growth, synergies and the BD Biosciences recovery support. Condition to upgrade: a price near $380 (implied equals base), or free cash flow visibly above $1.3bn a year once integration costs roll off.

Triage (Q04/Q06/Q08: "No", "digesting leveraged BD Biosciences merger", inorganic growth, integration/leverage risk) is confirmed on valuation and integration, but overturned on leverage: consolidated total debt of $5,086m (4 Jul 2026 debt note) against estimated EBITDA near $2.0bn (my estimate, not company-reported) is moderate, about 2.2x net.

**2. Business.** Waters sells liquid-chromatography and mass-spectrometry instruments and the consumables and service that run on them (recurring revenue), mainly to pharma, industrial and academic labs. Since 9 Feb 2026 it also owns BD's Biosciences and Diagnostic Solutions businesses (reverse Morris Trust; BD shareholders received 38.5m new shares, about 39.2% of the company, 10-Q Note 4). Four divisions: Analytical Sciences (Q2 revenue $669m), Biosciences ($368m), Advanced Diagnostics ($449m diagnostic solutions plus $72m clinical), Materials Sciences ($87m). Moat: installed base, regulated-method lock-in (pharma QC) and consumables pull-through.

**3. Why the model likes it.** b1 rank 481 of ~480 live set (composite_qvm 0.09); momentum 0.69 (12-1 month +38%), value 0.15, quality 0.015. The b1 inputs (asset growth +425%, net issuance +65%, SUE -3.6, ROE 1%) are merger artefacts. The model signal is momentum; fundamentals are good but the screen scores are not evidence.

**8. Bull / bear.** Bull: (1) instrument-replacement cycle and end-market recovery broadening (Pharma and A&G low-double-digit in Q2); (2) BDS growth accelerating under the 180-day plan (+4% in Q2 vs lower pre-close), with revenue synergies; (3) four consecutive EPS guidance raises (Aug 2025 to Aug 2026). Bear: (1) the multiple needs high growth for a decade; (2) 60% of FY EPS is in H2 and cash conversion in H1 is weak; (3) integration risk: deferred closings, TSA reliance, a new head of the largest division after the incumbent resigned (below), and 72% of assets in goodwill/intangibles.

4. Consolidated net debt (debt-note "Total debt" less balance-sheet cash) above $4.5bn at fiscal year-end 2026 (4 Jul 2026: $5,086m less $539m = $4.55bn).
5. Company-defined adjusted free cash flow for 2H26 below $500m (H1: $161m).
