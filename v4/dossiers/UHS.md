## 1. Verdict
**INCLUDE-SMALL** — the price (7.8x the midpoint of 2026 adjusted EPS guidance, ~8% TTM FCF yield) implies roughly zero 10-year FCF growth against a base case of +3% to +5%, so triage ("cheap but declining EPS") is overturned: EPS is not declining (FY2025 adjusted EPS $21.74 vs guidance midpoint $22.97 for 2026; TTM GAAP $24.50), but the named reservation is large and real: roughly $1.5bn of projected 2026 Medicaid supplemental-payment net benefit, about 56% of guided Adjusted EBITDA, is legislatively exposed from 2028. Half weight, horizon 24–36 months.

## 2. Business in plain English
UHS operates acute-care hospitals and a large network of behavioral-health facilities (facility counts not verified in this pass). Acute care sells inpatient and outpatient hospital services paid by commercial insurers, Medicare and Medicaid; behavioral health (psychiatric and addiction treatment) is paid mostly by Medicaid, commercial plans and state programs. The moat is regulated local capacity (certificates of need, licensing) and scale in behavioral care where supply is scarce; the economics depend heavily on state Medicaid supplemental payments.

## 3. Why the model likes it, and is it durable
Cheap multiple, strong buyback (Q2'25 65.0m diluted shares -> Q2'26 59.9m, about -8%), same-facility revenue +8.2% acute / +7.4% behavioral in Q2'26. Durability question is earnings quality: 2025 and 2026 results include large state-program catch-ups (Q2'25 +$101m pre-tax; Q2'26 +$72m net, incl. $100m Florida prior-period benefit less a $28m malpractice reserve increase).

## 8. Bull / bear
Bull: (1) implied growth is ~zero while same-facility revenue grows 7–8%; (2) repurchases shrink the share count ~8% a year; (3) Florida directed-payment approval beyond Sep 2025 would add upside not in the forecast.
Bear: (1) OBBBA caps on provider taxes and supplemental payments target the ~$1.5bn benefit; (2) 2026 guidance was cut in July and the 1.65% notes are being refinanced at ~5.5–6%; (3) behavioral-health litigation tail (Cumberland ~43 more plaintiffs; Nevada $500m punitive verdict vacated but retrial expected 2028).

## 9. Kill criteria
1. Full-year Adjusted EPS guidance midpoint cut again below $21.50 (now $22.97), or actual FY2026 adjusted EPS below $21.50.
2. Aggregate net benefit from Provider Tax Programs (10-Q Medicaid table) projected below $1.2bn for a fiscal year (now $1,481m for 2026), or a state directed-payment program is denied by CMS.
3. Consolidated net debt (carrying debt less cash) / Adjusted EBITDA net of NCI above 3.0x (now ~1.8x pre-Talkspace).
4. Same-facility acute and behavioral adjusted admissions both negative for two consecutive quarters (Q2'26: +2.9% and +0.5%).
5. A professional-liability or government settlement/judgment against UHS of Delaware above $250m becomes final and enforceable.
