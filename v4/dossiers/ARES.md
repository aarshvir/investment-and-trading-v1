# ARES - Ares Management Corporation (agent F163, standard depth, information cutoff 2026-09-25 close)

**1. Verdict: INCLUDE-SMALL** (horizon 24-36 months). Reservation (price as of 2026-09-25 close, d4 snapshot; figures from 10-Q acc. 0001628280-26-054538 and Q2-26 release acc. 0001628280-26-051191): the price ($122.01) already needs about 8% a year of 10-year growth in per-share distributable earnings, management fee growth slowed from +25% in Q1-26 to +15% in Q2-26, and the dividend is 100% of trailing four-quarter after-tax realized income. This reverses the triage ("advance: false", 9.1x "leverage"): that leverage figure is mostly non-recourse consolidated-fund debt, not corporate debt (section 6).

**2. Business in plain English.** Ares manages money for pension funds, insurers and wealthy individuals, mainly private loans (credit, 66% of AUM), plus real estate and infrastructure, secondaries and private equity. It earns management fees on committed or invested capital, performance fees (carried interest and incentive fees) and other fees. At 30 Jun 2026 AUM was $671.3bn and fee-paying AUM $409.9bn (both +17% YoY); 84% of AUM and 94% of management fees came from perpetual or long-dated funds (Q2-26 earnings presentation, EX-99.2, acc. 0001628280-26-051191).

**3. Why the model likes it.** b1 factors lean on growth and quality families (GAAP trailing P/E 55.7x is an artefact of GAAP EPS $2.19 after non-controlling-interest and carry accounting; on after-tax realized income the multiple is 23.6x trailing). Durable part: recurring fee base. Fragile part: realized performance income (Q3-26 prelim about $10m) and fundraising dependent on private-credit sentiment.
