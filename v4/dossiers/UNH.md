# UNH — UnitedHealth Group Incorporated

## 1. Verdict

**WATCH** — thesis horizon 12–36 months. The franchise is intact and 2026 is a genuine recovery year (guidance raised at every 2026 release), but at $376.59 the stock is priced at 19x 2026 adjusted EPS, a premium to managed-care peers, while the two issues that matter most are unresolved: formal criminal and civil DOJ requests about Medicare Advantage, and IRS transfer-pricing proposals. My base case returns about 6% a year over three years, below a 9% hurdle; it becomes INCLUDE-SMALL at about $350 or lower, or once the DOJ matter is closed without charge and H2 medical cost ratio lands inside guidance. Triage (Q08) also said not to advance, citing the DOJ probe; filings confirm the probe exists, so I agree on direction, but for valuation-plus-tail-risk reasons rather than "value trap".

## 2. Business in plain English

UnitedHealth is America's largest health insurer (UnitedHealthcare: 48.5m people served at 30 Jun 2026; Medicare Advantage 7.565m, commercial, Medicaid) and the owner of Optum, a services arm (Optum Health clinics and value-based care, Optum Insight data/software, Optum Rx pharmacy benefits). It earns premiums minus medical costs, plus fees. Scale in claims data, networks and pharmacy is the moat; the weakness is that most profit hangs on government-funded Medicare and Medicaid rates and on how coding and medical costs are policed.

## 8. Bull and bear

**Bull:** (1) Guidance raised at each 2026 release; reported operating earnings guide rose from ">$24,000" to ">$25,450" m. (2) Optum Health and Insight margins improving (Optum margin 6.2% vs 4.6%); MCR actions (pricing, benefit design) are working. (3) $5bn+ buyback and dividend growth at a 5.0% earnings yield (guided net earnings over market cap).
**Bear:** (1) Formal criminal and civil DOJ requests on Medicare programme conduct; 2017 False Claims Act suit still pending on the DOJ's challenge to the Special Master. (2) Shrinking book: MA members −965k since year-end 2025 and UHC people served −1.6m YoY, revenue flat. (3) Earnings rely on reserve development ($860m in Q2) and an implied H2 medical cost ratio step-up; IRS proposed transfer-pricing adjustments for 2017–2020.

## 9. Key risks and kill criteria (measurable thesis-invalidation triggers)

1. A criminal charge or civil complaint against UNH or an Optum unit on Medicare Advantage coding, or any DOJ settlement or penalty above $3bn.
2. FY2026 adjusted-EPS guidance cut below the "$19.50" low end at the October 2026 or later releases.
3. UnitedHealthcare people served below 47.0m at 31 Dec 2026 (48.5m at 30 Jun 2026), or Medicare Advantage below 7.2m (7.565m).
4. Consolidated debt-to-capital above 43% (41.2% at 30 Jun 2026).
5. Reported medical care ratio above 91.0% in Q3 or Q4 2026 (Q2: 86.7%; guide "88.1% ± 25 bps" implies about 90–91% in H2).

## 11. Red-flag scan

- **DOJ:** 8-K (acc. 0000731766-25-000224, 24 Jul 2025) says: "The Company has now begun complying with formal criminal and civil requests from the Department." The 10-Q (acc. 0000731766-26-000197, Note 7) states the DOJ's 2017 False Claims Act case (whistleblower 2011): a Special Master recommended summary judgment for UNH in March 2025, the DOJ asked the court to reject it in April 2025, and "The Company cannot reasonably estimate the outcome". RADV audits may adjust payments retroactively.
- **IRS:** Notices of Proposed Adjustment received 6 Mar 2026 for 2017–2020 "seeking to significantly increase taxable income"; UNH disagrees, no amount disclosed.
- **Governance:** new lead independent director and Public Responsibility Committee (Q2 release); director added (8-K, acc. 0000731766-25-000314); no auditor change or restatement found.
- **Insiders (Form 4, since 1 Oct 2025):** three open-market sales totalling 2,469 shares (one insider), no open-market purchases; other entries awards and tax withholding.
