**2. Business in plain English.** Mosaic mines potash (Canada, formerly also New Mexico) and makes phosphate fertilizer (Florida, Louisiana) and sells both to farmers worldwide, with a large distribution and production business in Brazil (Mosaic Fertilizantes). Potash is a steady, high-margin mine business (Q2 2026 MOP price $275 per tonne against $84 cash cost). Phosphate is a processing business squeezed between the fertilizer price and the cost of sulfur, ammonia and phosphate rock. It also holds about $1.76bn of Ma'aden (Saudi Arabian Mining Company) shares at fair value.
