# ARE - Alexandria Real Estate Equities (F159, wave 8, standard depth)

## 1. Verdict
**WATCH.** Thesis horizon 24-36 months. Cheap on cash earnings (about 8.6x run-rate FFO) and the triage's "disqualifying" label is too harsh, but leverage is 7.0x, FFO per share is falling about 25% year on year, and $2.9bn of asset sales needed to fix the balance sheet are only about 6% done. Condition to upgrade: asset sales above $1.5bn closed at or near book value, with quarterly leverage below 6.5x.

## 2. Business in plain English
Alexandria owns lab and office campuses clustered in Boston/Cambridge, San Francisco Bay Area and San Diego and leases them to biotech, pharma and technology tenants on long leases (weighted-average remaining term 7.7 years). It earns rent plus annual escalators (97% of leases) and builds new space. Its moat was its dominant cluster position; the current problem is weak biotech funding, empty new lab space and a heavy development pipeline funded with debt.

## 3. Why the model likes it, and is that durable
The b1 profile is mixed: very strong value (book-to-price percentile 0.94, earnings-yield family high on asset-based measures) with weak quality (ROE -6.6%, accruals and leverage poor). It is a cheapness signal, not a quality signal. The cheapness is partly an artefact: 2025 GAAP net loss of $8.44 per share reflects impairments, so GAAP earnings and book value understate and overstate in different ways; FFO is the right lens. Durable only if asset sales succeed.

## 7. Valuation and reverse DCF (25 Sep 2026 close $49.93)
V1 row: none, v1_verdict = null. Price is 7.8x guided 2026 FFO ($6.40, "midpoint of $6.40" per the 2Q26 release) and 8.6x the 4Q26 run-rate ($1.45 x 4 = $5.80). Market capitalisation $8.6bn; consolidated enterprise value about $25bn (aggregator). Reverse DCF: at a 9% equity cost of capital and 8.6x multiple (11.6% FFO yield) with roughly 2% of FFO needed as maintenance and leasing capital, the price implies FFO declining about 2% per year forever. My base is flat run-rate FFO of about $5.6-5.8 (asset sales dilutive, lease-up of 4% leased-not-occupied space offsets), bear $4.8, bull $6.2 with recovering lab demand. Implied growth is below my base; the discount is the risk premium for leverage and execution, hence WATCH rather than INCLUDE.

## 8. Bull and bear
Bull: (1) 1.4m RSF signed but not yet occupied adds about 4 points of occupancy by about Nov 2026; (2) 8.6x FFO already prices a shrinking company; (3) investment-grade balance sheet with 9.7-year debt term and a new $5bn revolver.
Bear: (1) leverage 7.0x and a $2.9bn disposal plan only 6% done; sales at weak prices would realise losses against book; (2) five development projects with unresolved strategy and pivots to lower-rent advanced-technology uses at "lower rental rates and stabilized yields than our initial underwriting"; (3) a securities class action (filed 25 Nov 2025; motion to dismiss filed 20 May 2026) and derivative suits.

## 9. Kill criteria (measurable)
1. Completed 2026 dispositions below $1.0bn at 31 Dec 2026 (company supplemental, versus the $2.9bn midpoint of "dispositions, sales of partial interests, and other capital sources" guidance).
2. Net debt and preferred stock to Adjusted EBITDA (consolidated, quarter annualised) above 6.5x at 4Q26.
3. FFO per share as adjusted guidance (midpoint now "$6.40") cut below $6.20 for 2026, or 2027 guidance midpoint (due 2 Dec 2026) below $5.60.
4. Operating occupancy (operating properties, 10-Q/supplement) below 85% for two consecutive quarters.
5. A further common dividend reduction below $0.72 per quarter.
