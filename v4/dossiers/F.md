## 1. Verdict
**INCLUDE-SMALL** (half weight), horizon 18–36 months. Triage said "not advanced: EV losses, recall costs, thin profitability". Half right: GAAP results are poor (TTM GAAP net loss of about $7.5B from special items **[Corrected 2026-10-06: release ROIC table: TTM net loss attributable to Ford is $7.4B (-8,182 - 435 + 1,221)]**) and the EV write-offs are real capital destroyed. But the EV bleed is now a bounded, guided line (Model e loss "approximately $4.0 billion" in 2026), guidance was raised twice in 2026, and at $12.71 the price implies roughly 1% a year of free-cash growth (section 7). Named reservation: adjusted FCF flatters cash (excludes restructuring and funded pension contributions), and H2 must deliver about $5.8–6.8B of the $6.0–7.0B FCF guide after only $0.2B in H1.

## 2. Business in plain English
Ford sells trucks, SUVs and vans (F-Series, Bronco, Explorer, Transit) through three segments: Ford Blue (gas and hybrid vehicles), Ford Pro (commercial vehicles plus paid software, 879,000 subscriptions), and Ford Model e (EVs, currently loss-making), plus Ford Credit, its captive finance arm. Profit comes from high-margin trucks and commercial customers; moat is the F-Series franchise and Ford Pro's fleet relationships, not technology. Highly cyclical, tariff- and commodity-exposed.

## 3. Why the model likes it
b1 shows a cheap value rank with weak quality and modest momentum. Durable part: Ford Blue margin went from 2.6% to 4.4% in Q2 2026 vs Q2 2025, material and warranty cost reductions are on track for about $1B. Artefact part: 1Q26 adjusted EBIT of $3.5B includes "a $1.3 billion one-time IEEPA tariff benefit" (1Q26 release), and the 2025 comparison base is depressed by the Novelis aluminium plant fire. I therefore treat 2026 adjusted EBIT ex-IEEPA (about $8.7–9.7B) as the run-rate starting point.

## 8. Bull and bear
**Bull:** (1) Ranges raised at every 2026 print, with Blue margin recovering and Novelis a second-half tailwind of about $1B. (2) Model e loss ("approximately $4.0 billion") is capped and the BOSK commitment removed; EV cash burn falling. (3) The FCF range "$6.0 billion to $7.0 billion" is 11.6–13.5% of the $51.7B market cap, plus a 4.7% dividend.
**Bear:** (1) H1 adjusted FCF only $0.2B; the full-year FCF range needs a steep H2, and adjusted FCF excludes restructuring and pension cash. (2) Recurring multi-billion special items, $17.6B warranty accrual, tariff and commodity exposure (about $2B commodity headwind per 1Q26 release). (3) Auto-cycle risk: a 25% EPS miss on volume alone takes EPS near $1.4.

## 9. Key risks and kill criteria
1. Company adjusted FCF below $1.0B in 2H 2026 cumulative (the full-year range "$6.0 billion to $7.0 billion" implies about $5.8B or more).
2. Full-year 2026 adjusted EBIT range cut below $9.0B (current "Adjusted EBIT of $10.0 billion to $11.0 billion"), or 2027 adjusted EBIT margin below 5%.
3. Ford Model e quarterly EBIT loss worse than $1.2B (2Q26: -$919M) in two consecutive quarters.
4. Warranty and field-service accrual (net of recoveries) above $19.0B, or pre-existing warranty revisions above $1.0B in a half-year (H1 2026: $475M).
5. Company excluding Ford Credit cash below $15B or the regular dividend reduced below $0.15 a quarter.
