# L - Loews Corporation (NYSE: L) - standard-depth diligence (agent F145)

**1. Verdict: WATCH** (thesis horizon 24-36 months). A well-run holding company (CNA insurance, Boardwalk pipelines, Loews Hotels) buying back stock, but at $104.82 it trades at 1.12x book value and 12.9x trailing earnings for a business earning about 8.9% on equity, and the "holding-company discount" that the triage relied on is not visible. Would become INCLUDE-SMALL at about $90-95 (about 1.0x book excluding AOCI) or if CNA's underlying combined ratio returns below 93%. Research only; not personal advice.

**Triage reconciliation.** Quick triage (L row: "Diversified holding co at NAV discount"; "Eps decline vs. one-time comp"; grouped with AFL, MET, PFG, PRU, AIG as "cheapness reflects average-to-declining quality rather than mispriced growth"). The EPS decline point is real for H1-26 at CNA (core income down, combined ratio up 3.1 points). Triage upheld.

**2. Business in plain English.** Loews is a holding company. Its main asset is about 92% of CNA Financial, a US commercial property-casualty insurer (62% of H1-26 and 67% of trailing-four-quarter net income attributable to Loews). It also owns 100% of Boardwalk Pipelines (natural gas transport and storage, earning fees under contracts), 100% of Loews Hotels & Co (hotels, many in joint ventures, including Universal Orlando properties) and 53% of Altium Packaging (rigid plastic packaging, equity-accounted, not consolidated). It has a long record of buying back its own shares; the moat is CNA's underwriting franchise and Boardwalk's contracted pipeline network, and the structure adds little by itself.
