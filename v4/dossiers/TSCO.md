## 2. Business in plain English
Largest US rural-lifestyle retailer (2,463 Tractor Supply stores in 49 states plus 209 Petsense stores at 27 Jun 2026; 10-Q 0000916365-26-000059). Sells needs-based goods (animal feed, fencing, tools, pet and livestock supplies) to hobby farmers, ranchers and rural homeowners. Earns merchandise margin (~37%) and grows through ~100 new stores a year, remodels and pet-health add-ons (Allivet online pharmacy, VIP Petcare mobile vet bought 28 May 2026 for $133.8M). Moat: rural store density and consumables traffic; weakness: discretionary/big-ticket and pet categories.

## 8. Bull and bear
Bull: (1) needs-based consumables stayed resilient and the company calls the May miss "unusually adverse conditions" with April and June comps positive; (2) 28 Tractor Supply stores opened in Q2 and new-store sales are 3.6% of sales; (3) consolidated net debt of 1.1x TTM EBITDA leaves room for buybacks and dividend growth ($0.24 vs $0.23).
Bear: (1) FY26 EPS guide (GAAP $1.78-$1.88; adjusted $1.90-$2.00) is below FY25 GAAP EPS of $2.06, which itself was barely above FY24's $2.04; (2) negative traffic (-1.7%) and inventory +13.9%; (3) strategy reset: Petsense restructuring (~75 closures), long-term targets withdrawn, FCF thin at ~2-4% of market cap.

## 9. Kill criteria
1. Comparable store sales below 0% in both Q3 2026 and Q4 2026 reports.
2. FY2026 adjusted diluted EPS guidance cut below $1.90 (the low end of the 23 Jul 2026 range).
3. FY2026 adjusted operating margin rate guidance cut below 8.5% (low end of "8.5% to 8.8%").
4. Consolidated net debt (10-Q "Total outstanding borrowings" plus finance leases less cash) above 2.0x TTM EBITDA (currently ~1.1x).
5. The updated long-term framework due with Q4 2026 earnings gives annual EPS growth below 5%.
