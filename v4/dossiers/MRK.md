Triage said "no: cheap but patent cliff"; the cliff is confirmed (Keytruda biosimilar competition "could begin in December 2028", 10-K), but the "cheap" part no longer holds: at $148.78 the stock trades at about 17x 2026 non-GAAP EPS after adding back the two one-time acquisition charges, and 15.6x FY27 consensus. A great franchise priced for more than I can underwrite: WATCH. What would change it: a price near $115 (my base value per share) or evidence that Keytruda Qlex plus Winrevair, Lipfendra and Ohtuvayre replace more than half of Keytruda's post-2028 loss.

## 2. Business in plain English
Merck sells prescription drugs and vaccines (about 89% of sales) and animal-health products (about 11%). Keytruda, an immunotherapy for many cancers, was $8.37B of $16.6B Q2 2026 sales (50%); the next largest are Gardasil (HPV vaccine, $1.17B), Winrevair (pulmonary hypertension, $588M, +75%) and Januvia (falling, -31%). It makes money from patent-protected pricing power, funded by about $16B a year of R&D plus buying outside biotechs. Its moat is patents and clinical data, which expire: Keytruda's primary compound patent in December 2028.

## 8. Bull and bear
Bull: (1) Keytruda is still growing (+5% Q2) and the subcutaneous Qlex ($463M in Q2) may defend a large part of the franchise past 2028; (2) new launches: Winrevair $588M (+75%), Welireg $271M (+67%), Ohtuvayre, and FDA approval of LIPFENDRA (first oral PCSK9) in Q2 2026; (3) strong cash generation ($16B FCF) funds a 2.3% dividend and continued deal-making.
Bear: (1) Keytruda is 50% of sales and loses exclusivity Dec 2028 (10-K), and the company expects Keytruda to be selected for IRA price-setting in 2027, effective 1 Jan 2029 (10-Q); (2) leverage rising: net debt about $45.5B after $16.0B of Cidara and Terns acquisitions in 2026 (plus Verona in Oct 2025), charged to earnings as acquired R&D, with little evidence yet of returns; (3) pricing pressure: MFN agreement, Medicaid GENEROUS model, Germany GKV-BStabG law ("significant downward pressure on sales in Germany" from 2027), plus Januvia -31%, Gardasil China/MDL exposure.

## 9. Key risks and kill criteria (thesis-invalidation triggers; measurable)
1. Keytruda/Keytruda Qlex worldwide sales growth below 0% year-on-year in any quarter before biosimilar entry (Q2 2026: +5%; Q1: +12%).

3. A further single acquisition or licence with a one-time charge or cash price above $5B announced within the next 12 months.

5. Full-year sales guidance midpoint for 2027 below $66B when first issued (2026 guide $66.3-67.3B).
