## 1. Verdict
**WATCH.** A genuinely excellent rural convenience-store and prepared-food compounder, but at $598.46 the price needs roughly 10.5% a year of free-cash-flow growth for ten years (reverse DCF, 8.5% discount rate), above my evidence-based base case of about 8%. Not the business, the price: a great business priced for more than it can deliver. Horizon 12-36 months. What would change it: a price near or below about $490 (my base-case value is about $486 per share), or evidence that free cash flow is compounding at 10% or more. Triage (Q03_triage.json: advance=false on price vs growth) is **confirmed**, but the stock is already 27% below its 25 Aug high of $824.92 (Yahoo daily closes), so it is closer to fair than triage's "27x NTM" implied.

## 2. Business in plain English
Casey's runs about 2,959 company-owned convenience stores in 19 states, about two-thirds in towns of 20,000 people or fewer (Investor Day deck, 8-K 0001140361-26-026167). It sells fuel, groceries and general merchandise, and prepared food (it is the fifth-largest US pizza chain by kitchen count, per the same deck). Prepared food earns about 59% gross margin, fuel about 14%. Its edge is rural density, owned distribution centres and its own fuel fleet, which let it serve small towns that competitors skip, and it grows by building and buying stores (Fikes/CEFCO closed 1 Nov 2024, 198 stores).

## 8. Bull and bear
**Bull:** (1) Plan delivery: FY24-FY26 EBITDA compounded 16% (Investor Day) and FY26 beat its guide by 3.6 points; a repeat lifts the FY27 guide at Q2. (2) Prepared food traffic and margin (59.3% in Q1, up from 58.0%) plus loyalty (about 10.5M members) drive inside share gains at 25 straight years of inside SSS growth. (3) Fikes synergies and at least 120 store additions a year with low leverage (1.56x gross).
**Bear:** (1) Fuel margin of 47.8 cents is 7-9 cents above FY25's 38.7; a normalisation to 40 cents removes about $3.2 of EPS (about 15%). (2) Inside SSS slowed to 3.2% in Q1 while same-store opex ex credit-card fees rose 5.0% (release). (3) Valuation: 28.8x TTM and a 2.9% FCF yield with capex rising to $800M; insiders sold about $29M of stock in the last six months (section 11).
