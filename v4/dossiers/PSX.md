# PSX - Phillips 66 (NYSE: PSX) - standard-depth diligence (agent F140)

**1. Verdict: WATCH. Horizon 12-36 months.** The triage "no advance" is confirmed, but for a sharper reason than "cyclical": the stock is priced at roughly my bull case. Q2 2026 earnings of $3.8B came from a refining margin spike (3:2:1 crack spread $41.63 a barrel against $21.65 a year earlier) caused by Middle East supply disruption, and the 25 Sep 2026 price of $255.75 needs free cash flow to stay near $8.5B a year for a decade, against my base of $4.5B once margins normalise. A well-run integrated refiner, midstream and chemicals group, priced for a disruption to last. Condition that would change this: a price near or below about $160 (my base-case DCF value), or evidence that refining margins have structurally reset (for example two more quarters with realized margin above $20 a barrel and a multi-year supply loss).

**2. Business in plain English.** Phillips 66 turns crude oil into gasoline, diesel and jet fuel at its refineries, moves and processes natural gas liquids (NGLs) through pipelines and fractionators, owns half of Chevron Phillips Chemical (CPChem, plastics), markets fuels and lubricants, and makes renewable diesel. Refining and Marketing earn money from the spread between product and crude prices, which swings enormously; Midstream and Chemicals are the steadier part. The Q2 2026 segment split (pre-tax adjusted, 8-K accession 0001534701-26-000030): Refining $3,086M, Midstream $785M, Marketing and Specialties $514M, Renewable Fuels $544M, Chemicals $404M, Corporate -$407M. Competitive position: scale in the US Central Corridor after buying the other 50% of WRB Refining (Wood River and Borger) on 1 Oct 2025, and an integrated NGL wellhead-to-export chain; no pricing power on refined products.

**3. Why the model likes it.** b1 composite decile 5 (rank 252). The drivers are not value or quality: family percentiles are quality 0.28, value 0.26, momentum 0.88, earnings surprise 0.61, with a 12-1 month price return of about +87% and an earnings surprise score of 2.26. Durable or artefact? Artefact of a commodity shock. The earnings surprise is the spread spike itself; the momentum is the price response to it. Neither persists if crack spreads mean-revert.

**8. Bull and bear.** Bull: (1) Midstream and Chemicals growth and cost actions (Golden Triangle polymers and Ras Laffan start in 2027, Iron Mesa gas plant Q1 2027, record NGL volumes) lift through-cycle earnings; (2) if the Middle East supply loss persists, refining margins stay wide and buybacks ($10B new authorization) retire shares; (3) debt falling toward $17B de-risks the equity. Bear: (1) crack spreads of $41.63 are at an extreme; if they return to the 2025 level ($21.65 in Q2 2025) earnings fall sharply (adjusted EPS was $2.38 in Q2 2025 against $9.41 in Q2 2026); (2) refining earnings in Q1 2026 were only $208M, so the Q2 figure shows how violently the segment swings; (3) insiders sold heavily into the rally (Section 11) and the $928M Propel accrual could rise if the appeal fails.

**9. Key risks and kill criteria (for any position or upgrade from WATCH).**

2. Consolidated total debt (short-term plus long-term, carrying value) above $22B at any quarter-end before 2027 ($20.565B at 30 Jun 2026), or the $17B target withdrawn.

4. Propel Fuels accrual raised above $1.2B or an appellate ruling affirming the $833M judgment (accrual $928M).
5. Return of capital below 35% of operating cash flow excluding working capital for two consecutive quarters, other than when debt reduction targets are still unmet (Q2 2026: 21%).
