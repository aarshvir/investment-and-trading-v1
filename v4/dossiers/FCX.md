# FCX - Freeport-McMoRan Inc. (NYSE: FCX) - Standard-depth diligence (agent F122)

**1. Verdict: WATCH** - A high-quality copper franchise whose 25 Sep 2026 price ($72.31) already capitalises a full Grasberg recovery plus roughly $6 copper; our evidence-based base case is worth well below the price, and only the bull case supports it. Horizon 12-36 months. Re-rate condition: a pullback toward the mid-$50s, or evidence that Grasberg reaches full rate before end-2027 with copper holding above $6.

**Triage check (Q13: advance=false).** Verified in filings: Grasberg at ~65% of capacity in H2 2026, ~80% by mid-2027, near full by end-2027; 2026 sales guidance cut once (Jan to Apr). The triage's "$8.7B OCF / $4,500 gold" is now stale: the July release guides ~$8.3B on a $4,000 gold deck. Triage conclusion stands, for valuation reasons rather than operating ones.

**2. Business.** FCX mines copper (plus gold, molybdenum) at Grasberg in Indonesia (PTFI, 48.76% owned but consolidated), Morenci and other US mines, and Cerro Verde (55.66%) and other South American mines. It sells concentrate and cathode at market prices, so earnings swing with copper and gold. Moat is reserve scale and long-life assets in a market with little new supply; the weak point is Indonesia concentration and partner/government dependence.
