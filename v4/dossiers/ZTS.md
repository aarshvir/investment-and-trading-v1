# Zoetis Inc. (ZTS) — Diligence Dossier
Agent F100 · Standard depth · As of 2026-09-25 close (px $71.05, mkt cap ~$29.4bn, consolidated basis throughout unless noted)

## 1. Verdict
**WATCH** (downgrade from triage "advance/quality 5"). Thesis horizon 12–24 months. The moat (global #1 animal-health
franchise, ~4,000+ patents, vet-relationship distribution) and margins are real, and the stock is no longer expensive —
but FY2026 guidance was cut twice in two quarters (organic revenue growth guided from +3–5% in Feb 2026 to **–3% to –1%**
by Aug 2026) on broad U.S. companion-animal softness, not just Librela destocking, and a securities-fraud class action
now alleges management misstated the demand deterioration. We want one more quarter of evidence that the U.S. companion-
animal decline is bottoming before sizing this as a full position.

## 2. Business in plain English
Zoetis is the world's largest animal-health company, spun off from Pfizer in 2013. It develops and sells medicines,
vaccines and diagnostics for pets (companion animal, ~65% of revenue) and farm animals (livestock, ~35%), selling
mainly through veterinarians and animal-health distributors in ~45 direct markets. It makes money on branded,
patent-protected products (parasiticides Simparica/Revolution, dermatology Apoquel/Cytopoint, osteoarthritis
Librela/Solensia) with gross margins in the mid-70s%.

## 7. Valuation snapshot & reverse DCF
d4 live snapshot (2026-08-06 basis, price 2026-09-25): NTM P/E 11.02x (pe_ntm), FY0 P/E 11.45x, FCF yield 6.87%,
EV/sales NTM 3.90x. **V1 systematic valuation: no row exists for ZTS** (not yet run in v1_valuation_table.csv/.json) —
v1_verdict = null, nothing to reconcile.

Reverse DCF (Gordon-growth on FCF yield, single-stage — appropriate given ZTS's steady-state margin profile):
required return (CoE) ≈7.5% (low-beta, investment-grade, health-care staple); price = FCF/(r−g) ⇒ implied perpetual
FCF growth g = r − FCF_yield = 7.5% − 6.87% ≈ **+0.6%/yr**. At an unlevered "no-growth" fair P/E of 1/r ≈13.3x, ZTS's
11.0x NTM P/E is *below* a flat-growth valuation — the market is pricing in only marginal (~0–1%/yr) long-run growth,
i.e., it has already substantially de-risked the demand slowdown. Our evidence-based cases:
- **Bear (10y FCF growth ≈ –2%/yr):** U.S. companion-animal softness persists, Librela franchise keeps shrinking,
  livestock cyclically rolls over; multiple stays at ~10x (fear discount).
- **Base (10y FCF growth ≈ +3%/yr):** U.S. companion-animal demand stabilizes at flat-to-low-single-digit by 2027,
  livestock and international carry the growth, buybacks continue funded by FCF not new debt; multiple re-rates
  modestly toward 12–13x.
- **Bull (10y FCF growth ≈ +7%/yr):** destocking/affordability headwinds prove temporary (as the original triage
  argued), new pain-management and dermatology launches reaccelerate companion animal; multiple re-rates to 15x+.

**Implied vs base:** the price implies growth (~0.6%/yr) **below** our base case (~3%/yr) → implied_vs_base = "below".

## 8. Bull case / Bear case
Bull: (1) 11x NTM P/E is below the no-growth fair multiple — cheap optionality if softness proves cyclical/affordability-
driven rather than structural. (2) Livestock (U.S. +23% in Q2'26) and international companion animal (+5% organic) are
genuinely accelerating and are ~55% of revenue. (3) FCF conversion (~85% of NI) and gross margins remain intact —
no evidence of price-cutting to defend share yet.
Bear: (1) Two guidance cuts in six months, the second one flipping organic growth negative, is a credibility problem —
management's Feb-2026 "temporary destocking" framing (echoed in the wave-6 triage) has not held up. (2) $3.8bn of new
debt funded buybacks into a decelerating top line — leverage has risen precisely when cash-flow visibility fell.
(3) A federal securities-fraud class action (filed 2026, S.D.N.Y., *City of Ann Arbor Retiree Health Care Benefit Plan
& Trust v. Zoetis Inc.*, No. 26-cv-04401) alleges management misrepresented weakening Librela adoption, Simparica Trio
share loss, and Apoquel/Cytopoint share loss to a competing canine treatment during Jan-2025–May-2026 — an unresolved
legal overhang and a signal the demand deterioration may be broader/older than the two guidance cuts suggest.

## 9. Key risks & kill criteria (measurable)
1. U.S. companion-animal organic revenue declines for a **3rd consecutive quarter** (Q3'26 report, due ~Nov 2026).
2. FY2026 adjusted EPS guidance is cut a **third time** at the Q3'26 or Q4'26 release.
3. Net debt/EBITDA (consolidated) rises **above 2.5x** (vs ≈1.6x now) without a specific offsetting M&A rationale.
4. The securities class action survives a motion to dismiss and/or the SEC opens a formal (not preliminary) investigation.
5. Livestock organic growth (currently the offset) decelerates below **+5% YoY** for two consecutive quarters.
