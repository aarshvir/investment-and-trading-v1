# PNW — Pinnacle West Capital Corporation (F135, standard depth, information cutoff 2026-09-25 close, price $91.74)

## 1. Verdict
**WATCH** (horizon 12-36 months; decision point is the Arizona Corporation Commission order on the 2025 rate case, which the Administrative Law Judge expects before the end of 2026). A regulated Arizona electric utility with real load growth (weather-normalized sales +5.6% in Q2 2026) whose price (17.0x NTM) is about in line with utility peers, but with negative free cash flow, consolidated debt-to-capitalisation of about 62% against a 65% covenant, continuous equity issuance, and consensus FY2027 EPS (+20%) that depends on a rate outcome staff has already recommended cutting. Base 3-year return +7.6% a year (bear -1.4%, bull +15.6%) sits at, not above, a utility cost of equity. Would become INCLUDE-SMALL on an order with ROE of at least 9.6% and a base revenue increase of at least $500M, or at or below about $88 (base then about 9%). V1 row: none exists, so v1_verdict = null. The triage's "net debt/EBITDA 7.7x" is reconciled in section 6: it includes $5.1B of operating leases.

## 2. Business in plain English
Pinnacle West's only material subsidiary is Arizona Public Service (APS), which sells electricity to about 1.5 million homes and businesses in Arizona. Earnings come from a regulated return on rate base (APS asked for a 10.70% return on equity in its pending case); more customers and more capital spent on plants and lines raise earnings only when the ACC lets the costs into rates. The moat is the monopoly franchise in one of the fastest-growing US regions; the risk is regulatory lag, heat/wildfire exposure and financing that must keep pace with about $2.6-2.7B of annual capex.

## 8. Bull and bear
Bull: (1) sales growth guided ("5.0% to 7.0%") a year through 2030 from data centers and manufacturing, well above the 3.9% weather-adjusted average of the last three years; (2) an ACC order near the request plus the formula rate plan (FRAM) would cut regulatory lag and lift 2027 EPS toward consensus; (3) 17x earnings with a 4% dividend yield is fair for a low-beta (0.44) compounder.
Bear: (1) the 62% debt-to-capitalisation ratio, $1.6B of debt due within a year on $9M of cash, and ACC headroom of roughly $0.8B on APS debt make every financing dependent on market access and approvals; (2) equity issuance ($900M done, $500M new, about $816M unsettled forwards) dilutes per-share growth by roughly 2.5% a year, and EPS already fell 9.5% in Q2 2026 on higher interest and depreciation; (3) staff's 9.55%-9.80% ROE and RUCO's 9.0%-9.2% would leave 2027 EPS near $5.0-5.3 rather than $5.64, and wildfire liability, the nuclear wage antitrust class action naming APS (filed 11 Jul 2025) and an appeal over the formula-rate policy statement add tail risk.

## 9. Kill criteria (measurable)
1. ACC final order on the 2025 rate case with ROE below 9.3% or a total base revenue increase below $350M (request $691.6M; staff $506.5M).
2. Pinnacle West consolidated debt to total capitalisation above 64% (10-Q covenant maximum 65%; 62% at 30 Jun 2026).
3. Any downgrade of Pinnacle West senior unsecured below Baa2 at Moody's or BBB at S&P, or either agency moving its outlook to negative (now Baa2 and BBB, both stable).
4. 2026 weather-normalized EPS guidance ("$4.55 to $4.75") cut below $4.55.
5. Diluted share count growth above 4% year over year (about +2.5% in Q2 2026) without matching EPS growth.
