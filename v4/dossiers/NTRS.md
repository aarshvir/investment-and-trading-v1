# Northern Trust Corporation (NTRS) - Fundamental Diligence Dossier (F133, standard depth)

**Verdict: WATCH**, 12-36 month horizon. A high-quality custody and wealth franchise with well-covered capital and real operating leverage, but at $175.73 (25 Sep 2026 close; 2.57x book) the price already assumes a return on equity near 18%, against about 16.5% on earnings stripped of one-offs; my base case returns about 8% a year, roughly the cost of equity. What would change it: a price near or below about $150 (about 2.2x book, 12.8x clean 2026 EPS), or ex-notable return on equity holding above 17% for two more quarters with expense growth under 3%.

## 1. Business in plain English
Northern Trust safeguards and administers assets for pension funds, asset managers and wealthy families (Asset Servicing and Wealth Management segments) and earns mostly fees, about two-thirds of revenue, that move with market values and client activity; it also lends to wealthy clients and earns interest on the client deposits it holds. At 30 Jun 2026 it had $20.0 trillion of assets under custody/administration and $1.97 trillion under management. Moat is scale and client stickiness in custody (few global custodians exist), but fees compete hard and are tied to equity and bond prices; net interest income depends on rates.

## 7. Bull and bear
Bull: (1) Eight straight quarters of positive organic trust-fee growth (the Q2 release says "eighth consecutive quarter") with consolidated fees +10% and AUC/A +11% to $20.0T; (2) operating leverage above 700bp for two quarters, and expense growth ex-notables only 5.4%; (3) Northern Trust Corporation (consolidated) CET1 12.2% against a 7.0% requirement, a 10% dividend raise and a live $2.5B buyback.
Bear: (1) Fees follow markets with a one-month and one-quarter lag (the release says so); S&P 500 was +27% YoY at 30 Jun 2026, so a 20% fall cuts fees and leaves a fixed cost base; (2) repeated restructuring and "one-off" items (severance in 2024, 2025 and 2026) and a 2026 expense line that includes $61.5M of software disposals suggest technology execution cost; (3) 2.57x book with consensus FY27 EPS (12.59) below FY26 (12.73): nothing in estimates supports further re-rating.
