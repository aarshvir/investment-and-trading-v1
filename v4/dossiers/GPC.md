# GPC - Genuine Parts Company (research only; not personal investment advice)

**1. Verdict: WATCH.** A durable, 70-year dividend-raiser at about 16.7x midpoint adjusted EPS, but the price already assumes a faster cash-flow recovery than the evidence supports, free cash flow has not covered the dividend, and the planned split into two companies (target Q1 2027) adds execution and dis-synergy risk. What would change it: TTM free cash flow above $700m with the separation on schedule, or a price near $105 (about 13.5x adjusted EPS). Horizon 12-36 months. The quant triage (rank 401/402, quality 0.49, value 0.48) is confirmed as no bargain.

**2. Business in plain English.** GPC distributes car parts (NAPA in North America, Repco and others internationally) and industrial parts and bearings (Motion). It sells to repair shops, retailers and factories and earns a roughly 38% gross margin by stocking large inventories close to customers. Two segments with different growth and margin profiles are being separated.

**3. Why the model likes it.** Gross-profit-to-assets 0.44 (percentile 0.85) and a low leverage percentile; earnings yield only 0.2% (GAAP, depressed by the Q4-25 pension settlement charge and restructuring). The quality score reflects distribution-model structure (high gross margin before heavy operating costs), not excess returns.
