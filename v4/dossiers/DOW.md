# DOW - Dow Inc. (Wave 8, agent F165)

## 1. Verdict
**WATCH** (thesis horizon 12-36 months). The quick triage (advance=false, "negative TTM margin, dividend halved, net debt/EBITDA 3.8x", Yahoo basis; consolidated Dow Inc. filing basis is ~2.9x) is **partly overturned on facts but upheld on the call**: Q2 2026 was a sharp upturn (Operating EBIT $1.65B vs a $21M loss; Operating EPS $1.44), so the "bottom of cycle" framing is stale. But that quarter was lifted by a Middle East supply shock, consensus already has FY27 EPS ($1.75) below FY26 ($2.39), H1 free cash flow excluding a one-off Nova receipt barely covers the dividend, and the price ($28.02, +40% in 12 months) already discounts a recovery to roughly 2024-level earnings. Upgrade condition: evidence of Operating EBITDA of at least ~$6B a year without the supply shock (three quarters of spreads holding), or a price near $22.

## 2. Business in plain English
Global commodity and specialty chemicals maker: polyethylene and olefins (Packaging & Specialty Plastics, 53% of Q2 sales), polyurethanes and industrial intermediates, silicones and coatings. Sells to packaging, infrastructure, mobility and consumer customers; earns the spread between feedstock (ethane in North America) and product prices. Advantage: low-cost US Gulf Coast and Canadian integrated assets; disadvantage: pure commodity pricing, European assets, and Middle East joint ventures (Sadara, Equate) that drain cash or are impaired.

## 3. Why the model likes it
b1 composite is middling (0.058); strengths are book value (bp percentile 0.81, depressed book/price) and 12-month momentum (0.56); gross profitability (0.11), earnings yield (0.04) and FCF yield (0.07) are near the bottom. The "cheap" reading is a trough-earnings artefact: trailing GAAP EPS is -$1.83 (d4), so P/E is meaningless, and 14.6x NTM P/E (d4) is on peak-ish near-term EPS. Not durable as a quality signal.

## 7. Valuation and reconciliation with V1
No V1 row exists for DOW (v1_verdict = null). Price $28.02, market cap ~$20.2B; EV ~$35.7B including NCI (consolidated Dow Inc. net debt $14.0B + NCI $1.5B); EV/TTM Operating EBITDA ~7.4x; consensus EPS FY26 $2.39, FY27 $1.75 (16x FY27); dividend yield 5.0%.
**Reverse DCF** (cyclical, so framed on normalized cash flow; 10% cost of equity, 2% terminal): the price implies a steady-state equity FCF of ~$1.6B (20.2B x 8%). With capex ~$2.5B, interest ~$0.85B, D&A ~$2.8B and 25% tax, FCF is approximately 0.75 x Operating EBITDA - $2.74B, so the price implies a **normalized Operating EBITDA of ~$5.8B**. (From the TTM ex-Nova FCF of ~$0.6B this is a 14.8% 10-year CAGR, which is not meaningful because the base is trough.)
**My cases (normalized Operating EBITDA by 2028-29):** bear ~$4.0B (supply shock reverses, European closures offset self-help), base ~$5.7B (about 2024's $5.5B plus a partial credit for the "at least $2 billion" programme), bull ~$7.5B (spreads stay firm and Transform to Outperform is fully delivered). Implied (~$5.8B) is within 2% of base: **implied_vs_base = in_line**; with the Sadara guarantee, pension and the Nova one-off excluded, I see no margin of safety, hence WATCH.
3-year scenarios (annualised total return from $28.02): bear -24.4% (dividend cut to $0.70), base +5.3%, bull +23.8%.

## 8. Bull and bear
Bull: (1) Q2 shows operating leverage: Operating EBITDA $2.3B in a quarter, and cost programmes ($1B structural, Transform to Outperform) are additive; (2) low-cost North American feedstock while Asia and Europe producers face constraints; (3) liquidity $13.5B, no maturities to 2029, dividend intact.
Bear: (1) the upturn is a geopolitical supply shock, which the 10-Q itself says has "changed over time" with "diplomatic efforts" continuing; consensus FY27 EPS falls 27% from FY26; (2) cash quality: H1 ex-Nova FCF ~$0.3B, receivables swelled, capex $2.5B; (3) legacy claims on cash: Sadara guarantee ~$1.2B, $4.5B pension deficit, $663M asbestos, 4,500-role restructuring ($472M charges to date).

## 9. Kill criteria (exit or do not initiate)
1. Quarterly Operating EBITDA below $1.0B in two consecutive quarters (Q2-26 $2.3B; Q3-25 to Q1-26 range $0.74B-$0.87B).
2. Quarterly dividend declared below $0.35 a share.
3. Consolidated net debt above 3.5x TTM Operating EBITDA (now ~2.9x; debt per balance sheet less cash).
4. TTM free cash flow (CFO less capex, excluding Nova receipts) negative at any 10-Q.
5. Cumulative cash paid under the Sadara guarantees above $500M (an $80M payment so far in 2026; ~$1.2B of principal guaranteed).
