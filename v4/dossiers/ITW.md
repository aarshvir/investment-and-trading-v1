# ITW - Illinois Tool Works (F141, STANDARD depth)

**1. VERDICT: WATCH** - a superb, steady compounder, but the 25 Sep 2026 close ($274.32) already discounts ~7.6% a year of free-cash-flow growth for ten years, slightly more than my evidence-based base case (~7%). Horizon 12-36 months. Triage (Q10/Q13: "legendary capital allocator, but low growth and no valuation margin of safety") is confirmed, not overturned. What would change it: a pullback to roughly $235-245 (implied growth at or below 6%) or organic growth sustained at 4%+ for another two quarters.

**2. Business in plain English.** ITW makes the small, specified-in component and equipment that other industries depend on: auto fasteners, commercial kitchen equipment, welding gear, test instruments, construction fasteners, polymers and fluids. Seven decentralised segments, about 43,000 staff, roughly $16bn revenue. It wins through the "80/20" operating system and customer-back innovation, earning a 26-27% operating margin that most industrials cannot. (Source: Q2-26 release, 8-K acc. 0000049826-26-000047.)

**3. Why the model likes it.** b1 composite_qvm 0.63, live_rank 255 (decile 5): Quality family 0.83 (GP/assets 92nd pct, ROE 98th, OCF/assets 89th, leverage 11th pct = levered, which inflates ROE). Value 0.36 and Sentiment 0.39 are weak. The ROE percentile is partly an artefact: stockholders' equity is only $2,895M because common stock held in treasury, a $28.0bn contra-equity balance (not an equity figure), offsets retained earnings (consolidated balance sheet, 30 Jun 2026). Quality is durable; the ROE ranking is a leverage/buyback effect.
