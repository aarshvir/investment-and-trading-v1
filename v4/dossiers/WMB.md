# WMB - Williams Companies (agent F168, standard depth, information cutoff 2026-09-25 close)

**1. Verdict: WATCH** (horizon 24-36 months). A best-in-class gas-pipeline franchise whose earnings are growing about 8% a year, but at $69.26 (d4/b1 snapshot, 25 Sep 2026 close) the stock already prices about 7% a year of per-share cash-flow growth for ten years, versus about 5% in my base case. Base 3-year return is about +4.3% a year, below an 8.5% cost of equity. What would change it: a price near $58 (about 11x trailing AFFO of $5.16 a share), or evidence that 2027 EBITDA growth exceeds roughly 10% without further cost-of-capital creep. Triage (Q04_triage.json: advance=false, quality 4, growth 2, price_vs_growth 1) is **overturned on growth** (it called growth "essentially flat"; Adjusted EBITDA rose 6% in Q2 2026, 9.5% in 2025 and the 2026 guide midpoint is +8.4%) and **confirmed on price**. Its "4.3x leverage" is also not supported: the company reports 3.67x (see section 6).

**2. Business in plain English.** Williams moves about a third of US natural gas. It owns Transco (the largest US gas pipeline, regulated by FERC), Northwest Pipeline, Gulf Coast offshore gathering, Marcellus/Utica and Haynesville-adjacent gathering, and is building dedicated power plants for data-centre customers ("Power Innovation"). Revenue is mostly fee-based and contracted (service revenue $2,152m of $3,053m in Q2 2026), so earnings follow volumes and new projects rather than gas prices.
