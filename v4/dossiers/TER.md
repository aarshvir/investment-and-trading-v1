# TER — Teradyne, Inc.

## 1. Verdict
**WATCH** — a genuinely excellent, net-cash business riding a real AI/HBM-driven semiconductor-test upcycle, but the
stock trades at a cycle-high multiple (36x NTM P/E) on what look like cycle-elevated earnings, and the company's own
Q3 guidance is a sequential step-down from Q2's record — the first sign the market's implied growth path is ahead of
what management itself is currently promising. Horizon if it re-enters: 12–24 months, on a valuation reset or on
confirmation that the "growth in 2027 and beyond" management flagged is actually materializing quarter by quarter.

## 2. Business in plain English
Teradyne makes the automated test equipment chipmakers use to verify semiconductors work before shipping them
(Semiconductor Test — its largest segment, especially memory and compute chips used in AI), plus test gear for other
electronics (Product Test) and collaborative/mobile industrial robots (Robotics, via Universal Robots and MiR). It
sells to chipmakers and their subcontractors; demand is tied to how much new silicon capacity the industry is
building (a cyclical, capex-driven market), and it is currently benefiting from an AI-driven memory and compute
testing supercycle.

multiple. **No data conflict** between the quant score and the qualitative growth story — both agree the business is
strong; the open question the quant score cannot answer is how much of current earnings is cycle-peak.

## 6. Earnings quality & balance sheet (Teradyne, Inc. — single consolidated reporting entity)
- **FCF conversion:** OCF for the six months ended 2026-06-28 was $734.3M (10-Q XBRL) against GAAP net income of

- **SBC:** $42.0M for the six months ended 2026-06-28 vs. six-month revenue $2,611.5M ≈ 1.6% of revenue — low,
  a genuine quality signal versus many AI-adjacent semis names.
- **Balance sheet (consolidated, 10-Q, 2026-06-28):** cash and equivalents $349.5M; **long-term debt = $0** (has been
  zero since FY2013 per XBRL history) — Teradyne carries essentially no funded debt. Net cash position, not net
  debt. Buybacks resumed modestly ($74.2M in H1 2026, versus $702M for all of FY2025) and dividends ($40.7M H1 2026).
- **Hidden asset not marked to fair value on the balance sheet:** Teradyne holds a 10% equity stake in Technoprobe
  S.p.A. (an Italian, publicly listed probe-card maker), accounted for under the equity method at a carrying value of
  $537.1M (2026-06-28); the 10-Q separately discloses the **fair value of the publicly traded shares was $2,457.9
  million** at that date — i.e., roughly $1.9B of unrecognized mark-to-market value (about 3% of Teradyne's own
  market cap) sits off the income statement/balance-sheet fair-value line, a real but easily overlooked NAV item.
- **Litigation:** the 10-Q's ordinary-course litigation language (patent, employment, commercial, environmental) is
  boilerplate; management states it believes potential losses are unlikely to be material. No restatement,
  going-concern, or investigation disclosure found.

## 7. Valuation snapshot
NTM P/E **36.3x** (d4, 2026-09-25 close $398.38); FCF yield **0.7%**; EV/Sales (NTM) **10.1x**. No V1 systematic
valuation row exists for TER → **v1_verdict = null**. Consensus: 15 analysts, mean target $446.47 (+12.1% upside),
rating "buy." My own reverse-DCF read, starting from NTM consensus EPS ≈$10.97: a **base case** assuming EPS growth
moderates from the current 100%+ pace to ~15%/yr over 3 years (a large deceleration, consistent with a cyclical
capex-driven business normalizing) with the multiple compressing from 36.3x to a still-generous 25x by year 3
implies a price near $417 — only ~1.5%/yr, i.e. essentially fully priced for a soft landing. A **bear case** (growth
reverses to a WFE-cycle downturn, EPS to ~$8 by year 3, multiple compresses to a trough 15x) implies roughly
**-33%/yr**; a **bull case** (the "2027 and beyond" AI-capex supercycle materializes, 30%/yr EPS growth, multiple
holds near 35x) implies roughly **+28%/yr**. The wide dispersion reflects genuine cyclicality at a multi-year-high
multiple on multi-year-high earnings — the definition of low margin of safety even for a high-quality franchise.

## 8. Bull case / Bear case
**Bull:** (1) two consecutive quarters of beating guidance at the high end on real AI/HBM/DRAM/NAND test demand, not
one-off; (2) management explicitly frames 2027+ as a continued growth runway on rising wafer-fab-equipment
investment; (3) essentially debt-free balance sheet plus a ~$2.46B mark-to-market Technoprobe stake provide real
downside cushion in a cyclical downturn.
**Bear:** (1) Q3 2026 guidance is a sequential step-down in both revenue midpoint and EPS high end versus the
just-reported Q2 record — the company's own numbers, not a bear-case assumption; (2) semiconductor test-equipment
demand is structurally tied to chipmaker capex cycles, which have historically reversed sharply after AI-style
demand spikes; (3) at 36x NTM P/E, the stock is priced closer to the bull case than the base case, leaving little
room for the normalization most capital-equipment cycles eventually deliver.

## 9. Key risks & kill criteria (measurable)
1. A quarterly revenue guidance midpoint sequentially below the *prior quarter's actual* revenue for **two**
   consecutive quarters (one instance already present in the Q3 2026 guide; a second would confirm a real turn).
2. Semiconductor Test segment revenue growth turns negative YoY (vs. +100%+ currently).
3. Consolidated GAAP operating margin below 20% for two consecutive quarters (Q1 2026 ≈37%, Q2 2026 ≈33%, per
   OperatingIncomeLoss/Revenues from the 10-Q income statement).
4. NTM P/E stays above 30x while quarterly guidance shows sequential deceleration for two straight quarters.
5. Management's own commentary on "2027 and beyond" WFE/AI-test capex (in the next two quarters' MD&A) turns
   cautious or is walked back.
