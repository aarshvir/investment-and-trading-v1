# GLW - Corning Incorporated (F143, wave 8 standard-depth diligence)

**1. Verdict: WATCH.** A genuinely improving business (core sales +17%, core EPS +30% in Q2 2026, four straight quarters at or above the top of its own guidance, Optical +32%), but the price of $156.74 asks for roughly 19% a year FCF growth for ten years and is 1.4-2.1% free-cash-flow yield; my evidence-based base case (13% sales CAGR) is worth about $104 a share. Horizon 12-36 months. Confirms the quick triage's "good business, re-rated" and quantifies it with filings.

**Condition that would move it to INCLUDE-SMALL:** a price near $105-$115 (base-case value), or evidence that the Springboard run rate is converting to free cash flow (TTM adjusted FCF excluding customer deposits above $3.5B while capex stays below 15% of sales).

## 2. Business in plain English
Corning makes specialty glass and optical fibre. Optical Communications (optical fibre, cable and connectivity for carriers and AI data centres) was $2.07B of Q2 sales; Glass Innovations (display glass, Gorilla Glass and specialty materials, combined from Display and Specialty Materials in Q1 2026) $1.46B; Automotive $471M; Solar (Hemlock polysilicon plus wafers/modules) $438M, up 90%; plus Life Sciences. Edge: materials-science IP, manufacturing know-how and long-term supply agreements with Amazon and NVIDIA (per the Q2 release). The AI build-out is the growth driver; Solar is the execution risk.

## 8. Bull and bear
Bull: (1) Amazon multibillion-dollar supply agreement and the NVIDIA partnership (10x optical connectivity capacity, 50%+ more US fibre capacity) are named customers, not hopes; (2) four straight quarters at/above guidance top, core margin 20.9% and +190 bps y/y; (3) Solar to improve from Q3 after the extended shutdown, with a "revenue stream of more than $3 billion" targeted.
Bear: (1) 42x NTM core EPS on a 1.4-2.1% FCF yield leaves no margin for an AI-capex pause; (2) quality of cash flow: $1.0B of H1 OCF was a customer deposit, SBC doubled, and the company has issued warrants ($180 strike) and opened a $2B ATM; (3) capital intensity and Solar execution (extended wafer shutdown, "additional $30 million of expense versus the first quarter") with heavy insider selling into strength.
