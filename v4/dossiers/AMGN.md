# AMGN - Amgen Inc. - Diligence Dossier (Agent F126, Wave-8 Standard)

## 1. Verdict
**WATCH** (12-36 month horizon). Good business, priced for slightly more than the base case delivers: the 25 Sep 2026 close implies 4.6% 10-year free-cash-flow growth versus my 3.9% base. The condition that would change it: a price near $350-360 (implied growth about 3.5% or less) or MariTide Phase 3 data that lifts the base case. The triage (WATCH, "+52% forward EPS growth") was right on the verdict but wrong on the number: +52% compares forward non-GAAP EPS ($24.45) with trailing GAAP EPS ($16.12); like-for-like non-GAAP growth is about +5% in FY26 and +6-7% in FY27.

## 2. Business in plain English
Amgen sells injectable biologic medicines, mostly to health systems and pharmacy benefit channels in the U.S. Its mix: General Medicine (Repatha cholesterol, Evenity, Prolia), Rare Disease (Tepezza, Krystexxa, Uplizna), Inflammation (Tezspire, Otezla, Enbrel) and Oncology (Blincyto, Imdelltra, Kyprolis). It earns money on patent-protected volume growth and cost control; the company says its six growth drivers grew 26% in Q2 and made nearly 70% of Q2 product sales. Position: scale manufacturer with 17 products above $1bn annualized, but old franchises (Prolia, Enbrel, Otezla, Kyprolis) are eroding from biosimilars and Medicare price setting, and the obesity drug MariTide is still in Phase 3.

## 8. Bull and bear
Bull: (1) six growth drivers +26% with Repatha, Tezspire, Uplizna, Imdelltra scaling; (2) MariTide has a broad Phase 3 programme (MARITIME-1, -2, -CV, -HF, OSA, SWITCH) plus T2D studies to start 2026, real optionality not in the base; (3) FCF recovered to $3.5bn in Q2 and FY26 guidance was raised twice.
Bear: (1) non-GAAP EPS +4% on +10% revenue, cost of sales and R&D rising faster than sales; (2) legacy erosion: Prolia -32%, Enbrel and Otezla under IRA price setting (selected for 2026 and 2027), MFN pricing actions; (3) $57bn of debt, equity of only $11.7bn, and a disclosed cyber incident.

## 9. Key risks and kill criteria
1. Non-GAAP operating margin below 42% in any quarter (range over the last five quarters: 42.8% in Q4'25 to 48.4% in Q2'26).
2. FY26 non-GAAP EPS guidance cut below the $22.30 low end, or FY27 guide midpoint under $23.50.
3. Total debt less cash above 3.0x trailing EBITDA (consolidated, 10-Q balance sheet; now about 2.5x) or FCF below $8bn on a trailing-four-quarter basis.
4. Prolia plus Enbrel plus Otezla combined net sales falling more than 25% year over year for two quarters (Q2'26: Prolia -32%, Otezla -21%, Enbrel -4%, combined about -21%).
5. Any amendment to the Item 1.05 cyber 8-K disclosing material financial impact, regulatory penalties or a class action above $500m.
