## Account and tax structure

The original Irish-fund suggestion needs qualification. For a qualifying nonresident noncitizen, $60,000 is generally an aggregate US-situs estate filing threshold, not a per-account allowance or a flat 40% tax calculation. UAE residence alone does not establish US citizenship, tax residence or estate domicile. Irish corporate fund shares generally reduce the direct US-share situs issue; US stocks in other accounts still count. [IRS estate guidance](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/frequently-asked-questions-on-estate-taxes-for-nonresidents-not-citizens-of-the-united-states).

Eligible Irish funds generally incur 15% US portfolio-dividend withholding at fund level. That is different from the usual 30% direct-dividend withholding for a qualifying nonresident individual without treaty relief. Accumulation reinvests net dividends; it is not tax-free treatment. [US–Ireland treaty](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-trty/ireland.pdf), [IRS Publication 519](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p519).
