## Accounting and valuation rules

Use a three-year holding period for these fundamental theses, with a weekly review and earlier exit on invalidation. No evidence here supports a one-day trading edge. All forecasts below are analyst assumptions, not management promises or consensus. They are not assigned invented probabilities. They are built from primary statements and management guidance, without importing opaque forward EPS.

Terminal price = terminal revenue × operating margin × (1 − tax rate) ÷ diluted shares × terminal earnings multiple. Margins include stock compensation and depreciation. Net nonoperating income is normalized to zero; investment gains do not become perpetual earnings. No separate repurchase yield, net cash premium, or terminal dividend is added twice. Dividends for years 1, 2 and 3 are separate annual cash flows. The reported annual return is the IRR of those dividends and the terminal price, not a CAGR that pretends every dividend arrives at the end. The machine-readable model retains every assumption and calculation.

The exact maximum purchase price at hurdle h is the present value of each annual dividend plus the terminal price discounted three years. NVIDIA's hurdle is 15%; Microsoft's is 12%, reflecting our judgment about their different earnings and financing risks. These are required returns, not forecasts of achievable performance. Chosen limits add small further discounts to those ceilings. Terminal multiples remain substantial judgment calls; a good earnings forecast does not remove valuation risk.

## NVIDIA — attractive economics, new financing exposure

The archived FY2026 10-K supplies three years of comparative statements: revenue rose from $130.497bn in FY2025 to $215.938bn in FY2026. Q2 FY2027 revenue was $96.221bn; operating income $63.734bn; diluted shares 24.285bn. Reported EPS $2.46 contains investment gains. Company-adjusted EPS $2.22 now **includes stock compensation**: the non-GAAP definition changed in FY2027, so old and new adjusted measures must be aligned. Our quarter-level core earnings check uses operating income × 83% / diluted shares, about $2.18, with zero recurring investment gains. [FY2026 10-K, income statement and segment notes](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000021/nvda-20260125.htm); [Q2 release and reconciliation](https://investor.nvidia.com/news/press-release-details/2026/NVIDIA-Announces-Financial-Results-for-Second-Quarter-Fiscal-2027/).

**What makes the opportunity real:** the next-quarter revenue guide is $108bn ±2%, with roughly 74% gross margin and $9.2bn GAAP operating costs. The base case starts with reported H1 revenue $177.837bn, guided Q3 $108bn and our Q4 assumption $120bn. It then assumes 30%, 15% and 10% annual growth. That is substantial growth, but a major deceleration from recent results. The terminal operating margin falls to 60%, below Q2's approximately 66%, allowing higher competition, service commitments and operating costs. This is a business forecast, not an extrapolation of the vendor's EPS growth percentage. [Q2 release, outlook](https://investor.nvidia.com/news/press-release-details/2026/NVIDIA-Announces-Financial-Results-for-Second-Quarter-Fiscal-2027/).

**The new risk is not just chip cyclicality.** The Q2 filing shows $279bn supply/capacity commitments, $36bn AI-cloud purchase arrangements, $25bn equity-investment commitments and a subsequent conditional $105bn SB Energy guarantee. These are different obligations, not interchangeable current debt. Customer payment terms can extend to a year, and five customers represent 70% of receivables. This links product demand, customer financing and NVIDIA's own capital commitments. Our bear case therefore combines declining sales, lower margins and multiple compression; it does not assume commitments vanish when sales slow. [Q2 10-Q, Notes 7 and 10, pp14 and 18–19](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000075/nvda-20260726.htm).

Share-count changes incorporate modest net repurchases after issuance; the base does not assume buybacks create an extra return on top of EPS.

**Purchase ceiling:** the base-case 15% hurdle gives $226.25. The proposed $215 limit is approximately 5.0% below that ceiling. At approximately $225, the base case only just clears the hurdle: that is too little extra compensation for customer-financing complexity. The bear/base/bull present values at 15% are $55.02/$226.25/$406.74; these describe conditional valuation outcomes, not a confidence interval. At unchanged base EPS, reducing the terminal multiple from 25x to 20x takes the terminal price from $340.62 to $272.50 and the hurdle ceiling to approximately $181.45.

The corrected $127.006bn TTM cash measure is a historical CFO-minus-capex statistic, not owner earnings available without reinvestment. H1 stock compensation, equity purchases, working capital and future commitments require separate treatment. We deliberately do not add this historical FCF yield to the earnings-based return.

**Thesis review and exit:** suspend new buys if revenue guidance falls more than 10% below our next modeled quarter, if gross margin drops below 68% for two quarters without a credible temporary explanation, or if trailing CFO/core earnings falls below 60% while receivable terms or financing support expand. A material customer default, guarantee call, export restriction or evidence that sales depend on uneconomic financed demand requires immediate re-underwriting and may justify an exit. These thresholds are analyst rules, not statistical laws. Do not sell mechanically on one cash-flow quarter. Catalysts are subsequent quarterly results, Rubin delivery and margin evidence, receivable collections and disclosure of the financing arrangements.

## Microsoft — recurring demand, capital intensity now matters

The FY2026 10-K includes FY2024–FY2026 income and cash-flow comparatives. FY2026 revenue was $331.839bn, operating income $155.237bn and diluted shares 7.453bn. Company-adjusted EPS of $17.28 removes OpenAI gains but still permits other gains. We normalize **all** nonoperating income to zero and use a 20% tax assumption, giving core baseline EPS of approximately $16.66; SBC remains an expense. The FY2025/FY2026 diluted-share counts barely changed despite repurchases. Our base holds shares flat, rather than assuming a large buyback benefit. [FY2026 10-K, pp35–44, income statement and Notes 2–3](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/789019/000119312526323660/msft-20260630.htm).

**Base economic forecast:** the three FY2026 segment revenues—Productivity $140.0bn, Intelligent Cloud $137.8bn, Personal Computing $54.1bn—are grown separately. Productivity grows 13%/12%/10%; Cloud 30%/25%/20%; Personal Computing −5%/+2%/+3%. These produce $517.54bn FY2029 revenue. The first-year cloud rate is below management's Q1 33–34% guide; the PC decline recognizes management's weak Windows outlook. A 46% terminal operating margin assumes that growth absorbs some AI costs without granting margin expansion. Tax stays 20%, consistent with FY2027 guidance. [FY2026 10-K, Note 19](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/789019/000119312526323660/msft-20260630.htm); [Q4 call, CFO outlook](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/investor/events/fy-2026/earnings-fy-2026-q4).

**Purchase ceiling:** at a 12% hurdle the base gives $519.15. Use a $500 limit, approximately 3.7% below that ceiling. Bear/base/bull present values at 12% are $268.87/$519.15/$722.83. The current $516.05 reference offers little margin above the hurdle. **Cash-flow challenge:** FY2026 CFO less cash capex less finance-lease principal is $63.886bn. SBC is $12.405bn. Treating SBC as an economic cost gives a separate conservative cash diagnostic of $51.481bn, approximately 1.34% of equity value at $516.05 using diluted shares. This is not reported FCF and is not added to EPS valuation. It warns that our earnings model depends on future cash conversion. Existing finance leases, new right-of-use assets and uncommenced leases must be tracked without deducting lease costs twice. The building-life change from 15 to 25 years also moves more future leases outside reported capex; a falling capex line cannot by itself establish improved economics. [10-K, cash flows and Notes 13/17](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/789019/000119312526323660/msft-20260630.htm); [CFO call, useful-life discussion](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/investor/events/fy-2026/earnings-fy-2026-q4).

**Thesis review and exit:** stop additions if cloud revenue growth falls below 20% for two quarters while capital spending obligations continue growing; if annual operating margin is headed below 43% without demonstrably attractive incremental returns; or if adjusted cash generation cannot cover dividends and dilution-offset repurchases over two annual periods. A material AI-counterparty default or evidence that depreciation lives overstate asset economics triggers immediate review. We would reduce or exit on persistent deterioration, not automatically sell a market drawdown. Catalysts are Azure capacity monetization, Copilot revenue per user and actual cash conversion as the infrastructure matures.

## What this clears

This work clears a **conditional, small-position investment decision at stated prices**, not a guarantee or the original concentrated allocation. It does not need the original defective strategy backtest to claim an edge: these are explicit, falsifiable fundamental scenarios. No assertion of calibrated success probability, exhaustive market screening or intraday timing is made. The accounting facts have source locators; forecast inputs are separately labeled. The next weekly review can update actuals, recompute ceilings and change a BUY BELOW to BUY, HOLD, REDUCE or EXIT based on economics and aggregate portfolio risk.

The raw original filings remain preserved. `technology.json` and `build_technology.py` reproduce all scenario arithmetic. The separate accounting audits remain the record of source reconciliation; they do not silently overwrite this valuation judgment.
