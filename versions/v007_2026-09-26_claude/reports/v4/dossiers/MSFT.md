# MSFT — Microsoft Corporation

## 1. Verdict
**WATCH.** Horizon 12–36 months. Best-in-class enterprise AI/cloud franchise with real, accelerating evidence behind the growth story, but at the 25-Sep-2026 close ($516.17) the price already requires ~12.7% constant 10-year revenue growth (vs. an evidence-based base case of ~10.9%) — the smallest overshoot of the four mega-caps in this batch, and one that could close on a normal pullback rather than a broken thesis.

## 2. Business in plain English
Microsoft sells productivity and collaboration software (Office/365, Teams, LinkedIn), enterprise cloud infrastructure and platform services (Azure), and consumer/gaming products (Windows, Xbox, Search/advertising via Bing/Copilot). It makes money mostly through recurring commercial subscriptions and cloud consumption contracts with large enterprises and governments, monetizing its entrenched position inside corporate IT stacks and increasingly reselling AI compute and Copilot seats on top of that base.

## 3. Why the model likes it — durability check
b1 composite 0.697 (decile 7, live_rank 152 — outside the quant top-30, which is why it was never diligenced under the original rank gate). Driven almost entirely by **Quality** (fam_Q 0.784: high gross-profit/assets, ROE, operating-cash generation, low leverage) and **Earnings momentum** (fam_S 0.674). **Value** (0.512) and **Momentum** (0.317) are unremarkable — the stock is not cheap and has not been a recent price leader. This is durable, not an artefact: Microsoft's quality metrics reflect a genuine, multi-decade recurring-revenue moat, not a one-off. The earnings-momentum score is corroborated by 8/8 EPS beats over the last 8 quarters (avg surprise +7.5%).

## 6. Earnings quality & balance sheet
- **FCF conversion / cash-flow challenge (Codex's independently verified figures, adopted here — see §7):** FY26 CFO − cash capex − finance-lease principal = **$63.886bn**. SBC = **$12.405bn** (≈3.7% of revenue) — a real economic cost, not added back. Treating SBC as a cost gives a conservative cash diagnostic of **$51.481bn** (~1.34% of equity value at $516) [MSFT FY26 10-K, cash-flow statement and Notes 13/17].
- Diluted share count essentially flat FY25→FY26 despite ongoing buybacks — do not assume a large repurchase tailwind.
- Capex/finance-lease intensity has stepped up materially; a useful-life extension (buildings 15→25 years) has reduced reported depreciation and moved future leases off the capex line — a **falling capex line alone does not prove improving economics** [MSFT CFO commentary, Q4 FY26 call].
- Net debt position is modest relative to EBITDA (d4: total debt $128.8bn vs total cash $76.8bn vs EBITDA $194.2bn) — balance sheet is not a constraint.

**My independent check (10-year reverse DCF, same discipline: SBC as expense, nonoperating income normalized to zero):** using Codex's own base-case terminal assumptions (46% margin, 28x terminal multiple, 12% discount rate) but solved over 10 years instead of 3, the $516.17 close requires a **constant 10-year revenue CAGR of ~12.7%** to be justified. My own base case — Cloud decelerating from today's 40%+ to high-teens by year 3 and to high-single-digits by year 10 as it scales past $500bn revenue, Productivity high-single-digit, PC flat-to-down — produces an explicit decelerating path (18%/15%/14%/12%/11%/10%/9%/8%/7%/6%) whose 10-year compound-equivalent growth is **~10.9%**, and which produces a base-case fair value of **~$444** — about **16% below** the current $516.17. **implied_vs_base: above.** This is the smallest overshoot of the four names reviewed under this program (GOOGL/AMZN/META all show larger gaps — see their dossiers). I agree with Codex's qualitative conclusion (limit-buy, not a market order) and adopt their $500 ceiling as a sensible near-term entry discipline; my own decelerating-growth base case is somewhat more conservative on the 10-year horizon, which is why my fair value ($444) sits below Codex's implied ceiling ($519) — the difference is entirely the growth-persistence assumption (Codex's explicit model is only 3 years; mine forces a view on years 4–10, where Azure/AI growth must structurally decelerate as the base compounds).

## 8. Bull case
1. Azure is *accelerating* (33%→40%→43% cc over the last three quarters), not decelerating, against an already-large ($137.8bn) Intelligent Cloud base — the opposite of typical large-base deceleration.
2. The Copilot attach motion is real and growing fast (15m→30m seats in ~9 months) inside a 464m-seat installed base — huge unmonetized runway even at modest penetration gains.
3. Diversified moat: no single regulatory or competitive shock (search remedies, ad-tech fines, FTC social-media suits) hits Microsoft's core businesses the way it hits Alphabet/Meta/Amazon.

## Bear case
1. Capex and finance-lease intensity are rising faster than the depreciation shows (useful-life extension flatters reported margins); the $51.5bn SBC-adjusted cash figure is barely growing relative to the capex commitment.
2. At $516, the stock is priced almost exactly at both Codex's and my own base-case ceiling — there is very little room for a growth disappointment before downside dominates (bear case is −9%/yr).
3. The EU Commission's preliminary view (25-Jun-2026) is that **Azure should be designated a DMA "gatekeeper"** service, with the investigation due to conclude by November 2026 and six months to comply if designated — a genuine, dated regulatory catalyst that could force interoperability/licensing changes to Azure's most profitable enterprise contracts [Concurrences, DataCenterDynamics, 2026-06/09].

## 9. Key risks & kill criteria (thesis-invalidation triggers — signposts to watch)
1. Azure constant-currency growth falls below **30% for two consecutive quarters** while capex commitments keep growing (Codex's own trigger, adopted).
2. Annual operating margin falls below **43%** without a demonstrably attractive incremental-return explanation.
3. SBC-adjusted cash generation (CFO − capex − finance leases − SBC) fails to cover dividends plus dilution-offsetting buybacks for **two consecutive annual periods**.
4. EU DMA gatekeeper designation of Azure (expected by **November 2026**) is finalized with material interoperability/pricing remedies attached.
5. Entry discipline: do not add fresh capital above **$500** (Codex ceiling) / **$444** (my base-case fair value); reassess on any pullback toward those levels.
