## 1. Verdict
**INCLUDE-SMALL** (half weight). Thesis horizon 12–36 months. Best-in-class industrial "growth via serial M&A"
compounder with three consecutive full-year guidance raises in 2026, but the reservation is concrete and dated: the
**$5.0bn all-cash Indicor Instrumentation acquisition closed 26 Aug 2026** (AME 8-K, 2026-08-26, Ex.99.1), funded by
a new $4.0bn senior unsecured term loan plus up to $1.0bn drawn on the revolver (AME 8-K, 2026-06-09/06-12, Ex.10.1/10.2;
confirmed in the 10-Q for the quarter ended 2026-06-30, filed 2026-08-04, Item 2 MD&A, "Liquidity and Capital
Resources"). This roughly triples consolidated debt and lifts net debt/EBITDA from ~0.6x to an estimated ~2.5x
pro forma — a material, undiversified re-leveraging event that has not yet shown up in a reported balance sheet
(Q3 2026 10-Q, due ~Oct 2026, is the first filing that will).

## 2. Business in plain English
AMETEK designs and manufactures specialized electronic instruments (process, aerospace, power, lab/medical
instrumentation — the Electronic Instruments Group, EIG) and electromechanical devices (precision motion, engineered
connectors/materials, aviation MRO — the Electromechanical Group, EMG). It sells to industrial, aerospace, medical
and scientific customers worldwide and makes money on high-margin, mission-critical niche products plus recurring
aftermarket/consumables revenue, growing organically and by continually acquiring bolt-on niche-technology businesses
(source: company profile, AME 10-K FY2025, Item 1 Business; FMP company profile).

## 7. Valuation snapshot & reverse DCF (own build; no V1 row exists for AME — not a top-70/lane name)
NTM adj. P/E on FY2026 guidance midpoint ($8.25): 250.74/8.25 = **30.4x**. TTM FCF/share ≈ $7.93 (pre-Indicor);
Price/FCF ≈ 31.6x; FCF yield ≈ 3.2%.
Reverse DCF (2-stage, 10-yr explicit + Gordon terminal; WACC 8.0% — a low beta of 0.989 vs the S&P but capital
structure is about to change materially, so I did not use a lower cost of debt-blended figure; terminal growth 3.0%):
solving for the FCF/share CAGR that reconciles today's price to the DCF value gives **≈8.4%/yr for 10 years**. AME's
own stated long-term algorithm is "double-digit EPS growth over the business cycle" (Ex.99.1, 2026-08-26 corporate
profile), i.e., ~10%+, of which a meaningful share is historically M&A-funded (like Indicor) rather than pure organic
compounding (organic growth has recently been mid-single-digit; the rest of the mid-teens to record growth in 2026 is
acquisition-inclusive). **Implied growth (~8.4%) sits modestly below my own base case (~10%)** — supportive of
inclusion — but the margin of safety is thin, and the very acquisitions that generate the assumed growth are what
just tripled leverage, so this is not "growth for free."

## 8. Bull case
1. Three straight guidance raises in 2026 with record orders (+28% Q2 2026, +23% Q1 2026) show broad-based demand,
   not one product cycle (Ex.99.1 releases).
2. Proven, repeatable M&A playbook (FARO 2025, First Aviation May 2026, Indicor Aug 2026) with disciplined integration
   and >100% FCF/NI conversion even through deal activity.
3. High-teens core margin expansion this cycle (EIG core margins 30–31%) shows genuine pricing/mix power, not just
   roll-up arithmetic.

## 9. Bear case
1. Pro forma leverage jump to an estimated ~2.5x net debt/EBITDA removes balance-sheet flexibility for 12–24 months
   and raises interest expense (term loan drawn at prevailing 2026 rates, not disclosed in the financing 8-K).
2. Reverse DCF requires ~8.4%/yr FCF growth for a decade — achievable only if the M&A pipeline (which needs the
   now-reduced balance-sheet capacity) keeps delivering; a 2027–2028 M&A pause would likely compress both growth and
   the multiple simultaneously.
3. 30x+ NTM P/E leaves little room for a demand air-pocket in AME's more cyclical end markets (semiconductor capex,
   process industries) despite currently strong orders.

## 10. Key risks & kill criteria (measurable triggers that would invalidate the thesis)
1. Net debt/EBITDA (consolidated, per the 10-Q "Non-GAAP Financial Measures" reconciliation) exceeds 3.0x in any
   quarter through 2027 without a stated deleveraging plan.
2. Organic (ex-acquisition) sales growth falls below 3% for two consecutive quarters.
3. FY2026 adjusted EPS guidance is cut (vs. the current $8.20–$8.30 range) at any subsequent quarterly release.
4. Adjusted operating margin contracts more than 100bp YoY in any quarter (signal of failed Indicor integration or
   pricing loss).
5. A term-loan covenant waiver or amendment is disclosed (would signal leverage stress beyond the base case).
