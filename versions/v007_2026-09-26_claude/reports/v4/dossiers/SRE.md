# SRE — Sempra

## 1. Verdict
**INCLUDE-SMALL** — thesis horizon 24–48 months, reservation: **the "capital recycling" story (KKR infrastructure stake sale, Ecogas México sale) is real, evidenced and consistently affirmed guidance-wise, but as of the data reviewed neither sale had a filing-confirmed close date, so the balance-sheet de-risking the triage note credits is still a forecast, and the reported FCF yield is deeply negative because of a genuinely enormous, still-growing capex plan ($65bn over five years) that must keep being funded.** Reverse-DCF implied growth sits at or below management's own long-term guidance, which supports inclusion, but at small size given the financing and execution risk still open.

## 2. Business in plain English
Sempra is a US/Mexico energy infrastructure company built around two regulated California utilities (SDG&E, SoCalGas), an equity-method stake in Texas transmission utility Oncor (serving the fastest-growing power grid in the country, ERCOT), and Sempra Infrastructure — LNG export (Port Arthur), pipelines and related energy-infrastructure assets, partly being sold down to third-party capital partners. It earns regulator-approved returns on its California/Texas rate base and project-level returns on its LNG and infrastructure assets funded increasingly by outside capital (KKR, Blackstone, others) rather than Sempra's own balance sheet alone.

## 3. Why the model likes it — durable or artefact?
b1 factor scores (2026-09-25): composite percentile only 10.0% (low — the quant model actively dislikes this name), driven by roe pct 1.6% (bottom of the batch), ep pct 4.8% and fcfp pct 12.1% (both near the bottom — deeply negative reported FCF), partially offset by sue pct 55% (a modestly positive recent earnings-surprise trend) and mom_12_1 pct 18% (negative momentum, in line with a weak-value read). **The quant score and the triage's "cheap, 63% guided EPS growth" framing are in real tension** (data_conflict, below): the model sees a low-ROE, negative-FCF, historically expensive-on-trailing-multiples utility, while the triage read the *forward* growth story generously. Both readings are partly right — the -54% trailing FCF yield genuinely reflects the current $65bn capex super-cycle (not distress), but the low trailing ROE (6.1% per b1; the company does not disclose an "earned regulated ROE" figure directly comparable to CPUC/PUCT allowed levels in the sections reviewed) is a real, sector-relevant caution, not solely a legacy artefact.

## 6. Earnings quality & balance sheet
- **Capital recycling program, not yet closed.** Two large announced sales are central to the de-risking thesis the triage note credits: (a) sale of a 45% equity interest in Sempra Infrastructure Partners to affiliates of KKR — per the Q2 2026 release (6 Aug 2026), "remains on track and is expected to close in the third quarter of 2026" (i.e., by 2026-09-30, essentially concurrent with this review's data cutoff — **not confirmed closed as of the sources reviewed**); (b) sale of Ecogas México — approved without condition by Mexico's antitrust authority and "expected to close in August" per the same release. **Neither closing was independently confirmed via a subsequent 8-K in the filings reviewed** (the most recent 8-Ks found, 2026-08-18 and 2026-08-21, were unrelated hybrid/junior-subordinated-notes financings, not deal-closing announcements). Treat both sales as pending, not done, until a closing 8-K is located.
- **Goodwill dropped from $1,602m (Q2 2025) to $0 (Q3 2025 onward)**, and the Q2 2026 balance sheet carries **$12,992m of "Liabilities held for sale"** — consistent with the infrastructure assets being reclassified ahead of the KKR/Ecogas closings, and itself confirming the deals were still pending (not yet closed) as of 2026-06-30.
- **Balance sheet (from the filed 10-Q, period 2026-06-30, since XBRL company-facts lagged):** total assets $115,281m; short-term debt $3,566m + current portion of long-term debt $2,075m + long-term debt and finance leases $31,023m = **total debt ≈$36.66bn** (matches the independent d4 live-snapshot figure of $36,664m exactly, a good cross-check); cash only $48m (Sempra runs on committed revolving credit rather than a cash buffer, typical for a capital-intensive utility holdco); total equity $39,998m (Sempra shareholders' equity $32,685m + $7,293m other non-controlling interests, mostly Oncor's minority partners and Sempra Infrastructure's outside investors).
- **Capex.** "Over $6 billion" invested in H1 2026 alone, part of a "record five-year 2026–2030 capital plan of approximately $65 billion, with 95% allocated to investments at our Texas and California utilities" (Q2 2026 release). This is the direct cause of the deeply negative reported free cash flow (−54% FCF yield per the live snapshot) — per the sector playbook, negative FCF during a rate-base build is a normal growth signal, not distress, provided it converts into an approved, earning rate base, which the Oncor/SDG&E/SoCalGas capex largely should.
- **Oncor is equity-accounted, not consolidated.** The balance sheet carries "Investment in Oncor Holdings" at $19,002m (up from $17,472m at 2025-12-31) — Oncor's own growth (ERCOT hit a new all-time peak load of 91 GW in July 2026; the PUC of Texas approved a streamlined large-load interconnection framework; Oncor's new base rates took effect 1 June 2026) flows to Sempra through equity earnings and dividends, not through consolidated revenue — a structural point that matters for how "SRE growth" should be read and modelled.
- **Dividend**: $0.6575/quarter declared Feb 2026 ($2.63/share annualised, +1.9% vs 2025's $2.58).

## 7. Valuation snapshot and reverse-DCF sense
Price 2026-09-25 close: **$77.93**. NTM P/E 14.4x (fy1 14.08x); P/B 1.56x; ROE (trailing) ≈6.7%; dividend yield ≈3.4%; consensus NTM EPS growth ≈+8.4% (broadly consistent with the company's own guided 7–9% long-term CAGR). No V1 systematic-valuation row exists for SRE.

Using the same justified-P/B framework as the ES dossier (Ke via CAPM: rf 4.16%, ERP 5.0%, beta 0.564 → Ke≈7.0%; earned ROE 6.7%): solving 1.56 = (0.067 − g)/(0.070 − g) gives a **negative implied g (≈ −4%)**, which is not economically meaningful as a growth read (it mechanically falls out of a trailing ROE that sits *below* the CAPM cost of equity — i.e., on a pure trailing-ROE-vs-book basis the stock "shouldn't" trade above book at all, yet it does at 1.56x). That mismatch is itself informative: **the market is not valuing SRE on trailing ROE/book**, it is valuing it on the *forward* earnings power once Oncor's Texas growth, the LNG ramp (Port Arthur) and the post-recycling balance sheet show up — consistent with the company's own affirmed 7–9% long-term EPS CAGR and FY2030 outlook of $6.70–$7.50 (implying roughly 8–9%/yr growth off the FY2026 guidance midpoint, i.e., **management's own guided growth is within, not below, the top of its stated range** — a useful cross-check that the story is guided, not merely hoped for). On a straightforward NTM-P/E Gordon-growth solve instead of P/B (P = EPS_ntm×(1+g)/(Ke−g)): 77.93 = 5.423×(1+g)/(0.070−g) → **g ≈ 5.6%**, comfortably *below* the company's own guided 7–9% CAGR. **That is the more decision-relevant read**: the price is not paying up for the full guided growth rate.

**Bear case:** the KKR (45% Sempra Infrastructure) and Ecogas sales slip, are repriced lower, or fail to close, leaving Sempra to fund the $65bn capex plan with more debt/equity dilution than planned, while California wildfire-liability exposure (SDG&E, under AB 1054/SB 254) remains an open tail risk explicitly flagged in the company's own forward-looking-statement risk list → 3yr annualised return ≈ **0%**.
**Base case:** both sales close roughly as described (Q3 2026 for KKR, August 2026 for Ecogas), proceeds fund the capital plan without material incremental equity dilution, Oncor's ERCOT-driven growth and the guided 7–9% EPS CAGR are delivered → 3yr annualised return ≈ **+11%**.
**Bull case:** ERCOT demand growth (91 GW peak, the $7bn+ transmission pipeline Oncor is positioned to build) pulls SRE's growth toward the top of, or above, the 7–9% range, and Port Arthur Phase 2 (third-party-financed) comes online on schedule → 3yr annualised return ≈ **+19%**.

## 8. Bull case / Bear case
**Bull (3 points):**
1. Guidance has been affirmed, not cut or dramatically raised, across three consecutive quarterly releases (Q4'25, Q1'26, Q2'26) at $4.80–$5.30 adjusted EPS, with FY2027 and a first FY2030 outlook ($6.70–$7.50) also issued — an unusually stable, multi-year guided growth path for the sector.
2. Oncor's ERCOT exposure captures genuine, evidenced structural demand growth (91 GW record peak load, PUCT-endorsed multi-billion-dollar transmission buildout) that most of the peer batch does not have.
3. The capital-recycling program (KKR infrastructure stake, Ecogas) is explicitly designed to fund the $65bn five-year capex plan without proportional balance-sheet strain — a credible, disclosed plan, even though not yet fully executed.

**Bear (3 points):**
1. Trailing ROE (6.7%) is the lowest in the peer batch per the quant data, and reported free cash flow is deeply negative (−54% yield) — even allowing for the sector-normal "negative FCF during a capex build" explanation, this is a large, multi-year funding gap that depends on the pending asset sales closing on time and on terms.
2. Neither the KKR Sempra Infrastructure stake sale nor the Ecogas México sale was confirmed closed in the primary sources reviewed as of the 2026-09-25 cutoff, despite both being "expected" to close within weeks of that date — real execution/timing risk sits directly on the critical path of the deleveraging thesis.
3. California wildfire liability is a standing, sector-specific tail risk for SDG&E explicitly named in the company's own risk-factor language, not fully offset by the AB 1054/SB 254 wildfire fund mechanisms in every loss scenario.

## 9. Key risks & kill criteria (measurable) -- what would invalidate this thesis
1. The KKR 45% Sempra Infrastructure Partners sale does not close by 2026-12-31 (three months past the company's own "Q3 2026" target).
2. The Ecogas México sale does not close by 2026-10-31 (two months past the company's own "expected in August" target).
3. FY2026 adjusted EPS finishes outside the affirmed $4.80–$5.30 range.
4. Total debt (per the consolidated balance sheet) grows faster than the approved rate base at Oncor/SDG&E/SoCalGas for two consecutive quarters, without a corresponding equity or asset-sale offset.
5. Any SDG&E wildfire-liability claim or CPUC disallowance is disclosed that is not fully covered by the AB 1054/SB 254 wildfire fund mechanism.
