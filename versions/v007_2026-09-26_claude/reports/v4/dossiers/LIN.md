# LIN — Linde plc — Diligence Dossier (Agent F53, wave 3)

## 1. Verdict
**WATCH** — the world's largest industrial-gas company, an genuine oligopoly (with Air Liquide, Air Products) with a record, largely contracted project backlog and a real, evidenced string of guidance raises. But the reverse DCF implies growth above Linde's own guided long-term earnings algorithm, and the current elevated-capex phase (funding that backlog) is temporarily depressing free cash flow, making today's price look even more demanding on an FCF basis. A great business, priced for more than its own guidance says it will deliver — the addendum's own standard for WATCH, not INCLUDE. Horizon: revisit if the multiple de-rates or backlog conversion accelerates growth above the current guided range.

## 2. Business in plain English
Linde produces and distributes industrial and medical gases (oxygen, nitrogen, hydrogen, argon, CO2, specialty gases) via on-site plants built next to large customers (steel, chemicals, refining, electronics/semiconductor fabs, healthcare), pipeline networks and bulk/cylinder delivery. On-site contracts are typically 15–20 year take-or-pay agreements passing through energy costs — very low volume/price risk once built. Moat: capital intensity, pipeline network density and long-term contracts make it nearly impossible for a new entrant to replicate a regional gas network; Linde, Air Liquide and Air Products effectively split the global on-site/merchant market.

## 8. Bull case / Bear case
**Bull:** (1) record $11B project backlog (record $8.1B sale-of-gas backlog) is largely contracted/take-or-pay, de-risking multi-year growth visibility; (2) three consecutive guidance raises show real execution momentum into a historically defensive, oligopolistic business; (3) electronics/semiconductor and clean-hydrogen end-markets provide a genuine secular growth optionality layer on top of the base industrial-gas business (data-center/AI-driven electricity and gas demand is a plausible tailwind, though **not confirmed in Linde's own Q2 2026 release**, which discussed "electronics" strength generically without naming AI/hyperscaler demand specifically — flagged as an unconfirmed narrative some investors use).
**Bear:** (1) the reverse DCF and the company's own long-term EPS algorithm disagree by ~3-4 points a year — the market is pricing execution above what Linde itself promises; (2) net debt/EBITDA of 1.67x plus capex guidance raised to $5.5–6.0B (from an original $5.0–5.5B) means the balance sheet is being leaned on harder to fund growth, at a time global industrial production (steel, chemicals — core end markets) is only modestly growing; (3) Q4 2025 GAAP net income fell 11% YoY on purchase-accounting/restructuring items — a reminder that the 2018 Linde AG/Praxair merger still carries real amortization drag on GAAP results, and "adjusted" EPS is doing a lot of the guidance-beat work.

## 9. Key risks & kill criteria (measurable)
1. Net debt/EBITDA > 2.25x (vs. 1.67x today) without a disclosed, value-accretive reason (e.g., large M&A).
2. Full-year adjusted-EPS guidance **cut** (vs. the immediately preceding range) — would break the three-quarter raise streak that is central to the bull case.
3. Project backlog shrinks (vs. the current $11B) for two consecutive quarterly disclosures — a leading indicator that contracted future growth is not being replenished.
4. Underlying/organic sales growth < 2% for two consecutive quarters (Q2 2026 was +4% organic — a decline back toward flat would signal industrial-cycle weakness).
5. GAAP net income growth negative for two consecutive quarters beyond what is explained by disclosed purchase-accounting/restructuring items.
