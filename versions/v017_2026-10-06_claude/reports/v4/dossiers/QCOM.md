# Qualcomm Inc. (QCOM) - Fundamental Diligence Dossier (F133, standard depth)

**Verdict: WATCH**, 12-36 month horizon. The business is strong and its data-centre and car push is real, but at $201.97 (25 Sep 2026 close) the price asks for roughly 14% a year of free-cash-flow growth after charging for stock pay, against about 5% in my base case; the quick triage said the same thing for a thinner reason. What would change it: a price near or below about $165 (about 16x the FY27 consensus of $10.18 non-GAAP EPS), or two quarters showing disclosed data-centre revenue while handset revenue stabilises and TTM free cash flow recovers above $9B.

## 1. Business in plain English
Qualcomm designs the chips and modems inside most premium Android phones (QCT segment: handsets, automotive, IoT) and licenses its patents to nearly every phone maker (QTL segment, about 70% pre-tax margin). QCT is 86% of quarterly revenue; handsets still gave $5.1B of $8.5B QCT revenue in Q3 FY26. Its biggest customer, Apple, is replacing Qualcomm modems with its own; the 10-Q (0000804328-26-000086) says it expects Apple "will increasingly use its own modem products". Growth is now meant to come from automotive (+61% YoY in Q3), IoT (+9%) and a new data-centre business.

## 7. Bull and bear
Bull: (1) Automotive revenue $1,588M in Q3 (+61%; "23 Consecutive Quarters of Double-Digit Year-Over-Year Growth") and data centre: on 3 Sep 2026 Qualcomm issued an Amazon affiliate a warrant tied to "up to a maximum amount of $60 billion in payments" of server-chip purchases (8-K 0001104659-26-105718), a named customer commitment. (2) Management says China handset revenue bottomed in Q3 FY26 and pricing actions should lift gross margin. (3) $20B buyback authorisation and a 1.9% dividend.
Bear: (1) Handsets, still 60% of QCT, fell 20% in Q3 and Apple roll-off continues; FY27 consensus EPS is below FY26. (2) The Amazon warrant is customer compensation: 25M shares at $161.26 against $201.97 is about $1.0B of intrinsic value (2.3% dilution) before any purchases beyond the 3,750,000 shares vested at issue; economics of the chips are not disclosed. (3) Cash conversion fell, inventory is up 28%, R&D and SG&A are growing 17-27% against falling revenue, and GAAP operating margin fell to 16%.

## 8. Key risks and kill criteria (for an existing or later position)
1. Non-GAAP diluted EPS below $2.05 for Q4 FY26 or below $1.80 in any later quarter.
2. QCT handset revenue falls below $4.5B in any quarter of FY27, or China handset revenue has not returned to sequential growth by Q1 FY27 as the April 2026 release (quoted in section 4) said it would.
3. TTM free cash flow (operating cash flow minus capital expenditures, consolidated) below $8.0B at any quarter-end.
4. Consolidated debt (short- plus long-term, carrying value) above $20B, or inventory above $9.5B while revenue is below $10B a quarter.
5. No disclosed data-centre revenue (reported as its own line) by the FY27 Q2 report (May 2027), or the Amazon warrant vesting above 12,500,000 shares without matching disclosed revenue.
