# BAX - Baxter International (agent F157, as of 2026-09-25 close, price $23.37)

## 1. Verdict
**WATCH** - the sales recovery is real but the 2026 guidance raise is mostly a one-off tariff refund, leverage is about 3.9x with a loosened covenant, and the price already implies more FCF growth than my base case. Horizon 12-36 months. Upgrade condition: two straight quarters of organic growth of at least 2% without tariff help, trailing FCF of at least $600M, net debt/adj. EBITDA under 3.5x, and the Novum IQ hold lifted. Research only, not personal advice.

## 2. Business in plain English
Baxter sells hospital essentials: IV solutions and drug compounding, infusion pumps, surgical sealants, hospital beds, patient monitors and connected-care software (Hillrom, acquired 2021). Customers are hospitals, supplied through group-purchasing contracts. It sold the kidney-care unit (Vantive) to Carlyle on 31 Jan 2025 and now reports two segments, Medical Products & Therapies (about $2.1B of Q2 sales) and Healthcare Systems & Technologies ($0.8B). Moat is installed base and GPO contracts; the recent record shows weak execution, not a collapsing franchise.

## 3. Why the model likes it (b1/d4) and is it durable
b1 ranks BAX 405 of 496 (decile 2): quality 0.42, value 0.35, momentum 0.34 and size/safety 0.37 are all middling-to-weak. The attraction is a low 11.5x NTM P/E. That is partly an artefact: adjusted EPS excludes about $0.6B a year of intangible amortisation ($291M in H1 26) and, in Q2 2026, includes a $0.11 tariff refund. GAAP continuing EPS was -$1.75 for FY2025. I agree with the Q07 triage on "do not advance" but not on the reason: leverage and margins are the issue, not a broken franchise.

## 8. Bull and bear
Bull: (1) sales have turned, +5% organic in Q2 26 with growth in every division; (2) adjusted operating margin rebuilt from 11.0% (Q1 26) to 14.2% (Q2 26), and Novum IQ release would add high-margin pump revenue; (3) at 11x earnings, small FCF gains move the equity a lot (equity is 62% of EV).
Bear: (1) Q2 guidance lift equals the one-off refund; (2) 3.9x leverage with a loosened covenant, negative rating outlooks and a $844M current maturity; (3) a new interim-CFO and securities-litigation cloud, plus further impairments possible.

## 9. Key risks and kill criteria
1. Organic sales growth below 1% in either of the next two quarterly reports (ex tariff effects).
2. Consolidated net debt / adjusted EBITDA above 4.2x (Baxter consolidated, 10-Q balance sheet and non-GAAP reconciliation), or a downgrade of senior unsecured debt to below investment grade by S&P or Moody's.
3. Full-year 2026 adjusted EPS guidance midpoint cut below $1.95.
4. Further goodwill or trade-name impairment on Front Line Care ($1.51B) or Welch Allyn ($390M).
5. Trailing-twelve-month FCF (operating cash flow less capex, continuing operations) below $500M at any quarter-end, or no Novum IQ US shipment resumption by 30 Jun 2027.
