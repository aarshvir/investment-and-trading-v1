# LDOS — Leidos Holdings, Inc.

## 1. Verdict

**INCLUDE-SMALL** (half weight) — thesis horizon 12–36 months. A diversified US federal-government IT/defense/intel/health/civil contractor with a $48.7bn backlog, a clean four-quarter beat-and-raise FY2026, and a genuinely cheap valuation (9.8x NTM P/E, 7.4% FCF yield — a reverse DCF implies only ~3.6%/year 10-year FCF growth against guided ~7% FY2026 EPS growth even before crediting further M&A) **[Corrected 2026-10-06: FY2026 non-GAAP EPS guide midpoint $12.35 vs FY2025 actual $11.99 is +3.0%, not 7%]**. The named reservation: leverage jumped from ≈2.0x to **≈2.45x net debt/EBITDA** **[Corrected 2026-10-06: per the 10-Q and releases net debt is $5,283M on TTM adjusted EBITDA of $2,417M = 2.2x, up from 1.5x at year-end 2025]** after a debt-funded $2.4bn acquisition (ENTRUST, closed 30 March 2026) that meaningfully changes the company's risk profile, and the triage's own flagged red flag — US federal budget/DOGE-era spending-cut policy risk to contract awards — is real, ongoing, and only partially tested so far (Leidos has weathered DOGE cancellations better than peers, but a 93-person WARN-notice layoff tied to a CBP contract change in March 2026 shows the risk is not merely theoretical). Half-weight reflects genuine business quality and a real margin of safety, offset by event-driven leverage and policy risk that a full-weight holding should not carry.

## 2. Business in plain English

Leidos is one of the largest US government IT-services and defense-technology contractors, organized into four reporting segments: **Intelligence & Digital** ($1,499m of Q2 2026 revenue, the largest segment — intelligence-community IT, cyber, digital modernization), **Health** ($1,086m — health IT and managed health services, including large VA/DHA contracts), **Homeland** ($1,018m — border/security/aviation screening technology, including TSA-related work), and **Defense** ($955m — defense systems, sensors, and the Dynetics missile-defense/hypersonics business). It earns money almost entirely from multi-year US federal government contracts (cost-plus, fixed-price, and time-and-materials mixes), competing on cleared personnel, incumbency, and technical differentiation rather than on price alone. The March 2026 acquisition of ENTRUST Solutions Group (energy-infrastructure engineering, ~3,100 professionals) is a deliberate diversification move — it roughly doubles Leidos' presence in electric-grid and natural-gas engineering, a non-federal, utility-customer revenue stream that reduces (modestly, at this scale) the company's near-total dependence on US government appropriations. **[Corrected 2026-10-06: the pending divestiture of Security Enterprise Solutions and Industrial Automation into the Analogic JV (41.5% retained; assets held for sale $935M; closed 5 Oct 2026) is not mentioned anywhere in this dossier and will deconsolidate part of Homeland revenue]**

## 8. Bull case / bear case

**Bull (3):**
1. Four consecutive quarters of guidance raises in FY2026 (revenue and non-GAAP EPS both raised at Q1 and Q2), a 1.1–1.3x book-to-bill and $48.7bn total backlog ($10.2bn funded) — real, filed evidence of demand, not merely a cheap multiple.
2. NTM P/E of 9.8x and a 7.4% FCF yield are genuinely inexpensive for a company guiding to high-single-digit EPS growth — the reverse DCF implies the market is pricing in almost no growth.
3. The ENTRUST acquisition is a disclosed, deliberate diversification into non-federal, utility-customer revenue (energy-grid engineering) that — if integration goes to plan — reduces Leidos' near-total dependence on US federal appropriations over time, a structural de-risking the market has not yet given credit for.

**Bear (3):**
1. The triage's own flagged red flag is real and only partially tested: US federal budget/DOGE-era spending-cut policy risk to contract awards and backlog conversion. Leidos has weathered DOGE cancellations better than named peers (Accenture Federal, Deloitte Federal, IBM all lost more in disclosed cancellations), but the March 2026 WARN notice (93 jobs, Ashburn VA, tied to a CBP contract change) is a concrete, dated instance of the risk materializing at a small scale — a larger recompete loss or a prolonged government shutdown (a risk explicitly flagged by analysts per Federal News Network reporting) remains a live, unhedged exposure.
2. Net debt/EBITDA rose from a comfortable level to ≈2.45x in a single quarter via a $1.4bn debt-funded acquisition — a real, disclosed change in risk profile that increases sensitivity to any combination of margin pressure and rate resets on the new notes (4.10% and 5.00% coupons).
3. A DOJ/SEC investigation stemming from a 2021 internal FCPA-related inquiry (international operations, third-party representatives) remains only partially resolved: the DOJ closed its investigation with no further action in December 2024, but the **SEC investigation was still open** as of the most recent 10-K reviewed in this search — an unresolved legal overhang, low in probability of material impact but not yet formally closed.
