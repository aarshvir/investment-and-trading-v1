# UNP — Union Pacific Corporation (Class I railroad) — diligence dossier, agent F120

Prices as of the 25 Sep 2026 close ($273.79). Research only, not personalised investment advice.

## 1. Verdict
**WATCH** (horizon 12-36 months). A top-quality franchise with improving operations and a record quarter, but at 21.0x 2026 EPS the price already embeds about 7.7% a year of EPS growth for ten years against my 6.5% base case, and the pending Norfolk Southern (NSC) merger adds a $2.5B break fee exposure, about 225M new shares and about $20B of new cash debt with approval not expected before Q3/Q4 2027. What would change it: a pullback to about $240 (18x), or an STB decision with acceptable conditions.

Triage (Q10) said not to advance on "19.7x NTM multiple already embeds merger synergies". I confirm the verdict but correct the reason: the quoted "29% margin, 40% ROE" are not what the filings show (Q2-26 operating margin 40.3%; return on equity about 35% on a book equity base that is small because of buybacks), and standalone valuation, not merger synergy, is what the price is testing. V1 could not run a reverse DCF (D&A missing) but gave "demanding", base 3y return +2.9%.

## 2. Business in plain English
UNP runs the western half of the US freight rail network ("Operating in 23 western states", 8-K acc. 0000100885-26-000249). It charges customers to move grain, chemicals, autos, containers and coal. Two railroads per region give it real pricing power: it has raised price above inflation for years ("Pricing dollars in excess of inflation dollars"). Earnings depend on volume, fuel surcharges and the operating ratio (costs as a share of revenue; lower is better). It has agreed to buy Norfolk Southern to create a coast-to-coast railroad.

## 3. Why the model likes it, and whether that is durable
b1 composite 0.867 (decile 9, live rank 66): momentum is the driver (M 0.70; 12-1 month return +29.9%), S 0.80, V only 0.56, Q 0.54. Momentum will not persist by itself; the quality part (pricing power, OR near 60%) is durable.

## 8. Bull and bear
Bull: (1) record Q2 with volume +2% and, per the outlook, "Pricing dollars in excess of inflation dollars"; workforce productivity +5%; (2) management raised the outlook to "Reported earnings per share growth increased to high-single digit"; (3) a transcontinental network could take truck share if the STB approves.
Bear: (1) fuel pass-through lags: Q2 OR worsened 70 bp y/y despite record revenue; (2) deal risk is asymmetric (10-Q: "approximately 225 million shares", "approximately $ 20 billion of cash consideration"), STB review calendar with comments due 18 Nov 2026 and close guided Q3/Q4 2027, plus NSC's Eastern Ohio liabilities; (3) 21x earnings with buybacks suspended and some 2026 cash flow from tax timing.

## 9. Key risks, kill criteria and thesis invalidation triggers (entity: consolidated UPC)
1. Reported operating ratio at or above 61.5% for two consecutive quarters (Q2-26 59.7%; 8-K Ex. 99.1 income statement).
2. Freight revenue excluding fuel surcharge growth below 1% y/y for two consecutive quarters (Q2-26 +4%).
3. Adjusted debt / adjusted EBITDA above 3.0x on a standalone basis (10-Q MD&A table; 2.5x at 30 Jun 2026), or management-stated pro forma leverage above 3.5x at closing.
4. STB denies the application, or UPC/NSC terminate (the $2.5B fee applies in certain cases).
5. Guidance cut: 2026 reported EPS growth outlook reduced below "mid-single digit" (currently "high-single digit").
