# MAA - Mid-America Apartment Communities, Inc. (research only; not personal investment advice)

**1. Verdict: INCLUDE-SMALL.** A Sun Belt apartment REIT at about 15.8x guided AFFO with a 5.2% dividend yield; the price implies only about 1.5% a year AFFO growth, below my base of about 3%. Named reservation: Core FFO has fallen three years running (2024 $8.88, 2025 $8.74, 2026 guide midpoint $8.53), leverage is creeping up (4.3x to 4.5x) and the recovery is prospective, not yet delivered. Horizon 24-48 months. Triage was model-gated; filings support a half-weight position, not full weight.

**2. Business in plain English.** MAA owns and runs apartment communities across the US Sun Belt (Texas, Florida, Georgia, the Carolinas, Tennessee). Residents pay monthly rent on leases of about a year; MAA keeps rent less operating costs, and adds value by developing new communities and redeveloping older ones. Its edge is scale, low resident turnover (39.6% trailing) and a long-dated, mostly fixed-rate debt book (6.0 years average maturity).

**3. Why the model likes it.** Quality family 0.79 (b1), value 0.40, momentum 0.22; composite rank 402/402; GAAP earnings yield 2.8% (REIT GAAP earnings understate cash because of depreciation, so FFO/AFFO is the right lens). Quality is real (stable rent collection) but price momentum is weak (-3% over 12-1 months).
