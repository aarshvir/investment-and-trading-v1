# GE HealthCare Technologies Inc. (NASDAQ: GEHC) — Diligence Dossier (Agent F67)

## 1. Verdict
**INCLUDE-SMALL** — a genuine oligopoly imaging/diagnostics franchise with record orders and backlog, but the reservation is specific and named: full-year 2026 guidance was **cut once already** (tariffs, input-cost inflation, a struggling Patient Care Solutions segment) and first-half free-cash-flow conversion is unusually weak (~19% of net income), so near-term earnings quality is softer than the headline 16.5% EPS growth suggests. Thesis horizon: 24–36 months.

## 2. Business in plain English
GE HealthCare designs, makes and services medical imaging equipment (CT, MRI, ultrasound, X-ray/mammography), patient-monitoring hardware, and pharmaceutical imaging agents (contrast media, radiotracers) used by hospitals and clinics worldwide. It earns roughly 40% of revenue from services/software on its large installed base (recurring, high-margin), spun off from General Electric in January 2023.

## 3. Why the model likes it — durable or artefact?
b1 factor scores: composite is mid-pack (live_rank 458, "full" composite from all 4 families) but quality/value metrics stand out — 19.4% ROE, 9.3% profit margin, momentum roughly flat (12-1m −0.3%). The triage case (12.6x NTM P/E, 19% ROE) is directionally right but **incomplete**: it did not capture the Q1-2026 guidance cut or the tariff/PCS drag, both filing-confirmed below. Growth is real (record orders +11.1% organic in Q2-26, backlog $23.9bn) but **near-term profit growth is partly an artefact**: Q2-26 net income and EPS both benefited materially from a one-off $129m IEEPA tariff refund (see §4/§11), which is not a repeatable driver.

## 8. Bull case / bear case
**Bull:** (1) Record Q2-26 orders (+11.1% organic) and $23.9bn backlog (book-to-bill 1.15x) point to accelerating revenue over 2027; (2) Pharmaceutical Diagnostics and the newly combined Imaging/Advanced Visualization segment are growing and profitable, funding continued buybacks and a (small) dividend; (3) Intelerad adds a cloud-enterprise-imaging software layer that could improve the overall margin mix over time.
**Bear:** (1) Patient Care Solutions is explicitly "challenged" and under strategic review by management's own words (CEO Arduini, Q2-26 release) — a segment sale or further charges are plausible near-term catalysts, positive or negative; (2) FY2026 guidance was already cut once on tariffs/inflation/PCS, and H1 FCF conversion of ~18-19% of net income is weak — if that does not normalize in H2, the ~$1.6bn FCF guide is at risk; (3) $129m of the Q2-26 EPS beat was a one-off tariff refund — normalized earnings growth is materially slower than the headline.

## 9. Key risks & kill criteria
1. **A second FY2026 guidance cut** (Adjusted EBIT margin below 15.4% or Adjusted EPS below $4.80) at Q3 or Q4-2026.
2. **Full-year 2026 free cash flow prints below ~$1.4bn** (a >10% miss to the already-cut ~$1.6bn guide), given H1's ~19% FCF/NI conversion.
3. **Net debt/EBITDA rises above 3.0x** (currently ~2.2x, post-Intelerad).
4. **Patient Care Solutions strategic review results in a value-destructive divestiture** (large impairment or dis-synergy loss) rather than a clean exit.
5. **Organic revenue growth falls below 3%** for two consecutive quarters (breaking the low end of the reaffirmed 3.0–4.0% guide).
