# RTX (RTX Corporation) — Diligence Dossier (Agent F105, wave 6)

## 1. Verdict
**INCLUDE-SMALL** (half weight) — reservation: the stock has already re-rated on repeatedly-raised guidance and
record backlog; this agent's own reverse DCF says the market is still not pricing in the full raised outlook, but
the position should stay smaller than a clean INCLUDE given (a) single-name concentration in a company whose
largest engine program (GTF) is mid-recovery, not fully resolved, and (b) defense-budget/export-policy headline
risk that is outside company control. Thesis horizon: 12–36 months.

## 2. Business in plain English
RTX is a diversified aerospace & defense prime formed from the 2020 Raytheon–United Technologies merger, organized
in three segments: Collins Aerospace (avionics, cabin, mechanical systems), Pratt & Whitney (jet engines,
including the geared turbofan/GTF for the Airbus A320neo family), and Raytheon (missiles, radar, air-defense
systems, sold mostly to the US government and allies). It makes money on new-equipment sales plus a large,
higher-margin recurring aftermarket/spares and services business, and from multi-year defense contracts with a
record backlog.

## 3. Why the model likes it / durability
b1 factor data: composite decile only 3/quintile 2 (below median), driven down by weak Value family (pct_ep 28%,
pct_bp 69% — mixed) and modest Quality (pct_gp_a 58%, pct_roe 23%) even though Momentum is strong (pct_mom_12_1

momentum/earnings-surprise story trading at a valuation that is not cheap on simple value factors — consistent

defense demand) is durable or a one-off catch-up matters more than the composite score here; see §11 for the
GTF-specific facts. (Gates: not a program-level disqualifying-gate score — this is the b1 quant screen's

## 8. Bull case / bear case
**Bull (3):**
1. GTF fleet recovery continues (AOG count down 25% YTD per Q2 2026 call) and GTF Advantage certification/deliveries
   (started in 2026) improve time-on-wing, reducing the inspection/AOG drag on both cash and reported earnings.
2. Record backlog plus repeatedly-raised guidance (organic sales growth guide moved from 5–6% to 8–9% within two
   quarters) signals genuine demand acceleration, not just a beat-and-raise ritual.
3. Aftermarket mix shift (higher-margin spares/services growing faster than OE) supports further margin expansion
   even without unit-volume acceleration.

**Bear (3):**
1. GTF issue is not fully resolved — full production transition to GTF Advantage is not expected until 2028; any
   slippage in AOG reduction or a new metallurgical/quality issue would hit both P&W margins and Airbus customer
   relationships.
2. RTX's revenue is government-budget-dependent (Raytheon/defense); a US defense-budget continuing resolution,
   sequestration, or allied-spending pullback is a macro risk outside company control.
3. Valuation has re-rated alongside the guidance raises (NTM P/E now ~26-27x vs the 24.6x this stock screened at
   for the triage) — most of the good news of the last two quarters is now in the price; a single guidance miss
   after three consecutive raises would likely see an outsized multiple correction.
