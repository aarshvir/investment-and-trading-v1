## 1. Verdict
**INCLUDE-SMALL** (half weight), horizon 12-36 months. Overturns the quick triage ("No": Sentinel Nunn-McCurdy overrun) only partly: the triage's concern is real but dated. The Nunn-McCurdy certification was July 2024 and Sentinel is now *adding* backlog and earning incentives, while the live overrun risk has moved to fixed-price B-21 and to missile/rocket-motor programs (SiAW, GEM 63XL). Named reservation: the raised 2026 EPS guide is mostly below-the-line tax, not operations (segment operating income guidance is unchanged), and an open DOJ civil / DCMA pension-accounting matter could be material. The reverse DCF implies ~3.8% 10-year FCF growth against my 6.5% base, so the price is not demanding, but the margin of safety is modest and the base return is only ~6%/yr.

## 2. Business in plain English
Top-tier US defense prime with four segments (Aeronautics, Defense, Mission, Space). It builds the B-21 bomber, Sentinel ICBM, E-130J TACAMO, IBCS, F-35 center fuselage and radars, rocket motors and satellites, mostly for the US Department of War/Air Force under long contracts; profit depends on cost estimates staying accurate (EAC adjustments) and on cash milestones. Backlog of $104.7bn (record, 10-Q accession 0001133421-26-000034) is ~2.4x annual sales.

## 8. Bull / bear
Bull: (1) record $104.7bn backlog and B-21/Sentinel/TACAMO ramp (Aeronautics +13% in Q2'26); (2) Mission Systems margin 15.4% and improving; (3) FCF inflects as B-21 investment completes while buybacks restart ($2.5bn authorization). Bear: (1) repeat fixed-price charges ($162m GEM 63XL in H1'26, $68m SiAW Q2'26, B-21 $157m Q1'26) - segment margin 10.6% vs 11.8%; (2) EPS raise is tax-driven and buybacks are paused; (3) DOJ CID/DCMA CAS-pension inquiry outcome unquantified ("reasonably possible ... material adverse effect").

## 9. Key risks and kill criteria
- Segment operating margin (consolidated, press-release "Segment operating margin rate") below 9.5% in any quarter of H2 2026, versus 10.6% in Q2'26.
- FY2026 adjusted free cash flow guidance cut below the $3,100m low end, or FY2026 actual below $3,000m.
- A new reach-forward loss or aggregate unfavorable EAC charge of $250m or more on a single program in any quarter (B-21, Sentinel, SiAW, GEM 63XL).
- Resolution of the DCMA/DOJ CAS-pension matter with a disclosed charge above $500m, or cost-disallowance applied to future billings.
- Net debt/EBITDA (consolidated) above 3.0x, or no share repurchases by Q2 2027 despite positive FCF.
