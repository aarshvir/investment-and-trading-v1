# Cadence Design Systems, Inc. (CDNS) — Diligence Dossier (F27, standard depth)

## 1. Verdict
**WATCH** — 12–24 month horizon. The electronic-design-automation (EDA) duopoly moat (with Synopsys) is real and durable, and the company has raised full-year guidance twice in 2026 on genuine AI-driven demand. But this analyst's reverse DCF shows the 25 Sep 2026 close ($326.13, mkt cap ≈$89.95bn) requires ~17%/yr free-cash-flow growth for a full decade — above a reasonable evidence-based base case — **and** the company pleaded guilty in 2025 to a federal criminal export-control violation with an active 3-year probationary compliance regime and ongoing China export-control uncertainty (13% of FY2025 revenue). Re-underwrite on a pullback to a base-case-consistent multiple, or on the probation period ending cleanly with no further BIS/DOJ action.

## 2. Business in plain English
Cadence sells electronic design automation (EDA) software — the specialized tools chip designers use to design, simulate and verify integrated circuits — plus IP blocks and, since a 2026 acquisition, mechanical/systems engineering simulation software. Customers are semiconductor companies and increasingly systems companies (hyperscalers, automotive) designing their own silicon. It is one of only two companies (with Synopsys) whose tools are effectively mandatory for advanced-node chip design, giving it very high switching costs and pricing power, monetized mostly through multi-year subscription/license contracts (hence its large "backlog" disclosure).

## 8. Bull case / Bear case
**Bull:** (1) EDA is a genuine software duopoly with very high switching costs — advanced-node chip design is not practically possible without Cadence's or Synopsys's tools; (2) record backlog ($8.1bn) and two consecutive 2026 guidance raises show real, forward-visible AI-driven demand, not just a one-quarter beat; (3) the Hexagon D&E acquisition diversifies revenue into mechanical/systems simulation, reducing pure semiconductor-cycle dependence.
**Bear:** (1) Cadence pleaded guilty to a federal criminal charge (conspiracy to commit export control violations) in July 2025, paid $140.6m in penalties/forfeitures, and is now under a 3-year DOJ probation plus an active BIS administrative settlement requiring annual compliance audits — a genuine, ongoing legal/regulatory tail risk, not a closed matter; (2) China was 13% of FY2025 revenue and BIS issued (then rescinded) an EDA-specific export-license requirement for China transactions in mid-2025, with a further interim rule referenced as of late September 2025 — the regulatory environment for exactly this company's China business is unusually unsettled; (3) at the current price the market requires ~17%/yr FCF growth for a decade, above even the company's own (already-decelerating) ~19% FY2026 guide extrapolated forward, let alone a conservative base case.

## 9. Key risks & kill criteria (measurable)
1. Any new BIS or DOJ enforcement action, or a finding of probation non-compliance, is disclosed (a single such event, given the active 3-year plea-agreement probation).
2. China-attributed revenue disclosure falls below 8% of total revenue in any 10-K/10-Q (an unexplained sudden drop signalling export-control impact) — track against the current 13% (FY2025).
3. YoY revenue growth decelerates below 12% for two consecutive quarters (vs. the ~19% FY2026 guide and 24.2% latest quarterly print).
4. Non-GAAP-to-GAAP operating margin gap widens above 20 percentage points for two consecutive quarters (currently ~14–17 points), signalling SBC/acquisition costs are outrunning underlying profitability.
5. FY guidance is cut (not just grown more slowly) at any quarterly release — breaking the current two-raises-in-a-row streak.

## 11. Red-flag scan
**Material red flag confirmed from the primary source (FY2025 10-K, Risk Factors, p.16 equivalent):** on 2025-07-27, Cadence reached a settlement with both BIS and the DOJ resolving export-control violations that occurred 2015–2021, involving ~$45.3m of sales and unauthorized technology transfer to a China-based customer/third party; Cadence pleaded guilty to one count of conspiracy to commit export control violations (3-year probation, ongoing compliance/reporting obligations); aggregate penalties and forfeitures of $140.6m were paid in Q3 2025. Separately, BIS informed Cadence on 2025-05-23 that certain EDA software/technology required a license for transactions involving China or Chinese "military end users," a requirement rescinded 2025-07-02, with a further interim BIS rule referenced as effective 2025-09-29 (not read in full in this pass). No auditor change, material weakness, restatement or going-concern language found in the FY2025 10-K. Insider Form 4 pattern: not reviewed in this pass (time-boxed); flagged as a gap.
