# LUV - Southwest Airlines Co. (NYSE: LUV) - standard-depth diligence (agent F134)

**1. Verdict: WATCH. Horizon 12-24 months.** Triage said not to advance because of negative free cash flow and an unproven revenue overhaul. Primary sources partly overturn the "unproven" point (Q2 2026 adjusted revenue +20.3%, adjusted operating margin 6.7% vs 3.4%) but confirm the risk: full-year guidance was cut at the midpoint ($3.25-4.25 from "at least $4.00") and is back-half loaded, earnings swing on fuel, and trailing free cash flow is negative. Condition that would move it to INCLUDE-SMALL: Q3 adjusted EPS at or above $0.75 and the full-year range narrowed to a low end of at least $3.75, or net capex held at or below $3.0B with positive free cash flow for two quarters.

**2. Business in plain English.** Largest US low-fare domestic airline (803 aircraft, 30 Jun 2026). Until 2025 it had open seating, free bags and no fare tiers. In 2025-26 it added bag fees, basic economy, assigned and extra-legroom seating (launched 27 Jan 2026), loyalty changes and a richer Chase co-brand card deal. About 60% of customers bought up from the base fare in Q1 2026 (vs about 20% in 2025, Q1 release). Moat is network density and brand; weakness is a single aircraft type, Boeing delivery dependence, and a fuel-exposed cost base.

**3. Why the model likes it.** Cheap on forward earnings (about 11x the midpoint of the "$3.25 to $4.25" range) with strong 12-month price momentum and improving margins; earnings revisions are the driver. Durable or artefact? The improvement is real but the 2026 comparison is flattered by a weak 2025 base (Q1 2025 GAAP loss) and by very high unit revenue growth (+16% in Q2) that will lap in 2027; the quality scores are distorted by the equity decline after $2.25B (H1 2025) and $1.25B (H1 2026) of buybacks.

**8. Bull (3):** (a) Revenue levers are showing up: managed business +30%, Rapid Rewards enrolments +35%, Chase card acquisitions +28% (Q2). (b) Cost discipline: CASM-X +3.4% vs guide above; fleet renewal (64 deliveries, about 60 retirements in 2026). (c) Every $0.50 a gallon fuel move is worth about $1.7 of EPS (my estimate from about 2.2B gallons), so fuel relief is large upside. **Bear (3):** (a) Fuel: Q2 fuel expense +$889M; guide assumes $3.70-3.75 a gallon for Q3. (b) Back-half loaded guide and seasonal Q4; revenue growth laps the 2025 bag-fee start in H2 (Q3 RASM guide is lower than Q2's +20%). (c) About $2.1B of 2027 debt, negative trailing FCF, and Boeing delivery and FAA certification risk for the 737-7.

**9. Kill criteria (measurable).**
1. Full-year 2026 adjusted EPS guide lowered below the $3.25 low end, or withdrawn.
2. Q3 2026 adjusted EPS below $0.50 (low end of guide).
3. Adjusted RASM growth below +10% YoY in Q4 2026 (Q2 2026: +20.1%; Q3 guide +17.5% to +19.5%).
4. Company gross leverage above 3.0x (2.1x at Q2) or liquidity below $4.0B ($5.3B at Q2).
5. 2026 net capital spending above $3.5B, or H2 2026 operating cash flow less gross capex below -$0.5B.
