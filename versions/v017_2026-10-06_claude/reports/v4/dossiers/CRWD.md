**Verdict: WATCH**, 12-36 month horizon. ## 1. Business in plain English
CrowdStrike sells a cloud security platform (Falcon) on multi-year subscriptions: endpoint protection first, now cloud security, identity, SIEM (Next-Gen SIEM) and AI-agent security, bought module by module (51% of subscription customers use six or more modules; Falcon Flex accounts carry $2.29B ending ARR). Customers are enterprises and governments; revenue is 95% subscription. The moat is a single lightweight agent plus a large telemetry data set, and consolidation of security tools onto one vendor. Main threats: Microsoft bundling, Palo Alto, Zscaler, and a repeat of the July 2024 outage.

## 2. Why the model likes it / durability
Quant context (d4, 25 Sep 2026 close): price $252.13 (after the 4-for-1 split effective 1 Jul 2026 per the 10-Q), revenue growth 25.8%, gross profit/assets high, but earnings yield about 0.02% and forward P/E 157x. The model's attraction is growth and quality; it is not value. Durable part: recurring subscription, 81% non-GAAP subscription gross margin, positive GAAP net income only since Q4 FY26. Artefacts: Yahoo FCF of $1,995M is not the company's FCF (TTM $1,518M, see section 6); the triage's "growth has decelerated to the mid-20s" is stale because net new ARR growth accelerated to 32% (Q1 FY27) and 51% (Q2 FY27).

## 8. Kill criteria (measurable)
1. Net new ARR growth below 20% year on year in any quarter (51% in Q2 FY27), or ending ARR growth below 20%.
2. DOJ or SEC matter on ARR/revenue recognition results in a restatement, an enforcement action or a formal investigation disclosed in a 10-Q or 8-K.
3. Non-GAAP operating margin below 22% for two quarters (25.3% in Q2 FY27), or FCF margin below 22% on a TTM basis (28% now).
4. SBC above 25% of revenue for two consecutive quarters (23.6% in H1 FY27), or diluted share count growing above 3% year on year.
5. A customer-impacting outage of the July 2024 type, or an adverse Delta ruling that implies damages above $500M.
