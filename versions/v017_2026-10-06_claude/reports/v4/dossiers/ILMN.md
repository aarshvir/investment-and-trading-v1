# ILMN - Illumina, Inc. (F136, standard depth, close 2026-09-25: $270.00)

**1. Verdict: WATCH.** Triage (Q07, advance=false) is confirmed, but for a sharper reason than "stock ran": the business really is accelerating, yet the 25 Sep price needs roughly 20% a year of free-cash-flow growth for five years (26% if stock comp is treated as a cost) against my base case of about 12%. Horizon 12-36 months. Would change to INCLUDE-SMALL if the price falls to the region where implied growth is at or below ~12-15% (about $175-$210 a share on my arithmetic) or if 2027 guidance supports mid-teens FCF growth.

**2. Business.** Illumina sells DNA-sequencing machines (NovaSeq X, MiSeq i100), the consumables (reagent kits) each run uses, and services. Customers are clinical labs, pharma, research institutions. Consumables on a growing installed base are the recurring engine; it is the dominant platform in clinical sequencing. Acquired SomaLogic (proteomics) on 2026-01-30. Its inclusion on China's "Unreliable Entities List" is a structural drag on China sales.

**3. Why the model likes it.** b1 composite 0.57 (decile 6, live rank 214, not top-30) is carried by the momentum family (0.97; 12-1 month momentum at the 97th percentile) and quality (0.80); the value family is 0.08 (NTM P/E 45.7x, FCF yield 2.0% in d4). The momentum is real (acceleration, two guidance raises in 2026) but is now an input to the valuation problem, not a margin of safety.
