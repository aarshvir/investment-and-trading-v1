# VMRK - Vivmark Residential (NYSE: VMRK; formerly Equity Residential, EQR) - standard-depth diligence (agent F134)

**1. Verdict: INCLUDE-SMALL. Horizon 24-36 months.** Triage could not identify the company and rejected it as unverifiable; that was a data failure, not a thesis failure. The entity is real: SEC CIK 0000906107 is Equity Residential, renamed Vivmark Residential when it merged with AvalonBay (AVB) on 17 August 2026 (8-K acc. 0001140361-26-033377). It is now the largest US apartment REIT (more than 184,000 homes). Named reservation: it is 6 weeks old as a combined company, has no combined earnings history and has withdrawn per-share FFO guidance; the first combined numbers arrive with Q3 results.

**2. Business in plain English.** It owns and operates apartment communities in high-cost coastal US cities (Northern California, New York, Boston, Seattle, Southern California, Washington DC), earning rent from residents, plus a $4.4B development pipeline. Scale is the argument: the merger press release claims "$175 million of gross synergies and $125 million of net synergies after real estate tax reassessments" (8-K acc. 0001193125-26-233381, Ex. 99.1). Moat is location and scale, not technology; rent growth is capped by supply and local regulation.

**3. Why the model likes it, and data conflicts.** b1 shows mktcap $23.1B (pre-merger share count) at price $61.50, and the triage card shows a 0% dividend yield: these are pre-merger or missing fields. After the merger about 400 million new shares were issued ("the Company issued approximately 400 million Company Common Shares", 8-K acc. 0001140361-26-033377) on top of 374.9M legacy EQR common shares (30 Jun 2026), so common shares are about 775M and market cap about $47.7B at $61.50 (triage says $47.6B). The annualised dividend is $2.81, about 4.6% yield, not zero. The earnings-yield and value factors therefore reflect the legacy EQR share count and must be recomputed; the valuation signal below is my own.
