# TRMB - Trimble Inc. (Nasdaq: TRMB) - wave 8 / F158

**Verdict: INCLUDE-SMALL (half weight)** - the quick triage rejected it as a joint-venture accounting artefact; the filings say otherwise (a real $562.0M goodwill impairment) but also show a business growing recurring revenue about 12% organically whose 25 Sep 2026 price ($57.85, market cap about $13.5B) implies only about 3-6% FCF growth for 10 years versus my 9% base. Named reservation: unremediated material weaknesses in internal control. Horizon 24-36 months.

**Thesis in plain English:** Trimble sells software and survey/positioning hardware to construction, field-work and trucking firms, with a rising share of subscription revenue, and the stock has de-rated to about 16x adjusted earnings while that recurring revenue keeps growing.

## 1. Business
Three segments (Q2'26 release, 8-K acc. 0000864749-26-000106): AECO (software for architects, engineers, construction, owners; Q2 revenue $388.5M, op margin 30.6%), Field Systems (hardware/software for surveying and field work; $442.5M, 32.9%), Transportation and Logistics, T&L ($141.0M, 24.0%). Annualised recurring revenue (ARR) $2.51B, up 14% (12% organic). Gross margin 69.4% GAAP.

## 2. Why the model likes it / durability
b1: cheap on FCF (fcfp 5.7%) and EBIT/EV (4.4%); ROE shows -2.0% because of the T&L impairment (GAAP TTM net loss). Quality ranks mid-pack. Durability: the value signal is real (FCF is genuine, see section 5); the negative ROE is a one-off non-cash charge, not an operating loss, but it signals that an acquired business is worth $562M less than carried. Triage claim ("JV deconsolidation artefact, unverified") is incorrect: the 10-Q Note 6 says "we performed an interim assessment of our goodwill and other long-lived assets and recorded a goodwill impairment of $ 562.0 million in our T&L reporting unit".

## 7. Bull / Bear
Bull: (1) ARR $2.51B growing 12% organically and gross margin at a record; (2) three straight quarterly beats and two FY raises with non-GAAP operating margin 26.8%; (3) capital-light FCF of $773M funds a $1.0B buyback authorisation (about 7% of market cap).
Bear: (1) $562M T&L impairment: management bought that business for more than it is worth, and $1.62B of T&L goodwill remains; (2) material weaknesses in internal control are still unremediated (10-Q Item 4: disclosure controls "were not effective ... because of the material weaknesses"); (3) non-GAAP excludes $170M/yr SBC and recurring "acquisition/restructuring" costs, and Field Systems hardware is cyclical.
