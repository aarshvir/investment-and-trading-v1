## 1. Verdict
**WATCH.** Statistically cheap (8.3x TTM GAAP EPS; 11.6x the underlying FY26 guide; net cash $1.39bn), and the price implies free cash flow shrinking about 5% a year for ten years, below my base case (+3%). But earnings are still falling faster in the second half than in the first (Q3 guide: EPS $0.93-0.98 vs $2.59 a year ago), full-year guidance has been cut twice, Americas comparable sales are -12%, and a new CEO started only on 8 Sep 2026. The probability-weighted 3-year return (about 6-7% a year) does not pay for the risk yet. Horizon 24-36 months. Change the verdict if Americas comps improve to better than -5% with gross margin (ex one-offs) back at or above 56%, or the price falls toward about $75 (near my bear-case value). Triage (Q01_triage.json: advance=false, "value trap") is **partly confirmed**: the falling revenue and guide-down are verified in filings; the named competitors (Alo, Vuori, Athleta) are not named in the documents I read, so that point is unverified.

## 2. Business in plain English
lululemon designs and sells premium technical athletic apparel (yoga, running, training) through 825 company-operated stores, e-commerce and some licensed partners, in North America (Americas), China and the rest of the world. It earns a high gross margin (about 55-60%) because customers pay full price for the brand, and it has grown by opening stores and entering new markets. The problem now is the United States: demand for its core products has weakened, discounting and tariffs have squeezed margins, and international growth, which used to offset it, has slowed.

## 8. Bull and bear
**Bull:** (1) New CEO Heidi O'Neill (27 years at Nike) and a refreshed board bring product and brand discipline; insiders bought (see section 11). (2) Balance sheet: $1.39bn net cash, about 11% of market cap returned yearly via buybacks; the share count is down 5.7% y/y. (3) Further tariff refunds (up to about $95M) and any tariff relief are not in guidance.
**Bear:** (1) Americas comps -12% and International growth slowed to +4% (China Mainland comps -8% in constant dollars): no growth engine is working right now. (2) Margin reset: gross margin ex one-offs 54.2%-54.9% vs 58.5% a year ago, SG&A deleverage of 400 bps, and management is raising marketing spend. (3) Three guide cuts or disappointments in 12 months plus a Q3 EPS guide of -63% y/y; brand-health damage is slow to reverse and discounting worsens inventory quality.
