## 1. Verdict
**INCLUDE-SMALL (half weight).** Thesis horizon 12–24 months. Cheap (9.9x NTM P/E), high-quality (44% TTM ROE, zero funded debt), and management just *raised* FY2027 guidance — but the brand-momentum evidence that should drive a re-rating is deteriorating in real time (HOKA revenue growth 18.5%→14.5%→7.7% over the last three reported quarters; operating margin fell 190bps YoY in the latest quarter). ## 2. Business in plain English
Deckers designs, markets and distributes footwear and apparel under five brands — HOKA (performance running, the growth engine), UGG (heritage lifestyle boots/slippers, the cash cow), Teva, Sanuk and Koolaburra (small, declining "other"). It sells through wholesale (department stores, specialty and national retail chains, ~59% of FY26 sales) and direct-to-consumer (owned stores + e-commerce, ~41%). Manufacturing is 100% outsourced to third-party contractors, predominantly in Vietnam (Deckers has cut China production to an estimated 4–5% of the total), which is the source of its tariff exposure.

## 8. Bull case / Bear case
**Bull:** (1) 8-for-8 quarterly EPS beats with a still-positive average surprise of +25%, a debt-free balance sheet, and a $5bn buyback runway systematically shrinking the share count. (2) Management raised guidance at every one of the last three releases even while absorbing tariff cost inflation — the "beat and raise" machine hasn't actually broken, only slowed. (3) International revenue (+25.5% Q4, +8.4% Q1FY27) and DTC (+13.0% Q1FY27) are still growing well above the group average, showing real runway outside the maturing US wholesale base.
**Bear:** (1) HOKA's growth has nearly collapsed sequentially (18.5%→14.5%→7.7%) in a category (performance running) that On, Nike, Asics and Brooks are all now contesting hard — this could be genuine category maturation, not noise. (2) Operating margin fell 190bps YoY in the most recent quarter as SG&A outgrew gross profit — the first sign that scale leverage is inverting. (3) Tariff cost disclosure is thin and reportedly moved materially higher intra-year ($150m→$185m per secondary sources); **[Corrected 2026-10-06: the 10-Q shows the IEEPA tariffs paid were about $120M gross and are being refunded, so this bear point is weaker than stated; any refund is unrecognized upside]** if true and not fully offset by pricing, FY27 guidance (GM ≈56.5%, down from 56.4% Q1 actual but below FY26's 57.7%) **[Corrected 2026-10-06: the FY27 gross-margin guide was raised to 'slightly better than 56.5%' on 23 Jul 2026, so it is at or above the 56.4% Q1 actual, not below it]** already implicitly assumes structural margin give-back.

## 9. Key risks & kill criteria
1. HOKA quarterly YoY growth falls below 5% for two consecutive quarters (from 7.7% in Q1 FY27) — would confirm structural, not cyclical, deceleration.
2. GAAP operating margin below 15% for two consecutive quarters (currently 15.2% Q1 FY27, down from 17.1%).
3. FY2027 guidance cut at any point (would break the "raised every time" streak that is the main remaining bull pillar).
4. Domestic (US) revenue growth turns negative for two consecutive quarters (was +2.7% to +3.2% in the last two prints — already anemic). **[Corrected 2026-10-06: the last two prints were +0.3% (Q4 FY26) and +3.2% (Q1 FY27); +2.7% was Q3 FY26. The kill threshold of two negative quarters is therefore one soft print away from the Q4 FY26 level]**
5. Evidence that tariff mitigation (pricing) is triggering wholesale sell-through weakness or channel pushback (watch wholesale growth, currently still positive at +2.2–7.1%).
