**WATCH** (reason: a real, accelerating AI-optics business, but at $941.65 the price implies about 31% annual FCF growth for nine years after FY27 versus a base case near 14%). Horizon 12-36 months. Triage said "negative margin needs cleaning up": verified, the -$6.9B GAAP loss is a $7.76B non-cash loss on debt extinguishment, not operating losses; the business itself earned $524.8M GAAP operating income in FY26. ## 2. Business in plain English
Lumentum makes lasers and optical modules that move data inside AI data centres and across telecom networks (components 64.5% and systems 35.5% of Q4 FY26 revenue). Customers are a few large cloud, networking and module buyers. Position: leading laser supplier with a capacity ramp; risk: commoditisation of optics and lumpy demand.

## 3. Why the model likes it, and durability

## 8. Bull and bear
Bull: (1) revenue doubled in a year with gross margin expanding every quarter; (2) NVIDIA's $2.0B preferred investment is a strategic endorsement; (3) CPO laser, OCS and 1.6T modules "are beginning to layer in" (CEO quote in the Q4 FY26 Ex 99.1, accession 0001628280-26-055726). Bear: (1) two customers are 41.6% of revenue and many customers buy without long-term volume commitments per the 10-K; (2) capex doubled to $451M and inventory is up 47%, classic late-ramp signals; (3) FY25 non-GAAP operating margin was only 9.7%, showing how fast the cycle can reverse.

## 9. Key risks and kill criteria
1. Quarterly revenue below the low end of the company's own guidance range in any quarter (Q1 FY27 guide: $1.225 billion to $1.275 billion).
2. Non-GAAP operating margin below 33% for two consecutive quarters (Q4 FY26: 36.6%; Q1 FY27 guide: 39.5% to 40.5%).
3. Either of the two largest end customers (26.6% and 15.0% of FY26 revenue) cutting or pausing orders, evidenced by sequential revenue decline of more than 10% in any quarter.
4. Inventory plus purchase commitments growing more than 2x faster than revenue for two quarters (FY26 inventory $691.6 million, up 47% versus revenue up 83%).
5. Capital expenditure above 20% of revenue for a full fiscal year while free cash flow stays below 15% of revenue (FY26: capex 15.0%, FCF 10.0% of revenue).
