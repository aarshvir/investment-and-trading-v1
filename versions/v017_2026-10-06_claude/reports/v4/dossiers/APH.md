## 1. Verdict
**INCLUDE-SMALL** — 24–36 month horizon. Named reservation: total debt roughly doubled (from $6.89bn to $18.81bn between FY2024 and 2026-06-30) to fund the transformational $10.5bn CommScope (Connectivity & Cable Solutions) acquisition that closed 2026-01-09; the underlying business and execution are excellent (four consecutive quarters beating the high end of its own guidance, book-to-bill >1.2x), and estimated net debt/EBITDA (~1.4–1.5x) is manageable for an investment-grade industrial, but this is real, recently-added leverage and integration risk that argues against a full-weight position until at least two post-close quarters confirm the deal is performing to plan. **[Corrected 2026-10-06: restated to WATCH in Correction section DV04: debt rose 2.7x (not 'roughly doubled'), and the valuation test no longer holds after fixing the reverse-DCF method]**

## 2. Business in plain English
Amphenol designs and makes electronic and fiber-optic connectors, cable assemblies, sensors and interconnect systems used inside almost every category of electronics — data centers/AI servers, telecom/IT networks, automobiles, industrial equipment, defense systems and mobile devices. It sells to a highly diversified base of OEM customers across those end markets and grows both organically and through a long, disciplined track record of bolt-on and, occasionally, large (CommScope) acquisitions, run by a famously decentralized, general-manager-owned operating model.

## 8. Bull case / Bear case
**Bull:** (1) a 30+ year track record of successfully integrating acquisitions at scale, now applied to the largest deal in company history (CommScope), already tracking above initial expectations; (2) record backlog/book-to-bill (1.23–1.24x in the last two quarters) evidences real, broad-based end-market demand, not just M&A math; (3) highly diversified end-market exposure (IT datacom, auto, defense, broadband, industrial) reduces single-customer/single-cycle risk relative to pure AI-networking peers.
**Bear:** (1) total debt nearly tripled in 18 months specifically to fund M&A — if the AI-datacom capex cycle or auto/industrial demand disappoints, deleveraging slows and equity-value creation stalls; (2) a one-time inventory step-up charge already shows up as GAAP earnings noise in Q1'26 — more such charges are likely as CommScope integrates; (3) the growth rate itself is not fully organic, so multiple-expansion re-rating on a "55% growth" headline risks disappointment once M&A-driven growth normalizes.

## 9. Key risks & kill criteria (measurable)
1. Net debt/EBITDA rises above 2.5x in any quarterly report (vs. an estimated ~1.4–1.5x today) without a specific deleveraging plan disclosed.
2. Organic (non-M&A) revenue growth, as separately disclosed by management, falls below 5% for two consecutive quarters.
3. GAAP-to-adjusted EPS gap (acquisition/integration-related costs + amortization of acquired intangibles) exceeds 20% of GAAP EPS for two consecutive quarters, signalling integration is costing more than communicated.
4. Company misses (rather than beats) the high end of its own quarterly revenue guidance for two consecutive quarters (vs. 4-for-4 beats over the last year).
5. A credit-rating downgrade is announced by Moody's or S&P specifically citing the CommScope-related leverage.

## Correction (verification DV04, 2026-10-06)

**Verification DV04 (research only; primary sources: SEC EDGAR).** Revenue, margins, GAAP EPS, every quoted quarterly guidance range, debt ($1,634.3M current + $17,177.0M long-term = $18,811.3M), cash flow and the TTM bridge verify against the releases, 10-Q and XBRL. The mechanical XBRL cross-tie debt flags (6,890 / 18,810 / 1,634.3) are false positives (they were matched to the long-term piece only). Failures:

1. **Unsupported claim (Sec 3 and Sec 5):** "management raised its full-year CommScope contribution estimate mid-year: from $4.1bn sales / $0.15 EPS accretion to $4.6bn sales / $0.30 EPS accretion for FY2026 (Q2'26 8-K)" and "FY2026 guidance explicitly separates out CommScope's now-expected $4.6bn". **Correct:** no such figures appear in the Q2 2026 Ex-99.1, the 8-K (acc 0001104659-26-087904), the Q1 release or the 10-Q (acc 0001104659-26-089194). Amphenol guides one quarter at a time; its only CCS guidance is the Q1 2026 outlook "approximately $900 million in sales and $0.02 in Adjusted Diluted EPS accretion from the CCS business" (not "Andrew/CCS"). The 10-Q (Note 11) reports CommScope net sales of $2,100.9M and net income of $190.2M from the acquisition date through 2026-06-30, which supports "tracking well" but not the stated raise. Treat the $4.6bn/$0.30 as unverified.
2. **Wording error (Sec 1):** debt "roughly doubled" from $6.89bn to $18.81bn: it rose **2.7x** (the Bear section's "nearly tripled" is the accurate one).
3. **Valuation-method error (Sec 7):** the dossier's result reproduces (EV $220.75bn, TTM FCF $4.71bn, WACC 8.5%, terminal growth 3.5% => 13.9%), but the 8.5% rate is not tied to the 10-year Treasury (5.17% on 2026-09-25) plus a stated equity risk premium; levered FCF (after interest) was discounted against enterprise value; and SBC (TTM $157.1M) was not deducted. **Recomputed on an equity basis:** FCFE = $4,713.7M - $157.1M = $4,556.6M against market cap $207.36bn, 3.5% terminal growth: implied 10-year FCF growth is **16.3%** at a cost of equity of 9.5% (5.17% + beta ~0.9 x 5.0% ERP, the lowest defensible), 18.8% at 10.5% (beta ~1.1) and 20.4% at 11.2% (beta ~1.2). The dossier's own base case is 12-14% (bull 18-20%), so **implied_vs_base is "above", not "in line"**. Put differently, if APH grows FCF 13% a year for ten years (the base case), the buyer's IRR at today's price is ~8.3% a year (bear 7%: ~6.6%; bull 19%: ~10.6%), below a cost of equity consistent with a 5.17% risk-free rate. Betas are assumptions, not filing figures.
4. **Verdict:** the dossier says the INCLUDE/INCLUDE-SMALL valuation test is satisfied because implied is in line with base, with only the leverage reservation capping size. After the fix the valuation test fails even at the lowest defensible cost of equity. **Verdict changes from INCLUDE-SMALL to WATCH** (business quality, 4-for-4 guidance beats, book-to-bill 1.23-1.24x and ~1.5x net debt/EBITDA are unchanged and verified). The base scenario return is restated from +11% to ~+8% a year (the 10-year IRR above); bear and bull scenario figures are 3-year price scenarios whose derivation is not given and are left as published.
5. **Minor:** (a) Q1'26 GAAP EPS $0.72 vs adjusted $1.06 is explained by the $0.08 inventory step-up **and** $248.9M pre-tax ($0.16) acquisition-related expenses (Ex-99.1 Notes 1 and 3), so the summary's "due to the step-up" is incomplete; kill criterion 3 (GAAP-adjusted gap >20% for two consecutive quarters) was met in Q1 but not Q2; (b) a two-for-one stock split was approved 2026-08-06 and effected 2026-09-02 (8-Ks acc 0001104659-26-091969 and -105485); per-share figures in this dossier are pre-split as reported (post-split halves them; Q3 dividend $0.25 becomes $0.125); (c) the Sec 6 SBC sentence is garbled: TTM SBC is $157.1M, 0.54% of revenue.
