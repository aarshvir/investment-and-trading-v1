## 2. Business in plain English
DuPont now sells specialty materials in two segments: Healthcare & Water Technologies (Q2'26 sales $856m: water filtration membranes, biopharma and medical protection materials) and Diversified Industrials (Q2'26 sales $963m: Building Technologies such as Tyvek and insulation, plus Industrial Technologies such as aerospace and EV materials). It earns money selling engineered materials that are specified into customer products, with operating EBITDA margins of 30% (Healthcare & Water) and 22% (Diversified). Electronics was spun off as Qnity (Nov 1, 2025) and Aramids was sold to Arclin (Apr 1, 2026). Competitive position is a set of niche leaders (water, protective fabrics) rather than one deep moat; construction end-markets are the weak spot.

## 8. Bull and bear
Bull: (1) organic growth stepped up from -1% to +4% in three quarters with healthcare and aerospace strength; (2) margin expansion at both segments and a de-levered balance sheet with $1.4bn net debt allows continued buybacks; (3) the PFAS share is now partly capped by the MOU structure and Qnity's 44% reimbursement.
Bear: (1) most of the 2026 EPS step-up is financing and tax rebasing, so FY27 growth is closer to 8-10% than 44%; (2) PFAS (NJ $188m liability, NC $455m settlement shared across three parties) and further state suits; (3) construction/EV/Middle East exposure and only a short clean track record as "new DuPont".

## 9. Key risks and kill criteria
1. Organic sales growth below 2% for two consecutive quarters (2Q'26: +4%).
2. FY26 adjusted EPS guidance cut below the $7.17 low end, or FY27 initial guidance implying adjusted EPS growth under 5%.
3. Consolidated net debt (10-Q total debt less cash and cash equivalents) above 2.0x trailing operating EBITDA (now about 0.8x).
4. New PFAS or legacy settlement that adds more than $500m to DuPont's accrued share beyond the $125m (NC) and $188m (NJ) liabilities at June 30, 2026, or Qnity failing to honour its 44% allocation.
5. Transaction-adjusted FCF conversion below 80% for FY26 (1H'26 is about 127% in 2Q).
