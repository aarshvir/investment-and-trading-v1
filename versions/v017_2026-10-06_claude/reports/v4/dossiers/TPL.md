# TPL - Texas Pacific Land Corporation (NYSE: TPL) - wave 8 / F158

**Verdict: WATCH** - an exceptional royalty and water toll-taker, but the 25 Sep 2026 close ($341.07, market cap about $23.5B, 43x trailing EPS) implies about 14.5% a year FCF growth for 10 years against my 9% base. Horizon 12-36 months; would turn INCLUDE-SMALL near $230-250 (about 30x trailing EPS) or if water/data-centre income lifts the base case past the implied rate.

**Thesis in plain English:** TPL owns about 894,000 acres of Permian land and royalty interests, so it is paid when others drill, produce and dispose of water on it, with almost no capex. The business is superb; the price already pays for a decade of high growth.

## 1. Business
Two segments (10-Q, acc. 0001811074-26-000058): Land and Resource Management (oil and gas royalties, easements, land sales) and Water Services and Operations (water sales, produced-water royalties). Q2 2026 revenue $246.1M: royalties $145.6M, water sales $39.7M, produced-water royalties $37.1M, easements $23.7M. Segment operating income Q2: Land $136.3M, Water $55.5M (consolidated $191.8M, 78% margin). Owns about 224,000 net royalty acres.

## 2. Why the model likes it / durability

## 7. Bull / Bear
Bull: (1) Permian volumes: Q2 royalty production 39.7 MBoe/d, DUC+permit inventory 18.4 net wells; (2) water is growing - produced-water royalty volumes 4,872 MBbl/d vs 3,990 H1'25 and desalination plus Chevron "Project Kilby" water rights; (3) zero debt and ~99% FCF conversion fund land and data-centre optionality.
Bear: (1) 43x trailing earnings with a 2.3% FCF yield - a derating to 30x alone costs 30%; (2) price-sensitive: oil $97.55 realised in Q2 vs $70.57 in Q1 and gas near zero; (3) operator-controlled volumes and rising capex and land purchases ($110M) add execution risk.

## 8. Kill criteria / what changes the verdict
1. Move to INCLUDE-SMALL only if the price falls to 30x TTM EPS or below (about $235).
2. Exit/REJECT if quarterly total royalty volumes fall below 3.0 MMBoe (Q2'26: 3.615 MMBoe) for two consecutive quarters.
3. Exit if produced-water royalty volumes fall below 4,000 MBbl/d for two quarters (Q2'26: 4,872).
4. Exit if capex plus land purchases exceed 50% of FCF for four quarters without a disclosed return.
5. Exit if the credit facility is drawn above $250M.
