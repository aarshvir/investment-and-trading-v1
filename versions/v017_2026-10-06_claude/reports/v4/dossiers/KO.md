**Agent:** F125 (wave 8 standard diligence). **Price basis:** 2026-09-25 close $87.81 (d4 snapshot, Yahoo); 4,302.5m shares -> mkt cap ~$377.8bn.

## 1. Verdict
**WATCH** — an exceptional, guidance-raising franchise, but at $87.81 (26.7–26.9x the implied 2026 comparable EPS of $3.27–3.30; ~3.3% FCF yield) the price implies ~6.5% 10-year FCF growth against my base of ~6%. Triage ("quality but wait for a pullback") is confirmed and the FCF-yield flag (1.4%) is explained: FY25 GAAP FCF carried a ~$6.1bn fairlife payment; normalised 2026 FCF guide is $12.4bn. Horizon 12–36 months. Would move to INCLUDE-SMALL near ~$78–80 (implied <= base) or on sustained 2027 EPS growth above 8% currency-neutral.

## 2. Business in plain English
Coca-Cola sells concentrates, syrups and finished beverages (Coca-Cola, Sprite, Fanta, Powerade, smartwater, Costa, fairlife) in 200+ countries, mostly via independent bottlers who buy concentrate; it earns a high margin (Q2'26 GAAP operating margin 34.9%) on brand and distribution, with low capital needs (capex ~$2.2bn). It is shedding capital-heavy bottling (African bottling operations sale to Coca-Cola HBC, held for sale).

## 3. Why the model likes it, and is it durable
Quality and stability factors plus a +36% 12-month run (triage). Durable: unit case volume +5% in Q2'26, price/mix +2%, value share gains. Less durable: currency added ~2–3 points to EPS growth, Q1 had six extra days, and Q2 includes World Cup activation timing.

## 8. Bull / bear
Bull: (1) three straight guidance raises and value-share gains; (2) cost reduction plus marketing investment with comparable margin up 90 bps to 35.6% in Q2; (3) IRS case: the Eighth Circuit's 3M ruling (1 Oct 2025) is, per the company, "highly supportive", with $6.0bn deposit at stake.
Bear: (1) 26.8x earnings and 3.3% FCF yield leave little margin for a consumer slowdown; (2) FX contributes ~3 points of 2026 EPS growth and reverses; (3) fairlife ransomware (8-K 2026-07-16, Item 8.01) suspended US production; the 10-Q (2026-07-29) says a majority of production has resumed and the incident is not "reasonably likely to have a material impact", but fairlife is a key growth asset.

## 9. Kill criteria
1. Organic revenue (non-GAAP) growth below 3% for two consecutive quarters (Q2'26 +6%, Q1'26 +10% incl. six extra days).
2. Global unit case volume growth negative for two consecutive quarters (Q2'26 +5%, Q1'26 +3%).
3. Full-year comparable EPS growth guidance cut below 7% (currently 9% to 10%, 7% to 8% currency neutral ex-M&A).
4. Consolidated net debt (carrying debt less cash, short-term investments and marketable securities) / (TTM operating income + D&A) above 2.5x (now ~1.7x).
5. An unfavourable appellate ruling in the IRS transfer pricing case adding more than $1.0bn of tax and interest beyond the $6.0bn deposit.
