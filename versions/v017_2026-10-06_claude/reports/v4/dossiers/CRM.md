## 2. Business in plain English
Salesforce sells cloud software that companies use to manage sales, customer service, marketing and commerce
("Customer 360"), plus Slack (workplace messaging), Tableau (analytics), MuleSoft (integration), and — since
November 2025 — Informatica (data management/integration), on top of a fast-growing Data Cloud/Agentforce AI
layer. Customers pay recurring subscription fees (over 90% of billings are subscription, invoiced annually in
advance), which funds a high-margin, high-switching-cost business once embedded in a customer's workflows.
Competitive position: category leader in CRM against Microsoft, Oracle, SAP, ServiceNow and HubSpot, now
pivoting the growth narrative to "agentic AI" (Agentforce) monetization.

## 5. Guidance track record (last 3 releases, quoted from 8-K Exhibit 99.1 press releases)
- **4Q FY26 release (2026-02-25), initial FY27 guide:** revenue $45.80–46.20bn (+10–11% Y/Y), GAAP operating
  margin 20.9%, non-GAAP 34.3%, operating cash flow growth ~9–10%, free cash flow growth ~9–10%, capex ~1.5%
  of revenue.
- **1Q FY27 release (2026-05-27):** FY27 revenue guide **raised (narrow)** to $45.9–46.2bn (+11%); GAAP
  operating margin guide **cut** to 20.6% (from 20.9%); OCF/FCF growth guide **cut sharply** to ~4–5% (from
  ~9–10%) — the release states explicitly this reflects "the impact of the $25 billion debt issuance for the
  ASR," i.e. management directly attributed the cash-flow guidance cut to the buyback's financing cost.
- **2Q FY27 release (2026-08-26):** FY27 revenue guide **raised again** to $46.1–46.4bn (+11–12%; includes
  $200M from the pending Contentful/Fin deals, $100M organic, offset by a $100M FX headwind); GAAP operating
  margin guide **cut again** to 20.1% (non-GAAP maintained at 34.3%); OCF/FCF growth guide **maintained** at
  ~4–5%.
- **Net:** a genuine "beat and raise" on the top line, but margin and cash-flow guidance have both moved the
  wrong way twice in a row, and management has been transparent about why (debt service on the buyback,
  M&A integration/amortization). This is a mixed, not a clean, guidance track record.

## 8. Bull case / Bear case
**Bull:** (1) cRPO growth of 14% YoY CC (2Q FY27) is the best forward-demand read in several quarters and
suggests bookings are genuinely reaccelerating, not just revenue recognition catching up; (2) Agentforce/Data
360 AI products are approaching a combined ~$4bn ARR run-rate per management commentary — real, incremental
monetization on top of the core suite, still early; (3) at 14.4x NTM P/E (cheapest in its own trading history
per V1) with high-90s recurring-revenue retention, the valuation has very little growth priced in — even
modest execution should re-rate the multiple. **[Corrected 2026-10-06: the 14.4x multiple uses gain-inflated EPS (clean about 17x)]**
**Bear:** (1) debt went from ~$10bn to ~$39bn in two quarters purely to fund buybacks/M&A — a genuine change
in risk profile for a company that used to run with net cash, and OCF/FCF growth guidance has already been
cut in half (9–10% → 4–5%) to reflect the new interest burden; (2) reported growth is increasingly inorganic
(Informatica ~4pts of 2Q FY27's ~11% growth; two more pending deals, Contentful and Fin, both cash-funded);
organic growth is closer to high-single-digits, not the double-digit headline; (3) GAAP operating margin
guidance has been cut in both revisions this year (20.9% → 20.6% → 20.1%) even as non-GAAP margin guidance
stayed flat — a sign that real integration/restructuring/interest costs are rising faster than the "adjusted"
story admits.

## 9. Key risks & kill criteria (measurable)
1. Reported revenue growth (GAAP, total) falls below 8% YoY for two consecutive quarters.
2. Net debt/TTM operating cash flow rises above 2.5x (from ~1.8x currently) without a disclosed plan to
   delever.
3. The Contentful or Fin acquisition fails to close by 2026-12-31, or closes with a materially adverse
   regulatory condition disclosed in an 8-K.
4. GAAP operating margin guidance is cut a third consecutive time (below the current 20.1% FY27 guide).
5. cRPO growth (constant currency) falls below 8% YoY for two consecutive quarters — the earliest read on a
   genuine organic demand slowdown.

- **M&A pace/integration risk:** three acquisitions in under a year (Regrello, October 2025; Informatica,
  November 2025; Qualified.com, April 2026) plus two pending (Contentful, Fin) — a high rate of integration
  activity for a company simultaneously running a large buyback and citing "reduction of workforce, office
