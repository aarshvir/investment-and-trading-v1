# MTB — M&T Bank Corporation — Fundamental Diligence Dossier

**1. Verdict: INCLUDE.** Thesis horizon 12–36 months. The best risk/reward of the three names reviewed in this batch: expanding NIM, falling deposit costs, improving credit quality (nonaccrual ratio at its lowest since 2007), the only net-*positive* sell-side FY1 estimate-revision trend of the three (11 up / 2 down), fair (not rich) valuation, and disciplined, guided capital return. Real but manageable risks: CRE is growing again with no office-specific disclosure in the materials reviewed, expenses are guided to the high end of the FY2026 range, and management's own FY2026 net-charge-off guidance (~37bps) is above the actual run-rate of the last four quarters (23–31bps), implying they expect some credit normalization.

## 2. Business in plain English
M&T Bank is a ~$210bn-asset super-regional commercial bank headquartered in Buffalo, NY, operating retail and commercial banking across the Northeast/Mid-Atlantic (NY, PA, MD, NJ, CT, VA, DC, WV, DE, MA, ME), plus a sizeable wealth-management and institutional-services business built up via the 2022 People's United acquisition. It earns money primarily from **net interest margin** — the spread between what it earns on commercial & industrial, commercial real estate, residential and consumer loans, and what it pays on deposits and other funding — plus fee income from wealth/trust, mortgage banking and institutional services.

## 5. Guidance track record
Unlike HIG/CINF, **MTB does give quantitative full-year guidance**, refreshed each quarter in investor-conference decks furnished via 8-K. Current (Sept 2026, for FY2026): **NII $7.2–7.35bn (tracking bottom half)**; **NIM "high 3.60s" full-year** (implying mid-3.60s in 2H26 — i.e., guided to *flatten/dip slightly* from the 3.70–3.71% just reported); **fee income $2.8–2.85bn (tracking high end)**; **expenses $5.5–5.6bn (tracking high end)**; **average loans $141–143bn**; **average deposits $165–167bn**; **CET1 10.0–10.5%**; **NCO ~37bps for FY2026**. This session did not retrieve the Q4'25 or Q1'26 vintage of this same guidance table (SEC 503 errors / time budget), so **a specific raised/maintained/cut call versus the prior range cannot be made** — flagged as an open item rather than asserted. What can be said: guided NII is tracking to the **bottom** half of its range while fee income and expenses are tracking to the **top** — a mildly negative-mix signal (revenue softer, costs firmer, than the guided midpoint), and guided NCO (~37bps) sits *above* the 23–31bps actually delivered in the last four quarters, implying management itself expects some credit-cost normalization into year-end.

## 6. Earnings quality & balance sheet
- **Funding tailwind is real and broad-based:** cost of interest-bearing liabilities fell from 2.88% (Q3'24) to 2.36% (Q2'26) while NIM rose — margin expansion is coming from the liability side, not just asset repricing, which tends to be more durable through a rate-cutting cycle.
- **Credit:** nonaccrual loans $1.21bn/0.84% of loans (Q2'26) vs. $1.57bn/1.16% (Q2'25); allowance for loan losses 1.53–1.58% of total loans across the period (broadly stable coverage even as NPAs fell — not a reserve-release-driven improvement). **CRE grew $1.1bn quarter-over-quarter in Q2'26** per the release, and the Sept-2026 deck flags "inflecting CRE balances" as a driver of loan guidance — **but no office-specific exposure, criticized-loan, or CRE sub-segment figure was disclosed in any of the three earnings releases or the investor deck reviewed.** This is a genuine disclosure gap for a regional bank in 2026, not evidence of a problem, and should be chased in the 10-Q before sizing.
- **One-off/methodology items:** **[Corrected 2026-10-06: also note the Q2 2026 ROTCE of 18.57% (vs 14.51% in Q1) includes a higher Bayview (BLG) distribution that management itself calls out in the FY2026 fee guide; Series L preferred ($600m, 6.625%) was issued 16-17 Jul 2026 (8-K acc. 0001193125-26-310413)]** effective Jan 1 2026, MTB elected fair-value accounting for residential-mortgage-servicing-rights, a **$197M after-tax benefit (+8bps CET1)** in Q1'26 — a real but non-recurring accounting-election gain, and a new general-ledger platform changed the taxable-equivalent NII calculation methodology in Q2'26 (flagged by the company itself as affecting comparability).
- **Capital return:** buybacks of $1.08bn (Q2'25) → $1.25bn (Q1'26) → $465M (Q2'26) — a deceleration quarter-to-quarter in Q2'26 consistent with CET1 approaching the low end of the guided band; common dividend raised ~11% in Q3'25.
- **Debt:** Medium-Term Note program (senior + subordinated tranches) refreshed Sept 22, 2026 via shelf registration (RBC Capital Markets lead agent) — a routine capital-markets housekeeping action (program refresh only; no specific issuance amount/rate/maturity disclosed in the 8-K itself), not a distress signal.

## 8. Bull case
1. Four consecutive quarters of NIM expansion driven by falling deposit/funding costs, not just asset yield — a durable mechanism into a rate-cutting cycle.
2. Credit quality is improving in absolute terms (nonaccrual ratio "lowest since 2007"), not just against a weak comp, while the bank keeps loss-absorption capacity roughly flat (allowance ~1.5% of loans) rather than releasing reserves to flatter earnings.
3. The only one of the three names with a net-positive sell-side estimate-revision trend (11 up/2 down) and a fair, not rich, valuation — sentiment and price have not yet run ahead of the fundamentals.

## 9. Bear case
1. Management's own FY2026 guidance implies NIM *compresses* slightly into 2H26 (from 3.70–3.71% now to "mid-3.60s"), NII tracking to the bottom of its range, and expenses tracking to the top — a mildly unfavorable mix even before any credit deterioration.
2. CRE balances are growing again ($1.1bn in one quarter) with **zero office-specific disclosure** in the sources reviewed — precisely the regional-bank risk category the market has been most sensitive to since 2023, and MTB is not giving investors the granularity to check it from the earnings release alone.
3. Guided FY2026 net charge-offs (~37bps) sit meaningfully above the 23–31bps actually delivered over the last four quarters — management's own base case is for credit costs to normalize upward, not stay at current levels.

## 10. Key risks & kill criteria
1. NIM falls below ~3.55% for two consecutive quarters (vs. 3.70% now, and below management's own "mid-3.60s" 2H26 guide).
2. Net charge-offs exceed ~50bps in any quarter (vs. ~37bps guided, ~23–31bps delivered).
3. CET1 falls below the guided 10.0% floor.
4. Once disclosed, CRE-office nonaccrual/criticized balances show material deterioration (currently opaque — treat the opacity itself as a watch item).
5. Efficiency ratio deteriorates persistently above ~60% (expenses already guided to the high end of range).
