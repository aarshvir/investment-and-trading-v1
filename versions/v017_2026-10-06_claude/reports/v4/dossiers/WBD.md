# WBD - Warner Bros. Discovery, Inc. (research only; not personal investment advice)

**1. Verdict: REJECT** (as a portfolio holding). The share price is pinned to a fixed all-cash takeover by Paramount Skydance (PSKY) at $31.00 per share plus a small daily "ticking" amount after 30 Sep 2026; upside is about 0.5% to 1% and the downside if the deal broke is large. Horizon: event-driven, weeks to months, not 12-36 months. The quant model ranked it 396 of 402 and flagged "pending deal"; the primary filings confirm the flag is the whole story.

**2. Business in plain English.** WBD sells HBO Max and discovery+ streaming subscriptions, licenses and produces films and TV (Warner Bros. studios, games), and sells ad-supported cable channels (CNN, TNT, Discovery) to pay-TV distributors. Streaming is growing; linear TV is shrinking (domestic pay-TV subscribers -10% in Q2-26). It carries about $33 billion of gross debt.

**3. Why the model likes it.** b1 factor snapshot: momentum family 0.97 (12-1 month momentum +46%) but quality 0.14 and value 0.12; composite rank 396/402 with `exclude_pending_deal = True`. Momentum is a takeover-bid artefact, not an operating trend; earnings yield is negative (-4.1%) because of a $2.8 billion termination-fee charge.

**8. Bull / bear.** Bull: (1) the deal closes and holders receive $31.00 plus ticking consideration (capped at $0.25 per 90 days); (2) streaming adj. EBITDA +75% in Q2-26 shows the standalone transformation works; (3) a $7.0bn reverse termination fee payable by PSKY deters it walking away. Bear: (1) 12 state attorneys general and the Writers Guild sued in July 2026 to block the merger; (2) if the deal fails WBD is a levered, linear-declining business with $32-33bn of debt; (3) revenue -11% and studios EBITDA -89% in Q2-26 show earnings volatility.

**9. Key risks and kill criteria (research triggers, not trade instructions).**
- Court enjoins the merger, or WBD/PSKY announce termination, before the 4 Mar 2027 outside date (extendable to 4 Jun 2027).
- WBD share price below $29.50 (about 5% under the cash price) for 5 consecutive sessions, signalling the market doubts closing.
- Net leverage above 3.8x (company definition, consolidated) in any quarterly release.
- Consolidated adj. EBITDA below $1.7bn in any quarter.
- Any 8-K reporting failure of the Ellison guarantee ($45.72bn of consideration) or a reduction of the $31.00 price.
