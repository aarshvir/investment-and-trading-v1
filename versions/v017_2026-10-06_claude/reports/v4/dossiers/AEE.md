## 2. Business in plain English
Ameren is a Missouri-headquartered regulated electric and gas utility holding company: Ameren Missouri (vertically
integrated electric + gas, ~65% of segment operating income) and Ameren Illinois (electric & gas transmission/
distribution, rate-regulated with performance-based formula rates). It earns a regulator-approved return on the rate
base it invests in poles, wires, generation and grid modernization; growth comes from capex (rate base) and constructive
regulatory outcomes, not from selling more electrons.

## 7. Valuation snapshot & reverse DCF
d4 snapshot: NTM P/E 17.38x, FY0 P/E 18.41x, dividend yield 2.94%, FCF yield –6.5% (not usable for a Gordon-model
reverse DCF given structurally negative FCF — see below for the utility-appropriate method). **V1: no row exists for
AEE** — v1_verdict = null.

Reverse DCF (dividend-discount model — the standard, business-model-appropriate method for a regulated utility,
since FCF is capex-suppressed by design): r = D/P + g ⇒ implied perpetual EPS/dividend growth g = r − D/P. Using a
required return (CoE) of ~7.7% (typical for a BBB+/A-rated regulated utility, low beta) and D/P = 2.94%:
**g_implied ≈ 4.8%/yr**. That sits *below* the company's guided 6–8% long-term algorithm — the stock is priced for
somewhat less growth than management guides, i.e., undemanding if the algorithm is delivered, but not a bargain if
Missouri/Illinois regulatory outcomes disappoint.
- **Bear (EPS growth ≈2%/yr):** an adverse Missouri or Illinois rate order (lower allowed ROE, disallowed capex)
  compresses the growth algorithm; multiple compresses to ~15x.
- **Base (EPS growth ≈5%/yr):** algorithm delivers toward the low-to-mid end of 6–8% after financing dilution from
  continuous equity issuance; multiple holds ~17x.
- **Bull (EPS growth ≈7–8%/yr):** full guided algorithm delivered, data-center load growth (2.2 GW of signed
  hyperscaler agreements per Q1'26 slides) adds upside without proportionate equity dilution; multiple re-rates to 19x+.

**Implied vs base:** implied growth (~4.8%) is **below** our base case (~5%, effectively in line) → we call this
"in_line" rather than "below" given the closeness and the estimation uncertainty in both inputs. **[Corrected 2026-10-06: method error (perpetuity growth compared with a ten-year base rate); on the programme's ten-year-then-3% dividend model the price implies about 7.9%/yr against a base of 5-5.75%, so implied vs base is ABOVE and the verdict becomes WATCH, see Correction section]**

## 8. Bull case / Bear case
Bull: (1) $33.1bn 2026–2030 capital plan with 10.6% rate-base CAGR is regulator-pre-approved-adjacent (multi-year
plans in both states) — unusually high growth visibility for a utility. (2) Data-center/hyperscaler load growth
(2.2 GW signed per Q1'26) is incremental, high-margin demand on top of the base capex plan. (3) H1'26 EPS growth
(+19.6%, +11.9%) shows the algorithm can run hot, not just meet plan.
Bear: (1) The FY2026 guided range ($5.25–5.45) brackets *below* the already-delivered FY2025 actual ($5.35) at the
low end — if H1 strength was weather/timing, H2'26 could show a deceleration that surprises the market. (2) Missouri
filed a **$343mm** rate increase request in 2026 (per Q2'26 call commentary) that is not yet decided — regulatory lag
and outcome risk are real, unhedged risks for a company this capex-heavy. (3) Continuous negative FCF means Ameren is
a perpetual capital-markets issuer (debt + equity); a spike in rates or an equity-market air-pocket raises the cost of
growth capital directly into the earnings algorithm.

## 9. Key risks & kill criteria (measurable)
1. Full-year 2026 diluted EPS comes in **below $5.25** (the low end of guidance) at the Q4'26 report.
2. The pending Missouri rate case (docket referenced in Q2'26 commentary; $343mm requested) is settled/ordered at
   **less than 60% of the requested revenue increase** — a specific, checkable threshold once the order is issued.
3. Consolidated debt/(debt+equity) rises **above 65%** (vs ≈60% now) without a matching equity raise already announced.
4. Rate-base CAGR guidance (currently 10.6%, 2025–2030) is **cut** at any quarterly update.
5. Two consecutive quarters of **EPS growth below 3%** YoY (would break the "hot algorithm" bull case and test whether
   H1'26 strength was one-off).
