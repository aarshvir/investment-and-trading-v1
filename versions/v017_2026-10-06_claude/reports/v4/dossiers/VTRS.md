# VTRS - Viatris Inc. (Nasdaq: VTRS) - standard-depth diligence (agent F145)

**1. Verdict: WATCH** (thesis horizon 12-36 months). A genuinely cheap, cash-generative generics/brands company whose 2026 guide was just raised ("Viatris is raising the midpoints of its 2026 financial guidance ranges", 6 Aug 2026 release), but the base-case 3-year return (about 7% a year) does not pay enough for a business with a live FDA site-compliance problem, an interim CFO, a securities class action and a structurally eroding generics book. Would move to INCLUDE-SMALL on a clean Nashik/Indore FDA outcome plus a permanent CFO, or at a price near or below $15 (base 3-year return above about 12%). Research only; not personal advice.

**Triage reconciliation.** The quick triage (Q-batch, VTRS row: "Generics, structural decline", "Negative margin, high leverage") was half right. The negative-margin point is a GAAP artefact (amortisation, a $2.9bn 2025 goodwill impairment, a $177.8m Tyrvaya write-down); cash flow is positive. Leverage is 2.9x gross (company definition), inside its own 2.8-3.2x target, not "high" for the sector. The "structural decline" point is still valid for generics (global Generics net sales -8% in FY2025). Triage overturned on leverage and cash, upheld on quality.

**2. Business in plain English.** Viatris sells established medicines worldwide: branded standbys (Lipitor, Norvasc, Viagra, Lyrica, EpiPen, Creon), generics, and a pipeline of new products and biosimilar/complex generics. About 59% of revenue is Developed Markets, with Greater China now about 19% and growing 16-22%. It competes on scale, breadth and manufacturing footprint, not on patents, so it is exposed to price erosion, supply failures and FDA inspections.
