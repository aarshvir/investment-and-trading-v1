## 2. Business in plain English
ResMed sells CPAP/APAP machines and masks for obstructive sleep apnea, plus ventilators and a growing
software-as-a-medical-device business (care-management platforms for home medical equipment providers). Revenue
comes overwhelmingly from a large, recurring installed base (masks/supplies replaced regularly) sold to patients
via durable-medical-equipment providers and, internationally, direct to clinics/hospitals. Its moat is the
device+consumables ecosystem, clinical/regulatory approvals, and payor relationships built over 30+ years.

## 8. Bull case / bear case
**Bull (3):**
1. GLP-1 fear continues not to show up in CPAP-start or resupply volumes (per company's 2.5M-patient analysis,
   GLP-1 + PAP patients are 11% *more* likely to start PAP than PAP-only patients) — multiple re-rates toward
   historical premium (>20x).
2. Software/SaaS (care-management) and international (masks, direct-to-clinic) growth continue to outgrow the
   core US CPAP replacement cycle, lifting the blended growth rate above the ~9-10% run-rate.
3. Net-cash balance sheet funds continued buybacks (>$0.9B/yr pace) at a reasonable multiple, providing an EPS
   growth kicker beyond organic operating growth.

**Bear (3):**
1. Astral ventilator Class I recall (supercapacitor leak, June 2026, ~168,069 devices globally, FDA record
   Z-2735-2026, https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/cdrh/cfdocs/cfres/res.cfm?id=221232) escalates beyond the
   guided $75M/$0.15 EPS FY2027 impact if replacement-part timelines slip or litigation/liability follows.
2. GLP-1 uptake eventually does dent CPAP compliance/therapy starts with a longer lag than the two years of data
   so far suggest — a slow-moving risk that would not show up until it is already embedded in the base.
3. Reimbursement or competitive pricing pressure in US DME channel compresses the historically stable ~33-35%
   operating margin.

## 9. Key risks & kill criteria (measurable)
1. Quarterly organic revenue growth < 5% YoY for two consecutive quarters (vs the ~9-11% run-rate of the last
   five quarters).
2. Any second FDA recall/correction notice on a ResMed device within 12 months of the Astral action (June 2026).
3. GAAP or non-GAAP gross margin falls below 56% for two consecutive quarters (last reported gross margin,
   FY2026 10-K: 3,451.7/5,653.4 = 61.1%; a 500bp compression sustained for two quarters would be the trigger).
4. Consolidated net debt/EBITDA turns positive and exceeds 1.0x (currently net cash, consolidated basis) — would
   signal a large, debt-funded acquisition or buyback that changes the risk profile.
5. FY2027 guidance ($5.75–5.85B revenue / $12.00–12.25 non-GAAP EPS, issued 6 Aug 2026) is cut at any subsequent
   quarterly update.
