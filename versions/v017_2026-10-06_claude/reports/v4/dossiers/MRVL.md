# MRVL — Marvell Technology, Inc.

## 1. Verdict
**WATCH** — real, durable AI-networking/custom-silicon franchise, but at 44.7x NTM P/E the stock already prices in
several more years of 30%+ growth without margin of safety; no evidence of a broken thesis, but no discount either.
Horizon if it re-enters: 12–36 months, contingent on a valuation reset or a confirmed multi-year Custom-silicon ramp.

## 2. Business in plain English
Marvell designs semiconductors that move and process data inside AI data centers and telecom networks: custom AI
accelerator chips ("XPUs") built for hyperscalers (e.g., an AWS-style custom-silicon program), high-speed networking
and optical/electrical interconnect chips, and storage controllers. It sells to a small number of hyperscale cloud
and networking-equipment customers, earning through per-chip royalties/sales on multi-year design-in programs that
are expensive for customers to switch away from once qualified into a system.

## 3. Why the model likes it — durable or artefact?
b1 factor scores: composite percentile 0.09 (bottom decile; **live_rank 450 of ~1,900, quintile 5** — the quant model
actually ranks Marvell poorly, mainly because Value (pct_ep 0.14, pct_fcfp 0.15, pct_bp 0.06) and Quality-leverage
components are weak). It cleared triage on qualitative growth/price-vs-growth judgment (quality 4, growth 5,
price_vs_growth 3 — "fairly priced," not cheap), not on the systematic score. **Data conflict:** the quant model
(bottom quintile) and the triage narrative (advance, high growth) disagree; the disagreement is real and not an
artefact — Marvell is expensive on every value factor (earnings yield, FCF yield, book/price) precisely because the
market is paying up for AI-cycle growth, which the sector-neutral value factors penalize by construction. Momentum
(pct_mom_12_1 0.86) and Earnings-momentum/SUE (pct_sue 0.20 — weak) are mixed. The growth is durable in kind (design
wins, multi-year programs) but its *magnitude* (mid-40s%–high-40s% revenue growth) is not guaranteed to persist.

## 6. Earnings quality & balance sheet (Marvell Technology, Inc. — single consolidated reporting entity; no
segment/subsidiary capital structure to disentangle)
- **GAAP vs non-GAAP gap is large and structural**, driven by acquisition-intangible amortization, SBC and deal
  costs: Q2 FY27 GAAP EPS $0.33 vs non-GAAP $0.94 (non-GAAP excludes SBC, intangible amortization, deal costs, and a
  contingent-consideration/forward-purchase-contract fair-value swing). SBC was $533.8M for the six months ended
  2026-08-01 (10-Q XBRL), ~10% of the six-month $5,157.1M revenue — high but typical for a fabless AI-semi grower.
- **FCF conversion:** OCF $1,244.3M for six months ended 2026-08-01 vs GAAP net income $342.5M for the same period —
  FCF/NI is not meaningful on GAAP net income because of the large non-cash intangible-amortization drag; against
  non-GAAP net income the ratio is closer to 1x, typical for the sector.
- **Balance sheet (consolidated, per 10-Q balance sheet, 2026-08-01):** cash $3,932.8M; total long-term debt (current
  + non-current) $4,962.9M; net debt ≈ **$1,030M**, low relative to run-rate earnings. **Capital-structure event:**
  on 2026-03-31 Marvell issued $2.0 billion of Series A Convertible Preferred Stock to **NVIDIA Corporation**
  (2,000,000 shares, $1,000 stated value, initial conversion price ≈$91.8355/share, ≈21.8 million common shares if
  converted) alongside a strategic partnership to connect Marvell's custom XPUs and scale-up networking to NVIDIA's
  AI ecosystem (10-Q, "Note 10 – Stockholders' Equity" and Item 2 MD&A). This is a strategic-investment/customer
  signal, not debt, but it does dilute common equity if converted and gives NVIDIA preferential dividend/consent
  rights disclosed as a risk factor. Stockholders' equity jumped from $14.31B (2026-01-31) to $18.53B (2026-08-01),
  largely reflecting this $2.0B raise plus $2.098B of stock issued for the Celestial AI and XConn acquisitions
  (completed 2026-02-02 and 2026-02-10 respectively; goodwill rose from $11.06B to $13.87B over the same period).
  Share count is rising via M&A stock issuance even as buybacks continue ($400M repurchased in the six months ended
  2026-08-01, alongside $107.7M of dividends).
- **Litigation/contingencies:** the 10-Q's Contingencies and Legal Proceedings note (2026-08-01) contains only
  boilerplate ordinary-course language; no named material litigation, restatement, or going-concern flag was found.

## 7. Valuation snapshot
NTM P/E **44.7x** (d4, 2026-09-25 close $261.94); FCF yield **1.0%**; EV/Sales (NTM) **14.2x**; no V1 systematic
valuation row exists for MRVL (not yet run) → **v1_verdict = null**. Consensus: 43 analysts, mean target $289.11
(+10.4% upside), rating "strong_buy," but Street upside has compressed sharply after a ~152% 12-month run (per
triage). My own reverse-DCF read: starting from NTM consensus EPS ≈$5.86, a **base case** of ~15%/yr EPS growth for
3 years with the multiple normalizing from 44.7x toward a still-generous 25x by year 3 implies a price near $417 —
only ~1.5%/yr price appreciation (plus a token dividend), i.e. the current price already assumes results close to
or better than this base case. This is a genuinely strong business priced for continued strong execution, not for a
margin of safety.

## 8. Bull case / Bear case
**Bull:** (1) AI data-center bookings are "exceptionally robust" and management is guiding accelerating growth each
quarter through FY27 (quotes above); (2) the NVIDIA strategic-investment/partnership both funds and validates the
custom-XPU/scale-up-networking roadmap; (3) Custom-silicon revenue is explicitly flagged to "significantly
accelerate" in 2H FY27, a second growth leg beyond networking.
**Bear:** (1) revenue is concentrated in a handful of hyperscaler custom-silicon design wins that can be re-bid or
in-sourced (flagged by triage; disclosed as a general customer-concentration risk in filings); (2) non-GAAP gross
margin guide for Q3 (57.5–58.5%) is sequentially flat-to-down versus Q2's 58.9% actual, an early sign that mix or
pricing pressure could cap further margin upside even as revenue grows; (3) at 44.7x NTM P/E and 1% FCF yield, any
deceleration below the ~30%+ pace the market is implicitly pricing would likely trigger multiple compression on top
of slower earnings growth — a double hit, as modelled in the bear scenario below.

## 9. Key risks & kill criteria (measurable)
1. Data Center segment revenue growth below 20% YoY for two consecutive quarters (vs. +46% YoY in Q2 FY27).
2. Public disclosure that a top-3 hyperscaler custom-silicon program (>10% of revenue) has been re-bid, cancelled, or
   is being in-sourced by the customer.
3. Non-GAAP gross margin below 57% (below the low end of the current 57.5–58.5% Q3 guide) for two consecutive
   quarters.
4. Consolidated net debt/EBITDA (per the 10-Q balance sheet, run-rate annualized EBITDA) rising above 2.0x (currently
   roughly 0.3–0.5x).
5. NTM P/E above 55x with no corresponding upward revision to FY28 revenue guidance (a pure re-rating with no new
   fundamental support).
