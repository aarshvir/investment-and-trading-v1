# COP - ConocoPhillips (NYSE) - diligence dossier F118 (STANDARD depth)

**1. Verdict: WATCH** (horizon 24-36 months). Excellent, low-cost, cash-generative producer, but at $127.30 (25 Sep 2026 close) the price already pays for my base case; base 3-year return ~5.6%/yr is below an ~8.5% hurdle. Would upgrade to INCLUDE-SMALL at about $115 or below, or if the 2029 free-cash-flow inflection is visibly on track with the Brent strip at or above the current ~$75-80.

**2. Business.** Largest independent oil and gas producer: Lower 48 shale (Permian, Eagle Ford, Bakken; 1,479 MBOED of 2,248 MBOED in Q2'26), plus Alaska/Willow, Canada oil sands, Norway, Libya, Qatar/Australia LNG. It sells crude, gas, NGLs and LNG at market prices, so earnings follow oil and gas prices; the edge is low cost of supply, deep inventory and a fixed pay-out rule (return about 45% of operating cash flow).

**3. Why the model likes it (b1, decile 10, live rank 30).** Quality 0.89 and the S family (sentiment/surprise) 0.87 lead, value 0.59, momentum 0.58; standardised earnings surprise 5.3 is an artefact of the oil spike (Q2'26 adjusted EPS $3.24 vs $1.42). Quality is durable; the earnings surprise and TTM margin are cyclical, not structural.
