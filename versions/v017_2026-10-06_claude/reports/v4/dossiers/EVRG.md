# EVRG - Evergy, Inc. (NASDAQ: EVRG) - Standard-depth diligence (agent F122)

**1. Verdict: WATCH** - A sound Kansas/Missouri regulated utility with real data-centre growth (about 2,600 MW of signed large-load contracts) and a long-term adjusted EPS target of "6% to 8%+" (acc. 0001193125-26-336661), but at $78.04 the price already capitalises roughly the top of that target while cash flow is deeply negative and 2025 guidance was missed. Horizon 24-36 months. Re-rate condition: a price near or below ~$70 (implied growth at or under ~6%), or a clean delivery of the 2026 guide plus a constructive Missouri rate order.

**Triage check (Q14: advance=false, "fair value for fair growth").** Confirmed, with one sharpening: the filings show the company missed its own narrowed 2025 guidance (adjusted EPS $3.83 vs narrowed range $3.92-$4.02), which the triage did not mention. Nothing disqualifying; it is a price, not a quality, problem.

**2. Business.** Evergy is a vertically integrated electric utility (Evergy Kansas Central, Evergy Metro, Evergy Missouri West) serving ~1.7 million customers. Earnings are regulated returns on a rate base that grows with capital spending ($21.6bn planned for 2026E-2030E per the Feb 2026 release), so EPS growth comes from investment recovery in rate cases plus load growth, with weather as the swing factor. Large data-centre and industrial customers now pay under new Large Load Power Service (LLPS) tariffs approved in Kansas and Missouri in Q4 2025.

Why the model likes it.** b1 composite 0.81 (decile 9, live rank 96 of 503) from balanced family scores (quality 0.60, value 0.44, momentum 0.79, surprise 0.66); FCF yield is poor (percentile 0.36) because capex exceeds operating cash flow. Durable in the sense that it is a regulated, low-beta (0.51, Yahoo) earner; it is a moderate rank, not an outlier. The b1 decile-level drawdown base rate (~50% chance of >20% drawdown in 12 months, cal_dec_p_maxdd_worse_20) is generic and likely overstates risk for a 0.5-beta utility.

**8. Bull / bear.** Bull: (1) 2,600 MW of large-load ESAs with minimum-demand contracts ("contractual minimum amounts typically equal 80% of customers' expected annual capacity demand", 10-Q) and LLPS protections, with more ESAs expected; (2) 6-8%+ growth target rising above 8% from 2028 on a $21.6bn plan; (3) Kansas Central's 2025 rate case settled and the KCC accepted its 2025 earnings review with no refund (July 2026). Bear: (1) 2025 guide missed and H2 weather exposure again; (2) funding: FCF about -$1.4bn a year, rising coupons (5.3-6.4% on new issues), equity dilution via ATM forwards, debt/capital ~62%; (3) Missouri regulation: Evergy Metro's 2026 rate case asks for ~$140m at a 10.5% ROE with hearings in Oct 2026; a lower ROE or smaller award would trim earnings growth, and affordability politics are a risk for a region raising rates while adding load.
