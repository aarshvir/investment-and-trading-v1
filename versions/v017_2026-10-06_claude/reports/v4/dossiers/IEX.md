## 1. Verdict
**WATCH** (horizon 12-36 months). The triage's "price ahead of growth" call needs one correction: the business is genuinely inflecting (Q2 2026 organic orders +28%, guidance raised twice this year) and the balance sheet is clean, but at 24.4x NTM and about 26x the 2026 adjusted-EPS midpoint the price implies about 9.3% a year of owner-FCF growth for ten years versus my base of about 8%. ## 2. Business in plain English
IDEX makes engineered pumps, flow meters, valves, fluidics and fire/rescue tools in three segments: Health & Science Technologies (HST, 45% of Q2 2026 sales; data-center power, semiconductor, life-science and space/defense components), Fluid & Metering Technologies (FMT, 34%; pumps and metering for water, chemical, energy, agriculture) and Fire & Safety/Diversified Products (FSDP, 21%; rescue tools, dispensing, BAND-IT). It sells specified-in, mission-critical components to OEMs and utilities, so prices are sticky and margins high (GAAP gross margin 46.3% in Q2 2026). The edge is niche leadership across more than 50 small businesses run with the "80/20" playbook, not scale.
