# LYB - LyondellBasell Industries (F141, STANDARD depth)

**1. VERDICT: WATCH** - a cyclical petrochemical producer whose Q2-26 profit spike came from a Middle East supply disruption, not from structural improvement; dividend already halved, free cash flow negative in H1-26, and the price ($58.14, 25 Sep 2026 close) roughly matches a normalised-cash-flow view, so there is no margin of safety. Horizon 12-24 months. Triage (Q10/Q13: rejected as "mid-cycle-trough... flagged as watch items") is partly overturned: REJECT overstates it, but it is not investable at full or half weight. Condition to upgrade: price below ~$48 (implied FCF growth under 3% on normalised cash) or two quarters of positive FCF after the Middle East premium fades.

**2. Business in plain English.** LYB turns natural-gas liquids and naphtha into polyethylene and polypropylene (plastics), propylene oxide and derivatives, fuel additives (oxyfuels), methanol and acetyls. Customers are packaging, auto and construction makers. Its edge is low-cost US Gulf Coast feedstock; its weakness is that products are commodities, so profit swings with industry spreads. It has just sold four European assets, is exiting its refinery, and is cutting costs (Cash Improvement Plan).

The value reading is an artefact: TTM operating cash flow was boosted by a working-capital release in late 2025, and the earnings estimate (FY26 consensus $9.04 vs FY27 $6.31, d4) peaks on a temporary shock.
