# Church & Dwight Co., Inc. (CHD) - Fundamental Diligence Dossier (F132, standard depth)

**Verdict: WATCH**, 12-36 month horizon. Reason: a high-quality, volume-led consumer compounder whose organic growth has genuinely re-accelerated (5.0% in Q1 2026, 5.8% in Q2 2026), but at 25.5x the 2026 adjusted EPS guide midpoint the price already discounts roughly the growth management is guiding to, and the guide needs a steep back-half EPS ramp (Q4 adjusted EPS of about $1.01-1.08 versus $0.86 a year ago). Condition that would change it: a pullback toward about $85 (about 22x guided EPS, implied FCF growth near 3-4%), or delivery of the Q3/Q4 ramp. The triage (Q03, advance=false) reached the same conclusion, but one of its stated reasons ("1.6% revenue growth") was wrong: reported sales growth is depressed by 2025 brand exits; organic growth is 5.8%.

## 1. Business in plain English
CHD sells everyday consumer brands: ARM & HAMMER (baking soda, cat litter, laundry), OXICLEAN, TROJAN, BATISTE, WATERPIK, and fast-growing acquired brands THERABREATH, HERO, TOUCHLAND and (from 28 May 2026) MISS MOUTH'S. About 75% of sales are Consumer Domestic, about 19% Consumer International and about 5% a Specialty Products (baking-soda-based chemicals and animal nutrition) division. Sold mainly through retail and e-commerce (global online sales were 25.5% of consumer sales in Q2 2026); moat is brand equity, number-one or -two category positions and a productivity programme that funds marketing. It is a low-beta (Yahoo beta 0.47) cash-generative staple, but it competes with much larger P&G, Colgate and Unilever.

## 2. Why the model likes it / durability
Quant context (d4 snapshot, 25 Sep 2026 close, price $96.38): ROE 17%, low volatility, FCF yield about 4.7%, forward P/E 23.9x on Yahoo forward EPS $4.04 (a next-year figure; the company's 2026 adjusted EPS guide is $3.74-3.81). The appeal is stability and quality, not cheapness. Durable: yes, but Yahoo trailing FCF ($939M) differs from the filings (see section 6), and headline revenue growth of 1.6% understates the underlying trend because 2025 exits (VMS, Flawless, Spinbrush, Waterpik showerheads) removed about $400M of revenue.

## 7. Bull and bear
Bull: (1) Volume-led organic growth of 5.0% and 5.8% with share gains and 22.7% e-commerce growth in Q2; (2) gross margin expansion of 100-120 bps guided for 2026 on productivity and mix; (3) disciplined M&A record (THERABREATH, HERO) that keeps compounding growth brands.
Bear: (1) H1 adjusted EPS flat and Q4 needs +17-26%; (2) Touchland amortization, marketing at 11-12% of sales and SG&A above 2025 levels cap margins; (3) FCF guide down about 4% and a 25x multiple with retailer, tariff (IEEPA refunds are one-off, about $15M expected) and Middle East cost exposure.

## 8. Kill criteria (measurable)
1. Organic sales growth below 2% for two consecutive quarters (5.8% in Q2 2026).
2. Full-year 2026 adjusted EPS guide cut below $3.74, or Q3 2026 adjusted EPS below $0.85 (guide about $0.89).
3. Consolidated net debt/EBITDA above 3.0x after any acquisition (about 1.5x now).
4. Adjusted gross margin year-on-year expansion below +50 bps for two quarters (+40 bps in Q2 2026; guide +100-120 bps for the year).
5. 2026 cash from operations below $1.05B (guide about $1.175B).
