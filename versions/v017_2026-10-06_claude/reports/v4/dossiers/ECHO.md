# ECHO - EchoStar Corporation (F143, wave 8 standard-depth diligence; filing ticker SATS)

**1. Verdict: WATCH (event-driven special situation, not a fundamental compounder).** The equity is now mainly $14B of post-AT&T cash plus a fixed-price claim on about 262M SpaceX shares due around 30 Nov 2027, inside a group with two subsidiary bankruptcies and unquantified cash taxes. Market price implies a SpaceX value of about $87 a share against my base of $100 (hence "below"), but that base rests on SpaceX marks that are not in any filing, so I will not size it as a full position. Horizon 12-36 months (closing date of the SpaceX leg is the key event). Confirms the quick triage's "event-driven" framing and overturns its "legacy net debt/EBITDA 8.6x" red flag: that data card pre-dated the 28 Jul 2026 closing and the 30 Jun 2026 deconsolidation.

**Condition that would move it to INCLUDE-SMALL:** (a) an audited cash-tax figure for the AT&T and SpaceX gains below $4B in total, (b) bankruptcy court confirmation of the DISH DBS plan and the Hughes (HSSC) plan with no parent claims above $1B, and (c) a public SpaceX price that, after any lock-up, supports at least $100 a share.

## 2. Business in plain English
EchoStar owns Boost Mobile (a hybrid MNO/MVNO on AT&T's network, post-sale), Hughes (satellite broadband and enterprise/government networks; HughesNet), and, until 30 Jun 2026, DISH pay-TV and Sling (DISH DBS) and the legacy 5G network. It sold spectrum: 3.45 GHz and 600 MHz to AT&T (closed 28 Jul 2026) and AWS-4/H-block/AWS-3 to SpaceX (transferred to a trust on 22 May 2026; final acquisition closing "targeted for November 30, 2027"). Control sits with the Ergen family through Class B (131.3M Class B vs 159.1M Class A shares at 21 Jul 2026, 10-Q cover); I did not verify the voting ratio.

**Consolidated debt table (Note 10, carrying amounts):** 10 3/4% Secured 2029 $5,506M; 6 3/4% Secured 2030 $2,373M; 3 7/8% Convertible Secured 2030 $1,943M (fair value $6,045M; about 58M shares); 11 3/4% Secured 2027 $3,500M (redeemed 28 Jul 2026); DISH 2021 Intercompany Loan 2028 tranche $2,844M (repaid 28 Jul 2026); **Hughes Satellite Systems (HSSC, subsidiary):** 5 1/4% Secured 2026 $627M and 6 5/8% 2026 $750M, both due 1 Aug 2026 and unpaid. The "Seller Notes" (10 3/4%, 6 3/4% and the convertible) total $9.821B and are to be paid off from the SpaceX consideration.
**AT&T closing (8-K 0001415404-26-000035, 28 Jul 2026):** "Seller received proceeds of $20,250,000,000 and an additional $2,400,000,000 was deposited by the Buyer into the mandated FCC Trust". Uses: "approximately $3.686 billion outstanding (inclusive of early redemption premium and accrued but unpaid interest) of the 11 3/4% Senior Secured Notes ... was redeemed in full"; the $2.844B intercompany loan was settled to DBS, which repaid its $2.0B 7.75% notes (8-K Item 1.02).
**SpaceX leg (10-Q Note 2):** total consideration about $20B, "up to $11 billion to be paid in SpaceX's Class A common stock", "approximately 261.8 million shares ... at a fixed value of $42.40 per share"; interim debt service of about $2B is advanced by SpaceX and cancels at closing; $828M of it already recorded as a long-term liability to be recognised as a gain on closing.
**Bankruptcies:** DISH DBS and DISH Wireless filed prepackaged Chapter 11 on 30 Jun 2026 (RSA supported by holders of "more than 88%" of DBS notes). Hughes (HSSC) and 11 subsidiaries filed Chapter 11 on 2 Aug 2026 (8-K 0001415404-26-000038, filed 3 Aug): "constitutes an event of default under the applicable indentures ... which results in automatic acceleration". Retained equity in the deconsolidated subsidiaries was measured at a fair value of "$0" (10-Q Note 3; net liabilities $11.4B). ECHO is bidding for the DISH Wireless assets as stalking horse (Section 363) with up to $85M of DIP claims to credit bid.
**Taxes:** the 10-Q says the deconsolidation gain "is not currently taxable"; it does not quantify cash taxes on the $22.65B AT&T proceeds or the $20B SpaceX consideration. This is the largest unverified number in the valuation.
**Share count:** 290.5M basic (159.1M A + 131.3M B) plus about 58M convertible shares = about 348.6M diluted; the 10-Q reports 351.6M diluted weighted shares for Q2 26. Repurchase authorization extended on 30 Jul 2026 (10-Q item 2). Stock comp is small ($10M in Q1 26).

## 7. Valuation (no V1 row, so v1_verdict = null)
Price $90.11 (b1, 25 Sep 2026); fully diluted market cap about $31.4B (348.6M x $90.11); b1 shows $31.7B. A DCF of the operations is not meaningful (Boost loses money; Hughes is in Chapter 11; DBS equity carried at $0). **Reverse sum-of-the-parts on the equity** (my estimates, $B):
- Cash at parent/consolidated after 28 Jul: 0.5 (30 Jun free cash) + 20.25 (AT&T) - 3.69 (11 3/4% notes) - 2.84 (intercompany loan to DBS) = **14.2**.
- Less estimated cash taxes on AT&T gain (assumption $2.7B; not disclosed), ops burn (assumption $1.0B over 3 years): net about **10.5**.
- SpaceX leg: surplus cash after paying the non-convert Seller Notes about +1.1, less assumed tax on the SpaceX gain about -3.0, giving about -1.9; total fixed adjustments **8.6**.
- Equity value = 8.6 + 0.2618 x (SpaceX price per share). At the contractual $42.40 that is $19.7B ($56.5 a ECHO share); at $100, $34.8B ($99.8); at $149, $47.6B ($136.6).
- **Reverse DCF equivalent:** the market cap of $31.4B implies a SpaceX price of about **$87 a share** (range $79-$95 if taxes and burn are off by +/- $2B). SpaceX is private; the web sources I searched (analyst commentary, unverified) cite about $149-$201, which would be 70-130% above the price implied here. Those figures are not in any filing and are not used as primary evidence.
**Scenarios (3-year annualised total return, no dividends):** bear **-14%** (SpaceX valued at the contract $42.40, taxes as assumed), base **+3.5%** (SpaceX $100, about two-thirds of the highest private marks), bull **+15%** (SpaceX $149). Basis: equity = $8.6B + 261.8M x P over 348.6M diluted shares; start $90.11; no dividend. Implied ($87) is below base ($100) by 13%, within the error of the tax and burn assumptions; I therefore label it "below" but WATCH.

## 8. Bull and bear
Bull: (1) $14B of cash already in hand with all secured holding-company debt above it retired; (2) 261.8M SpaceX shares at a contractual $42.40 that secondary-market commentary values several times higher; (3) Hughes and DBS restructurings remove the largest liabilities while DBS pay-TV cash flows may return on reconsolidation.
Bear: (1) two subsidiary bankruptcies, with a Special Committee (8-K 3 Aug 2026) reviewing "related party transactions between HSSC ... and EchoStar", so claims against the parent are possible; (2) unquantified cash taxes (DTL $3.4B already on the balance sheet); (3) the SpaceX closing is 14 months away, depends on the Seller Notes make-whole period, and SpaceX is not a public price; plus Boost, now hybrid, still loses money (DISH Wireless pre-tax loss $418M in Q2 26 alone).

## 9. Key risks and kill criteria (measurable)
1. Cash taxes disclosed for the AT&T plus SpaceX transactions above $6B in total (10-Q/10-K tax note, consolidated).
2. SpaceX acquisition closing not completed by 31 Mar 2028, or the $11B equity amount cut by amendment (8-K under Item 1.01/8.01).
3. Bankruptcy court orders or a Special Committee finding that shifts more than $1B of HSSC or DBS claims to the EchoStar parent (docket / 8-K).
4. Consolidated unrestricted cash plus marketable securities below $12B on the 31 Dec 2026 balance sheet (10-K consolidated balance sheet).
5. Wireless segment operating loss above $1.0B on an annualised basis after the hybrid transition (10-Q segment table).
