## 1. Verdict
**WATCH**, horizon 12–24 months. Triage said "not advanced: low growth, price-competitive category". On valuation I partly overturn it: the price implies free cash shrinking about 1–3% a year, below my ~3% base, and base 3-year return is about 9% a year. But the evidence quality is poor right now. Condition to move to INCLUDE-SMALL: the 2026-10-20 investor update shows FIFO operating profit growing (not just EPS via buybacks) and identical sales without fuel back above 1%. The reasons to wait: adjusted net earnings fell about 4% in FQ2 while EPS rose 5% purely on a 9% smaller share count, sales guidance was cut, and strategy is about to be reset by a new CEO.

## 2. Business in plain English
Kroger is the largest US traditional supermarket operator: supermarkets under regional banners (serving more than 11 million customers daily, per the release), with pharmacies, fuel centres and a growing e-commerce and retail-media business (Kroger Precision Marketing). It earns a thin store margin (2.8% GAAP operating margin) plus higher-margin "alternative profit" streams ($1.5B operating profit in FY25). Moat: regional density, loyalty data and private label; pressure from Walmart, Costco, Aldi and Amazon, and now drug-pricing rules (Inflation Reduction Act, IRA) that reduce reported pharmacy sales.

## 3. Why the model likes it
b1 flags a cheap value rank and a positive earnings-surprise family with negative 12-month momentum (-12.7%). The surprise component is real at the EPS line (FQ2 adjusted EPS $1.09 vs $1.04) but, as shown below, it is buyback-driven; operating profit and sales momentum are weakening. I treat the value signal as durable only if operating profit stabilises.

## 8. Bull and bear
**Bull:** (1) Near-zero growth and falling FCF already priced in; 8% FCF yield plus a 2.7% dividend. (2) E-commerce turning profitable ($400M improvement targeted for 2026) and media growing (Precision Marketing profit +24%). (3) Low leverage (1.91x vs 2.3–2.5x target) leaves room for buybacks.
**Bear:** (1) Adjusted net earnings -4% with EPS supported only by a 9% lower share count; FQ2 adjusted FIFO operating profit -1.4%. (2) Identical sales 0.2% with IRA drag 138 bps, brand-to-generic shift 61 bps and a Cyclospora outbreak about 35 bps; range cut. (3) New CEO reset (2026-10-20 update) likely prioritises price investment; Albertsons litigation ($600M fee claimed) and opioid payments continue.

## 9. Key risks and kill criteria
1. Identical sales without fuel (company definition) below 0.0% for any fiscal quarter, or the "0.2% - 0.8%" full-year range cut again.
2. Adjusted FIFO operating profit below $1,000M in FQ3 2026 (12 weeks; FQ2 2026: $1,076M), or the full-year range "$5.0 - $5.2 billion" cut below $5.0B.
3. Full-year adjusted FCF range "$2.7 - $2.9 billion" cut below $2.4B, or net total debt to adjusted EBITDA (consolidated, company definition) above 2.5x after the Giant Eagle deal.
4. Adjusted EPS range "$5.10 - $5.30" cut, or adjusted EPS growth below +3% in two consecutive quarters with share count falling more than 5%.
5. Adverse Albertsons ruling above $600M (Delaware Court of Chancery) or opioid settlement cost above the disclosed "up to $1,200" million (states and subdivisions) plus $36M (tribes).
