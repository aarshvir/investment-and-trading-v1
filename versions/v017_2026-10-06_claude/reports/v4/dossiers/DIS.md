## 1. Verdict
**INCLUDE-SMALL** (12–36 month horizon) **[Corrected 2026-10-06: verdict restated to WATCH on the corrected valuation method, see Correction section DV13]**, reservation: near-term free-cash-flow volatility (ESPN DTC content-cost phasing, cruise-ship capex, one-off impairments) makes the "streaming inflection" thesis real but not yet cash-confirmed at the consolidated level.

Thesis (plain English): the best-in-class content IP portfolio (Marvel, Star Wars, Pixar, ESPN, Disney+) is finally turning its decade-long streaming investment into profit while Parks keeps compounding through cruise-ship and international expansion — but reported free cash flow fell yet again this year, and that gap between "adjusted EPS up" and "cash down" is the thing to watch.

## 2. Business in plain English
Disney sells stories and experiences: film/TV/streaming content (Entertainment segment: Disney+, Hulu, studios, linear networks), live sports rights and distribution (Sports/ESPN, including ESPN's direct-to-consumer app launched Aug 2025), and theme parks, resorts, cruises and consumer products (Experiences). It makes money from subscriptions, advertising, licensing, box office, and guest spending, monetizing the same IP (a Marvel film feeds streaming, theme-park attractions, and merchandise). Its competitive position is a content library and physical-park moat that no single streaming or media peer can fully replicate, offset by intensifying streaming competition (Netflix, YouTube, Amazon) and a linear-TV bundle in structural decline.

## 8. Bull case / bear case
**Bull:** (1) Disney+/Hulu/Hotstar SVOD hit a 13% margin in Q3 and are guided to double digits for FY26 — the decade-long streaming investment is inflecting to cash generation, not just accounting profit. (2) Experiences keeps compounding on cruise-fleet expansion and domestic per-cap spend (+4% YoY) even as international softens. (3) $9bn+ of FY26 buybacks at a sub-15x NTM P/E is a credible capital-return underpin.

**Bear:** (1) Consolidated FCF is down 24% YTD through Q3 FY26 despite "record" adjusted-EPS headlines — the cash the thesis depends on has not shown up yet. (2) ESPN operating income fell 17% YoY in Q3 on a carriage dispute plus sports-calendar randomness (playoff sweeps), and the segment sits inside a linear-TV bundle in secular decline. (3) The A+E Networks $812m impairment and repeated "one-off" items (severance, tax charges from Fubo/NFL) suggest more GAAP noise ahead, making adjusted EPS harder to trust at face value.

## 9. Key risks & kill criteria (measurable)
1. TTM free cash flow does not recover to at least $10bn by FY26 year-end (vs FY25's $10.1bn) — signals the FCF decline is structural, not timing.
2. ESPN/Sports segment operating income declines YoY for two consecutive quarters excluding sports-calendar effects (playoff timing).
3. Disney+/Hulu/Hotstar combined SVOD operating margin fails to reach double digits for full FY26 (vs the guided target).
4. Net debt/EBITDA rises above 2.5x (vs ≈1.7x now), signalling buybacks are outrunning cash generation.
5. FY27 guidance (to be given with FY26 Q4 results, Nov 2026) is cut from the currently-reiterated "double-digit adjusted EPS growth."
