## 2. Business in plain English
Baker Hughes sells equipment and services to oil-and-gas producers (Oilfield Services & Equipment, OFSE: drilling, completions, artificial lift, subsea) and turbines, compressors and related services to LNG plants, pipelines, power generators and data-centre power users (Industrial & Energy Technology, IET). IET is the growth engine, paid through multi-year backlog plus a service stream on the installed base. Moat: installed base of gas turbines and compressors, scale in LNG compression and subsea.
