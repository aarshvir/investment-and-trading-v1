# Lead audit of the v3 "conviction portfolio" — verified findings (2026-09-26)

**Verdict: the v3 14-stock allocation should not be funded as delivered.** Its 90% headline is an assumption, not a finding. The evidence behind its momentum tilt was inflated by look-ahead bias. Its only out-of-sample test pointed the wrong way. Its reported risk was understated by roughly 10 points of drawdown.

Every item below was re-computed by the lead from the legacy bundle (`equity_project/`, scripts `v4/code/lead_check_*.py`) or checked in the v3 source code. Items first found by the prior external (Codex) review are marked [C]; items verified or first quantified in this audit are marked [L]. The external review's reproduced numbers matched ours to the decimal wherever we re-ran them.

| # | Severity | Finding | Evidence (re-computed) | Consequence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Critical | The "90% five-year odds of profit" comes from the assumed 10% market drift. The bootstrap re-centres history to it. [C][L] | 50/50 blend five-year P(profit), same data and method: 66.1% at a 4% drift, 75.3% at 6%, 82.7% at 8%, 88.2% at 10%, 89.8% at 10.68% | Confidence is conditional on a market assumption that sits at the top of institutional forecasts. It is not evidence of stock-picking skill. |

**What v3 got right (credit where due):** it rejected per-stock 90% certainty. It measured survivorship bias against RSP. It read primary-source earnings releases for its finalists; the external check found no fabricated headline figures. It flagged the UCITS/estate-tax structure. Its dashboard and code are reproducible.
