# CARR — Carrier Global Corporation — Diligence Dossier (Agent F73, standard depth)

## 1. Verdict
**INCLUDE-SMALL** (half weight). One-sentence reason: a genuine, verified data-center-cooling order boom
(orders +300% in Q2 2026) is layered on top of a core HVAC/refrigeration business whose organic growth only just
turned positive again (+3% in Q2 2026, after -1% in Q1), leverage is the highest among named HVAC peers, and full-year
guidance — while raised twice in 2026 — has been raised on the strength of the data-center order book, not yet on
delivered core-business acceleration; named reservation: **do not size this as if the data-center order surge is
already converted to recognised, de-risked earnings** — it is orders/backlog, not yet trailing revenue.
Thesis horizon: 12–30 months (the next 2–3 quarters should show whether data-center orders convert to shipped revenue
and whether core CSA/CSE Residential stabilises).

## 2. Business in plain English
Carrier makes and services heating, ventilation, air-conditioning and refrigeration (HVAC&R) equipment — residential
and light-commercial air conditioners and furnaces, commercial building HVAC and controls, and transport/commercial
refrigeration — plus a fast-growing niche supplying precision cooling systems to AI/cloud data centers. It sells to
homebuilders, contractors, building owners, transport/cold-chain operators and (increasingly) hyperscale data-center
developers, earning both equipment margin and higher-margin recurring aftermarket service/parts revenue. Competitive
position: one of the "big three" global HVAC players (with Trane Technologies and Lennox/Johnson Controls-adjacent
peers), historically weaker on margin and leverage than Trane, but currently the beneficiary of an unusually large
data-center cooling order wave.

## 5. Guidance track record (prior range vs. new range, exact numbers)
All figures from 8-K Ex-99 earnings releases, "Full-Year Guidance" comparison tables (Carrier explicitly shows
"Current Guidance" vs. "Prior Guidance" side by side in each release):
- **Initial FY2026 guidance (Feb 5, 2026, Q4 2025 release):** Sales ~$22 billion (includes a ~$350M revenue headwind
  from the Riello divestiture, assumed to close by end-Q1 2026); organic growth "flat to up LSD"; Adjusted operating
  profit ~$3.4 billion; **Adjusted EPS ~$2.80**; Free cash flow ~$2 billion; ~$1.5 billion in share repurchases.
- **As of Apr 30, 2026 (Q1 release):** guidance **reaffirmed unchanged** at Sales ~$22B / Adj. op. profit ~$3.4B /
  Adj. EPS ~$2.80 / FCF ~$2B — only the Riello-divestiture-timing assumption changed (headwind trimmed from ~$350M to
  ~$250M as the deal's expected close slipped from Q1 to Q2 2026). Release headline: "Reaffirms full year outlook."
  **Verified: no EPS/profit change at Q1, contrary to a reading that might assume every release raises guidance.**
- **As of Jul 28, 2026 (Q2 release):** guidance **raised**: Sales to **~$23 billion** (from ~$22B), organic growth to
  "up ~mid-to-high-single-digit" (from "flat to up LSD"), Adjusted operating profit to **~$3.5 billion** (from ~$3.4B),
  **Adjusted EPS to ~$2.90** (from ~$2.80) — a real, verified raise against the prior range, with the release itself
  labelled "Increases Full-year Outlook for Sales, Adj. Op. Profit and Adj. EPS." Free cash flow guidance held at
  ~$2 billion (unchanged despite the sales/EPS raise — worth noting as a mild quality flag: cash generation is not
  scaling with the guided profit raise, consistent with the higher receivables/inventory buildup discussed in §6).
  The raise explicitly **includes a ~$0.05 adjusted-EPS drag from the NORESCO exit and new U.S. factory costs**,
  meaning the underlying operating raise, before that self-inflicted drag, is somewhat larger than the headline
  $2.80→$2.90 delta suggests.
- Net: two releases with unchanged guidance/reaffirmation-only language do not exist in this window — it was
  reaffirm (Q1) then raise (Q2); no cuts in the trailing four releases reviewed.

## 6. Earnings quality & balance sheet
**Entity scope: Carrier Global Corporation, consolidated**, balance sheet as of June 30, 2026, per 10-Q filed
2026-07-28 (accession 0001783180-26-000032).
- **FCF conversion:** Q2 2026 FCF of $810M against adjusted net income roughly in the $500–600M range for the quarter
  (adjusted EPS $0.86 on ~617M shares ≈ ~$530M) — a healthy quarter of cash conversion, but Q1 2026 FCF was **negative**
  ($(15)M) against a positive adjusted profit quarter, and working-capital build (accounts receivable rose from
  $2,639M at Dec 31 2025 to $3,246M at Jun 30 2026, +23%, faster than the ~4% net-sales growth over the same period)
  is consistent with the data-center ramp requiring working-capital investment ahead of cash collection — not
  alarming on its own, but a real use of cash that offsets some of the order-book optimism, and explains why FY26 FCF
  guidance (~$2B) was **not** raised alongside the sales/EPS raise.
- **GAAP vs. adjusted gap:** Q2 2026 GAAP operating profit $825M vs. adjusted operating profit $1,095M (a $270M gap,
  ~25% of the adjusted figure) — driven by restructuring, acquisition-amortisation and separation/divestiture
  (Riello/NORESCO exit) costs. This is a large and persistent GAAP/adjusted gap; the size of the gap itself is not
  disclosed as newly widening, but it means investors relying on adjusted EPS alone are looking through a materially
  different (lower) GAAP earnings reality.
- **Balance sheet:** cash $1,344M (Jun 30, 2026) vs. $1,555M (Dec 31, 2025); total debt $11,952M vs. $11,833M; **net
  debt $10,608M**, up slightly from $10,278M at year-end (net debt actually *grew* in H1 2026, driven by working
  capital and continued shareholder returns outrunning FCF, not by new M&A). Total equity $13,472M (down from
  $14,128M). Net debt/adjusted EBITDA: using FY26 guided adjusted operating profit (~$3.5B) as an EBITDA proxy (Carrier
  does not separately disclose a clean adjusted-EBITDA guide in the extract reviewed), net debt/adjusted-op.-profit ≈
  **3.0x**; on trailing GAAP EBITDA (~$3.09B per d4/Yahoo), net debt/EBITDA ≈ **3.4x** — close to, slightly below, the
  triage's cited ~3.6x (the small gap is attributable to different EBITDA bases; both readings confirm Carrier
  carries the highest leverage of the named HVAC peer set, as triage flagged). Goodwill $15,313M (Jun 2026) against
  equity of $13,472M — goodwill again exceeds equity, a legacy of the UTC Climate/Carrier 2020 spin-merger history.
  [10-Q filed 2026-07-28, balance sheet]
- **Capital return:** ~$0.5B returned in Q1, ~$0.64B in Q2 (dividends + repurchases); board-authorized buyback
  capacity of $4.58B remaining as of June 30, 2026 against a $12.1B cumulative authorization since Feb 2021 — capital
  return continuing at a steady pace even as net debt ticks up, which is the leverage risk to watch (§9).
- **Share count:** trending down via ongoing repurchases (see table in §11 buyback activity — not separately broken
  out here, but consistent with steady buyback cadence).
- **Divestitures, not M&A adds:** Riello (European heating) divestiture completed **July 1, 2026** (closed slightly
  later than the "end of Q2 2026" target stated in the Q1 release — a one-quarter slip from the original Q1 2026
  target, now the second slip disclosed); NORESCO exit also announced/in progress. Both reduce revenue (~$225M and
  ~$125M FY26 headwinds respectively per the Q2 guidance table) but are described as margin/strategic-focus positive.
  No pending acquisitions disclosed.

- Peer context (qualitative): Trane Technologies (TT) and Lennox (LII) — the peers triage cites — both trade at
  premium multiples to Carrier (historically TT and LII have carried mid-20s to low-30s forward P/E vs. Carrier's
  high-teens), which the model and Street partly explain by TT/LII's higher margins and lower leverage; Carrier's
  discount is not unearned.
- **Reverse DCF (own build, 10-year explicit + Gordon terminal, WACC 8.0%, terminal growth 3.0%):** using trailing TTM
  FCF (~$0.86B) as the starting point implies an unrealistically high required growth rate (~17.8%/yr) — this is a
  **known distortion** from the negative-FCF Q1 2026 quarter dragging the TTM figure down, and should not be used.
  Using the company's own FY26 guided FCF (~$2.0B) as a cleaner starting point, the current enterprise value (~$56.6B)
  implies the market is pricing a **10-year FCF CAGR of roughly 7.0%**. Carrier's own guided algorithm (data-center
  order book converting to sales, continued double-digit commercial-HVAC/aftermarket growth, offset by a
  low-single-digit-growth-at-best residential core) plausibly supports high-single-digit-to-low-double-digit earnings
  growth *if* the data-center ramp continues at anything like its current order pace — meaning the priced-in growth
  (~7%) looks **at or below** a reasonable evidence-based base case, not above it.
- Verdict on implied growth: **implied_vs_base = in_line** (base case ≈ 7–9% FCF/EPS CAGR over 10 years, blending a
  decelerating data-center growth rate with a slowly recovering low-single-digit core; the 7% implied by the reverse
  DCF sits within, at the low end of, that range).

## 8. Bull case and bear case
**Bull (3 points):**
1. Data-center cooling backlog "fully covers expected 2026 data center sales" (Q1 2026 release) — i.e., 2026 revenue
   from this line is already substantially locked in, de-risking near-term delivery even if new order growth slows.
2. Organic sales inflected positive in Q2 2026 (+3%) after two quarters of decline, and management attributes this to
   broad CSA (commercial) strength, not just data centers — a genuine core-business green shoot.
3. Guidance has been raised, not cut, twice in the two most-recent releases reviewed, on both sales and adjusted EPS,
   which is a real (verified) signal of improving visibility, not just optimistic messaging.

**Bear (3 points):**
1. Net debt actually **grew** in H1 2026 (to $10.6B from $10.3B) even as guidance was raised — cash is being consumed
   by working-capital build (receivables +23% vs. sales +4%) and continued buybacks, and FCF guidance was **not**
   raised alongside the sales/EPS raise, a mismatch worth watching.
2. Adjusted operating margin **compressed** ~190bps YoY in the very quarter (Q2 2026) that produced the guidance
   raise — the EPS growth story currently rests more on lower share count/financing costs than on margin expansion,
   which is a lower-quality growth mix than the headline "raises outlook" framing suggests.
3. The core Residential/Light Commercial business (ex-data-center, ex-divestitures) is still only flat-to-low-single-
   digit at best; if data-center order growth decelerates faster than the core recovers, the 2027 guide could
   disappoint even if 2026 delivers as guided.

## 9. Key risks & kill criteria (measurable)
1. Data-center segment orders growth decelerates below +50% YoY for two consecutive quarters (from the current
   +300–500% pace), signalling the boom is normalising faster than the backlog can be replenished.
2. Net debt/EBITDA (consolidated, using guided adjusted operating profit as the EBITDA proxy) rises above **3.5x**
   (from ~3.0x currently).
3. FY26 adjusted EPS guidance is **cut** from the current ~$2.90 at any future quarterly release.
4. Core (non-data-center) organic growth falls back below 0% for two consecutive quarters after the Q2 2026 +3% print.
5. Free cash flow for FY26 comes in below the guided ~$2.0 billion (currently un-raised despite the sales/EPS raise —
   a miss here would confirm the cash-conversion concern flagged in §6).
