## 1. Verdict: INCLUDE-SMALL (half weight) — thesis horizon 12–36 months
Financially the cleanest of the three names — earnings are programmatic rather than cyclically inflated, guidance has been raised four consecutive times with full transparency on the prior range, and capital (RBC 316%) is solid. Held to half weight, not full, because of a real, unresolved, business-model-specific tail risk: a 2+ year old securities class action tied to 2024 short-seller allegations about sales practices at its largest subsidiary has just reached the class-certification stage, and GL's whole model depends on recruiting and retaining the career agents those allegations targeted.

## 2. Business in plain English
Globe Life is a niche life and supplemental-health insurer selling simplified-issue term life, whole life, and supplemental health products to lower-middle- and middle-income U.S. households, primarily through multiple career-agency distribution channels: American Income Life (labor-union-affiliated), Liberty National, Family Heritage (payroll/worksite health), United American (Medicare supplement), and a smaller Direct-to-Consumer arm. It earns money on the underwriting margin between premium and claims/expenses on relatively small, simple policies, plus investment income on reserves — a capital-light model whose main "raw material" is a large, constantly-recruited independent/career agent force.

## 3. Why the model likes it — and whether that's durable
b1-family prelim scores: Q 0.726, V 0.480, M 0.750 (12-1m return +21.8%), composite 0.734 — the lowest composite of the three, reflecting a smaller, steadier growth profile rather than a cyclical spike. **This is the one name where the model's read looks reasonably durable, not an artefact:** unlike TRV/ALL, d4 shows GL's FY2027 consensus EPS ($16.52) is **+5.1% above** FY2026 consensus ($15.71) — no forward air-pocket (`ntm_flags` blank, `ntm_quality = "ok"`). Life/health earnings emerge programmatically over the life of a policy rather than swinging on a single quarter's catastrophes, which is exactly what the data shows: net operating income per share grew in **8 of the last 9 quarters** by double digits (company's own claim, corroborated by the EPS series in §4). The main caveat: even GL's own guidance implies deceleration — FY2026 guidance (now $15.55-$15.95, midpoint $15.75) is only **+8.5%** over FY2025's actual $14.52, versus FY2025's own **+17.4%** growth over FY2024 — so "durable" does not mean "re-accelerating."

- **SBC:** $53.4M FY2025 = ~0.9% of revenue, growing (+74% over 2 years from $30.7M) — still immaterial but the fastest-growing of the three; worth a watch-item, not a red flag.
- **Capital/RBC:** consolidated Company Action Level RBC ratio **316% for 2025**, within GL's own 300-320% target band (sector playbook: "well above 300-400%" is comfortable — GL sits at the lower end of comfortable by its own design, not distressed).
- **Debt:** senior notes + junior subordinated debentures book value ≈ $1.9-2.0B (10-Q fair-value table: $548M 2028 4.55%, $398M 2030 2.15%, $392M 2032 4.80% (a $150M tranche was sold from insurance-subsidiary holdings to a third party during Q2-26), $445M 2034 5.85%, ~$125M+ 2057 junior subordinated 5.275%), plus a contingent, **undrawn** $500M trust facility. **Data conflict:** Yahoo/d4's `y_totalDebt` for GL is **$3.427B — roughly 70-80% higher** than the actual senior-notes-plus-junior-subordinated total per the 10-Q. The b1 prelim csv's own `debt_lt` ($1.630B) is actually *closer* to the true figure than Yahoo's. This means any generic screen using Yahoo's debt field would overstate GL's leverage materially — flag for correction.
- **Bermuda captive reinsurance:** GL does have Bermuda-domiciled insurance subsidiaries (confirmed, 10-Q), the structure the 2024 short-seller reports focused on. The 10-Q states Bermuda subsidiary capitalization "exceeded the required minimum solvency margins for the year ended 2025" — company's own primary-source representation; independent verification of the captive economics was outside the scope of this pass.
- **Buybacks:** FY2025 $685M (5.4M shares, avg $126.41); Q1-26 $203M (avg $141.24); Q2-26 $175M (1.1M shares) — consistently large relative to net income (~50-60% of NI), a genuine capital-return story. Book value/share $78.18 (+18% YoY); ex-AOCI $100.04 (+11% YoY) — solid, less inflated than ALL's +49.7%.

## 8. Bull case / Bear case
**Bull:** (1) Four consecutive guidance raises with full transparency, broad-based growth across all five distribution channels, RBC within target — the most "boring and programmatic" of the three earnings streams, which is a genuine virtue for a diligence process that just got burned by a v3 audit citing omitted adverse facts. (2) Large, sustained buyback program (~50-60% of NI) directly compounding per-share value. (3) Valued at a discount to a reasonable life-insurer peer (AFL) despite comparable-or-better guidance execution — if the litigation resolves without a material loss, there is a re-rating case.
**Bear:** (1) The City of Miami Matter securities class action — derived from the April 2024 Fuzzy Panda/Viceroy short-seller reports alleging fraudulent policy issuance, forged signatures, and unauthorized withdrawals at American Income Life — reached the class-certification-motion stage on 2026-05-15 and remains fully unresolved; several derivative suits are stayed pending its outcome. This is exactly the kind of adverse fact the prior (v3) process was found to have omitted. (2) The alleged misconduct centers on the agent-recruitment/sales-practice engine that *is* GL's entire distribution model — even absent a large monetary judgment, reputational damage to agent recruiting/retention is a plausible, harder-to-quantify risk the numbers wouldn't show until it shows up in net sales or agent-count growth. (3) Guidance growth is decelerating (FY26 guided +8.5% vs FY25's actual +17.4%) even as it beats-and-raises — the easy comps may be behind it too.

## 9. Key risks & kill criteria — thesis is invalidated if any of the following are observed (source: company 10-Q/8-K disclosures each quarter)
1. FY guidance is **cut** (not just a smaller raise) versus the prior quarter's range — would break the multi-year beat-and-raise pattern and signal real deterioration.
2. Consolidated Company Action Level RBC ratio falls below ~280% (below its own 300-320% target band).
3. Adverse class-certification ruling, or a settlement/verdict in the City of Miami Matter exceeding roughly 5% of shareholders' equity (~$310M on $6.16B equity).
4. Life or health underwriting margin growth turns negative for two consecutive quarters at any major division.
5. Net operating ROE ex-AOCI falls below ~12% (vs. 14.3% 1H26).
6. Disclosure of a formal DOJ/SEC enforcement action or order (an escalation from the current "no mention" status in the Q2-26 10-Q — see §11).

## 11. Red-flag scan
**This is the one name in the batch with a live, material legal overhang and it must be weighed accordingly.** Per the Q2-26 10-Q (Note 5, Commitments and Contingencies): a consolidated federal securities class action (City of Miami Gen. Emp. & Sanitation Emp. Ret. Trust v. Globe Life Inc., E.D. Tex., Case No. 4:24-cv-00376) derived from the April 2024 Fuzzy Panda and Viceroy short-seller reports; Lead Plaintiffs moved for class certification 2026-05-15. Related shareholder-derivative suits (Hsiao; Plymouth County Retirement Association/Sugarbaker) are largely stayed pending that outcome. GL's Audit Committee, assisted by WilmerHale and FTI Consulting, completed an independent review of the allegations in 2024. **No mention of an active DOJ subpoena or SEC investigation appears anywhere in the Q2-26 10-Q** (searched directly — zero hits for "subpoena," "Department of Justice," "SEC investigation"); a July 2025 trade-press piece (InsuranceERM, paywalled beyond the headline) characterized the DOJ/SEC probes as "winding down" as of that date — this is a secondary source and could not be independently verified in full, but is *consistent with* the primary-source silence 13+ months later. Item 1A of the 10-Q states "no material changes" to FY2025 10-K risk factors. No auditor change, no restatement, no going-concern/material-weakness language found. 40 Form 4 filings between 2026-02-27 and 2026-09-23 (one a 4/A amendment) — direction not individually tabulated in this pass; no 13D/G activist stake surfaced.
