**Agent:** F24 | **As of:** 2026-09-25 close ($272.36) | **Depth:** Standard

## 1. Verdict

**INCLUDE-SMALL** — 24-36 month horizon; reservation stated below. The triage's "consistent above-market growth... cheap relative to quality" framing (16.7x NTM P/E, 35% street upside) is broadly upheld, but it omitted two real 2026 shocks — a March cyberattack and a persistent peripheral-vascular/joint-replacement supply constraint now guided to run through Q4 2026 — that the triage's "general knowledge" sourcing could not have known. The business has proven resilient (guidance held through both), which is itself a positive finding, but the supply overhang is unresolved and warrants half-weight until it clears.

## 2. Business

Stryker makes orthopaedic implants (hips, knees — increasingly via the Mako robotic-arm platform), MedSurg/neurotech equipment, and trauma/extremities products, sold to hospitals and surgery centers globally. Growth has been driven by Mako placements pulling through implant volume and share gains in trauma/craniomaxillofacial.

## 3. Why the model liked it, and is it durable

b1 scored SYK on quality/growth (ROE, gross profit/assets) consistent with the Mako-driven ortho compounding story. Durable in the core franchise; **not fully durable in the near term** because two operational shocks (cyberattack, supply constraint) are actively suppressing 2026 volume in specific segments — a genuine, disclosed headwind the triage missed.

## 8. Bull case
1. Guidance has been held (narrowed, not cut) through two separate 2026 shocks — evidence of real operating resilience in the core Mako/ortho franchise.
2. Reverse DCF shows the market pricing in less growth (4.8%/yr FCF) than the company's own guided pace (~10%/yr adjusted EPS).
3. Leverage is comfortable (~1.8x net debt/EBITDA) and FCF conversion is strong, giving room to absorb the supply-chain remediation cost without balance-sheet stress.

## 9. Bear case
1. The peripheral-vascular/joint-replacement supply constraint has now been disclosed as persisting for **three consecutive quarters** (Q2, Q3, guided into Q4 2026) — longer than initially expected, and management admits it is costing new-business wins, not just revenue timing.
2. Reported EPS growth this year is partly a one-off illusion (tariff reversal + tax items); underlying adjusted growth, while still solid, is the more honest 11-18% figure, not the 44% GAAP headline.
3. A second cybersecurity incident inside 18 months (after March 2026) would compound the reputational and operational risk in a business that ships patient-specific implants on tight surgical schedules.

## 10. Kill criteria (measurable — the specific, observable conditions that would invalidate this thesis)
1. Full-year 2026 organic sales growth guidance (currently 8.3%-9.3%) is **cut below 7%**.
2. The peripheral-vascular/joint-replacement supply disruption is **not resolved by Q1 2027** (a fourth consecutive quarter of disclosed drag).
3. Adjusted diluted EPS guidance (currently $14.95-$15.10) is **cut**.
4. Net debt/EBITDA rises **above 2.5x** (from ~1.8x today).
5. A second disclosed cybersecurity incident or a Mako/ortho product-safety recall.
