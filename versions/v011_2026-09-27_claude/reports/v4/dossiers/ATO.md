# Atmos Energy Corporation (ATO) — Diligence Dossier (Agent F99, standard depth)

**1. Verdict: INCLUDE-SMALL (half weight), 24–36 month horizon.** Top-quintile-quality, A-rated regulated gas
utility with visible rate-base-driven earnings growth, but (a) a fatal Dallas gas-explosion lawsuit filed 2026
introduces an open-ended safety/litigation tail specific to this name, and (b) heavy equity issuance to fund a
now-larger capex plan structurally caps per-share growth. Reservation: size at half weight until the explosion
litigation and any resulting regulatory response are better defined.

**2. Business in plain English.** Atmos Energy is a pure-play, fully regulated natural-gas distribution and
intrastate-pipeline utility serving over three million customers across eight states, concentrated in Texas,
Louisiana and the Mid-States (Atmos Pipeline–Texas + distribution segments, consolidated, FY2025 10-K filed
2025-11-14). It earns a regulator-approved return on an expanding rate base by spending on pipe replacement and
system growth, recovered through rate cases and cost-of-service adjustment mechanisms; it does not take material
commodity price risk (gas costs are largely pass-through).

**3. Why the model likes it / durability.** b1_live_scores.csv: composite decile 7/10, driven by earnings-yield
(pct_ep 0.60), book/price (pct_bp 0.69) and an extreme SUE percentile (0.98) — i.e., valuation and a recent earnings
surprise, not momentum (mom_12_1 percentile only 0.44, in fact raw 12-1m momentum is slightly negative). Durable:
the surprise reflects real rate-base growth and a step-up from Texas House Bill 4384 / rate mechanisms (per
management's own FY2027 guide, see §5), not a one-off. Not durable: the ongoing dilution from ATM equity issuance
(below) mechanically caps EPS growth below rate-base growth.

**8. Bull case:** (1) FY2027 6–8% EPS growth guide is credible given the disclosed rate-mechanism step-ups and a
9.8% ROE running below allowed ROEs in most of its jurisdictions (regulatory lag still to close); (2) A-grade
balance sheet (S&P/Moody's high investment grade, not independently re-verified this pass) gives cheap access to
the $4.2bn capex plan; (3) stock priced for less growth (≈5.4%) than guidance (6–8%), a margin of safety.
**Bear case:** (1) the May 2026 Dallas explosion litigation (below) could force accelerated, unbudgeted safety
capex or fines beyond the $4.2bn plan, pressuring the ROE/rate-base math; (2) continuous equity issuance (+2.2%
shares in two quarters) caps EPS growth well below rate-base growth if capex needs keep stepping up; (3) 9.8% ROE
already below several allowed ROEs implies regulatory-lag risk cuts both ways — a rate case could also go against
the company on cost recovery.

**9. Key risks & kill criteria:** (i) Diluted EPS growth (consolidated, YoY) falls below 4% for two consecutive
fiscal quarters; (ii) consolidated long-term debt + capital-lease obligations (noncurrent) rises above $11.5bn
without a matching, regulator-approved rate-base step-up; (iii) any regulatory finding, fine, or settlement tied
to the May 2026 Dallas/NTSB investigation exceeding $150m not already reserved for; (iv) FY2027 EPS guidance
initiated below the previously flagged 6–8% range; (v) diluted share count grows >6% in any rolling 12 months
(equity-issuance pace outrunning the capex plan's own math).
