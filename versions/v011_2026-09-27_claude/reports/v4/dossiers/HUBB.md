# HUBB — Hubbell Incorporated (Diligence dossier, F85)

## 1. Verdict
**INCLUDE-SMALL** — genuine, accelerating grid-modernization/data-center-electrification demand and a beat-and-raise
quarter, sized down (not full weight) for a specific, named reservation: leverage (debt/EBITDA proxy) is the highest
among peer machinery names per the triage note, and momentum (+6.6%) has lagged the fundamental growth (+15.3%),
i.e. the market is already discounting balance-sheet risk even as results beat. Horizon: 12–24 months.

## 2. Business in plain English
Hubbell makes electrical equipment in two segments: **Utility Solutions** (transmission/distribution hardware,
grid-automation and metering equipment sold to electric utilities) and **Electrical Solutions** (wiring devices,
lighting, enclosures and components sold to contractors, industrial and, increasingly, data-center customers). It
makes money as a picks-and-shovels supplier to two structurally growing end markets right now: aging-grid
replacement/modernization spend by utilities, and electrical infrastructure build-out for AI data centers.

## 3. Why the model likes it / durability
Triage (Q10_triage.json): quality 4/5, growth 4/5, price-vs-growth 3/5; "fastest revenue growth in the group (15.3%)
... but weak momentum (+6.6%) ... suggests the market wants leverage to come down first"; red flag "leverage 3.4x is
the highest among peer machinery names." **Confirmed and updated with Q2 2026 primary data:** total net sales
+15% YoY to $1,711.8M, with Electrical Solutions (the data-center-exposed segment) up 25% YoY (18% organic) — this is
a genuine, broad-based acceleration (both segments grew, not one segment masking weakness in the other), not an
artefact. The growth is durable to the extent utility capex cycles and data-center buildouts continue multi-year,
which is the current consensus view across the sector, but it is capital-goods/cyclical by nature, not a structurally
guaranteed compounder.

## 8. Bull case
1. Two consecutive quarters (Q1 and Q2 2026) of accelerating, broad-based (both segments) organic growth with
   guidance raised each time — a real, primary-sourced beat-and-raise pattern, not a single-quarter fluke.
2. Electrical Solutions' 25% YoY / 18% organic growth is directly levered to AI data-center electrification capex,
   a theme with a long visible order book across the industry, not a mature/decelerating end market.
3. Low SBC (~1% of revenue) and reasonable FCF conversion (~75–80% of net income in H1 2026) mean the growth is not
   being bought with heavy dilution or aggressive non-GAAP add-backs.

## 9. Bear case / kill criteria
1. **Net debt (consolidated, per the most current `LongTermDebtNoncurrent` tag) rises above $2.5B** without a
   commensurate increase in trailing operating income — would confirm the triage's leverage concern rather than my
   more benign estimate.
2. **Organic sales growth decelerates below 9%** (the low end of the just-raised FY2026 guidance) in either of the
   next two quarters — the guidance itself is the benchmark to hold the company to.
3. **GAAP-to-adjusted EPS gap widens further** (currently $4.52 vs $5.52, a ~22% gap in Q2 2026) without clear,
   one-off justification in the MD&A — would indicate increasing reliance on adjustments to hit the raised adjusted
   guidance.
4. **Utility Solutions segment growth turns negative YoY** (it decelerated to +6% organic in Q2 2026 from stronger
   prior quarters per the release) — would signal the grid-capex leg of the thesis, not just the data-center leg,
   is rolling over.
5. **A guidance cut** versus the just-raised FY2026 range ($17.25–$17.55 GAAP EPS; 16–18% total sales growth) at the
   next release — a hard, binary trigger.
