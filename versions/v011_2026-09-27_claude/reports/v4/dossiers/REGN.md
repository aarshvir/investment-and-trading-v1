# REGN — Regeneron Pharmaceuticals, Inc. (F47, wave-2 diligence)

## 1. Verdict
**INCLUDE-SMALL** (half weight), thesis horizon 24–36 months. Reason: a diversified, still fast-growing biopharma (Dupixent +38% YoY, oncology scaling) priced by the market for very modest 10-year growth (reverse-DCF implied ~2.5–5%/yr) — but the reservation is real and named: Dupixent, ~40%+ of revenue, loses US composition-of-matter exclusivity around 2031, and the market has already been shown (Eylea) that Regeneron's biosimilar/patent moats erode faster than guided.

## 2. Business in plain English
Regeneron discovers, develops and sells biologic drugs. Its two big commercial franchises are Dupixent (an anti-inflammatory antibody for eczema, asthma, COPD and other allergic/inflammatory diseases, co-marketed 50/50 with Sanofi) and the Eylea/Eylea HD franchise (anti-VEGF injections for wet age-related macular degeneration and diabetic eye disease). A smaller but growing oncology business (Libtayo, Lynozyfic/linvoseltamab) and an early-to-mid-stage pipeline (Factor XI anticoagulants, an obesity GIP/GLP-1 agonist, itepekimab for COPD) round out the portfolio. Revenue comes from US product sales (Eylea family, Libtayo) plus Sanofi collaboration revenue/profit share on Dupixent and other partnered assets.

## 3. Why the model liked it — durable or artefact?
Triage (Q06, quality 4/5, growth 4/5, price_vs_growth 4/5) flagged: "even with Eylea's steep biosimilar-driven decline, aggregate revenue is still growing +17% and forward EPS +51%... 15.3x NTM P/E looks cheap, and the Eylea risk is already visible and quantifiable." b1 quant: composite 0.407 (decile 5, live_rank 295 — mid-pack, not a top-decile name), fam_S (earnings momentum) only 0.188 — the SUE signal is actually weak/negative despite the headline growth, because GAAP EPS has been volatile (see §6). fam_V (value) 0.696 is the strongest family. **Durability check:** the Dupixent growth is durable near-term (broad label, multiple ongoing launches — COPD, bullous pemphigoid, chronic spontaneous urticaria) but is not indefinite; it is a patent-protected franchise with a known 2031ish LOE, not a structural moat like a network effect. The Eylea decline is a real, already-in-progress biosimilar erosion, not a one-off. Both are correctly treated as durable-but-time-limited, which is why the reverse DCF (§7) matters more than the current EPS growth rate.

## 5. Guidance track record (quote the numbers; versus prior range)
Regeneron does not guide revenue directly (typical for a company whose two largest lines move on payer/biosimilar dynamics it does not fully control). It does guide GAAP/non-GAAP R&D, SG&A, gross margin and capex. Per the Q2 2026 release (2026-07-30, source: SEC 8-K Ex-99.1 `sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000872589/000087258926000023/exhibit991q22026.htm`):
- Non-GAAP R&D: **$5.95B–$6.05B, raised/narrowed from the prior $5.90B–$6.10B** (midpoint essentially unchanged, range tightened upward).
- Non-GAAP SG&A: **$2.50B–$2.60B, narrowed from $2.50B–$2.65B** (top end cut — modest positive).
- Non-GAAP gross margin: **84%–85%, raised from 83%–84%.**
- Capex: $985M–$1.10B (full-year).
For H2 2026, management guided **legacy Eylea demand to keep declining sequentially "low-to-mid-teens"** each quarter while Eylea HD demand grows sequentially low-to-mid-teens, and Dupixent growth to "moderate versus H1" on tougher comps. These are specific, quantified, and falsifiable — good practice — but they confirm the Eylea erosion is ongoing and guided to continue, not a one-off already fully absorbed.

## 6. Earnings quality & balance sheet
- **GAAP vs non-GAAP gap:** ~15–20% most quarters, driven by equity-securities mark-to-market and acquired-intangible items, not core operations. Analysts and the company both use non-GAAP; this dossier uses GAAP as primary and flags the gap each time, per program convention.
- **Cash conversion:** TTM operating cash flow (Q3'25+Q4'25*+Q1'26+Q2'26) ≈ **$4.68B**; guided FY26 capex ≈ $1.0–1.1B → TTM FCF ≈ **$3.6B**, FCF/GAAP-NI TTM (NI ≈ $4.33B) ≈ 84% — reasonable conversion, not a red flag.
- **Balance sheet (2026-06-30, 10-Q):** total assets $41.73B, total liabilities $10.02B, stockholders' equity $31.71B — essentially unlevered; long-term debt only $1.99B (a single legacy note), cash & equivalents $2.46B plus a much larger marketable-securities/strategic-equity portfolio (not fully broken out here). Net debt is negative (net cash) even before counting the securities portfolio.
- **SBC:** ~$257M in Q1 2026 alone (≈7% of that quarter's revenue) — normal for large-cap biotech, rising in dollar terms with headcount (15,410 FTEs) but not flagged as excessive.
- **Capital return:** REGN pays a small dividend, initiated 2023 ($3.76/share annualized, ~0.46% yield per 2026-09-25 close) and buys back stock opportunistically; no announced debt-funded M&A pending.
- **M&A:** none material pending as of this writing; REGN has historically been an organic-pipeline company plus small bolt-ons/licensing (e.g., partnered obesity asset via Hansoh Pharma licensing, per public deal history) rather than a serial acquirer.

## 8. Bull case / bear case
**Bull (3):**
1. Dupixent is still growing 38% YoY with multiple recent-launch indications (COPD, CSU, bullous pemphigoid) not yet mature — the deceleration priced into the reverse DCF may be too pessimistic for 3–5 more years.
2. Eylea HD (52% YoY growth, now outselling legacy Eylea on physician demand) is converting the franchise onto a longer-duration, harder-to-biosimilar product faster than the market gives credit for.
3. Optionality from Factor XI (Phase 3 starting H2 2026), the GIP/GLP-1 obesity asset (global Phase 3 starting H2 2026, positive China Phase 3 already reported), and a maturing oncology franchise (Libtayo +29%, Lynozyfic newly launched) is not in most reverse-DCF base cases.

**Bear (3):**
1. Dupixent, now the large majority of revenue and profit, carries US patent exclusivity that ends around 2031 — a single-product concentration risk on the scale of the Eylea situation the market has just watched play out.
2. Legacy Eylea US sales are down ~45% YoY and guided to keep falling "low-to-mid-teens" sequentially each quarter through 2026 — a real, ongoing, quantified headwind, not a completed one-off.
3. GAAP earnings are volatile and partly driven by mark-to-market swings on equity holdings unrelated to the operating business, making headline "EPS growth" numbers (the 51% figure the triage cited) less trustworthy than they look without the GAAP/non-GAAP split shown in §4.

## 9. Key risks & kill criteria — thesis-invalidation triggers (measurable, would break the thesis; verbatim into summary JSON)
1. "Legacy Eylea US net sales decline exceeds 50% YoY in any quarter through 2027" (currently ~45% YoY and guided to keep worsening — this is the escalation trigger, not the current level).
2. "Dupixent global net sales growth falls below 15% YoY for two consecutive quarters" (current 38% YoY; a sharp deceleration earlier than the modelled LOE-driven slowdown would be a thesis break).
3. "Any Phase 3 readout failure or negative FDA action on the Factor XI anticoagulant program or the GIP/GLP-1 obesity asset" (both are the main sources of bull-case optionality; AERIFY-2 already missed its primary endpoint for itepekimab in COPD, a partial precedent).
4. "GAAP-to-non-GAAP EPS gap exceeds 25% for two consecutive quarters without a disclosed one-time driver" (currently ~15-20%; a widening, undisclosed gap would be an earnings-quality red flag).
5. "Net debt turns materially positive (>$3B) to fund an acquisition without a clearly accretive, disclosed rationale."

## 11. Red-flag scan
- **Litigation:** the Eylea/aflibercept biosimilar patent litigation (BPCIA, multidistrict in N.D. West Virginia) has now **settled with Mylan/Biocon, Celltrion, Formycon, Sandoz and (Feb 2026) Samsung Bioepis**; each settlement sets a specific staggered launch date through end-2026. **Amgen is the sole remaining litigant and has already won and launched** — this is the one biosimilar entrant not on a negotiated, predictable timetable, and is a genuine (if bounded) source of forecast uncertainty (source: Bloomberg Law / FiercePharma / Big Molecule Watch reporting, cross-checked against multiple outlets, 2026).
- **Regulatory:** no open FDA warning letters, import alerts or consent decrees identified for Regeneron manufacturing sites in this pass (not exhaustively verified against the FDA inspection database — flagged as a gap given the standard depth time-box).
- **Accounting:** no restatement, auditor change, or going-concern language found. GAAP/non-GAAP gap discussed above is a disclosed, not concealed, feature.
- **Insider activity:** several Form 4 sales by directors/EVPs in 2026 (e.g., EVP Commercial Marion McCourt, Sept 2026; directors A. Ryan, H. Zoghbi, K. Guarini) appear to be routine 10b5-1/compensation-related sales, not clustered pre-catalyst selling; CEO Schleifer's most recent identified sale predates this window. Not treated as a red flag on the evidence gathered.
