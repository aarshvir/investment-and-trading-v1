# ADSK — Autodesk, Inc. — Diligence Dossier (Agent F38, Wave-2 Standard)

## 1. Verdict
**INCLUDE-SMALL** (12–36 month horizon). Reservation: Autodesk closed its largest-ever acquisition (MaintainX,
~$3.6bn all-cash) on 2026-08-03 — after the most recent balance-sheet date this dossier can source from SEC
XBRL — funded roughly half by cash and half by new debt. The clean, high-growth, high-margin, near-net-cash
story in this dossier's numbers is about to become a levered-up integration story; that transition, not the
underlying business, is the reservation.

## 2. Business in plain English
Autodesk sells subscription software for people who design and build physical things: AutoCAD and Revit for
architecture/engineering/construction (AECO), Fusion/Inventor for manufacturing, and Flame/Maya for media and
entertainment. Customers pay annual or multi-year subscriptions; switching costs come from decades of files in
Autodesk's proprietary formats, industry-standard certifications built around its tools, and deep workflow
integration on large capital projects. With the August-2026 MaintainX acquisition (a mobile-first CMMS/asset-
maintenance platform), Autodesk is extending from "design and build" into "operate," per its own August 2026
release.

## 3. Why the model likes it — durable or artefact?

mainly by momentum (`mom_12_1` 1.47, an outlier vs peers) and value (`fcfp` 6.5%, `ep` 3.8%). d4: NTM P/E 15.3x,

score partly reflects a re-rating already underway (price up from the deep discount ADSK traded at during the
2024 SEC/DOJ free-cash-flow investigation, resolved August 2025 — see §11) rather than fresh news this quarter.

## 6. Earnings quality & balance sheet
- **FCF conversion:** TTM ≈ FY2026 annual OCF $2,452M + 1H FY27 OCF $1,468M − 1H FY26 OCF $1,024M = **$2,896M
  OCF**; capex over the same window ≈$57M → **≈$2,839M FCF**, broadly consistent with Yahoo/d4's TTM FCF of
  $3.157bn (d4 `freeCashflow`; some definitional difference not reconciled). FCF conversion vs GAAP net income
  is strong (FCF well above reported GAAP net income in most recent quarters) — no red flag.
- **SBC:** not separately quantified this pass (limitation).
- **RPO/deferred revenue nuance (from the Q2 FY27 release):** deferred revenue +11% YoY, but unbilled deferred
  revenue **-8%** and total RPO only **+2%** YoY — the company attributes this to "our sustained program reducing
  multi-year discounts, including winding down multi-year Maintenance-to-Subscription renewals," which
  mechanically shrinks unbilled/RPO even as underlying demand is fine. This is a real nuance an investor should
  not read as demand weakness, but it does mean RPO/billings growth understates true momentum right now — flag
  for whoever monitors this name next.
- **Leverage (before MaintainX close):** at 2026-07-31, long-term debt $2.5bn (unchanged for six straight
  quarters — a pre-existing term facility, not new), plus a new **$994M** of short-term borrowings that appeared
  only in the 2026-07-31 balance sheet (nil at 2026-01-31) — likely early draw-down ahead of the MaintainX
  closing, not independently confirmed to the dollar this pass. Cash was $4.1bn same date. **This entire picture
  changes on 2026-08-03**, when Autodesk closed MaintainX for ~$3.53bn net of cash acquired, funded by "roughly
  $1.6 billion in cash" plus new borrowing (Bloomberg, 2026-05-28, and company release) — i.e. most of the cash
  balance shown here will be gone and debt will rise by roughly $2bn, not yet visible in any SEC filing sourced
  this pass (the next 10-Q, for the quarter ending 2026-10-31, will be the first to show it).
- **M&A:** MaintainX — agreed 2026-05-28, closed 2026-08-03, ~$3.6bn announced / ~$3.53bn final consideration,
  all-cash — is explicitly flagged by the company as its **largest acquisition in Autodesk's history** (per
  contemporaneous press coverage of the deal; not itself a company-filed superlative claim, so treated as
  secondary-sourced context, not a primary fact).
- **Share count:** not separately tracked this pass; GAAP EPS growth outpacing net income growth in some
  quarters would suggest buybacks, not confirmed (limitation).

## 7. Valuation snapshot and reverse DCF
d4 (2026-09-25 close, price $209.40): NTM P/E 15.3x, FY1 P/E 14.7x, trailing P/E 27.1x, FCF yield 7.2%, beta 1.31.
No V1 systematic-valuation row exists for ADSK (not in the 92-name `v1_valuation_table.csv`) → `v1_verdict = null`;
this section is this analyst's own reverse DCF (skill script `valuation.py`).

- EV bridge: market cap $43.8bn + total debt $3.5bn (2026-07-31, pre-MaintainX-close) − cash $4.1bn = **EV $43.2bn**
  (net cash position of ~$0.6bn at this date — will flip to a net debt position once MaintainX's ~$2bn of new
  financing and cash use are reflected).
- Reverse DCF (central case): WACC 10.5% (beta 1.31, rf≈4.2%, ERP≈5.5%), terminal growth 4.0%, base FCF $2.9bn,
  10-year stage-1 horizon → **implied FCF growth ≈3.0%/yr for 10 years**, fading to 4%.
- Sensitivity: at WACC 12% (reflecting integration/leverage risk from MaintainX), implied growth rises to
  **≈5.9%/yr**.
- My evidence-based base case: 14–18% revenue growth has held for eight straight quarters even through a
  restructuring year; with MaintainX cross-sell and continued operating leverage (non-GAAP operating margin
  already ~41%), **11–14%/yr FCF/EPS growth** over the next decade is a reasonable base case (below recent
  revenue growth to allow for deceleration as the base scales and integration costs).
- **Implied vs base: BELOW**, even under the higher (12%) WACC sensitivity. The market is pricing Autodesk for
  materially slower compounding than its demonstrated run-rate, which is consistent with the stock still
  carrying some discount from the 2024–25 SEC/DOJ overhang and now the MaintainX integration-risk discount.

## 8. Bull case / bear case
**Bull:** (1) 8-for-8 revenue beats over the trailing two years (d4 `beats_last8`=8, `misses_last8`=0) with
accelerating non-GAAP margins (41% in Q2 FY27 vs 39% a year earlier). (2) MaintainX extends Autodesk from
"design/build" software into recurring "operate" software for the same industrial customer base — a genuine
adjacency, not a diversification into an unrelated market. (3) The Starboard Value activist situation resolved
constructively in 2025 (two new independent directors, no ongoing proxy fight), removing a governance overhang
while its cost-discipline pressure likely contributed to the margin gains now showing up.

**Bear:** (1) MaintainX is Autodesk's largest-ever deal, all-cash, funded partly with new debt, closing right at
the point this dossier's balance-sheet data runs out — integration risk is real and unpriced in the numbers
shown here. (2) The 2024 Audit Committee investigation into free-cash-flow and non-GAAP operating-margin
practices (closed without restatement in August 2025) revealed that Autodesk had historically "engaged in
programs designed to incentivize customers to accept multiyear upfront billing, renew early, and/or pay before
the end of the fiscal year" to hit FCF targets — a genuine historical earnings-quality flag on how FCF/billings
were managed, even though no restatement resulted; worth discounting FCF-based valuation multiples slightly
until a few more clean quarters build a track record post-investigation. (3) RPO/unbilled deferred revenue
growth (+2% YoY) is running well below revenue growth (+16%), partly explained by the multi-year-discount
wind-down (§6) but still a metric to watch for any real demand deceleration hiding behind that explanation.

## 9. Key risks & kill criteria (measurable)
1. Quarterly revenue growth decelerates below 10% YoY for two consecutive quarters (from the current 16-18% run-rate).
2. Non-GAAP operating margin contracts more than 200bps YoY for two consecutive quarters (from ~41% now),
   signaling MaintainX integration costs are structural rather than one-time.
3. Net debt/EBITDA (post-MaintainX-close) exceeds 2.0x with no disclosed deleveraging plan.
4. Total RPO growth stays below 5% YoY for three consecutive quarters after the multi-year-discount wind-down
   should have annualized out of the comparison (i.e., the explanation in §6 stops holding).
5. A new SEC/DOJ inquiry or restatement is announced (the 2024–25 investigation closed clean, but the underlying
   billing-incentive practices it examined warrant continued vigilance).

## 11. Red-flag scan
- **Regulatory (resolved):** In March 2024, Autodesk's Audit Committee opened an internal investigation into
  free-cash-flow and non-GAAP operating-margin practices and voluntarily notified the SEC; the SEC and USAO
  closed their respective matters in August 2025 with **no restatement or adjustment** of any GAAP or non-GAAP
  financial statements (per company disclosure, cross-checked via web search of SEC filing summaries). Treated
  as resolved but disclosed in full per §8 bear case — the practices examined are a genuine historical
  earnings-quality data point even without a restatement.
- **Governance (resolved constructively):** Starboard Value built a ~$500M stake and pushed for a proxy fight
  ahead of the 2025 annual meeting; Autodesk settled by adding two new independent directors (Jeff Epstein,
  Christie Simons), who became full voting board members after the 2025-06-18 annual meeting. Not an ongoing
  governance red flag.
- **M&A/leverage (emerging, not yet in the filed numbers):** MaintainX closing (2026-08-03) and its financing
  are the single largest near-term swing factor for this name and are not reflected in any balance sheet sourced
  in this dossier — explicitly flagged as the reservation behind the INCLUDE-SMALL verdict rather than folded
  silently into the numbers.
- **Data conflict:** the Q1 FY27 press release's own year-over-year EPS comparison table (§4, footnote 3)
  produced an internally inconsistent read when extracted programmatically; XBRL-sourced GAAP EPS is used as
  the source of record instead, and this is flagged rather than silently resolved by picking one number.
- No auditor change, restatement (beyond the closed 2024–25 investigation, which explicitly found none was
  required), or short-seller report identified this pass.
