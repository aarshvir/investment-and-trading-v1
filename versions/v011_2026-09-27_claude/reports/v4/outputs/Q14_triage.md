## Reasons (not advanced)
- **CPT**: Flat revenue and a 69x optically-inflated NTM P/E (small EPS base) with no near-term catalyst; oversupply needs another 12-18 months to clear before rent growth reaccelerates.
- **MAA**: Metrics are marginally better than CPT (positive revenue growth, higher FCF yield) but the structural supply overhang is identical and there is no differentiated catalyst to prioritize it now.
- **VMRK**: Unable to verify company identity, filings, or business description with available tools this session; treating as an unresolved data-quality flag rather than advancing on unverifiable numbers.
- **ARE**: Confirmed disqualifying red flags: a large dividend cut, collapsing biotech leasing demand, negative profitability, and elevated leverage into large 2026 lease expirations in Boston/Bay Area/San Diego with 6-24 month downtime risk.
- **BXP**: Best-in-class assets, but office demand is still recovering unevenly and leverage is elevated versus peers; not a clear enough case yet to prioritize over cleaner names.
- **KIM**: Solid, unremarkable: 26x P/E for ~2% forward EPS growth and higher leverage (6.1x) than better-positioned peers like Regency; not differentiated enough to prioritize.
- **INVH**: 36x NTM P/E on decelerating EPS is rich relative to a business now facing both new build-to-rent supply and a real (if uncertain-timing) regulatory overhang on corporate SFR ownership; agree with the prior excessive-valuation call.
- **SBAC**: Reasonably priced but weaker growth and higher leverage than AMT/CCI in the same triage batch, with no differentiated catalyst to prioritize it over those two.
- **WY**: The highest street-target upside in the batch (55%) reflects optionality on a housing-cycle recovery, but current cash generation is very thin; better as a watch item for a rate-cut/housing inflection than an immediate full-diligence priority.
- **EVRG**: Fair value for fair growth (9.3% ROE, below the group's better names) with a less pronounced data-center demand story than AEP/ETR/DUK/LNT; nothing disqualifying but nothing that earns priority over stronger peers in this batch.
