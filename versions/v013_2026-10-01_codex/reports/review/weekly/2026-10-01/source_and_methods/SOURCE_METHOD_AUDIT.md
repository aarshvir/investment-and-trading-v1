## Decision for the investment process

Adopt the pasted audit's call for better forecast calibration, explicit return bridges, a prospective prediction ledger, and portfolio construction that compares eligible ideas together. **Do not adopt its asserted all-stock “moderate-risk” mandate.** The direct user's 15–20% temporary-loss tolerance and the completed v012 planning policy retain a 25% maximum equity allocation until the user explicitly changes that budget with an informed view of the loss distribution. Beta targets of 0.6–1.1 for a 100% stock sleeve therefore cannot set this client's total-portfolio risk. At a 10% direct-stock sleeve, the marginal decision is how to use that 10%, after index overlap and issuer caps, not whether a 100% equity optimizer is efficient in isolation.

The pasted audit is stale in one important administrative respect: it calls for a “v012” next version, but **v012 has already been published**. A future release must reserve the next shared number using `tools/publish_research_release.py`. Its publication does not establish that its analysis beats v011 or v012.

## Evidence strength, coverage and methodological upgrades

- **Original mechanical strategy:** B1 reports top-30 net CAGR **12.5%** versus SPY **15.1%** from Jan 2012–Aug 2026, a **−2.6 percentage-point** historical gap and weak rank IC. B1X replicates a close variant within 0.04 percentage points, but it uses the same upstream point-in-time inputs and historical price-availability gaps. In 2012, only **357 of 499** PIT members have a valid price; mean priced share over the backtest is **86.9%** and mean composite share **85.7%**. The audit correctly rejects a claim of demonstrated alpha; the unresolved causal and survivor-data defects limit the exact CAGR. Current-member hindsight restriction yields spurious +4.7pp excess in B1X, which shows why historical membership matters.
- **Current analyst portfolio:** all 20 v011 names enter via the analyst route, not a separately validated selection rule. Order-sensitivity counts fell to six names present in all tested orderings. This is a useful instability test, not proof that each of the other 14 selections is wrong. The 20-name historical crisis replay applies **today's choices and weights** to past prices, excludes unlisted names and is not an executable historical strategy. Its 0.59 beta and 13.55% recent volatility describe the sleeve, not the whole 15/10/75 client model. Probability outputs condition on imposed drifts/zero alpha and are not calibrated personal odds.
- **Coverage:** the v011 release documents 336 completed full dossiers, 214 eligible verdicts, and 20 holdings as of its 25 September cutoff; the v012 weekly release refreshed 34 quotes and reused 469 older universe prices. “Queue complete” means the then-defined screened queue was empty. It does not make every S&P 500 constituent equally deeply researched or the 469 reused observations fresh. Future weekly passes should target actual holdings (if supplied), proposed purchases, near-miss replacements and material events, with explicit numerator/denominator and provider failures.
- **Scenario comparability:** use a common record with price timestamp, accounting period, source accession, enterprise/equity value, shares and repurchases, normalized starting cash/earnings, annual driver bridge, dividends, terminal multiple, taxes/costs and a no-expansion case. Keep sector-specific operating models. A reverse DCF is a statement of what the price implies under a specified discount rate, not a second independent confirmation of an analyst's forecast. Bear/base/bull probability weights should remain blank until calibrated; do not manufacture a probability-weighted return from three subjective cases.
- **Expected-return shrinkage and joint selection:** worth implementing as a **challenger** once a frozen prospective ledger has enough forecast errors to calibrate confidence. Prior means and shrinkage strengths should be explicit and sensitivity-tested; otherwise an optimizer will maximize forecast noise. Include zero-alpha benchmark, valuation-contraction and correlated-downside scenarios; enforce whole-portfolio index look-through and the 2% direct-name, 5% direct-sector and 3.5% known-issuer planning caps. Compare after dividend withholding and trade costs. Do not equate a higher expected-return spreadsheet field with proven expected return.
- **Quarter/entity checks:** v012 independently found the automated quarter-label check narrower than its prose claim: 13 checkable, seven skipped; it matched target revenue rows for only seven holdings. Extend to fiscal-quarter start/end, annual-minus-nine-month Q4, units, entity scope, earnings/cash flow and source accession. A silent check is not full validation.
- **Current price availability:** the root market audit has identified conflicting September 30 stock-history/header observations, including an impossible close below the listed intraday low for AMP, and Cboe throttling. This note makes **no October 1 quote or executable-entry claim**. Preserve provider payloads and market timestamps, mark unresolved identities, and hold affected new buys pending reconciliation; a price-only limit crossing never authorizes a trade.

Refuse a recommendation whose decision-critical data have unresolved source/period identity.
3. Rebuild corrected PIT signals from truncated raw inputs for several historical cutoffs; publish old/new stock-month share choices, ratio ranks, eligible universe, trade list and CAGR with cost/survivorship sensitivity. Record remaining delisted-price gaps, never fill them with today's survivors. Retire the defective B2 covariance from selection. Reconcile financial book, shares and buyback cash in a new engine before using P/B scenarios.
4. Build a prospective, append-only forecast ledger as of each publication: bear/base/bull **assumptions** without pseudo-probabilities; 1-, 3- and 5-year price/earnings/cash-flow expectations; valuation bridge and tax/cost-adjusted buy ceiling; thesis-break conditions; eventual realized observations. Benchmark forecasts against the index and simple sector prior before claiming alpha.
5. Test several **challenger** portfolios under the same total-capital risk budget, data cutoff and assumptions. Report membership stability, return sensitivity, factor/sector and index overlap, concentration, crisis paths, trading/tax drag and an explicit no-trade option. Promote a challenger only when its improvement survives reasonable changes in forecasts, covariance and costs. AI audit scores are quality gates, not a return objective.

## New GPT implementation and focused verification

`methodology_v2.py` implements causal split-basis repair, a scaled-identity covariance estimator with the sample-length correction, and explicit approved-verdict precedence. It does not import or overwrite the old engines. `test_methodology_v2.py` runs six tests against SHA-pinned archived-derived gzip CSV fixtures and synthetic prefix/lineage cases; **all six passed** on 1 October 2026 using the bundled Python 3.12 runtime without pyarrow. `run_portable_tests.ps1` also passed end to end. `fixtures/manifest.json` records the original Parquet hashes and portable fixture hashes; `README_IMPLEMENTATION.md` gives the exact environment and commands. `validate_methodology_v2.py` writes `methodology_v2_validation.json` with the exact archived-input counts and covariance estimates. The default share repair rejects the 16 future-dated share observations; split-choice changes remain 90, with 78 in the scored headline period. The new verdict selector requires an explicit approved chain, source digest and dated market/filing cutoffs; it refuses parallel approved opinions. Historical FRT summaries do not contain all this approval metadata and therefore must be migrated through a reviewed manifest before use.

The implementation is a tested **primitive**, not a new factor strategy. In particular, no complete B1 signal reconstruction, stock rank comparison, trade replay, transaction-cost recalculation or CAGR delta has been run. It would be misleading to replace the old backtest headline with a new number until that work and delisted-price coverage are completed.
