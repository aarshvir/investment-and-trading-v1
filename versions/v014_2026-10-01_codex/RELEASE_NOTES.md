## Validation and methodological limits

The model checks 20 unique direct-stock tickers, exactly 100.00% target weights, no nonstock instrument or strategic reserve, max 8% issuer, max 25% GICS sector, and $50k/$100k dollars summing to the mandate. Every final name has a dated Sep30 USD close cross-checked with a second public feed, raw quote hashes and a separate Oct1 delayed ticker/session check. The three replacement filings were retrieved and archived as original HTML with size/hash/period locators. The independent risk script preserves raw adjusted Yahoo price responses and hashes; it reruns six historical episodes with the original draft, chosen triple replacement, the frozen v011 slate and SPY comparator. PG/PEP/PEG owner-cash bridges and three-year terminal-multiple sensitivity were independently built from primary cash-flow statements. Do not treat several agents reading a filing as independent financial sources.

The chosen stock-only basket's six-episode endpoint losses for GFC, 2011, 2015–16, Q4 2018, COVID and 2022 are −41.81%, −16.25%, −7.37%, −18.03%, −35.51% and −20.80%, respectively. Peak-to-trough GFC/COVID/2022 paths are −44.08%/−35.51%/−21.03%. The first draft was −53.06%/−38.74%/−21.82% peak-to-trough. These are **current-survivor, quarterly-rebalanced hindsight diagnostics**, with missing early histories (13% GDDY/VEEV/ALLE) and no investor-specific withholding, costs or account turnover. The SPY-filled sensitivity is a simulation aid, never an ETF target. 753 common trading days ending Sep25 show 12.99% sample annualized volatility and 0.645 beta to SPY for the chosen slate, not a maximum-loss estimate. The swaps were chosen after observing history and can overfit.

The broader archived approximately 503-name price universe was **not refreshed** in full for this correction; 90 names have Sep30 parent prices, 23 names were considered in the stock-only price challenge and the final 20 have dual-feed Sep30 identities. Full current primary re-underwriting covers the three replacement names and the decision-critical eight-company v013 set; most remaining final company theses retain dated Sep25 dossiers with a narrower Oct1 event/price check. The original 214-name quant selection, 336 dossiers and complete backtest were not revalidated here. A workbook and HTML dashboard reproduce the target and gates without altering the prior 29-tab workbook or convenience reports. Unknown actual holdings, tax residence, cost basis, full trading/FX fees and forthcoming earnings prevent personalized sell instructions or an executable order sheet. Future weekly reviews must validate changed filings/quotes and receive a new version even if no portfolio change is justified.
