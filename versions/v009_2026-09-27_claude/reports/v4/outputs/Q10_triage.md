## Notable findings
- **NSC / UNP**: confirmed via STB.gov that the Union Pacific–Norfolk Southern merger is still under active, contested regulatory review (procedural schedule adopted, summary-denial motions rejected as of 18 Sep 2026) — not yet approved. Both should be treated as a merger-arbitrage situation rather than standard fundamental diligence; neither advanced.
- **HON**: confirmed the Aerospace spin-off completed 29 Jun 2026, leaving "Honeywell Technologies" as a pure-play automation company — explains the odd trailing P/E and negative trailing EPS growth in the data card.
- **DD**: confirmed the Qnity Electronics spin-off (Nov 2025) — trailing financials are distorted by separation one-offs; recommend a second look once standalone quarters accumulate.
- **ADBE**: confirmed AI-disruption sell-off (down ~30-40% from 2025 peak on competition from AI-native design tools) alongside strong Firefly ARR growth — advanced as a likely overreaction given still-double-digit revenue growth at 8.7x NTM P/E.
