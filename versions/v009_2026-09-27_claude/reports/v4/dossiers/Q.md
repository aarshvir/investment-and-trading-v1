# Qnity Electronics, Inc. (NYSE: Q) — Diligence Dossier

**Agent:** F78 | **As of:** 2026-09-25 close | **Depth:** Standard

## 1. Verdict

**WATCH**, thesis horizon 12–24 months. Reason: a real, evidenced business (revenue +18–22% YoY, Adjusted EBITDA growing faster, guidance raised twice in its first two standalone quarters) sitting at a valuation that requires ~17%/yr free-cash-flow growth for a decade to be justified — well above this analyst's own base case (~12%/yr) for a materials supplier exposed to the historically cyclical semiconductor-capex cycle. Add a brand-new (~10-month) standalone track record, GAAP net income falling in both quarters since the spin-off (on new leverage), and an as-yet-unrated/thinly-covered credit, and the combination argues for full diligence to keep tracking, not for buying at full or half weight yet.

## 2. Business in plain English

Qnity Electronics is DuPont's former Electronics & Industrial segment, spun off as an independent public company on 1-Nov-2025. It makes the specialty **materials** semiconductor fabs consume to build chips — photoresists, CMP (chemical-mechanical planarization) slurries and pads, and other process chemistries (**Semiconductor Technologies** segment) — plus flexible circuit materials, thermal-management and connector/interconnect products used in advanced packaging and electronic assemblies (**Interconnect Solutions** segment). Its products are "qualified into" a customer's manufacturing process (a multi-quarter approval process), which is the source of its switching-cost moat: once a fab qualifies a given photoresist or slurry chemistry for a process node, changing suppliers requires re-qualifying the whole line. It sells into the same AI/HPC-driven capex upcycle as the semicap-equipment names (AMAT, KLAC, LRCX) the triage also flagged, but as a **consumable materials** supplier rather than a capital-equipment maker.

## 6. Earnings quality & balance sheet

- **New debt from the spin:** long-term debt was **$0** at 31-Dec-2024 (as part of DuPont) and **$4.0bn** at 31-Dec-2025 and since (spin-related notes issued around the Nov-2025 separation). Interest expense (`InterestAndDebtExpense`) went from $0 in pro forma 2025 quarters to **$61m in both Q1'26 and Q2'26** (~$244m annualised — roughly a 6.1% effective rate on the $4.0bn), which is the primary driver of the GAAP EPS decline in §4.
- **Cash:** $961m at 30-Jun-2026 (up from $166m at 31-Dec-2024, pre-spin) — a large post-spin cash cushion, partly from the spin-off's own cash allocation and partly from FCF retained since.
- **Net debt/EBITDA:** net debt ≈ $3.04bn (30-Jun-2026). Against trailing 4-quarter Adjusted Operating EBITDA (Q3'25 pro forma + Q4'25 349 + Q1'26 411 + Q2'26 431 ≈ $1.5–1.55bn, Q3'25 figure interpolated as it was not independently pulled) ≈ **~2.0x**; against the FY2026 guide-midpoint Adjusted EBITDA (~$1.70bn) ≈ **~1.8x**. The triage's cited "2.3x" is in the same neighbourhood; the small difference is which EBITDA base (trailing vs. forward guide) is used — flagged as a reconciled, not conflicting, data point. Either way, leverage is moderate for an industrial spin but the credit is new and thinly covered (triage notes only ~8 sell-side analysts), so there is limited external validation of the capital structure's durability through a cycle.
- **Adjusted vs. GAAP gap:** Adjusted Earnings were **up 33% (Q1'26) and 53% (Q2'26)** YoY even as GAAP net income fell 19–31% — a large and widening GAAP/adjusted gap. Management's add-backs (per the releases) are chiefly the pro forma interest-expense/standalone-cost normalisation for the comparative period **and** items in the current period the Company treats as non-recurring (integration/separation costs, intangible-amortisation from spin-related basis step-up). This dossier has **not** independently re-derived the full non-GAAP reconciliation table line-by-line within the time budget — flagged as a limitation; the direction (adjusted diverging positively from GAAP) is itself the fact worth monitoring, regardless of which side is "more right."
- **Stockholders' equity** fell from $10.64bn (31-Dec-2024, as part of DuPont) to $7.10–7.26bn post-spin — consistent with a leveraged spin-off (debt-funded distribution back to former parent/shareholders is typical in these structures) rather than any operating loss of equity value.

## 8. Bull case / bear case

**Bull:**
1. Two consecutive quarters of guidance raised across every metric (sales, EBITDA, EPS, FCF) is a strong, evidenced execution signal for a company only ~10 months old as a standalone reporter.
2. Both segments (Semiconductor Technologies +15.5% and Interconnect Solutions +30.2% YoY in Q2'26) are growing double digits, and the qualified-into-the-process nature of photoresists/slurries/CMP consumables gives genuine switching-cost protection once a fab has adopted a given chemistry.
3. The AI/HPC-driven "shrink and stack" trend management describes (more process layers per chip = more materials intensity per wafer) is a real, structural tailwind specific to advanced packaging, not just a generic semicap-cycle call.

**Bear:**
1. GAAP net income has fallen YoY in **both** quarters since the spin (−19%, then −31%), and while this dossier attributes it chiefly to new interest expense, a newly-listed, thinly-covered (~8 analysts), leveraged (~1.8–2.0x net debt/EBITDA) credit with under a year of standalone filings is inherently harder to underwrite with confidence than an established issuer.
2. The valuation (27.7x forward P/E, 17.1x forward EV/EBITDA, and a reverse-DCF-implied ~17%/yr decade-long FCF growth requirement) is priced for the current AI-capex upcycle to persist without the cyclicality that has historically characterised semiconductor capital spending (a risk the triage separately flags for the semicap-equipment names AMAT/KLAC/LRCX/STX/WDC — the same macro driver applies here).
3. Standalone track record is ~10 months; there is no independently-verified evidence yet of how the business performs through a downcycle, a refinancing, or a period without guidance raises.

## 9. Key risks & kill criteria (measurable signposts that would break this thesis)

1. Net debt/trailing-4-quarter Adjusted Operating EBITDA rises above **3.0x** (from ~1.8–2.0x currently).
2. FY2026 guidance (raised twice: Adj. EBITDA to $1.675–1.725bn, Adj. EPS to $4.40–4.60) is **cut** in a future release — a reversal of the current two-for-two raise streak.
3. GAAP net income declines YoY for a **third** consecutive standalone quarter (two consecutive already: Q1'26 −19%, Q2'26 −31%).
4. Semiconductor Technologies or Interconnect Solutions segment revenue growth decelerates below **10% YoY** for two consecutive quarters (from the current 15–30% range) — a proxy for the AI-capex cycle turning.
5. A major customer publicly cuts capex/capacity guidance materially, given Qnity's short standalone credit history and thin (~8-analyst) sell-side coverage.
