# LOW — Lowe's Companies, Inc. (Diligence dossier, agent F69, standard depth)

## 1. Verdict
**INCLUDE-SMALL** (24–36 month horizon), with a specific reservation: comparable sales growth has **decelerated for three straight quarters** (+1.3% → +0.6% → +0.2%) and fiscal-2026 guidance has been trimmed to the **low end of every metric** twice in six months, even as headline sales growth (+8–10% YoY the last two quarters) is now mostly acquisition-driven (Foundation Building Materials, Artisan Design Group), not organic. The #2 U.S. home-improvement retailer is priced for very little growth — a real margin of safety — but momentum is fading, not building, into a still-pressured DIY/housing macro.

## 2. Business in plain English
Lowe's operates ~1,760 big-box home-improvement stores in the U.S., selling building materials, appliances, tools and home décor to both do-it-yourself (DIY) consumers and professional contractors (Pro). It competes head-to-head with Home Depot. Its "Total Home" strategy is shifting the mix toward Pro, online and installed/home-services revenue (all growing) to offset a structurally weaker DIY segment tied to existing-home-sales activity and discretionary renovation spend, which has been depressed by high mortgage rates for several years.

## 6. Earnings quality & balance sheet
**Entity scope: Lowe's Companies, Inc. consolidated, from the 10-Q for the quarter ended 2026-07-31 (filed 2026-08-27) and FY2025 10-K (period ended 2026-01-30, filed 2026-03-23).**
- **FCF conversion (fiscal year ended 2026-01-30):** OCF $9,864M vs. prior fiscal year $9,625M (+2.5%); capex $2,213M vs. $1,927M; **FCF ≈ $7,651M vs. $7,698M prior year (roughly flat, -0.6%)** — i.e., despite +3.1% sales growth and continued profitability, free cash flow did not grow.
- **Capital allocation shift:** share repurchases fell to **$211M in the fiscal year ended 2026-01-30, from $4,053M the prior year** — buybacks were paused to help fund the FBM acquisition. Net income $6,654M vs. $6,957M prior year (-4.4%).
- **Stockholders' equity is deeply negative** (a long-standing structural feature from years of aggressive buybacks, not a distress signal): **-$9,917M at 2026-01-30**, improving to **-$9,270M at 2026-05-01** and **-$7,437M at 2026-07-31** as buybacks stayed paused. This is consolidated GAAP equity per the 10-Q/10-K balance sheet.
- **Total debt (consolidated, incl. finance leases, 2026-07-31 10-Q):** $35,204M (noncurrent) + $2,352M (current) = **$37,556M**; cash $3,172M; **net debt ≈ $34.4bn**. Using guided FY2026 operating income (~11.2% of $92.0bn ≈ $10.3bn) plus an estimated ~$2.2bn D&A, **estimated net debt/EBITDA ≈ 2.7–2.8x** (approximate; a true trailing EBITDA figure was not directly disclosed in the sources reviewed — flagged as an estimate, not a filed ratio).

## 8. Bull case
1. Pro/online/home-services growth is real and multi-quarter: online sales +15.5% (Q1 FY26) and +15.7% (Q2 FY26) YoY; this is the fifth consecutive quarter of positive comparable sales, even if the magnitude is now very small.
2. Reverse DCF implies the market is pricing in almost no growth (~2.3%/yr) — even a modest, evidence-based base case (Pro mix shift + eventual housing-turnover recovery) clears that bar comfortably.
3. Balance sheet, while nominally "negative equity," reflects decades of shareholder-friendly capital return from a durable, profitable business (FCF ≈ $7.65bn in the year just ended) rather than operating losses — management has now demonstrated the discipline to pause buybacks (from $4.05bn to $0.21bn) to delever after a large acquisition, rather than over-leveraging further.

## 9. Bear case
1. Comparable sales growth has decelerated in three straight quarters (+1.3% → +0.6% → +0.2%), and FY2026 guidance has been trimmed to the low end of every metric twice in six months — the "positive comp streak" the company emphasizes is arithmetically true but directionally weakening.
2. Reported headline sales growth (+8–10% YoY, last two quarters) is now substantially acquisition-driven (FBM/ADG), masking much weaker underlying organic performance — investors reading only the headline sales-growth number would overestimate organic momentum.
3. Net debt (~$34.4bn) rose materially to fund the FBM deal at a rich 13.4x EBITDA multiple; if DIY/housing stays weak for longer, deleveraging (and the buyback resumption bulls are counting on) could be delayed well beyond current expectations.

## 10. Key risks & kill criteria (measurable — what would break this thesis / invalidation triggers)
1. Comparable sales turn negative (below 0.0%) for two consecutive quarters (trend has already gone +1.3%→+0.6%→+0.2%).
2. FY2026 adjusted diluted EPS guidance is cut below the current $12.25 (already the low end of the original $12.25–12.75 range) at any subsequent quarterly release.
3. Estimated net debt/EBITDA (consolidated) rises above 3.5x (from an estimated ~2.7–2.8x currently).
4. Share repurchases remain at or near zero ($211M FY2025 run-rate) for four more consecutive quarters while GAAP stockholders' equity fails to improve from the 2026-07-31 level of -$7.4bn (deleveraging stalling rather than progressing).
5. Free cash flow (OCF − capex) for fiscal year 2026 (ending ~2027-01-29) comes in below fiscal 2025's ~$7.65bn, i.e., the multi-year FCF plateau turns into an outright decline.
