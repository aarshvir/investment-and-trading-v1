# BDX — Becton, Dickinson and Company — Diligence Dossier (Agent F73, standard depth)

## 1. Verdict
**INCLUDE-SMALL** (half weight). One-sentence reason: a genuinely durable, diversified med-tech franchise now trading
as standalone "New BD" post spin-off, with guidance raised in both quarters since the separation and cash generation
ahead of plan — tempered by a live, unresolved FDA quality-system problem (repeat warning letters, potential
ChloraPrep recall) and thin quality-of-earnings visibility in only two clean post-separation quarters.
Thesis horizon: 24–36 months (let the standalone story re-rate once BD Excellence margin gains and the FDA remediation
are proven, not assumed).

## 2. Business in plain English
BD makes the disposable and durable hardware hospitals and clinics use every day — needles and syringes, IV catheters
and infusion pumps, medication-dispensing cabinets (Pyxis), patient monitors, and devices used in surgery, urology and
interventional care, plus injection systems supplied to pharma/biotech customers (BioPharma Systems) for drugs like
GLP-1s. It sells mostly to hospitals, ambulatory care and pharma manufacturers on razor/razor-blade economics (placed
equipment, recurring consumables). Competitive position: #1 or #2 share in most of its categories (vascular access,
infusion, specimen management, pharmaceutical delivery systems), which is also why its FDA quality-system record
matters — its products touch nearly every hospital bed in the U.S.

## 6. Earnings quality & balance sheet
**Entity scope: BDX consolidated (continuing operations), balance sheet as of June 30, 2026, per 10-Q filed
2026-08-06.**
- **GAAP vs. adjusted gap, and why:** Q2 FY26 GAAP net loss of $(311)M / $(1.11) diluted EPS vs. adjusted diluted EPS
  of $2.90 for the same quarter. The gap is driven by: (a) a **$450 million non-cash asset impairment charge**
  (Property, Plant & Equipment $238M + Developed Technology intangibles $134M + other, across all reportable segments)
  recorded in the "Excellence Unleashed" cost-efficiency/restructuring program, upon BD's "commitment to exit certain
  operational activities and projects"; (b) a **$(274)M net-of-tax loss from discontinued operations** tied to
  completing the Waters combination on Feb 9, 2026; (c) ongoing separation/integration/transaction costs and product
  liability charges ($52M pre-tax legal/product charges in the quarter). All are labelled non-recurring/non-cash by
  the company and excluded from adjusted EPS — this is a legitimate GAAP/adjusted split (not aggressive add-backs of
  recurring costs), but it does mean **two of BDX's four post-spin quarters carry large one-time GAAP noise**, so the
  trailing-twelve-month GAAP earnings series is not yet a clean read on run-rate profitability. [10-Q filed
  2026-05-07, Notes 10/11/13; income statement]
- **FCF conversion and normalisation (important entity-scope flag):** Vendor-aggregated (Yahoo/d4) TTM free cash flow
  of $4.48B mixes pre-spin (consolidated, including the now-divested Biosciences segment) and post-spin quarters and
  should **not** be used as New BD's run-rate. The cleaner figure is the 9-month **continuing-operations** cash flow
  from the Q3 FY26 10-Q: operating cash flow (continuing ops) of **$2,104M** for Oct 2025–Jun 2026, up 33.3% YoY from
  $1,578M in the comparable prior-year period, and capex of $376M (vs. $383M), giving 9-month FCF of **$1,728M**, up
  44.6% YoY — both figures stated by the company itself in the Q3 release headline. Annualising the 9-month run-rate
  gives an approximate **New-BD FCF run-rate of ~$2.2–2.3B**, which is the number used in §7's reverse DCF (not the
  $4.48B TTM aggregator figure). FCF/NI is not meaningful this year given the GAAP net-loss quarter; on a
  continuing-ops-adjusted basis cash conversion looks healthy (OCF up faster than adjusted EPS).
- **Balance sheet, consolidated:** cash $708M; long-term debt (noncurrent) fell from $16,916M (Dec 31, 2025) to
  $13,511M (Jun 30, 2026) — BD retired **$2.1 billion of debt** in Q2 FY26 alone, funded from separation-related cash
  proceeds, while also executing a **$2.0 billion accelerated share repurchase**. Stockholders' equity $24,416M;
  goodwill $25,944M (exceeds equity — a legacy of the Bard/CareFusion/C.R. Bard-era acquisition history, now normal
  for BD's business mix but a reason GAAP book value understates cash-generative capacity less than it overstates
  downside protection in a real impairment scenario). Net debt/EBITDA (TTM GAAP EBITDA ~$6.1B per d4) ≈ 2.1x —
  moderate, and improving post-spin deleveraging. [10-Q filed 2026-08-06, balance sheet and debt footnote;
  8-K Ex-99.1 filed 2026-05-07]
- **Share count:** reduced via the $2.0B ASR (Q2 FY26); dilution from SBC not separately quantified this cycle but
  historically modest (~1% of revenue).
- **No pending M&A.** No net-new acquisitions in the period; the only "deal" is the completed spin-off/RMT.

## 7. Valuation snapshot and reverse DCF
No V1 systematic valuation row exists for BDX (`outputs/v1_valuation_table.csv` and `v1_valuation.json` have no BDX
entry) — **v1_verdict is null**, consistent with BDX being a triage-advanced (not top-70) name not yet run through V1.
- NTM P/E (d4 snapshot, 2026-09-25): **13.8x** (pe_ntm 13.84, pe_fy1 13.83) vs. FY26 adjusted-EPS guidance midpoint
  ~$12.67 and price $183.82. FCF yield ~8.9% on the (overstated) trailing FCF figure; on the normalised ~$2.2–2.3B New
  BD FCF run-rate, FCF yield is closer to **4.3–4.6%** of the $50.1B market cap — a more honest number, and materially
  less "cheap" than the headline aggregator FCF yield implies.
- Historical/peer context (qualitative — no D2/D3 own-history series exists yet for "New BD" as a distinct entity
  since it only started trading in this form Feb 2026): pre-spin BDX historically traded 15–20x forward EPS; med-tech
  large-cap peers (Zimmer Biomet, Stryker, Boston Scientific, Stryker/Becton peer set) trade roughly 14–22x forward
  earnings depending on growth profile. At 13.8x, New BD is priced at a discount to the peer group, which is
  consistent with (a) genuine post-spin index/investor-base churn and thin trading history, and (b) the market
  pricing in the unresolved FDA quality-system risk (§11).
- **Reverse DCF (own build, 10-year explicit + Gordon terminal, WACC 7.5–8.0%, terminal growth 3.0%):** using the
  normalised New-BD FCF run-rate (~$2.3B) against the current enterprise value (~$64.9B), the market is pricing in a
  **10-year FCF/earnings CAGR of roughly 5.6–7.0%**. That is **in line with, or modestly below**, BD's own algorithm
  (low-single-digit-plus revenue growth + BD Excellence margin expansion + buybacks, historically framed as a
  high-single-digit adjusted-EPS growth algorithm). Using the overstated $4.48B TTM FCF figure instead would imply a
  *negative* required growth rate (an artefact of the transition-year cash flow being inflated by non-recurring items,
  not a genuine "priced for decline" signal) — flagging this as a **data-quality caveat for any downstream valuation
  agent that ingests Yahoo/d4 FCF for BDX without adjusting for the spin.**
- Verdict on implied growth: **implied_vs_base = in_line** (base case ≈ 6–7% FCF/EPS CAGR, which is what the reverse
  DCF requires at the low end of the WACC range, and modestly above it at the high end).

## 8. Bull case and bear case
**Bull (3 points):**
1. BD Excellence cost program continues to expand adjusted operating margin (10.5%→13.3%→15.6%+ quarterly progression
   in FY26 even through a loss-making impairment quarter suggests underlying margin trajectory is intact).
2. Standalone "New BD" re-rates toward peer med-tech multiples (16–20x) as trading history lengthens and index
   inclusion/ownership normalises post-spin, without needing any upgrade to the growth outlook.
3. BioPharma Systems (prefillable syringes/injection pens for GLP-1s and biologics) is a genuine structural
   volume-growth tailwind tied to obesity-drug demand, largely outside BD's control but a real multi-year tailwind.

**Bear (3 points):**
1. The FDA quality-system problems are not resolved, not one-off, and touch multiple facilities/product lines (§11) —
   a further Warning Letter, an actual ChloraPrep recall, or a consent-decree expansion would directly cut into the
   Medical Essentials/Connected Care franchise the model likes.
2. Only two clean post-spin quarters exist; "New BD" stand-alone cost allocation, TSA income roll-off (as corporate
   overhead currently borne by Waters via TSA unwinds), and dis-synergies are not yet stress-tested by a full fiscal
   year.
3. Hernia-mesh and other product-liability litigation (~7,000 pending claims even after the FY24 settlement) is a
   recurring cash drag ($52–63M pre-tax legal charges in a single quarter) that could re-escalate.

## 9. Key risks & kill criteria (measurable)
1. A new FDA Warning Letter, Form 483, or consent-decree expansion covering any BD manufacturing site within the next
   4 quarters.
2. Full-year FY26 adjusted diluted EPS guidance is **cut** (vs. the current $12.62–$12.72 range) at any future
   quarterly release.
3. Organic/reported revenue growth falls below the "low single-digit" floor of guidance for two consecutive quarters.
4. Net debt/EBITDA (consolidated) rises above 3.0x (from ~2.1x currently), signalling debt-funded buybacks outrunning
   deleveraging.
5. Any voluntary or FDA-mandated recall of a top-5-revenue product line (e.g., BD Alaris, Pyxis, ChloraPrep, or a
   BioPharma Systems platform).

## 11. Red-flag scan
- **FDA quality-system issues, ongoing and multi-site:** CareFusion 303 (Pyxis dispensing) received a Form 483 in May
  2024 and a Warning Letter in November 2024 (San Diego facility, Dispensing quality-management system); BD is also
  operating under a pre-existing Infusion-systems Consent Decree (Alaris pumps). Separately, an **El Paso Warning
  Letter** (drug/combination products) is unresolved as of the Q2 FY26 10-Q, with the company stating potential
  outcomes could include "additional corrections and corrective actions, including potential product recalls"; BD
  has **voluntarily** paused/adjusted its ChloraPrep product line pending resolution as of May 6, 2026. This directly
  confirms and sharpens the triage's "history of FDA warning letters" flag — it is not purely historical, it is live.
  [10-Q filed 2026-05-07, Legal Proceedings/Regulatory Matters notes]
- **Litigation:** ~6,995 pending hernia-mesh product-liability claims as of March 31, 2026 (down slightly from 6,905
  at Sep 30, 2025 — note the count is not obviously declining despite the FY24 settlement, since new claims continue
  to be filed); other mass-tort matters referenced but not separately quantified in the extract reviewed.
- **Auditor/accounting:** no restatements, no material-weakness or going-concern language found in the reviewed 10-Qs.
- **Insider selling / Form 4 pattern:** not reviewed in this pass (time-boxed) — flagged as a gap, not a finding.
- **No pending M&A** beyond the completed Waters spin-off/combination.
