# Align Technology, Inc. (ALGN) — Diligence Dossier

**Agent:** F23 | **As of:** 2026-09-25 close ($146.78) | **Depth:** Standard

## 1. Verdict
**INCLUDE-SMALL** (12–36 month horizon). Named reservation: the stock is genuinely cheap on its own 14-year
valuation history, but that trough partly reflects real, disclosed growth deceleration (FY26 guided revenue
growth of just 3–4%, likely the slowest since IPO) and recurring GAAP one-off charges; size small pending
confirmation that the FY27 margin-recovery commitment is met.

## 2. Business in plain English
Align makes Invisalign clear aligners (its core, ~82% of revenue) and iTero intraoral scanners plus exocad
CAD/CAM software, selling to orthodontists, GP dentists and increasingly DSOs (dental service
organizations) worldwide. It earns money per case shipped (aligners) and per scanner/software seat, and is
the clear share leader in clear aligners globally, though it now competes with lower-cost aligner entrants
and Straumann/others in scanners.

## 3. Why the model likes it — durable or artefact?
Triage (Q07) called it "the standout price-vs-growth setup in this batch" on ">100% implied forward EPS
growth" against a 12x NTM P/E. Verified: the ">100%" figure is a base-year artifact, not real operating
momentum — Q2'25 EPS ($1.72) vs Q2'26 EPS ($1.51) is actually *down* 12% YoY, and Q3'25 EPS ($0.78) was the
weakest print in two years. The apparent explosive forward growth comes from comparing to a temporarily
depressed base (Q3'25/Q4'25 carried heavy restructuring, impairment and accelerated-depreciation charges).
The 12x NTM P/E being at the ~1st percentile of ALGN's own 14-year history is real and confirmed (V1), but
it is priced that way for a disclosed reason (below), not an oversight.

## 5. Guidance track record (last 4 releases, quoted verbatim from each release)
- **3Q25 release (Oct 2025) → guided Q4'25** revenue "$1,025M to $1,045M, up sequentially"; **actual $1,047.6M
  — beat the high end.** FY2025 revenue guided "flat to slightly up from 2024"; actual FY25 $4,034.96M vs
  FY24 $3,999.01M = +0.9%, in line.
- **4Q25/FY25 release (Feb 2026) → guided Q1'26** revenue "$1,010M to $1,030M, up 3–5% YoY"; **actual
  $1,040.1M — beat the high end.** Guided Q1'26 GAAP op margin "12.4%–12.8%"; actual 13.6% — beat.
  Guided **FY2026 GAAP operating margin "slightly below 18.0%"** (+~400bps YoY) and non-GAAP "~23.7%."
- **1Q26 release (Apr 2026) → guided Q2'26** revenue "$1,040M to $1,060M, up 3–5%"; actual $1,056.2M, within
  range. **Reaffirmed** the FY26 GAAP op-margin guide of "slightly below 18.0%."
- **2Q26 release (Jul 2026) → guided Q3'26** revenue "$1,000M to $1,020M" (a *sequential decline*, guided
  seasonality) and **cut FY2026 GAAP operating-margin guidance to "15.1%–15.6%"** — roughly 250–290bps below
  the "slightly below 18.0%" guided just one quarter earlier — because of newly disclosed one-time charges:
  a $37.5M UK VAT contingent liability (below), ~$30.6M of Q1'26 legal settlements, and further restructuring/
  accelerated depreciation, now totaling an expected $90–110M for FY26. **Non-GAAP FY26 operating-margin
  guidance was left unchanged at ~23.7%.** This is the single most important adverse fact in this dossier:
  revenue guidance has not moved (3–4% growth reaffirmed all year and beaten on the top line every quarter),
  but the *GAAP* profitability guide was cut materially inside two quarters — an increase in one-off cash
  costs, not a demand problem. Treat any "GAAP margin beat/miss" narrative with that context.

## 6. Earnings quality & balance sheet
- **UK VAT dispute:** clear aligners were ruled VAT-exempt by the UK First-tier Tribunal (2025); ALGN
  stopped charging UK VAT from Aug 2025. On 2026-07-07 the Upper Tribunal **overturned** that ruling. ALGN
  recorded a **$37.5M estimated liability** (inclusive of interest) in Q2'26 and will charge 20% VAT on UK
  invoices from 2026-09-07; it intends to appeal, with the outcome "subject to uncertainty" (its own words).
- **Tariffs:** subject to Section 122 (Trade Act of 1974) tariffs after the Supreme Court ruled the prior
  IEEPA tariffs unlawful (2026-02-20); the Section 122 tariffs are themselves under appeal at the Court of
  International Trade. Company states it does not expect a material impact currently — a live, disclosed,
  unresolved policy risk rather than a settled cost.
- **Balance sheet:** no debt on the balance sheet; cash $1,102.6M (2Q26) vs $1,094.9M (FY25 year-end); total
  liabilities are dominated by deferred revenue ($1,187.2M) and accrued liabilities, not borrowings — a
  genuinely strong, low-risk balance sheet.
- **Capital return:** $1.0bn buyback program (announced Apr 2025); $733.3M remaining as of 2026-06-30;
  guided to repurchase $400–500M in 2026. Diluted share count down from 72.59M (2Q25) to 71.52M (2Q26), -1.5%.
- **FCF/SBC:** H1'26 operating cash flow $343.8M vs $181.3M H1'25 (+90%); FY26 capex guided $125–150M. SBC
  ~4.3% of revenue (2Q26), stable.

## 7. Valuation — reconciled with V1
V1 (systematic) calls ALGN **"attractive"** (score 0.48), citing NTM P/E of 12.0x at the **1.2th percentile**
of its own 171-month (14-year) history. **I agree the multiple is genuinely at a generational low** for this
name — that fact is confirmed and I do not dispute it.
However, V1's *base-case reverse DCF* return of **+59.65%/yr (3yr)** is not credible and I overturn it: it
requires revenue growth of 15.6%/yr (4x the company's own guided 3–4% for FY26) *and* an exit multiple of
39.4x (up from today's 12.0x) simultaneously. V1's own sanity check flags this: the base-case 3-year price
target ($597) exceeds even the Street's current 12-month **high** target ($240, mean $208.60) by 2.5x. I
attribute this to V1's reverse-DCF using a TTM EBIT margin (13.2%) that is itself depressed by the 3Q25/4Q25
one-offs (§4) — a low earnings base plus an 11.2% WACC forces the model to assume unrealistic growth just to
justify today's EV, which is not the same as the market actually pricing that growth in.
My own base case: revenue growth 4–6%/yr (current guide plus modest recovery as one-offs roll off and
management's own "at least ~100bps" FY27 margin-improvement commitment is met), GAAP operating margin
recovering toward 16–17% by FY27, and the NTM P/E re-rating partially — to 15–17x, not V1's 39x — as growth
stabilizes. **valuation_view_vs_v1: dossier_view = fair (in_line with my base case), consistent = false**
(I reject V1's base-case return and exit multiple, not its multiple-percentile finding).

## 8. Bull case / Bear case
**Bull:** (1) Clear Aligner volume +7.4% YoY in 2Q26 on double-digit international/DSO growth, with revenue
beating guidance every quarter this year; (2) net-cash balance sheet funds continued buybacks (-1.5% share
count YoY) regardless of near-term margin noise; (3) management has explicitly committed to further ~100bps
non-GAAP margin improvement in FY27 on top of FY26's, a specific, checkable promise.
**Bear:** (1) FY26 revenue-growth guidance (3–4%) is the softest in the company's history as a public
company, and Systems & Services revenue is guided down 6–8% YoY on a structural mix shift to lower-price/
leased scanners; (2) GAAP operating-margin guidance was already cut once this year (§5) and the UK VAT
appeal and Section 122 tariff litigation are both unresolved, open-ended cost risks; (3) the stock's beta
(~1.5–1.7) means any confirmation that growth deceleration is structural (not cyclical) could re-rate the
multiple down further even from today's 14-year low.

## 9. Kill criteria
1. Quarterly Clear Aligner volume growth turns negative YoY.
2. FY2026 GAAP operating-margin guidance is cut again below the current 15.1%–15.6% range (excluding
   charges already disclosed as of this dossier).
3. Full-year revenue growth guidance is cut below the current 3%–4% range.
4. Net cash (cash − debt) falls below $500M.
5. Cumulative UK VAT / Section 122 tariff cash outflows exceed $150M beyond the $37.5M already reserved.

## 11. Red-flag scan
No going-concern language, material weakness or restatement found in the reviewed 10-Q/10-K/8-Ks. Open
items: UK VAT liability/appeal and Section 122 tariff litigation (both above, both genuinely unresolved);
two restructuring programs in twelve months (4Q24 and 3Q25) — a real, if modest, signal of cost-structure
strain, not a governance red flag. No auditor change noted.
