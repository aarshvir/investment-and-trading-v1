# Rollins, Inc. (NYSE: ROL) — Diligence Dossier
Agent F71 | Standard depth | As of 2026-09-25 close ($30.03/share, mkt cap ≈ $14.46bn, consolidated)

## 1. Verdict
**WATCH** — 12–24 month horizon. Rollins is a genuinely high-quality, recurring-revenue pest-control roll-up with a
very strong balance sheet, but its own management confirmed in the Q2 2026 release that results "fell short of
expectations" on both demand and margin — a real, two-quarter-old deceleration, not merely the "sentiment-driven
de-rating" the triage note hypothesized. The price does not look expensive against a cautious base case, but the
base case itself is now in question until the company shows the demand/margin trend has turned. Re-underwrite at
Q3 2026 earnings (~28-Oct-2026, unconfirmed).

## 2. Business in plain English
Rollins is the parent of Orkin, HomeTeam Pest Defense, Clark Pest Control and many other regional/international
pest-control brands. It sells recurring residential and commercial pest-control, termite and mosquito-control
services, financed by monthly/quarterly service contracts, and grows partly organically and partly by acquiring
small, local pest-control operators ("tuck-ins") at attractive multiples and integrating them onto its national
platform. Its moat is route density, brand trust (Orkin) and a fragmented industry that keeps supplying accretive
tuck-in targets.

## 5. Guidance track record
Rollins does not publish formal quarterly numeric revenue/EPS guidance in its earnings releases (no "Outlook"
section found in the Q1 or Q2 2026 releases, or the FY2025 10-K release). What exists instead is management
commentary on trend and a long-stated algorithm (high-single-digit organic growth + ~30–50bps annual margin
expansion, from investor-day materials, not independently re-verified here). Against that qualitative bar:
- **Q1 2026 (22-Apr-2026):** positive tone — "strong acceleration of demand during March... peak season is off to
  a strong start" — organic growth 6.6%, margin down 100–120bps YoY (attributed to reinvestment, not weakness).
- **Q2 2026 (22-Jul-2026):** explicit miss — *"Our second quarter results fell short of our expectations due to
  slower growth in parts of our residential pest control business... lead volume declined in the quarter... margin
  performance was below our expectations"* (CEO Jerry Gahlhoff Jr. and CFO Will Harkins, Ex-99.1 to the 8-K filed
  2026-07-22). Management describes "organizational and operational changes" already underway.
This is a genuine, company-acknowledged shortfall against internal expectations, occurring the same quarter a new
CFO (William Wayne Harkins II) took office (effective around 1-Jul-2026, per Form 4 and 8-K Item 5.02 dated
2026-05-27) — a leadership-transition and demand-softening coincidence worth monitoring, not yet a governance red
flag.

## 8. Bull case / Bear case
**Bull (3 points):**
1. 99 consecutive quarters of revenue growth (per the company's own Q2 2026 release headline) — an exceptionally
   durable, recession-resistant recurring-revenue model.
2. Net debt/EBITDA under 1x gives large, low-risk capacity to keep buying tuck-ins in a still-fragmented industry.
3. Management says lead volume "improved toward the end of June and has maintained this momentum through the first
   few weeks of July" (Q2 2026 release) — an early, not-yet-confirmed sign the Q2 softness was transitory.

**Bear (3 points):**
1. Two consecutive quarters of YoY GAAP operating-margin contraction (−130bps, −110bps), explicitly attributed by
   management to a demand/cost mismatch it did not anticipate — a real forecasting miss, not weather noise.
2. Residential, consumer-initiated demand (search/digital lead generation) is softening even as relationship-based
   channels (home builders, door-to-door) hold up — a genuine end-market signal, not only a company-specific issue.
3. A new CFO started mid-crisis (1-Jul-2026); execution risk during a leadership transition compounds the demand
   problem.

## 9. Key risks & kill criteria (measurable)
1. Consolidated organic revenue growth below 5% for two consecutive quarters (Q2 2026 was 5.7%; per company
   earnings releases, organic growth line).
2. GAAP operating margin down more than 100bps YoY for a **third** consecutive quarter (two already: Q1 −130bps,
   Q2 −110bps; per 8-K Ex-99.1 releases).
3. Net debt/EBITDA exceeding 1.5x (from ≈0.5x currently; per 10-Q balance sheet + TTM operating income).
4. FCF/NI conversion below 80% for two consecutive quarters (TTM currently ≈118%; per 10-Q cash-flow statement).
5. A second CFO or CEO departure within 12 months of Harkins' mid-2026 appointment (per Form 8-K Item 5.02
   filings).
