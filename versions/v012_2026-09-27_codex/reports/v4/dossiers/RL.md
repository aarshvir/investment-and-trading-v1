## 2. Business in plain English
Ralph Lauren designs, markets and distributes premium/luxury apparel, footwear, accessories, home goods and fragrances under the Ralph Lauren, Polo, Double RL, Lauren and Chaps brand tiers, sold through wholesale (department/specialty stores, ~30% of FY26 revenue) and direct-to-consumer (own stores, concessions and digital, ~70%) across North America, Europe and Asia. The strategy of the last several years — "PELOTON"/brand-elevation, reducing promotional depth, raising average unit retail (AUR) — is the single thread running through every metric in this dossier.

## 3. Why the model likes it — durable or artefact?
Prelim rank: **Q 0.829, V 0.509, M 0.672, composite 0.670** — notably, RL's Quality score is the *lowest* of the three names in this batch (still 83rd percentile) and its Value score is barely above the sector median (51st percentile, consistent with its higher absolute multiple). Independently verified: FY26 GAAP operating margin 14.5% (adj. 16.0%, +200bps YoY), FY26 diluted EPS GAAP $15.11/adj. $16.59 (+~30% YoY), Q1 FY27 gross margin 73.7% GAAP (+140bps YoY). **This is durable, not an artefact.** The improvement is broad-based (every region positive, DTC comps up low-double-digits *and* wholesale accelerating to mid-teens in the latest quarter — not one channel cannibalizing the other) and is explicitly attributed by management, quarter after quarter, to AUR growth and mix, i.e., pricing power from brand elevation — the same causal story for eight consecutive quarters, which is exactly what "durable" should look like. The comparatively modest Q score (vs. DECK/TPR) mainly reflects RL's lower absolute ROE-type profile and higher leverage-adjacent metrics inherent to a company that carries real estate/showroom and wholesale receivables exposure — a structural, sector-normal feature, not a warning sign.

## 8. Bull case / Bear case
**Bull:** (1) The most consistent execution of the three names — AUR-led margin expansion for eight straight quarters, broad-based across every region and both DTC and wholesale channels simultaneously (not a zero-sum channel shift). (2) Asia/China is now the fastest-growing and re-accelerating region (Q1 FY27 China "+more than 40%"), the opposite of the China-consumer-weakness narrative affecting some luxury peers. (3) Balance sheet is net-cash, dividend was just raised 10%, and the company has now raised guidance four releases running with no sign of the streak breaking.
**Bear:** (1) The multiple (18x NTM) and 4x-off-lows share price already reflect a lot of this good news — the lowest Value-factor percentile of the batch means less margin for disappointment. (2) Tariff cost is real (explicitly cited as a headwind every quarter) but never quantified — if AUR/mix gains ever stall, the offset mechanism is opaque and un-auditable from public disclosure alone. (3) A greater-than-40%-growth China quarter is a spectacular number that also raises the bar very high for the next comparison and increases single-region concentration risk (geopolitical/tariff-retaliation and China-consumer-cyclicality exposure) precisely where the growth is most concentrated.
**Note on this v3 holdover specifically:** the original v3 thesis for RL is not contradicted by anything found here; if anything the AUR/margin trend has strengthened in the last four quarters relative to what v3 would have seen.

## 9. Key risks & kill criteria
1. Constant-currency revenue growth falls below 5% for two consecutive quarters (from 10–14% currently).
2. Adjusted operating margin expansion reverses (YoY compression) for two consecutive quarters, after 8 straight quarters of expansion.
3. FY2027 guidance is cut at any point (would break the four-release raise streak — the cleanest of the three names).
4. China/Asia growth decelerates below 10% YoY (from the current 22–40% range) — would signal the single largest current growth driver is fading.
5. Any quantified tariff-cost disclosure (if RL ever provides one) showing a materially larger drag than peers' bps-level impact, which would retroactively make the current "offset via AUR" narrative harder to trust.
