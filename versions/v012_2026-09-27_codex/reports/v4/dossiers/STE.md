# STERIS plc (NYSE: STE) — Diligence Dossier
Agent F71 | Standard depth | As of 2026-09-25 close ($208.93/share, mkt cap ≈ $20.45bn, consolidated)

## 1. Verdict
**INCLUDE** — 24–36 month horizon. A high-quality, non-cyclical infection-prevention/sterilization leader
(Healthcare, Applied Sterilization Technologies (AST), Life Sciences segments) delivering broad-based double-digit
segment growth and margin expansion, with FY2027 guidance reiterated (not cut) at its first-quarter update, its
long-running ethylene-oxide (EO) personal-injury litigation substantially settled, and a reverse DCF that implies
less growth than management is currently guiding to. Reservation: valuation is full (≈20x TTM adjusted P/E), so this
is a quality-compounder holding, not a bargain.

## 2. Business in plain English
STERIS sells the equipment, consumables and services hospitals and life-science/pharma manufacturers need to
sterilize surgical instruments and prevent infection: sterilizers, surgical tables and lights, endoscope
reprocessing, and — through its Applied Sterilization Technologies segment — outsourced contract sterilization
(much of it ethylene-oxide-based) for medical-device makers. Revenue is roughly two-thirds high-recurring
consumables/service tied to procedure volumes, financed by hospital capital and operating budgets. Moat: regulatory
approvals, installed equipment base, and switching costs for hospitals and device makers already qualified on
STERIS's sterilization processes.

- **FY2027 initial guidance (11-May-2026, with FY26 results):** revenue +7–8% reported / +6–7% organic; adjusted

- **Q1 FY27 (5-Aug-2026):** **"reiterating its prior guidance for revenue and earnings"** — adjusted EPS range

  guidance **maintain** on the P&L line and a **disclosed, project-linked reduction** on the cash-flow line — not a

## 8. Bull case / Bear case
**Bull (3 points):**
1. All three segments (Healthcare, AST, Life Sciences) grew both revenue and operating income YoY in the latest
   quarter — broad-based strength, not a single-segment story.
2. FY2027 P&L guidance was reiterated, not cut, even while capex/FCF guidance was revised for a disclosed,
   growth-oriented reason (new manufacturing capacity) rather than a demand miss.
3. The multi-year ethylene-oxide personal-injury litigation overhang (Lake County/Cook County, Illinois) is
   substantially settled (binding settlement agreements dated 3-Mar-2025 and 29-Oct-2025), removing a tail risk
   that depressed the multiple for years.

**Bear (3 points):**
1. Valuation is full (≈20x trailing adjusted P/E, ≈25x GAAP) for a company now also absorbing two concurrent
   multi-year manufacturing-consolidation projects (Mentor, OH and the new NC Center of Excellence) — execution
   risk is real even if the guidance holds.
2. Q1 FY27 operating cash flow fell 12.6% YoY on a working-capital swing; if that repeats it would compress the
   already-guided-down $800m FY27 FCF target further.
3. EO litigation risk is a class of tail risk that can resurface in new jurisdictions or via new plaintiffs even
   after the currently-known matters settle — not fully retired as a risk category, only the specific known cases.

## 9. Key risks & kill criteria (measurable)
1. Any subsequent quarterly update that lowers FY2027 adjusted EPS guidance below the current $11.10 floor (per
   8-K Ex-99.1 releases).
2. AST segment constant-currency organic revenue growth below 3% for two consecutive quarters (Q1 FY27 was 5%;
   AST is the segment most exposed to device-maker capex cycles and EO-capacity regulation).
3. Net debt/EBITDA exceeding 2.0x (from ≈0.7x currently; per 10-Q/10-K balance sheet and TTM operating income+D&A).
4. Total cash restructuring costs for the NC Center of Excellence exceeding $60m (25% above the high end of the
   disclosed $40–50m cash-cost range).
5. A new, unresolved ethylene-oxide trial verdict or judgment against a STERIS subsidiary in a jurisdiction other
   than the currently-settled Illinois matters.
