## 1. Verdict
**INCLUDE** — durable, oligopolistic waste-services franchise with genuine pricing power; the price implies 10-year FCF growth modestly *below* our base case. Thesis horizon: 24–36 months.

## 2. Business in plain English
Republic Services is the #2 US solid-waste/recycling company: it collects, transfers, and disposes of residential, small-container (dumpster) and large-container (roll-off) waste, owns landfills and recycling centers, and runs an "Environmental Solutions" segment (industrial/hazardous waste, field services). Revenue is ~68% collection, ~11% landfill (net), ~10% Environmental Solutions, rest transfer/recycling. It earns money via long-term, often exclusive municipal contracts and a "restricted vs open-market" pricing structure that lets it pass through cost inflation plus margin. Competitive position: local density/route economics and landfill ownership create high barriers to entry (permitting), so it and Waste Management effectively duopolize most metro markets.

## 3. Why the model likes it, and durability
Quant flags (triage_cards.csv, 25 Sep 2026 snapshot): quality 4/5, growth 2/5, price_vs_growth 3/5; "27x NTM P/E justified by recurring, non-discretionary waste collection and consistent pricing power (14% forward EPS growth despite slow revenue growth)." This is durable, not an artefact: Q2 2026 core price was +5.3% on total revenue (+6.4% on related-business revenue) against only 4.6% total revenue growth (volume actually *fell* 1.6%), i.e. margin expansion is being generated by price over cost inflation, a structural feature of exclusive-contract, permitted-landfill economics, not a one-off. Adjusted EBITDA margin held at 32.1% in Q2 2026 despite a stated 50bp headwind from prior-year event-driven landfill volumes — evidence of resilience, not accounting engineering. ## 7. Valuation & reverse DCF
NTM P/E 27.0x, FCF yield ~3.0% (triage snapshot). Reverse DCF (two-stage, 10-yr explicit + Gordon terminal, WACC 7.3%, terminal growth 2.5%, EV ≈ $79.5bn [mkt cap $65.3bn + consolidated net debt $14.2bn], FY26E adjusted FCF midpoint $2.5575bn): **implied 10-year FCF growth ≈ 7.2% p.a.** Our base case (mid-single-digit organic growth + margin expansion + ~1.5–2%/yr buyback accretion) is ~8%. **Implied vs base: BELOW** — the market is not pricing in RSG's demonstrated ability to compound adjusted FCF/EPS at a high-single-digit rate; it is pricing a modest deceleration toward the low end of that range.

## 8. Bull / bear
**Bull:** (1) core pricing (5.3–5.7%/qtr) continues to outrun cost inflation, sustaining 32%+ adjusted EBITDA margins; (2) capital-light Environmental Solutions and RNG projects add incremental high-margin growth without landfill capex; (3) continued disciplined M&A ($860m H1'26) plus buybacks compound FCF/share faster than headline FCF.
**Bear:** (1) volume has been negative every quarter shown (−1.6% to −0.8%) — if price realization ever normalizes toward inflation, growth could stall; (2) consolidated net debt/adjusted EBITDA at ~2.5x with continued buybacks/M&A leaves less room before covenant/rating pressure in a rate-up scenario; (3) recycled-commodity price fell to $136/ton (−$13 YoY), a swing factor on the "Other" revenue line that isn't fully hedged.
