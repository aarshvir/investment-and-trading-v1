# Ecolab Inc. (ECL) — Fundamental Diligence Dossier

**Verdict: WATCH** — thesis horizon 12–36 months if valuation resets.
One-sentence reason: a genuine, durable water/hygiene/pest moat with a well-documented raise-not-cut guidance record, now layering on a $4.75bn, debt-funded, 29x-EBITDA bet on AI-data-center liquid cooling (CoolIT Systems) — but at 30–34x NTM adjusted EPS the price already assumes more 10-year growth (≈14%/yr) than this analyst's evidence-based base case (≈9%/yr) delivers, so it is priced for perfection rather than a demand or integration miss.

## 1. Business in plain English
Ecolab sells water treatment, cleaning, sanitizing and pest-elimination chemistries, dosing/dispensing equipment and digital monitoring services to restaurants, hotels, hospitals, food and beverage plants, data centers and industrial/energy customers worldwide, organized into four segments: Global Industrial (largest, incl. Water), Global Institutional & Specialty, Global Pest Elimination and Global Life Sciences. It makes money on a razor/razor-blade model — installed equipment plus recurring consumable chemistry and service visits — which is small relative to a customer's own operating or downtime cost, giving Ecolab real pricing power and switching costs. In March 2026 it entered a new, adjacent growth vector: data-center liquid cooling for AI infrastructure (CoolIT Systems), extending "water/fluid management" into cooling-as-a-service for hyperscalers.

## 2. Why the model likes it / is it durable
b1 quant composite is actually **weak** (composite 0.127, decile 2, live_rank 434 of ~500) — low despite the fundamental quality story, because the model's Quality family (gross profit/assets 0.248 raw) penalizes capital-intensive chemicals manufacturing, and Value (earnings yield 2.7%, FCF yield 2.4%, both raw) and Momentum (12–1m return 4.3%, modest) are unexciting for a stock trading at ~30x. This stock was **not** surfaced by the quant top-70 gate; it reached diligence via the fundamental triage pass (quality 5/5, growth 4/5), which is itself evidence the quant composite and the fundamental read disagree here (recorded as a data conflict below) — the model sees an expensive, capital-intensive industrial; the analyst read sees a recurring-revenue compounder with real ROE (22.0% TTM) and pricing power. The durable part (customer switching costs, energy-surcharge/pricing pass-through, One Ecolab productivity program) is verifiable in the filed numbers below; whether the CoolIT bet is durable growth or overpaying into an AI-capex cycle is the open question.

## 5. Moat and competition
- **Switching costs & recurring revenue:** equipment/dosing systems plus consumable chemistry create high switching costs versus commodity chemical suppliers; pricing has offset commodity-cost inflation for 8+ consecutive quarters per company commentary.
- **CoolIT Systems acquisition (data-center liquid cooling):** agreed 20-Mar-2026 for **$4.75bn cash**, "29x and 24x estimated next-12-month and 2027 adjusted EBITDA" (company's own figure); CoolIT ~$550M revenue base; closed 2-Jul-2026. Expected to lift Global Water organic growth by 2pts and total company organic growth by 1pt beginning one year after close; doubles Ecolab's "Global High-Tech" addressable market from $5bn to $10bn. This is the single largest strategic and valuation swing factor in the name — a genuine new growth engine if AI data-center buildout continues, a rich (29x EBITDA) and dilutive-through-2027 bet if it slows.
- **Ovivo Electronics** (semiconductor ultrapure water), closed 16-Dec-2025 for $1,596M net of cash — a second, smaller adjacent-growth deal in the same 12-month window; together the two deals add ~$6.3bn of debt-funded M&A.
- Competitors include Diversey/Solenis (institutional cleaning), Veralto (formerly Danaher water) and, in the new cooling business, established thermal-management players (Vertiv, Schneider) — Ecolab is a scale challenger there, not the incumbent.

## 7. Valuation — reverse DCF (no V1 systematic valuation row exists for ECL)
Inputs: EV ≈$86.0bn (market cap $78.3bn + net debt); TTM FCF $1,874.5M (primary-source); WACC 8.0% (beta 0.89, ~90/10 equity/debt); terminal growth 2.5%; 10-year horizon.
**Solving for the constant FCF growth rate that justifies the price: ≈14.1%/year for 10 years.**
- **Bear (10y FCF CAGR ≈4%):** CoolIT integration disappoints or the AI-data-center capex cycle slows, organic growth falls back toward 3–4%, net debt/EBITDA stays above 3x past the guided 2-year window.
- **Base (10y FCF CAGR ≈9%):** management's own "5–7% organic sales, margin well beyond 20%" algorithm continues, CoolIT becomes modestly accretive from 2028 as guided, buybacks resume once leverage normalizes.
- **Bull (10y FCF CAGR ≈15%):** CoolIT/Ovivo prove to be the start of a genuine "AI infrastructure fluid management" annuity, cross-selling across >1,000 existing data-center relationships accelerates faster than guided.
**Implied vs. base: ABOVE.** ~14% priced in vs. a ~9% evidence-based base case — the market is already crediting Ecolab with CoolIT succeeding well beyond management's own 2028 accretion timeline.

## 8. Bull case
1. CoolIT Systems positions Ecolab inside the AI-data-center buildout (custom cooling designs already used by NVIDIA/AMD chip partners per the deal announcement) — a genuinely new, high-growth adjacency, not financial engineering.
2. Guidance has been raised or held at every one of the last three checkpoints even while absorbing $6.3bn of acquisition financing costs — execution discipline is real and filed, not asserted.
3. Organic operating income margin is trending toward the "well beyond 20%" algorithm (18.8% and rising in Q2 2026) with pricing offsetting commodity inflation for two straight years.

## 9. Bear case
1. The price already implies ~14%/yr sustained FCF growth for a decade — well above the company's own 5–7% organic sales algorithm even with margin expansion and buybacks layered on.
2. Pro forma leverage (~3x net debt/adjusted EBITDA at CoolIT close) removes near-term buyback flexibility and raises interest expense (Q2 2026 net interest expense +$10M YoY "reflecting…new debt"); CoolIT is explicitly **not** EPS-accretive (ex-amortization) until 2028.
3. Two large, fast-closed acquisitions in 12 months (Ovivo Dec-2025, CoolIT Jul-2026) raise integration-execution risk simultaneously across two new end-markets (semiconductor ultrapure water, data-center cooling) neither of which is Ecolab's historical core.

## 10. Key risks & kill criteria — thesis-invalidation triggers (what would change this verdict)
1. Organic sales growth falls below 5% for two consecutive quarters (breaks the "5–7%" algorithm).
2. Net debt/adjusted EBITDA remains above 3.0x beyond FY2027 (past management's own 2-year delever target from the 20-Mar-2026 announcement).
3. FY guidance is cut in an upcoming release (breaking the maintain/raise pattern of the last three checkpoints).
4. Organic operating income margin stalls below 19% for two consecutive quarters (fails to progress toward "well beyond 20%").
5. CoolIT/Ovivo integration and acquisition-related special charges exceed the guided ~$0.75–$0.80/share (FY2026) for two consecutive quarters.

## 12. Red-flag scan
- **TPC Group litigation:** Ecolab subsidiary Nalco was named in a multi-district litigation stemming from a Nov-2019 Port Neches, TX butadiene plant explosion (TPC Group's plant; Nalco supplied process chemicals, did not operate the plant). TPC filed Chapter 11 in 2022 and was discharged from the MDL via a $500M claim-allowance settlement; Nalco opted out of third-party releases and continued defending. **In July 2026 Nalco reached an additional settlement in principle with substantially all remaining plaintiffs** (10-Q, Note 16); company states it does not expect a material adverse effect and believes any loss is largely insurance-covered.
- Environmental remediation ongoing at ~25 sites (accrued, per 10-K/10-Q) — routine for this industry.
- No material weakness, restatement or going-concern language found in the sources reviewed.
- Two large debt-funded acquisitions in 12 months and a $5.0bn note issuance are a genuine, filed leverage step-up (not a red flag in isolation, but the single largest change to the risk profile this year).
