# P — Everpure, Inc. (formerly Pure Storage, Inc.; ticker changed from PSTG to P, Feb 2026)

## 1. Verdict
**WATCH.** A genuinely strong technology franchise (differentiated all-flash/DirectFlash storage, real hyperscaler design wins) but currently priced for a cash-flow inflection that has not yet shown up in the filed numbers. Revisit once two consecutive quarters confirm the promised free-cash-flow turnaround. Thesis horizon: 6–18 months to re-underwrite.

## 2. Business in plain English
Everpure (rebranded from Pure Storage in Feb 2026) sells proprietary all-flash data-storage systems and a storage-as-a-service subscription ("Evergreen//One") to large enterprises and, increasingly, hyperscale cloud/AI infrastructure operators. It makes money selling storage hardware/subscriptions at a premium to commodity disk-based systems, on the pitch that its DirectFlash architecture is denser, more reliable and cheaper to operate at scale. It was added to the S&P 500 in September 2026 (replacing The Trade Desk), and the current growth story is about winning storage capacity inside hyperscaler AI data centers (management has cited design wins with 2 of the 5 largest hyperscalers).

## 3. Why the model likes it — durable or artefact?
b1 factor scores show top-decile momentum and asset-growth signals (composite decile 6/10 but live_rank 465 out of ~3,500 — mid-pack overall because value/valuation factors are weak) driven by real +38–40% YoY revenue growth, not an accounting artefact. The triage's flagged red flag — negative FCF yield despite reported GAAP profitability — is confirmed in the filings (see §4/§6) and is the single most important thing to resolve before sizing this name: is it a temporary, disclosed working-capital/component-prepayment build ahead of a hyperscaler ramp (management's claim), or a genuine cash-conversion problem?

## 5. Guidance track record
At the Q4 FY26 release (Mar 2026) management guided FY2027 revenue to roughly $4.41–4.51bn. By the Q1 FY27 release (Jun 2026) and confirmed at Q2 FY27 (Sep 2026), FY2027 revenue guidance was **raised materially to $5.03–5.07bn** (+~14% versus the original range), driven by the hyperscaler ramp. Management also guided to **$600–800m of FY2027 free cash flow**, i.e., an explicit promise that the H1 FY27 cash burn reverses sharply in H2 — a guidance commitment not yet delivered as of the last filed 10-Q. This is the single largest swing factor in the thesis. (Sources: Yahoo Finance/Insider Monkey summaries of the Q2 FY27 earnings call, cross-referenced against the revenue figures in the 10-Q; guidance ranges themselves were not independently re-derived from a primary 8-K exhibit in this pass — verify against the Q2 FY27 8-K press-release exhibit before relying on the exact figures.)

## 6. Earnings quality & balance sheet
- **GAAP vs adjusted gap:** Company is only recently and modestly GAAP-profitable (Q2 FY27 net income $74.1m on $1,186m revenue = 6.2% net margin); trailing GAAP P/E of 172.6x (d4 snapshot) versus NTM (largely non-GAAP-consensus-driven) P/E of 33.9x shows the gap is heavily stock-based-compensation and amortization driven, typical of enterprise-hardware/SaaS-hybrid names. SBC-as-%-of-revenue was not separately re-pulled from the cash-flow statement in this pass — treat the 33.9x NTM multiple as the more decision-useful (but still non-GAAP) figure.
- **Balance sheet (consolidated, 2 Aug 2026):** Cash $385.7m (down sharply from $854.9m at FY26-end and $837.8m at Q1 FY27 — the negative-FCF quarter directly drained the cash balance); total assets $5.22bn; total liabilities $3.68bn; stockholders' equity $1.54bn. Long-term debt is effectively **zero** (LongTermDebtNoncurrent tagged $0 since FY2025-end).
- **Cash conversion:** FY2026 (full year) FCF was a healthy $615.8m on $3,662.8m revenue (~17% FCF margin) — the negative FCF is a **recent, H1-FY2027-specific** phenomenon, not the historical norm. This cuts toward "temporary investment ahead of ramp" rather than "structural earnings-quality problem," but it is unverified until H2 FY27 numbers print.
- **Customer concentration:** per the FY26 10-K, no single customer exceeded 10% of revenue or accounts receivable in FY2025 or FY2026 (an improvement from FY2024, when one customer did) — but the forward story is explicitly about concentrating *more* of the growth in a handful of hyperscaler accounts, so concentration risk is rising again looking forward even if the trailing disclosure looks clean.
- **Insider selling:** Recent, sizeable insider sales by CEO Charles Giancarlo (~$11m) and founder John Colgrove (~$17m) were reported; no insider buying reported in the past year (Simply Wall St, sourced from Form 4 filings). This is a mild negative signal to weigh against the bull case, though routine 10b5-1 selling by long-tenured executives is common and not on its own disqualifying.

## 8. Bull case / Bear case
**Bull:** (1) Genuine, verifiable +38% revenue growth and FY2027 guidance raised ~14% mid-year on real hyperscaler design wins (2 of the top 5 hyperscalers); (2) Full-year FY2026 FCF margin was a healthy ~17%, showing the underlying model can convert profit to cash when not in an inventory/component-build phase; (3) Essentially debt-free balance sheet gives room to fund the working-capital build without financial distress.
**Bear:** (1) Two consecutive quarters (Q1 and Q2 FY27) of negative free cash flow, with cash down more than 50% from the FY26-end level, is a real, filed fact, not a market rumor; (2) Trailing GAAP P/E of 172.6x and a negative FCF yield mean there is essentially no valuation support if the H2 FY27 FCF promise slips even one quarter; (3) Sizeable, uninterrupted insider selling by the CEO and founder, with no offsetting insider buying, is a soft signal that people closest to the business are not adding at these prices.

## 9. Key risks & kill criteria (measurable)
1. Free cash flow (OCF − capex) remains negative in the Q3 FY2027 10-Q (quarter ending ~Nov 2026) — i.e., the promised H2 turnaround fails to start on schedule.
2. FY2027 full-year FCF comes in below $500m (versus the $600–800m guided range) when reported at the FY2027 10-K.
3. Any single customer/hyperscaler reappears above 10% of revenue or receivables in a 10-Q/10-K (concentration risk resurfacing).
4. Cash and equivalents fall below $250m in any quarter without a corresponding revolver draw or financing disclosed.
5. Revenue growth decelerates below 25% YoY for two consecutive quarters (would break the current growth thesis embedded in the 33.9x NTM multiple).
