# Trane Technologies plc (TT) — Diligence Dossier

**Agent:** F57 (wave 3) | **As of:** 2026-09-25 close (px $454.44, mktcap ≈$99.99bn, per d4_live_snapshot.parquet 2026-09-25)

## 1. Verdict
**INCLUDE-SMALL** (half weight). Thesis horizon 24–36 months. Top-tier commercial HVAC/climate-solutions franchise
with a record, rapidly-growing backlog and a genuine, three-times-raised FY2026 guide — but the reverse DCF shows the
premium multiple is, in the triage's own words, "largely earned": implied growth sits right at the edge of a
defensible base case, so there is essentially no valuation cushion for a bookings slowdown.

## 2. Business in plain English
Trane Technologies (Trane brand) designs, makes and services commercial and residential HVAC systems (including
data-centre and mission-critical cooling) and, through Thermo King, transport refrigeration units. It sells to
building owners, contractors and OEMs, and increasingly monetizes long-lived equipment through service, parts and
controls contracts on top of the initial system sale (source: TT FY2025 10-K, Item 1 Business, filed 2026-02-05).

## 3. Why the model likes it / is this durable
Q09_triage.json: quality 5/5, growth 4/5, price_vs_growth 3/5, no red flags. "Revenue +10.6%, margin 13.3%, ROE
36.6%, leverage only 0.8x — best balance sheet and consistency in HVAC peer set, with real commercial/data-center
cooling backlog... Premium 26.8x NTM multiple is largely earned, but Street upside is only 16%." This dossier
confirms the backlog/bookings acceleration is real and getting stronger, not weaker: organic bookings were +24% YoY
in Q1 2026 and **+37% YoY in Q2 2026**, book-to-bill 123% (>100% in every segment), record backlog $12.1bn (+70% YoY,
Americas Commercial HVAC bookings +90% YoY) (Q2 2026 earnings release, Ex.99.1, 2026-07-30). This is a durable,
structural signal (data-centre cooling demand, electrification of commercial HVAC, aging-building replacement cycle)
rather than a one-off — but it is also now very well recognized by the market (see §7).

## 6. Earnings quality & balance sheet (consolidated, GAAP; entity = Trane Technologies plc (Ireland-domiciled parent)
and subsidiaries — no bank/insurer/REIT segment-scope issue)
- **FCF conversion:** company-reported **FY2025 free cash flow conversion of 98%** of net income (FY2025 NI $2.919bn
  → FCF ≈$2.86bn). TTM through Q2 2026, derived by differencing YTD SEC XBRL cash-flow tags (OCF $3.877bn TTM, capex
  ≈$0.337bn TTM) ≈**$3.54bn** — a step up on FY2025, but **likely inflated by a working-capital tailwind from the
  record bookings/backlog (customer advances)** that may not repeat every year; this dossier's reverse DCF (§7)
  uses the more conservative, NI-conversion-based figure (~$2.95bn) as its central case for this reason, flagged
  explicitly.
- **Balance sheet (10-K FY2025, filed 2026-02-05):** long-term debt $4.615bn, cash $1.763bn at 2025-12-31 → net debt
  ≈$2.85bn; TTM EBITDA (vendor) $4.308bn → **net debt/EBITDA ≈0.66x** — very low leverage, matching the triage's
  "leverage only 0.8x" read (small difference is period/definition, not a conflict worth flagging).
- **SBC and share count:** ~220M diluted shares; buybacks and dividend both funded from the high FCF conversion; no
  large debt-funded M&A found in the reviewed filings.
- **Domicile note:** TT redomiciled to Ireland (plc) in 2020 for a prior corporate restructuring; all figures above
  are consolidated Trane Technologies plc, the single public reporting entity — no separate bank/insurance subsidiary
  scope issue applies here.

Reverse DCF (2-stage: 10-yr explicit constant growth + Gordon terminal; WACC 8.0%, terminal growth 3.0%):
- Using the **TTM FCF ≈$3.54bn** (includes the possibly-non-repeating working-capital tailwind): implied growth
  **≈7.4%/yr**.
- Using the **more conservative, NI-conversion-based FCF ≈$2.95bn**: implied growth **≈9.7%/yr**.
My own base case for a 10-yr FCF/share CAGR — organic mid-to-high-single-digit revenue growth off the current
backlog (management's own long-term algorithm is high-single-digit adjusted EPS growth, now running ahead of that
at 9% organic near-term) plus continued high-conversion buybacks — is **≈9–10%/yr**. The implied range (7.4–9.7%)
sits **right at or modestly below** this base case — **implied_vs_base = in_line** (a close, disclosed judgement
call; using the higher trailing FCF figure alone would read "below," but I do not trust that figure enough to lean
on it alone). This matches the triage's own qualitative read almost exactly ("premium is largely earned... Street
upside only 16%") — a genuine confirmation, not an overturn, of the triage call.

## 8. Bull case
1. Record backlog ($12.1bn, +70% YoY) and record book-to-bill (123%, >100% in every segment) give unusually strong
   multi-quarter revenue visibility (Q2 2026 release).
2. Three consecutive FY2026 guidance raises, each quoting the exact prior range, show genuine, accelerating
   execution — not a low-bar sandbagged guide.
3. Net debt/EBITDA ≈0.66x and ~98% FCF/NI conversion give ample capacity to keep funding buybacks/dividends and
   selective bolt-on M&A without balance-sheet strain.

## 9. Bear case
1. GAAP operating margin has contracted YoY for two consecutive quarters (−190bp, −100bp); if the adjusted-vs-GAAP
   gap widens further (more acquisition/restructuring charges), the "flat underlying margin" read could prove
   optimistic.
2. The record bookings surge is concentrated in Americas Commercial HVAC / data-centre cooling — a real but
   narrower demand driver than "HVAC broadly"; a pause in AI-data-centre capex would hit the highest-growth segment
   hardest.
3. Reverse DCF shows no cushion (implied 7.4–9.7% vs. a 9–10% base case) at a 26.8x NTM multiple — a bookings
   deceleration would likely compress the multiple and the growth estimate simultaneously.

## 10. Key risks & kill criteria (measurable)
1. Organic bookings growth turns negative YoY for two consecutive quarters (vs. +24%/+37% in Q1/Q2 2026 — a sharp,
   measurable reversal of the current trend).
2. Book-to-bill ratio falls below 100% for two consecutive quarters (vs. 123% in Q2 2026).
3. Net debt/EBITDA (consolidated) exceeds 2.0x.
4. FY2026 guidance (currently adjusted EPS $15.20–15.30) is **cut** vs. the immediately preceding range at either of
   the next two quarterly releases.
5. Adjusted operating margin contracts more than 150bp YoY for two consecutive quarters (vs. −20bp/−60bp in Q1/Q2
   2026 — a meaningful widening of the current small gap).
