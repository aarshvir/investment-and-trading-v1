# BR — Broadridge Financial Solutions, Inc.

## 1. Verdict

**INCLUDE** — thesis horizon 12–36 months. A ~90%-recurring-revenue processor of proxy voting, mutual-fund/annual-meeting communications and multi-asset trading/post-trade technology, compounding recurring revenue at high-single digits and adjusted EPS at low-double digits for a decade, trading at 15.2x NTM P/E and a 6.3% FCF yield — a valuation that (on a conservative reverse DCF) prices in only ~2.9%/year 10-year FCF growth against a company that has never grown FCF that slowly and has just guided to another year of 6–8% recurring-revenue / 8–12% adjusted-EPS growth. Four straight quarters of raised guidance in FY2026, a clean balance sheet (net debt/EBITDA ≈1.7x), and a 14th double-digit dividend increase in 15 years support the quantitative "cheap growth compounder" read from the triage. The reservation that keeps this from a larger weighting: BR's own recurring-revenue growth is itself decelerating on a multi-year view (FY24 +6.6%, FY25 +5.9%, FY26 +8% cc — noisy, not a clean trend) and a portion of FY2026's growth and cash flow was flattered by non-recurring digital-asset mark-to-market gains that will not repeat every year. **[Corrected 2026-10-06: the digital-asset gain did not flatter cash flow: the FY2026 cash-flow statement deducts 'Digital assets change in fair market value (231.4)' from operating cash flow, so free cash flow is unaffected. It flattered GAAP net income only. See Re-assessment (RA6).]**

## 2. Business in plain English

Broadridge is the plumbing behind shareholder democracy and a large piece of the back office of Wall Street. When a public company or mutual fund needs to send proxy materials and collect votes from millions of retail and institutional shareholders, Broadridge does it — its ProxyEdge platform handles the vote processing for the large majority of US institutional share volume. That is the **Investor Communication Solutions (ICS)** segment ($5,561m of FY2026's $7,477m revenue, 74%). The **Global Technology and Operations (GTO)** segment ($1,916m, 26%) sells outsourced technology and operations platforms — trading, clearing, wealth/investment-management middle- and back-office systems — to banks and broker-dealers, recently expanded via the CQG acquisition (multi-asset futures/options execution and connectivity, completed 1 May 2026) into global futures/options trading infrastructure. Both businesses monetize on long-term, multi-year contracts with high switching costs (a bank does not re-platform its clearing operations casually), which is the source of the ~90%+ recurring-revenue mix.

## 8. Bull case / bear case

**Bull (3):**
1. Four consecutive FY2026 quarters of guidance raises that were still exceeded at year-end (delivered 8% recurring-revenue growth and 12% adjusted-EPS growth, both at or above every range issued during the year) — a rare, clean, filed-and-verified beat-and-raise record.
2. >100% FCF conversion, net debt/EBITDA ≈1.7x, and a 14-of-15-year streak of double-digit dividend increases — a genuinely shareholder-friendly, low-risk capital-allocation record that funds bolt-on M&A (CQG) without straining the balance sheet.
3. High-switching-cost, regulatorily-anchored recurring revenue (proxy voting/vote-processing scale, multi-year bank/broker outsourcing contracts) gives real visibility into the FY2027 guide, and tokenization/digital-asset initiatives (mutual-fund/repo infrastructure) are a disclosed, early-stage option not embedded in the base case.

**Bear (3):**
1. 12-month price momentum is negative (the stock has *not* rewarded the beat-and-raise pattern) — either the market is right to discount recurring-revenue growth that is itself decelerating on a multi-year view (FY24 +6.6% → FY25 +5.9% → FY26 +8%, a noisy rather than clean acceleration), or this is a genuine, durable mispricing; the base case in this dossier assumes the latter, which is a real judgment call, not a certainty.
2. A real, disclosed chunk of FY2026's reported growth was non-operating: a $227m full-year gain on digital-asset holdings inflated GAAP net income and cash flow, and the unexplained convergence of GAAP and adjusted EPS to exactly $9.60 for FY2026 is an unresolved data conflict this dossier could not fully reconcile from public disclosure alone.
3. ICS "event-driven" revenue (M&A/reorg communications, contested proxy contests) is inherently market-cycle-dependent — a sharp slowdown in corporate-action volume would hit the segment that carries the bulk of BR's revenue and margin, and is the most plausible mechanism for the bear case above.

## 9. Key risks & kill criteria (measurable) -- signposts that would change our verdict

1. Recurring-revenue growth (constant currency) falls below **5%** for two consecutive quarters (below the low end of every guidance range issued in FY2026/FY2027).
2. Adjusted EPS growth guidance is **cut** versus the immediately prior quarter's range (zero cuts to date across FY2026's four releases).
3. Net debt/EBITDA rises above **3.0x** (from ≈1.7x currently) absent a disclosed, board-approved acquisition financing plan.
4. Full-year FCF conversion (FCF/net income) falls below **90%** for a full fiscal year (FY2026: >100%).
5. Digital-asset/tokenization mark-to-market losses exceed **$50 million** in any single quarter (Q4 FY26: −$11m), which would signal the strategic initiative is a source of earnings volatility rather than a disclosed side item.
