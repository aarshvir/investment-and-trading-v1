# NSC - Norfolk Southern Corporation (F159, wave 8, standard depth)

## 1. Verdict
**WATCH.** Thesis horizon 12-36 months. A good railroad whose price is now mostly a bet on the pending Union Pacific (UNP) takeover clearing the Surface Transportation Board (STB). Standalone, the stock already prices more growth than my base case. Triage said "merger-arb, not advanced"; I confirm the situation but the verdict is WATCH, not REJECT, because the deal terms leave real upside if approval comes (see section 7).

## 2. Business in plain English
Norfolk Southern runs a 22-state eastern US freight railroad (coal, intermodal containers, autos, chemicals, agriculture) and earns money by hauling carloads at a price per unit, plus fuel surcharges. Its moat is the physical network, which cannot be replicated, and it competes mainly with CSX. On 28 July 2025 it agreed to be acquired by UNP.

## 3. Why the model likes it, and is that durable
b1 screen: quality (ROE 16%), moderate momentum, mid percentiles on most families; no single standout. The ROE is real but flattered by high leverage (leverage factor 0.37) and by 2024's one-off line-sale gains. Durable as a franchise; not a deep-value or growth signal.

## 8. Bull and bear
Bull: (1) STB approves with manageable conditions, delivering 1 UNP share plus $88.82 cash, a 16% gain over $313 before time value; (2) record Q2 revenue with 4% volume growth shows demand recovery; (3) $215m of 2025 productivity savings shows cost discipline.
Bear: (1) STB rejects or burdens the deal; standalone value is about $265-300; (2) the incident litigation and Ohio AG case remain open; (3) adjusted operating ratio worsened year on year (65.5% vs 63.4%) and free cash flow fell about 48% in H1.

## 9. Kill criteria (measurable)
1. STB issues a final decision denying the UNP-NSC merger, or either party gives notice of termination.
2. NSC adjusted operating ratio above 67.0% for two consecutive quarters (company-defined adjusted basis, consolidated, quarterly release).
3. Consolidated debt-to-total capitalization (10-Q MD&A, consolidated) above 55%.
4. Incident accruals plus environmental liabilities (10-Q note, consolidated) increase by more than $300m in any 12-month period.
5. UNP share price below $240 while NSC trades above $300 (deal spread would then exceed 25%).
