# Halliburton Company (HAL) - Fundamental Diligence Dossier (F133, standard depth)

**Verdict: INCLUDE-SMALL** (half weight), 12-36 month horizon. The price ($32.76, 25 Sep 2026 close) implies about 4% a year of free-cash-flow growth at a 9.5% cost of equity, against about 6-8% in my base case, and the balance sheet is strong (consolidated net debt about 1.2x EBITDA; ratings A3/BBB+). Named reservation: earnings have fallen three years running (adjusted EPS $2.99 in 2024, $2.42 in 2025, $2.37 TTM), the Q2 2026 adjusted margin was below a year ago, and insiders sold $25.6M with no buying; the North America recovery rests so far on one quarter and management's word. The triage said "a cyclical trade", which is fair, so this is a half-weight, evidence-gated position; it is not a fit as a full-weight holding given a 55% worst drawdown over three years.

## 1. Business in plain English
Halliburton sells well-drilling, completion and production services and equipment to oil and gas producers in more than 70 countries (46,000 employees). Two segments: Completion and Production (fracking, cementing, well intervention, artificial lift; $3.2B of Q2 revenue) and Drilling and Evaluation (drilling services, logging, software; $2.5B). It earns when producers spend; 37% of first-half 2026 revenue was US. Moat: technology, scale and integrated contracts (new Saudi Aramco and Suriname awards), but North American pressure pumping is a price-competitive, cyclical market.
