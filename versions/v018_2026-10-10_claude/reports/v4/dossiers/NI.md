# NiSource Inc. (NI) — Diligence Dossier (Agent F111, Wave 7)

## 1. Verdict
**INCLUDE-SMALL** (24–48 month horizon; regulated-utility, longer-dated thesis). Reason: a regulated gas/electric utility running a credible, guided ~9–11% rate-base growth plan funded partly by minority-stake sales to Blackstone, with reverse-DCF-implied growth well below both guidance and my base case — but GAAP earnings quality is currently distorted by large one-off gains, and a live state regulatory investigation into gas-meter accuracy is an open, unresolved item, so I withhold full weight until the investigation resolves and one clean organic-EPS quarter is reported.

## 2. Business in plain English
NiSource owns regulated gas and electric utilities in Indiana and Ohio (NIPSCO and Columbia Gas of Ohio being the largest), delivering energy to homes and businesses under state-regulated rates that are set to recover costs plus an allowed return. It makes money by investing in infrastructure (grid modernization, renewable generation, gas-system replacement) and earning a regulator-approved return on that capital base — growth comes from capital investment (rate base), not volume. It has recently sold minority equity stakes in its generation business (GenCo) and in NIPSCO itself to Blackstone Infrastructure Partners affiliates to help fund its capital plan without diluting common shareholders as much as a straight equity raise would.

## 8. Bull case
1. Guided $28.6bn 2026–2030 capital plan (9–11% rate-base growth) is regulator-blessed infrastructure spend, not speculative growth capex.
2. Data-center demand is already contracted, not merely pipeline: **"$1.4 billion in savings for existing customers through recently approved Amazon and Alphabet agreements"** (2Q'26 release) — named counterparties, approved agreements, quantified customer savings.
3. Reverse-DCF-implied growth (~2.3%) is roughly a quarter of the guided 9–10% CAGR, leaving substantial room for re-rating if guidance is delivered.

## 9. Bear case
1. **Live, open regulatory investigation:** per the FY2025 10-K (filed 2026-02-11, accession 0001111711-26-000027), **"In November 2025, the IURC initiated an investigation into the accuracy of NIPSCO's gas meters. This investigation followed NIPSCO's disclosure of a latent issue with a small percentage of the meter indexes in NIPSCO's gas meters, which was discovered during roll-out of new AMI communications modules for gas meters. A procedural schedule has been established and a hearing is expected in July 2026."** This is a named, dated, unresolved regulatory matter, not a generic risk factor.
2. GAAP earnings are currently a poor proxy for organic run-rate given the Blackstone-transaction gains (Section 4) — anyone using GAAP EPS to size the position (as the quant screen may be doing, per Section 3) is likely overstating growth.
3. Heavy reliance on continued constructive rate-case outcomes and on two large counterparties (Amazon, Alphabet) delivering on data-center commitments; either a less-constructive regulatory environment or a slip in large-load demand would directly hit the 9–11% rate-base growth assumption underlying guidance.
