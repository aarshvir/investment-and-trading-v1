# J - Jacobs Solutions Inc. (Wave 8, agent F165)

## 1. Verdict
**INCLUDE-SMALL** (half weight; thesis horizon 12-36 months). Named reservation: the large gap between GAAP and adjusted earnings (9-month FY26 GAAP net earnings from continuing operations $219M vs adjusted $607M) and a working-capital-dependent cash flow, while leverage rose after the PA Consulting buyout. The quick triage ("cannot assign conviction" because of the 2024 divestiture distortion) is **overturned**: continuing-operations results since FY25 are comparable and show accelerating organic growth, a record backlog and three consecutive guidance raises.

## 2. Business in plain English
Engineering, design and project-delivery consultancy (Dallas). Two segments: Infrastructure & Advanced Facilities (I&AF: data centers, semiconductors, life sciences, water, transport, energy) and PA Consulting (UK-based consulting, 100% owned since 20 Mar 2026). Revenue is mostly fees on cost-reimbursable projects; "adjusted net revenue" strips pass-through subcontract costs billed at little or no mark-up. Moat: engineering talent, long-term client frameworks (utilities, hyperscalers, chip makers) and an asset-light model (capex ~$80M a year on ~$12B gross revenue).

## 3. Why the model likes it
b1: gross profitability percentile 0.59, FCF yield 0.44, leverage 0.42, asset growth 0.69 and net share issuance 0.64 (buybacks), but momentum 0.31 and SUE 0.12 are weak. Durable: asset-light margins and buybacks. Artefact risk: trailing GAAP EPS ($3.00 in d4) is depressed by one-offs and the PA tax effect, so the earnings-yield family understates earnings power and the data card misleads in both directions.

## 8. Bull and bear
Bull: (1) record $28.9B backlog (+27%) with data-center, semiconductor and water work and ENR ranking #1 in 18 categories (company claim); (2) three straight raises, adjusted EBITDA margin 14.7%-14.8% and cost synergy target of $20M+ at PA within 24 months; (3) net leverage ~1.8x after buying out PA while still repurchasing $614M.
Bear: (1) AI-capex dependence: a hyperscaler pause would hit I&AF; (2) quality of earnings: GAAP is ~36% of adjusted over 9 months, receivables +26%, and adjusted net-revenue book-to-bill is only 1.1x; (3) leverage and goodwill: $3.6B debt, goodwill 1.5x equity, plus a legacy "Consolidated JV Matter" (a 50% joint venture hit by an unfavorable interim ruling in FY25; reserve size not stated in the 10-Q text I read).

## 9. Kill criteria
1. Adjusted net revenue growth below 5% year-on-year in any quarter of fiscal 2027 (Q3-26: 8.3%).
2. TTM adjusted-net-revenue book-to-bill below 1.0x, or backlog down year-on-year in two consecutive quarters (now $28.9B, +27.3%).
3. Consolidated net debt above 2.5x TTM adjusted EBITDA (now ~1.8x; debt per Note 12 table less cash).
4. Fiscal 2027 adjusted free cash flow margin guidance below 7.0% of adjusted net revenue (FY26 guide ~8%).
5. Fiscal 2027 adjusted EPS guidance midpoint below $7.25 (the FY26 midpoint).
