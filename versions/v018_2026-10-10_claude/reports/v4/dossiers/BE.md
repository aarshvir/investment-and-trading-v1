## 1. Business in plain English
Bloom makes solid-oxide fuel-cell "Energy Servers" that run on natural gas (or biogas, hydrogen) and generate electricity on site without combustion. Customers are now mostly AI data-center operators and hyperscalers who cannot wait for grid connections, plus industrial and utility buyers. Revenue is product sales (about 88% of Q2'26 revenue), installation, 5-20 year service contracts (terminable annually for convenience) and a shrinking electricity-sales line. The moat is proprietary stack technology, US manufacturing scale and speed-to-power; the weak spot is extreme customer concentration.

The score is momentum-and-growth driven, not cheapness: d4 shows P/E 106x FY26 and 58.6x FY27 consensus, FCF yield 0.6%, and a Street mean target of $280 (below price). Durable only if the order book converts; the cyclical/one-off risk is an AI-capex pause.

## 7. Bull and bear
Bull: (1) hyperscalers "approved" Bloom for AI factories and backlog is large (Q4'25 release: current backlog about $20B, product backlog about $6B, 2.5x YoY); (2) operating leverage, non-GAAP operating margin 22.5% in Q2; (3) guidance has been raised twice and consensus FY27 EPS $4.93 is still rising.
Bear: (1) 66% of receivables and over 60% of revenue sit with two or three buyers; (2) valuation needs about 31% a year for a decade, with stock at 58.6x FY27 EPS and a Street target below price; (3) dilution via warrants and convert conversions, a July 2026 short-seller report on raw-material supply, tariffs and a possible AI-capex pause.

## 8. Kill criteria / exit triggers (measurable)
1. Any single quarter's revenue below $900M or full-year 2026 revenue guidance cut below the $3.9B low end.
2. A top customer above 40% of quarterly revenue in the 10-Q for two consecutive quarters, or customer-deposit balance falling by more than 25% quarter on quarter.
3. Non-GAAP gross margin below 30% in any quarter (versus 34.3% in Q2'26).
4. Diluted share count rising more than 10% year on year, or any new equity-linked consideration to customers above $250M.
5. Trailing-twelve-month FCF below zero for two consecutive quarters while revenue is still growing.
