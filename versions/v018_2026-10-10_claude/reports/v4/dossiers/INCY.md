# INCY — Incyte Corporation

## 1. Verdict
**WATCH** (12–36 month horizon; revisit as a full position candidate if ex-one-off growth holds up through 2027). Reported Q2 2026 growth (+38% revenue, +45% net income) is meaningfully inflated by a one-time non-cash CMS-settlement benefit in Opzelura; on a cleaner run-rate basis growth is good but not extraordinary, and it is happening while the clock runs on Jakafi's 2028–29 patent cliff, which today's price already seems to roughly reflect.

## 2. Business in plain English
Incyte is a profitable biotech: it discovers, makes, and sells prescription drugs, primarily Jakafi/Jakafi XR (ruxolitinib, for myelofibrosis/polycythemia vera/GVHD), Opzelura (topical, for eczema/vitiligo), and a growing hematology/oncology portfolio (e.g., Monjuvi, Niktimvo). It makes money the standard pharma way — patent-protected pricing on specialty drugs sold mainly in the US, distributed via specialty pharmacies and hospitals.

## 3. Why the model likes it / durability
b1 shows strong momentum and quality (ROE 25.5%, revenue growth signal high) which the triage read as "real new-launch momentum, not just cost cuts." Confirmed to a point: Opzelura ex-one-off grew 24% YoY and hematology/oncology grew 69% — genuine launch-driven growth. But the reported headline (38% revenue, 44.6% net income growth) is **not fully durable**: management itself disclosed that Opzelura's reported $450m in Q2 2026 net sales includes a one-time non-cash benefit tied to a CMS settlement, and excluding it Opzelura sales were $204m (+24% YoY, not +173%). The composite quant score, built off reported (not adjusted) revenue, is picking up some of this one-off.

## 8. Bull case
1. Heme/onc portfolio (+69% YoY, still small) and Opzelura ex-one-off (+24% YoY) show Incyte can generate real growth beyond Jakafi, which derisks the eventual patent cliff somewhat.
2. Debt-free balance sheet with growing equity gives full flexibility for M&A/buybacks/dividend initiation without needing external financing.
3. Multiple confidential settlements already reached with generic challengers (e.g., Hikma, Oct 2025/Feb 2026) suggest Incyte is managing the patent-cliff timeline actively (e.g., negotiated entry dates) rather than facing an abrupt cliff.

## 9. Bear case
1. Jakafi (~63% of FY2026 guided net sales) faces ANDA challenges from multiple generic manufacturers (Dr. Reddy's, Alkem, Alembic, MSN, Eugia) with an estimated generic launch as early as March 2029 — a known, dated, and large overhang on ~2/3 of revenue.
2. This quarter's headline growth (+38% revenue, +45% net income) is significantly inflated by a one-time non-cash CMS settlement item; the market may be capitalizing a run-rate that doesn't exist once that benefit rolls off.
3. Jakafi itself only grew 7% YoY in Q2 2026 — already showing the deceleration you'd expect as it matures and management leans on newer, smaller products to carry growth.

## 10. Key risks & kill criteria
1. Jakafi net sales growth turns negative YoY for two consecutive quarters (ahead-of-schedule cliff signal).
2. Opzelura net sales, excluding any further one-time items, decelerate below 15% YoY for two consecutive quarters.
3. Any additional generic-manufacturer ANDA litigation loss or settlement that moves the earliest generic entry date materially earlier than March 2029.
4. Heme/onc portfolio growth falls below 30% YoY for two consecutive quarters (loses its role as the "second engine").
5. Full-year net sales guidance is cut (vs the raised $5.13–5.26bn range) at any subsequent quarter.
