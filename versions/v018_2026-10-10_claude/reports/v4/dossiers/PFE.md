# PFE - Pfizer Inc. (NYSE: PFE) - F162 diligence dossier (wave 8, STANDARD depth)

**1. Verdict: WATCH, thesis horizon 12-36 months.** Reason: at $28.67 (25 Sep 2026) the shares trade at ~9.9x 2026 adjusted EPS guidance with a 6.0% dividend, but the price already implies about 2% a year of free cash flow growth for ten years against a flat-to-slightly-falling base case once patent expiries and US price resets bite; dividend cover from free cash flow is only ~1.1x with $63bn of debt and no buybacks. Condition to upgrade: evidence that launched and acquired products can fill the post-2027 gap (operational growth of that group at or above 10% for two more quarters, a Phase 3 obesity or oncology readout that supports 2029+ revenue) or a price near $24-25 (about 8.5x). The triage (not advanced; quality 2, growth 1) is confirmed; the "weak organic pipeline productivity" and "leverage" flags are supported by the filings, but the leverage is moderate (consolidated net debt ~$51.5bn, ~2x EBITDA cross-check).

**2. Business.** Pfizer sells prescription medicines and vaccines worldwide (Eliquis, Vyndaqel family, Prevnar, Padcev, Ibrance, Xtandi and others, plus COVID products Comirnaty and Paxlovid) and contract manufacturing (Pfizer CentreOne). Moat: global scale, oncology (Seagen) and cardiology franchises, but dependence on products facing price controls (US Inflation Reduction Act negotiation) and generics.

**3. Why the model likes it.** Value and cash-return families (forward P/E 9.9x, dividend yield 6.0%, low beta 0.3 per d4); weak quality and growth ranks (b1). The cheapness is partly structural (known expiries, pricing policy), so I treat it as a fair discount for flat earnings rather than a mispricing.
