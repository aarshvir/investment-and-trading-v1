# KMI - Kinder Morgan, Inc. (diligence agent F166, wave 8)

**1. Verdict: WATCH** (thesis horizon 12-36 months). A high-quality, fee-based gas-pipeline owner with a record first half and a raised outlook, but at $30.75 the shares already price about 5% a year of free-cash-flow growth for ten years, versus about 3.5% that the backlog supports. Condition to upgrade: a price near $27 (implied growth equals my base case) or evidence that FY26 beats are repeatable rather than weather-driven.

Triage (Q04: "no", "forward EPS growth is flat") partly overturned: management now guides 2026 Adjusted EPS at least 12% above the $1.36 budget (about $1.52, +17% on 2025's roughly $1.30), so EPS growth is not flat in 2026. The WATCH call rests on valuation, not growth.

**2. Business.** Largest US natural-gas pipeline network (more than 65,000 miles, over 700 Bcf working storage, Q1 release), plus refined-products pipelines, terminals (Jones Act tankers) and CO2. Revenue is mostly take-or-pay or fee-based with creditworthy shippers. Demand drivers are LNG feed-gas, power generation and exports to Mexico. Five major gas systems ran at 90% utilization in 2025 vs 74% in 2016 (Q1 release).

**3. Why the model likes it.** b1: rank 480 of ~480 live (composite_qvm 0.03); value family 0.32 (ebit/ev 5.3%, fcf yield 4.6%), quality 0.06, SUE +1.2 (earnings surprises). The value and surprise legs are partly weather (winter storm Fern lifted Q1). Durable base, cyclical help in H1.
