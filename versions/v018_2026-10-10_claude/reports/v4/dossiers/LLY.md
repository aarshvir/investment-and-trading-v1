# Eli Lilly and Company (NYSE: LLY) — Diligence Dossier

**Agent:** F24 | **As of:** 2026-09-25 close ($1,183.46) | **Depth:** Standard

## 1. Verdict

**INCLUDE-SMALL** — 24-36 month horizon; reservation: valuation now prices in a full decade of mid-teens FCF growth, and the position size should reflect that a premium multiple this large amplifies any deceleration or US drug-pricing shock. The triage's growth framing is strongly confirmed by primary filings; its **26.5x NTM P/E claim is not confirmed** by this pass (see §7 — a real data conflict, not a rounding difference).

## 2. Business

Eli Lilly is a global pharmaceutical company. Its incretin franchise — Mounjaro (tirzepatide, diabetes) and Zepbound (tirzepatide, obesity) — now drives the large majority of growth, alongside an established diabetes/oncology/immunology base and a late-stage pipeline (oral GLP-1 orforglipron, filed for obesity/diabetes in 2026).

## 3. Why the model liked it, and is it durable

b1 scored LLY on quality and growth (ROE, gross profit/assets, momentum) squarely on the incretin ramp. **Durable so far by the evidence**: revenue and guidance have both accelerated for three consecutive quarters (Q4'25 → Q1'26 → Q2'26), not decelerated — the opposite pattern from BSX/SYK in this batch. The main durability question is not the current quarter but whether mid-teens *compounding* is achievable for the next decade at this base (see §7).

## 7. Valuation (V1 row: none — `v4/outputs/v1_valuation_table.csv` has no LLY row; **v1_verdict = null**)

Trailing GAAP P/E ≈ 41.7x. On the company's own raised FY26 non-GAAP EPS guide midpoint ($36.00), forward P/E ≈ **32.9x**. **This is a material data conflict with the triage**, which cited "26.5x NTM P/E" — my figure, built directly from the current close and the company's own just-raised guidance, is roughly 6 points higher. I could not reconcile the triage's 26.5x to any guidance vintage in this pass (even the initial Feb-2026 guide midpoint of $34.25 gives ~34.6x); flagging as an unresolved `data_conflict` rather than asserting either number is wrong.

**Reverse DCF** (`scripts/valuation.py`; TTM FCF $18,190m, WACC 7.5%, terminal growth 4.0%, 10-yr stage-1): **implied FCF growth ≈ 13.2%/yr for 10 years**. This is the correct comparison horizon (a decade), and it is a **demanding, not modest**, assumption for a company this large — it requires GLP-1/obesity to remain one of the largest and most durable drug categories in history for ten straight years. My base case for the *same 10-year horizon* (front-loaded high growth 2026-2029 fading toward high-single-digit by the mid-2030s as the category matures and competition/pricing pressure build) implies roughly ~15%/yr average — modestly **above** the market-implied rate → **implied_vs_base = below**. This satisfies the INCLUDE/INCLUDE-SMALL growth test, but the margin of safety is thin given how far out the assumption reaches; sized as INCLUDE-SMALL rather than INCLUDE for that reason.

**Scenarios (3-yr annualised, prob-weighted from `valuation.py`):**
- Bear (30%): GLP-1 competitive intensity rises (Novo Nordisk, oral entrants, compounding), US pricing policy (MFN/IRA) bites; FY28 non-GAAP EPS ~$42, exit 22x → **≈ −8.0%/yr**
- Base (45%): orforglipron launches successfully, international uptake continues, growth moderates to ~20%/yr; FY28 non-GAAP EPS ~$52, exit 28x → **≈ +7.1%/yr**
- Bull (25%): obesity/GLP-1 TAM proves larger than modelled, capacity buildout pays off in margin; FY28 non-GAAP EPS ~$60, exit 32x → **≈ +17.5%/yr**

## 8. Bull case
1. Guidance has been raised in both updates since the initial FY26 guide, with revenue growth accelerating (48-56% YoY) for three straight quarters — the strongest, cleanest momentum in this batch.
2. The ~$10bn/yr capacity capex is a disclosed, deliberate investment in an under-supplied, still-growing category (obesity), not a distress signal.
3. Orforglipron (oral GLP-1) broadens the addressable market beyond injectable-tolerant patients if approved.

## 9. Bear case
1. At ~33x forward earnings on the company's own guidance, the stock has very little room for a growth disappointment — and the reverse DCF shows the market is already pricing in a full decade of mid-teens FCF growth, a historically rare feat.
2. US drug-pricing policy risk is real and multi-fronted: a $220m False Claims Act judgment (Medicaid rebate misreporting) was left standing by the Supreme Court in 2026, and Lilly faces active 340B-pricing litigation (Tampa General Hospital suit, 2026-07) — none individually thesis-breaking, but indicative of sustained political/pricing pressure on the category that carries the growth.
3. The capex-heavy build-out means FCF conversion (~68% of NI) is already below typical mega-cap-pharma norms; a demand disappointment after this much capacity is committed would hit margins harder than at a less capital-intensive competitor.

## 10. Kill criteria (measurable — the specific, observable conditions that would invalidate this thesis)
1. Combined Mounjaro + Zepbound quarterly revenue growth decelerates **below 25% YoY** for two consecutive quarters.
2. FY guidance (revenue or non-GAAP EPS) is **cut** versus the prior range — would be the first cut since the current GLP-1 ramp began.
3. A US government pricing action (MFN/IRA-related) cuts realized US GLP-1 net pricing by **more than 15%** beyond what is already embedded in guidance.
4. Net debt / TTM net income rises **above 1.75x** (from ~1.46x **[Corrected 2026-10-08: 1.72x on total debt incl. $7,050m current debt]**) without a clearly disclosed capacity-linked justification.
5. Orforglipron fails to win FDA approval, or a pivotal readout shows a material efficacy/safety setback.
