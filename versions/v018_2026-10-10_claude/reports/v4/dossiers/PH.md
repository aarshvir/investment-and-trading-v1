# PH - Parker-Hannifin - Diligence Dossier (Agent F131, Wave-8 Standard)

## 1. Verdict
**WATCH** (12-36 month horizon). A first-class industrial-aerospace compounder, but at $978.33 (25 Sep 2026 close) the price implies more 10-year cash-flow growth (about 8.0% a year, pro forma for the $11.8bn of acquisitions) than my evidence-based base case (about 5.6% a year); even my bull case values the enterprise below today's price. Condition that would change it: a pullback of roughly 20% (toward $780), or evidence that the Filtration Group deal lifts organic growth and margins well above the 30% adjusted margin target. The triage said WATCH on multiple alone (26.8x NTM P/E); primary filings confirm the business quality (triage quality 5 is fair) and add a new fact the triage missed: leverage has just stepped up sharply.

## 2. Business in plain English
Parker sells motion and control parts (hydraulics, pneumatics, filtration, sealing, aerospace flight controls and fuel systems) in two segments: Diversified Industrial (FY26 sales $14,438m, industrial and mobile machinery, through distributors and direct) and Aerospace Systems (FY26 sales $7,061m, commercial and military aircraft original equipment plus a high-margin aftermarket). Parts are specified into customer equipment and then replaced for decades, so revenue is sticky and aftermarket-rich; the company runs "The Win Strategy" operating system and has lifted adjusted segment margin from 25.1% (FY25) to 27.3% (FY26) **[Corrected 2026-10-08: 25.1% is the Diversified Industrial FY25 margin; the company total was 26.1% in FY25 (the section 4 table is right) (DV33)]**. Competitive position: number-one or -two in most niches, deep distributor network; not a monopoly and industrial end markets are cyclical.

## 8. Bull and bear
Bull: (1) Aerospace Systems is compounding at a 13% organic rate with an $8.5bn backlog and an 18% rolling order rate, and the company just raised its 2031 adjusted margin target to "30% by fiscal 2031"; (2) Filtration Group adds aftermarket-heavy (about 85% of sales per the deal announcement, secondary source) recurring revenue in life sciences and HVAC; (3) four straight guidance raises and a consistent beat pattern.
Bear: (1) the price already discounts about 8% annual FCF growth for a decade while the company's own target is "4 to 6% organic growth"; (2) leverage is rising from 1.3x to about 3.0x pro forma with BBB+ at S&P and a 0.65 covenant, buybacks fall from $1.3bn a year while the company integrates two large deals at 19.6x EBITDA; (3) FY27 guide excludes the two deals, so reported FY27 numbers will blend in deal accounting (inventory step-up, amortisation) and lower GAAP margins.

## 9. Key risks and kill criteria
1. Consolidated debt to debt-plus-shareholders'-equity ratio (10-K/10-Q covenant definition, Parker-Hannifin consolidated) above 0.60 at any quarter-end (0.36 at 30 Jun 2026; covenant maximum 0.65), or a downgrade of S&P below BBB.
2. Organic sales growth below 3% for two consecutive quarters (4Q FY26 +8.0%; FY27 guide "Organic sales growth of 5.5% to 8.5%").
3. FY27 adjusted EPS guidance (ex-acquisitions) cut below the $34.25 low end, or adjusted segment operating margin below 26.5% for two consecutive quarters (FY26 27.3%; FY27 guide "27.5% to 27.9% on an adjusted basis").
4. Rolling 12-month total order growth below 0% in either segment (Parker +12% and Aerospace +18% in 4Q FY26).
5. Filtration Group or CIRCOR integration: any goodwill impairment above $500m, or CIRCOR not closed by 30 Jun 2027 (expected second half of calendar 2026 per the 10-K acc. 0000076334-26-000105).
