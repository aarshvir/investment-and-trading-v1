# Vistra Corp. (VST) — Diligence Dossier

**Agent:** F50 · Wave 3 · Standard depth · **As of:** 2026-09-25 close ($138.46/share; mkt cap ≈$46.5bn per `v4/data/b1_live_scores.csv`)

## 1. Verdict
**WATCH.** Thesis horizon 12–24 months for a re-entry decision. This is a genuinely strong, well-run merchant generator with confirmed guidance beats, real hyperscaler demand, moderate leverage and an aggressive buyback — but on a leverage-adjusted (EV/EBITDA) reverse-DCF the current price already prices in more than a conservative, filed-evidence base case delivers. Per the wave-3 instruction: a great business priced for more than it can deliver is WATCH, not INCLUDE.

## 2. Business in plain English
Vistra is an integrated merchant power generator and retail electricity provider, concentrated in ERCOT (Texas) with growing gas, nuclear and battery-storage positions in PJM, ISO-NE, NYISO and CAISO after two 2025–26 acquisitions (Lotus, Cogentrix pending). It sells electricity and capacity into competitive wholesale markets and to retail customers; unlike a regulated utility it bears commodity price risk directly, hedged through forward sales and derivatives.

## 3. Why the model likes it / is it durable
Q15 triage scored VST quality 3, growth 5, price_vs_growth 4, framing the ~30–36% pullback from 2026 highs as fear-driven and citing "EBITDA and guidance keep beating" and a PJM price collar ($175–$325/MW-day) capping upside. b1 factor data shows VST decile 1 (top decile composite), `pct_mom_12_1`=0.37 (moderate momentum), high `vol_1y` (49%) and `mktcap_flag` empty (i.e., large-cap, full coverage). **This dossier confirms the core facts primary-source**: Vistra's July-2026 PJM capacity auction for planning year 2028–2029 cleared at exactly **$325.00/MW-day across every zone** where VST has capacity (RTO, ComEd, DEOK, EMAAC, MAAC, ATSI, DOM; 10,924 MW total cleared) — the 10-Q's own table (filed 2026-08-10) is the primary-source confirmation of the collar/cap dynamic the triage cited from secondary press. The growth (Lotus +2,600 MW closed Oct-2025; Cogentrix +5,500 MW pending, FERC-approved Aug-2026; AWS/Meta/Helix data-center-adjacent deals) is real and well-documented, not an artefact. What the triage under-weighted is that **the stock is not merely "de-rated on fear" — it is still priced at a premium to the sector's normal merchant multiple** (see §7), so much of the good news is already reflected.

## 8. Bull case / Bear case
**Bull:** (1) Guidance beaten in FY25 and maintained twice in 2026, with real, signed additional capacity (Cogentrix, FERC-approved Aug-2026) and demand (AWS ~3,800 MW nuclear PPA at Comanche Peak, Meta 2,600+ MW across PJM) **excluded** from current guidance — genuine embedded optionality; (2) leverage is moderate (net debt/EBITDA ≈2.9x) versus NRG's post-acquisition leverage, giving more balance-sheet room to execute Cogentrix without a de-rating; (3) the July-2026 PJM auction clearing at the $325/MW-day cap across every zone VST bid into is a primary-source-confirmed, near-certain revenue floor through the 2028-29 planning year.

**Bear:** (1) EV/EBITDA at ~9.1x is well above the sector's normal merchant-generation range (4–7x); a re-rating toward that historical band — plausible once the AI/data-center narrative cools or a PJM capacity-market reform lowers the effective cap — would produce a large price decline even if EBITDA keeps growing; (2) the PJM cap is a ceiling as well as a floor: "clearing at the cap in every zone" means realized capacity prices cannot go higher from here without a market-design change, capping the upside case exactly where the bull case most wants it; (3) the Moss Landing remediation cost estimate has already grown once (+$65m in a single quarter) — a reminder that "resolved" incident costs can still move adversely.

## 9. Key risks & kill criteria (what would break the thesis / thesis-invalidation triggers)
1. EV/EBITDA (as computed in this dossier, §7) re-rates below 6.5x without a corresponding EBITDA increase — i.e., a de-rating event, not a fundamentals event.
2. FY26 ongoing Adjusted EBITDA guidance ($6.8–7.6bn) cut at any release (currently reaffirmed twice).
3. Cogentrix transaction fails to close by mid-2027 (guided "late 2026") or its financing materially increases net debt/EBITDA above 3.5x.
4. Net debt/Adjusted EBITDA (per this dossier's method, §6) rises above 3.5x for two consecutive quarters (currently ≈2.9x).
5. The Meta/AWS/Helix data-center-adjacent deals fail to convert into guidance-additive, signed long-term contracts within 18 months of this dossier's date.
