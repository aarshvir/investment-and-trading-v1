# PG — The Procter & Gamble Company

## 1. Verdict
**INCLUDE-SMALL** (24–48 month horizon), with a named reservation: **organic growth has decelerated to ~1% for FY2026 and was flat (0%) in the most recent quarter, while operating margin compressed ~220bps YoY in that same quarter** — a real deceleration the triage's "modest 1.5% revenue growth as expected" framing understated. This **modifies** (does not overturn) the triage's "advance": the quality/moat case still holds, but full weight is not warranted while growth and margin trend the wrong way.

## 2. Business in plain English
P&G sells branded household and personal-care staples — Tide, Pampers, Gillette, Charmin, Crest, Olay — through ten market-leading category positions sold mostly to large retailers worldwide. It makes money on scale (R&D, media buying, supply chain) and pricing power that let it defend margins through commodity cycles better than smaller peers. Its competitive position is best understood as a portfolio of #1-or-#2 share brands in daily-use categories with low elasticity of demand.

## 3. Why the model likes it — durable or artefact?
b1 flags PG as Quality 5 / Growth 3 / Price-vs-Growth 3 (composite decile 3, quintile 2 — mid-pack, not a top decile pick; `pct_gp_a`≈0.53, `pct_roe`≈0.79 — solidly profitable but not extreme). This is a quality/stability signal, durable by nature of the category (staples demand is inelastic), not an artefact of a one-off. The triage's "no red flags" call is fair on quality grounds but did not weigh the FY2026 growth/margin deceleration documented below, which is the main thing this dossier adds.

## 7. Valuation snapshot and reverse DCF

At the 25 Sep 2026 close of **$146.23** (mkt cap ≈$339.9bn per `b1_live_scores.csv`, implying ≈2,324.6M shares), core P/E on FY2026's $6.89 core EPS is **21.2x**, matching the triage's "fair 21x" characterization. FCF yield = $15,147M / $339.9bn ≈ **4.46%**.

I ran a simplified two-stage reverse DCF (10 years of FCF growth at rate *g*, 3% terminal growth, ~7.3% cost of equity appropriate for PG's low-beta staples profile) and solved for the *g* implied by the current price: **≈2.2–2.8% aggregate FCF growth**. Layering in PG's ~1.5%/year buyback-driven share-count reduction gives an implied **per-share** FCF/EPS growth of roughly **3.5–4.5%** — close to what the business is actually delivering (organic sales +1%, core EPS +1%, plus buyback lift). **My view: valuation is "in_line"**, i.e. neither cheap nor demanding. **[Corrected 2026-10-08: with the FY2027 guide (core EPS in line to +3%, midpoint +1.5% including buybacks, FCF productivity guided 85-90% versus 94%) aggregate cash flow is flat to down in FY27, so the 3.0%-3.4% a year implied at Ke 7.58% (5.17% + Blume beta 0.583 x 4.14%, SBC $524M deducted) exceeds what the company guides; implied_vs_base is above and the verdict moves to WATCH. That means there is little valuation cushion if organic growth or margins deteriorate further from here.

## 8. Bull case / Bear case
**Bull:** (1) Ten #1-or-#2-share categories with genuine pricing power have historically absorbed cost shocks within 2–3 quarters — the FY26 Q4 headwind is plausibly transitory; (2) balance sheet supports continued ~$15bn/year buybacks+dividends regardless of a soft patch, which mechanically supports per-share EPS even if organic growth stays low; (3) valuation is not demanding (21x core EPS, implied FCF growth of only ~2–3%) — little needs to go right for the stock to hold its multiple.

**Bear:** (1) Organic growth went from "in-line to 4%" guided to **flat in the most recent quarter** — a real deceleration, not noise, and the second consecutive quarter of margin compression; (2) the $150M cost headwind was framed as transitory but has now shown up for two quarters running (Q3 and Q4 FY26) with compounding basis-point damage (-150bps then -220bps); (3) capital returned to shareholders now slightly exceeds FCF generated, leaving no cushion if growth disappoints again — the next negative surprise has to be funded by leverage, not spare cash flow.

## 9. Key risks & kill criteria
1. Organic sales growth is **negative** for any full quarter (currently 0%, i.e. one step from this trigger).
2. Operating margin compression exceeds **-200bps YoY** for **two consecutive quarters** (it already has, in FY26 Q2 and Q4 — this is close to breached and should be watched every quarter from here).
3. Core EPS growth guidance for FY2027 is issued **below** "in-line to +4%" (i.e. a guide-down at the very next annual guidance-setting release). **[Corrected 2026-10-08: this trigger has already occurred. The FY2027 core EPS guide (in line to +3%) is below the in-line to +4% test (DV33)]**
4. Consolidated long-term debt rises **above $26bn** without a corresponding acquisition that adds accretive growth (signals funding shareholder returns with leverage rather than FCF).
5. FCF/NI conversion falls **below 85%** for a full fiscal year (currently 94%).
