## 2. Business in plain English
Bio-Techne sells the "ingredients" and instruments scientists use in biology labs: proteins, antibodies and assay kits (R&D Systems, the Protein Sciences segment, about 72% of sales), spatial-biology instruments and diagnostics controls (the Diagnostics and Spatial Biology segment, about 28%). Customers are large pharma, biotech and universities, which reorder consumables. Margins are high (adjusted operating margin about 32%) and growth has stalled: fiscal 2026 revenue was flat at $1,215.0M with flat organic growth.

## 8. Bull and bear
**Bull:** (1) Shareholders (77.8% of outstanding shares voted for) and the HSR clock have cleared the two largest hurdles; Merck KGaA is a strategic buyer with a $576M reverse fee. (2) Large-pharma demand has grown double digits for six quarters (Q3 FY26 release) and FY26 adjusted margins expanded. (3) Possible competing interest: two other strategic parties expressed interest before (proxy background), though the no-shop limits it.
**Bear:** (1) Asymmetric payoff: +$0.39 if it closes vs about -$15 to -$18 if it breaks. (2) Remaining non-US antitrust or investment-screening reviews could run toward the 25 Mar 2027 outside date; closing timing is uncertain. (3) Standalone fundamentals are weak (flat organic growth in FY26, high multiple on standalone value), so a break has no fundamental floor near $73.

## 9. Key risks and kill criteria (measurable thesis-invalidation triggers)
