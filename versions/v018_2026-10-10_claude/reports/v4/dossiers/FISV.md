# FISV - Fiserv, Inc. (NASDAQ: FISV) - F162 diligence dossier (wave 8, STANDARD depth)

**1. Verdict: WATCH, thesis horizon 12-36 months.** Reason: the price ($46.48 on 25 Sep 2026) discounts a steady shrinkage of roughly 4-5% a year in free cash flow, far below my base case, but the operating trend is still worsening (organic revenue -4% in Q1, -5% in Q2 2026; Financial Solutions -8%), management has churned and the guidance was cut three months after being reaffirmed. Condition to upgrade to INCLUDE-SMALL: two consecutive quarters with organic revenue no worse than -2% and adjusted operating margin at or above 31%, with FY26 adjusted EPS tracking at or above $7.20. The triage verdict (not advanced) is confirmed, but one triage fact is wrong: guidance was cut once in 2026 (6 Aug), not twice; the 5 May release reaffirmed it.

**2. Business.** Fiserv sells core account-processing and digital banking software to banks and credit unions (Financial Solutions) and card acceptance, the Clover point-of-sale platform and e-commerce to merchants (Merchant Solutions). Revenue is largely recurring processing fees. Moat: switching costs in bank core systems, installed base, merchant distribution through banks. Weakness now visible: the Financial segment is shrinking and Clover growth has stalled.

**3. Why the model likes it.** Value families dominate (d4 forward P/E 5.8x; FCF yield ~16%); momentum and revisions are poor (b1). The cheapness is real, but it is the market pricing falling earnings, so it is partly an artefact of a downgrade cycle, not durable evidence of mispricing.
