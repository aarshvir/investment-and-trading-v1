**2. Business in plain English.** Moderna makes vaccines and medicines from messenger RNA. Revenue is almost all respiratory vaccines: Spikevax and mNEXSPIKE (COVID), mRESVIA (RSV), and, since 5 Aug 2026, mFLUSIVA (flu; FDA approval on 5 Aug 2026 per press reports, no filing found; the 31 Jul release still called it a pending PDUFA date of 5 Aug). Sales are lumpy and seasonal (about 55% of H2 2026 revenue falls in Q3). Pipeline value rests on intismeran (individualised cancer vaccine with Merck, nine Phase 2/3 trials), rare-disease programmes and combination vaccines. The moat is the platform and manufacturing scale; the weakness is that COVID demand fell from $19.3bn of total revenue in 2022 (10-K XBRL) to $1.8bn of 2025 product sales.

The only durable-looking signal is price momentum, which is an artefact of a low base and of the September cancer-data run; it is a reason for caution, not for owning.
