# LRCX — Lam Research Corporation (diligence agent F29, standard depth)

## 1. Verdict
**WATCH** (12–36 month thesis horizon). Lam is a genuinely excellent, share-gaining wafer-fab-equipment (WFE) franchise riding a real AI/leading-edge/HBM-packaging capex wave with record margins — but at $315.21 (25-Sep-2026 close) the stock is priced, on V4's own systematic reverse DCF, for **sustained growth roughly 2.5–4x its own trailing history**, and the triage's "override to advance" rationale (net-cash balance sheet, ~8% required growth) does not match V1's own math. I side with V1's "excessive" verdict, not the triage override.

## 2. Business in plain English
Lam Research makes wafer-fab-equipment — principally etch and deposition tools — used by memory and logic chipmakers to build semiconductors. It is one of a handful of companies (with Applied Materials, KLA, Tokyo Electron, ASML) that customers have no real alternative to for advanced nodes and, increasingly, HBM/advanced-packaging manufacturing steps. Roughly a quarter of revenue still comes from China, a share that has been shrinking under US export controls.

## 7. Valuation — reconciling with V1
V1 (`outputs/v1_valuation.json`, priced 2026-09-25): **verdict "excessive"** (score −0.881), reverse-DCF FCFF, WACC 11.8%. NTM P/E **31.4x**, at the **80th percentile of its own 177-month (14.75-year) history** — i.e., this is one of the more expensive points in Lam's own trading history, not a discount. V1's implied 10-year FCFF growth is **48.5%/yr**, against a **historical 5-year CAGR of only 9.7%** and **10-year CAGR of 14.7%**, and even the sell-side's own next-fiscal-year consensus growth is 18.1% — a fraction of the 48.5%/yr implied. V1's base case (20.3% assumed growth, 20.4x exit multiple) still produces a **3-year annualised return of −20.2%**, its bull case (28.1% growth) only reaches +2.4%/yr, and its street-flag again notes the base case sits below the Street's own 12-month low target ($290 vs $315.21 spot).
**My reconciliation:** the triage's "override to advance" argument (net-cash balance sheet and "net-cash-implied required growth of ~8%") looks like it referenced a different, easier hurdle than V1's actual reverse-DCF framework — the ~8% figure resembles a bond-like "growth needed to cover the multiple via earnings yield alone" heuristic, not the 10-year FCFF-growth-at-WACC reverse DCF V1 (and this dossier) actually solve for. On the harder, filing-grounded test, **implied growth (48.5%/yr) is well ABOVE any defensible base case (my own base case: high-teens to low-20s%/yr, i.e., roughly consensus-to-slightly-above, allowing 2–3 more years of AI/HBM-driven WFE strength before the capex cycle normalises)**. `valuation_view_vs_v1.implied_vs_base = "above"`.

## 8. Bull case / Bear case
**Bull:** (1) Genuinely record, company-verified non-GAAP operating margin (38.4% FQ4'26) and gross margin (52.0%) from pricing actions, mix and efficiency, not just volume. (2) HBM/advanced-packaging and leading-edge etch/deposition steps are structurally gaining share of the WFE dollar as chipmakers add process steps — a real, multi-year tailwind, not merely a spending-cycle peak. (3) China revenue mix has fallen from 43% to 26% of sales in three quarters, and the company says Taiwan (27%) and Korea (20%) demand "more than offsets" the loss — i.e., the China de-risking that many feared is already mostly priced through the P&L rather than a future shock.
**Bear:** (1) WFE is the most amplified link in the semiconductor value chain; the forward guide implies the largest single-quarter sequential jump (+20.5%) in the two-year data reviewed — a hard bar to clear twice in a row. (2) A Shanghai-based, state-backed Chinese toolmaker has begun shipping homegrown immersion DUV lithography systems (5 units in 2026, 20 planned for 2027) to SMIC/CXMT/Hua Hong, and the pending US MATCH Act would tighten export controls further — a two-sided China risk (less addressable China revenue, and slowly emerging Chinese-equipment competition at the margin) that is not fully reflected in a 31.4x, 80th-percentile multiple. (3) V1's own reverse DCF, using Lam's own trailing cash flows and a conservative 11.8% WACC, requires the company to more than triple its own 10-year historical growth rate for the next decade to earn the current price.

## 9. Key risks & kill criteria
1. Reported (GAAP or non-GAAP) revenue growth decelerates below **15% YoY** for two consecutive quarters (roughly half the current run-rate).
2. China revenue share stabilises or reverses its decline (i.e., a further adverse export-control tightening) **and** Taiwan/Korea demand fails to keep offsetting it, evidenced by a guidance cut.
3. Non-GAAP gross margin falls back below **48%** (below the FY26 average) for a full quarter.
4. Book-to-bill (or qualitative order commentary) turns negative for two consecutive quarters — the sector's cleanest leading indicator of a WFE downturn.
5. The MATCH Act or a further export-control tightening is enacted with a disclosed revenue impact beyond the ~$600M/year headwind already guided.
