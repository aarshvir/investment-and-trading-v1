# CDW — CDW Corporation (F144, wave 8 standard diligence)

**1. Verdict: INCLUDE-SMALL (half weight), 12–36 month horizon.** A cheap (11.5x NTM P/E, about 6% FCF yield on FY25) technology reseller with real but modest moat, growing sales about 10% with falling gross margin. Named reservation: earnings growth is slow (GAAP EPS flat for three years), cash flow is lumpy (H1 2026 FCF fell 58%), and the hardware cycle may be pulling forward. The triage said cheap but unexceptional; I agree on quality and overturn only the "not a priority" call because the price asks for less growth than I expect.

**One-line thesis:** The biggest US reseller of business computers, software and cloud services is priced for almost no growth while sales are still growing about 10%.

## 2. Business in plain English
CDW buys hardware, software and services from over 1,000 vendors (Microsoft, Cisco, Dell, HP, Apple, Nvidia and others, per the FY25 10-K) and resells them with integration and support to corporate, government, education, healthcare customers and in the UK and Canada. Gross margin is about 20-22% and operating margin about 6.5-8% (non-GAAP 8-9%). The moat is purchasing scale, 250,000+ customers and the technical sales force; vendors and rivals (Insight, SHI, Connection, Amazon Business) make it a modest, not a wide, moat.

## 3. Why the model likes it, and is it durable
b1/d4 flag cheap valuation (11.5x NTM P/E, FCF yield 4.0% TTM on Yahoo data) and buyback yield. Durability: the cheapness is real but partly reflects a low-growth history: GAAP EPS was $8.10 (FY23), $7.97 (FY24), $8.08 (FY25) (XBRL).

## 8. Bull and bear
Bull: (1) infrastructure and AI-related demand keeps sales growth near 10%; (2) $1B buyback authorization adds about 3% a year to EPS; (3) services mix and netted-down revenue lift margin.
Bear: (1) gross margin fell 70 bps in Q2 on hardware mix, and S&A rose 8.6%; (2) DSO 93 days and inventory up 74% since December tie up cash while rates on new debt are 5.7%-6.4%; (3) GAAP EPS has not grown since FY23 and demand may be pull-forward.

## 9. Key risks and kill criteria
1. Non-GAAP operating margin below 7.5% in two consecutive quarters (Q2 26: 8.5%; H1 26: 8.2%).
2. Gross profit dollars down year on year in two consecutive quarters (Q2 26: +6.3%).
3. Consolidated net debt (company definition, release table) over 3.5x TTM non-GAAP operating income (now 2.7x, my calc).
4. FY26 adjusted FCF (company definition) below $700M (FY25 $1,085.5M; H1 26 $278.4M).
5. Sales growth below 3% in any quarter for two consecutive quarters (Q2 26: +10.0%).
