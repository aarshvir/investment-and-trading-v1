# Generac Holdings Inc. (GNRC) — Diligence Dossier (Agent F75, Wave 4)

## 1. Verdict
**WATCH.** Thesis horizon: 12–24 months. Generac has a real, primary-source-verified new growth driver (a large hyperscale data-center backup-power relationship, most recently formalized with Amazon on 2026-09-16), but the stock re-rated ~40% in a single session on that news **[Corrected 2026-10-08: the single-session move on 17 Sep 2026 was +18.3% ($175.11 to $207.23), DV19]** and, on my own reverse DCF, the current price already implies more growth than a fair (not just bearish) base case. Condition to upgrade: confirmed delivery against the 2027–28 order book, or a pullback that restores margin of safety.

## 2. Business in plain English
Generac makes backup power generation equipment: residential standby and portable generators (its historical core, sold through dealers and retail), and a faster-growing Commercial & Industrial (C&I) segment selling industrial generators, energy-storage and grid-services equipment. It is now selling meaningfully into data-center/hyperscale backup power — a new, large addressable market tied to AI-driven electricity demand.

## 3. Why the model likes it — durable or artefact?

## 6. Earnings quality & balance sheet
- **Balance sheet (consolidated, 10-Q as of 2026-06-30, filed 2026-08-04):** cash $264.9M; total debt $1,281.0M (current $32.1M + long-term $1,249.0M); stockholders' equity $2,877.6M; total assets $5,772.2M.
- **Net debt ≈ $1.0bn.** Against FY2025 adjusted EBITDA of ~$716M (17.0% margin × $4.21bn sales), **net debt/adj. EBITDA ≈ 1.4x** — conservative, with headroom for the capacity investment the new hyperscale business will need.
- **FCF:** TTM free cash flow ≈ $289M (fcf_yield 2.4%, d4 snapshot) — modest but positive; capital being redirected into M&A (see below) rather than large buybacks this year.
- **M&A:** $348.5M deployed in H1 2026 across three bolt-on acquisitions (Allmand, Enercon, Wolter Power Systems) expanding C&I/large-genset capacity — funded from the balance sheet.
- **Buybacks paused:** a new $500M, 24-month repurchase authorization was approved 2026-02-09, but **zero shares were repurchased in Q1 or Q2 2026** (2025 saw ~1.1M shares repurchased for $148M). Shares outstanding were 59.0M as of 2026-07-31, down only 0.77% YoY.
- **The Amazon transaction (2026-09-16, post the quarter reported above):** Generac and Amazon signed a Transaction Agreement plus a long-term supply agreement for backup generators for Amazon data centers. Amazon received a warrant for up to 1,693,745 shares (~2.8% of shares outstanding) at a $200.9266 strike, vesting in tranches tied to cumulative gross payments from Amazon up to **$8 billion** through 2033; **initial deliveries are expected to total $2.4 billion in 2027–2028.** Stock rose >40% on the announcement. **[Corrected 2026-10-08: +18.3% on 17 Sep 2026, +18.9% by 25 Sep from the 16 Sep close, DV19]** This is the single largest disclosed item in the file and is the main reason the current share price ($208.14) differs so much from the fundamentals in the table above.

## 7. Valuation snapshot and reverse DCF
NTM P/E ≈ 17.8x (d4 pe_ntm), FY2026 P/E (pe_fy0) 21.4x, FY2027 (pe_fy1) 16.7x, no dividend, beta 1.91. No V1 row exists for GNRC (outside V1's 69-name universe) — `v1_verdict` is null.

**Reverse DCF (own model):** using FY2026E adjusted EPS of ~$8.10 (built from H1 2026 actual $4.71 plus a seasonally-typical H2), an 18x exit multiple (near today's NTM multiple) and a 9.5% cost of equity (beta 1.91), the price of $208.14 requires a **10-year adjusted-EPS CAGR of ≈13.5%.** My own honest **base case is ≈10.5%/yr** — a blend of high-teens growth in 2027–28 (as the confirmed $2.4bn of Amazon deliveries lands) decelerating toward high-single-digits thereafter as the initial ramp normalizes and Residential stays a low-single-digit-to-flat grower. **`implied_vs_base = "above"`** — the market is pricing in more than my base case, largely on the untested remainder of the $8bn framework (only $2.4bn is delivery-dated; the rest is optionality). This is exactly why the verdict is WATCH rather than INCLUDE despite a genuine, verified catalyst.

## 8. Bull case
1. The Amazon supply agreement and warrant are real, SEC-filed, economically aligned (Amazon's warrant vests only as it actually pays Generac), and open a data-center backup-power market far larger than Generac's legacy residential base.
2. Balance sheet is conservative (net debt/adj. EBITDA ~1.4x) with capacity to fund the capex needed to serve confirmed 2027–28 volumes without straining the balance sheet.
3. Legacy securities-litigation overhang from the COVID-era demand normalization and later channel/warranty-reserve allegations has now been resolved in Generac's favor (see §12). **[Corrected 2026-10-08: dismissal with prejudice of 30 Apr 2026 is under appeal; derivative suits and a DOJ/EPA/CARB emissions investigation remain open per the 10-Q, DV19]**

## 9. Bear case
1. Only $2.4bn of the headline "$8bn" Amazon framework is delivery-dated (2027–28); the rest requires the relationship to keep scaling for years, which is unproven.
2. The core Residential segment is still shrinking (-2% YoY in Q2 2026, +1% in Q1) — the "home-standby recovery" the triage flagged for verification has **not** shown up yet; all growth is C&I.
3. My reverse DCF shows the market already pricing more (≈13.5%/yr) than a fair base case (≈10.5%/yr) after the 40% single-day re-rating **[Corrected 2026-10-08: single-day move was +18.3%, DV19]** — limited margin of safety at $208.

## 10. Key risks & kill criteria (measurable)
1. Residential segment net sales decline resumes or deepens for two consecutive quarters.
2. The disclosed data-center/hyperscale order backlog (currently $1.6bn per the Q2 2026 release) fails to grow, or is reduced/cancelled, in a subsequent 10-Q or 8-K.
3. FY2026 adjusted EBITDA margin guidance is cut below the current 20.0%–21.0% range.
4. Net debt/adjusted EBITDA rises above 3.0x (from ~1.4x currently) without a clearly disclosed link to funding confirmed hyperscale capacity.
5. A future filing shows Amazon's cumulative gross payments materially missing the disclosed $2.4bn 2027–2028 initial-delivery plan (warrant-vesting disclosure is the natural tripwire).
