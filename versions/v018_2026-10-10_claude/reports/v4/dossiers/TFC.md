## 2. Business in plain English
Truist is a Southeast and Mid-Atlantic US bank holding company (about $556bn of assets) formed by the 2019 BB&T and SunTrust merger (10-Q acc. 0000092230-26-000099). It takes deposits ($409bn), lends to consumers, small businesses and companies ($329bn average loans), and earns fees from wealth management, cards and treasury services, investment banking and mortgage. Competitive position: a top-10 US bank with a sticky, low-cost retail deposit base, but returns have lagged the best super-regionals and loan spreads are compressing; Mike Lyons became CEO on 1 Sep 2026 (Bill Rogers is executive chair through the 2027 annual meeting, per the 10-Q).

The quotes below are therefore from call transcripts (secondary sources: Motley Fool for 1Q26, Investing.com for 2Q26), not from filings; a fact-checker should confirm against the slides.
- **4Q25 call, 21 Jan 2026 (8-K acc. 0000092230-26-000023):** per the 1Q26 call, prior guidance was, in the summary's wording, "3%-4%" NII growth, "4% to 5%" revenue growth, "1.25%-2.25%" noninterest expense growth, "16.5%" tax rate and "$4 billion" buybacks (prior ranges as stated in the 1Q26 transcript; not seen first-hand).
- **1Q26 call, 17 Apr 2026 (acc. 0000092230-26-000039):** "we now expect 2026 net interest income growth of 2% to 3%" (**cut** from 3%-4%); "we are modestly refining our revenue outlook to the low end of the prior 4% to 5% range" (**trimmed**); "we now expect full year GAAP noninterest expense to increase approximately 1.75% in 2026" (from 1.25%-2.25%, midpoint 1.75%, unchanged midpoint); "there is no change in our expectations for net charge-offs to be about 55 basis points in 2026"; "we now expect our effective tax rate to approximate 14.5%" (**better**, from 16.5%); "we're now targeting $5 billion of share repurchases in '26" (**raised** from $4bn). ## 8. Bull and bear
Bull: (1) consolidated ROTCE rose to 15.4% in 2Q26 with efficiency at 58.0% and investment-banking income +72% year on year; (2) capital return is about 13% of market cap a year with CET1 still at 10.9% and $7.7bn of authorisation left; (3) loan growth is positive (+5.7% average year on year) with charge-offs and the ALLL ratio falling.
Bear: (1) NII guidance ("1% to 1.5%") cut at consecutive calls, deposit mix shifting to higher-yielding products and loan spread compression: the revenue base is growing at only 3.5%-4%; (2) 2Q26 earnings include a $106m quarter-on-quarter rise in other income, so run-rate ROTCE may be nearer 14%; (3) a new CEO since 1 Sep 2026 may reset targets, and credit (NPLs up to $1.69bn, commercial real estate, consumer) is late-cycle.

## 9. Key risks and kill criteria
1. CET1 ratio (Truist Financial Corporation consolidated, 10-Q/10-K capital ratio table) below 10.0% at any quarter-end before the end of 2027 (10.9% at 30 Jun 2026).
2. ROTCE (company non-GAAP, earnings release) below 13.0% for two consecutive quarters (15.4% in 2Q26, 13.8% in 1Q26).
3. Full-year 2026 net interest income growth guidance cut below 1.0%, or revenue growth guidance cut below 3.0% (now "1% to 1.5%" and "3.5%-4%" per the 2Q26 transcript).
4. Net charge-off ratio above 0.75% in any quarter, or nonperforming loans above 0.75% of loans HFI (0.50% and 0.51% in 2Q26).
5. NIM-TE below 2.85% (2.98% in 2Q26) or average deposits down more than 2% year on year (up 1.1% in 2Q26).
