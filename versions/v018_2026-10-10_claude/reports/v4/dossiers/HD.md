# HD — Home Depot, Inc. (Agent F80, wave 5, standard depth)

## 1. Verdict
**WATCH** — best-in-class home-improvement retailer with a credible Pro-market growth lever, but the 25 Sep 2026 close
(~$293.20) already prices in a housing-turnover recovery that has not shown up in the comp numbers yet. Horizon 12–24 months;
revisit on a comp-sales inflection or a pullback that restores margin of safety.

## 2. Business in plain English
Home Depot is the largest home-improvement retailer in the US, selling building materials, décor and tools to DIY
homeowners and, increasingly, professional contractors (~$100B+ addressable Pro market) through ~2,350 warehouse stores
plus the SRS Distribution/GMS trade-distribution platforms acquired in FY2024–25. It earns money on merchandise margin
(~33%) and Pro-services scale; its moat is store density, supply-chain scale and now a national Pro-distribution network
that DIY-only rivals (Lowe's) lack outright.

## 3. Why the model likes it / durability
b1_live_scores.csv (as_of 2026-09-25): quality factors are elite — gp_a 0.51 (pct 0.60), roe 0.856 (pct 0.89), fam_Q
percentile 0.56 — but the value/momentum families are weak (composite decile 3, quintile 2; mom_12_1 pct 0.38). This is a
quality-factor name, not a value or momentum name: the model likes HD for capital efficiency and margin stability, which
is durable (decades of consistent ROIC), not an artefact — but that quality is already reflected in the price, which is
the valuation agent's job and where this dossier pushes back (Section 7).

## 7. Valuation — reconciliation with V1

- **Reverse-DCF sense (qualitative, 25 Sep 2026 close):** at a ~4.3% FCF yield and a retail-appropriate WACC of ~8%, the
  price implies perpetual FCF growth of roughly **3.5–4%** (WACC minus FCF yield) **[Corrected 2026-10-09: [Corrected: at Ke 9.47% (5.17% + 4.5% x beta 0.955) and SBC-adjusted FCF the implied perpetual growth is ~5.0-5.1%]]** — i.e. the market is pricing HD for
  low-single-digit *durable* growth, not a housing-recovery re-acceleration. That bar looks *achievable* given Pro/SRS

  zero for another 2–3 quarters while multiple holds at ~20x, the implied growth assumption becomes stretched relative to

- **implied_vs_base:** "in_line" — my base case (mid-single-digit revenue growth from Pro/SRS, DIY roughly flat, modest
  margin recovery) sits close to what the ~20x multiple implies; it is not obviously cheap or expensive, but it is not
  the "best-in-class way to play a housing recovery" bargain the triage note assumed — comp growth to date does not
  support that framing yet (see kill criterion #1).

## 8. Bull case
1. Pro/SRS/GMS platform gives HD a distribution moat DIY-only peers cannot replicate; Pro outperforming DIY comps [1][2].
2. Eventual mean-reversion in existing-home turnover (multi-year low) is optionality not yet in guidance.
3. FCF conversion (~89% of NI) funds the dividend and debt paydown even during a soft comp environment.

## 9. Bear case
1. Comp growth has decelerated for three straight quarters (0.4% → 0.6% → 1.7%, **[Corrected 2026-10-09: [Corrected: this sequence is an acceleration, not a deceleration]]** still below the top of the "flat-to-2%"
   guide range) while headline revenue growth is flattered by M&A roll-up, not organic recovery.
2. Consolidated debt (`DebtAndCapitalLeaseObligations` $51.3B) is elevated post-SRS/GMS with management's own stated need
   to delever back toward ~2.0x by FY26 year-end — that target could slip if comps stay weak.
3. At ~20x forward P/E with only flat-to-+4% EPS guidance, any tariff-driven gross-margin hit (company flags "ever-changing
   tariff policies" as a risk) has no valuation cushion.

## 10. Key risks & kill criteria (measurable)
1. US comparable sales below 1.0% for two consecutive quarters (vs the FY26 guided range of flat-to-+2.0%).
2. Consolidated `DebtAndCapitalLeaseObligations` (XBRL) does not decline toward the ~2.0x adjusted debt/EBITDA target by
   Q4 FY26 (period ending ~Jan/Feb 2027).
3. FY26 diluted EPS guidance cut below the initial $14.23-flat floor at any of the remaining FY26 releases.
4. GMS acquisition fails to close as expected in early 2026 or closes at materially worse terms/financing cost. **[Corrected 2026-10-09: [Corrected: GMS closed 4 Sep 2025 for $5.1bn cash; criterion is moot]]**
5. Gross margin falls below 32.5% (vs ~33.1% FY26 guide) for a full quarter, signalling tariff pass-through failure.

Wrong text 3: leverage. `DebtAndCapitalLeaseObligations` ($51.308B at 2026-02-01) excludes commercial paper. Wrong text 4: reverse DCF. Restated on equity FCF: g = (Ke x price - FCF)/(price + FCF) = about 5.0% (FCF $12.6bn) to 5.1% (SBC-adjusted $12.1bn) on market cap $292.5bn, versus 3.5-4% stated. This sits at the top of the dossier's mid-single-digit base case, so implied_vs_base stays in_line (upper end) and the WATCH verdict is unchanged. No F80_summary field changes.
