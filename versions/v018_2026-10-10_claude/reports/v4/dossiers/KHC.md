# KHC - The Kraft Heinz Company (F138, wave 8, standard depth)

**1. Verdict: WATCH** (horizon 12-36 months). The shares look cheap on cash flow, but adjusted earnings have fallen about a third in two years, FY26 guidance is still for a 16-18% drop in adjusted operating income, and the balance sheet and dividend leave little room for a miss. The triage "no" is partly overturned: this is not a REJECT because cash conversion is real and leverage is manageable, but it is not investable until volumes stop falling.
Condition that would change it to INCLUDE-SMALL: two consecutive quarters of organic volume/mix decline better than -1.5 pp with adjusted gross margin flat, and Moody's review resolved without a downgrade below Baa3.

**2. Business.** Sells packaged food (Heinz ketchup, Kraft cheese and dressings, Oscar Mayer meats, Philadelphia, Lunchables, Capri Sun, coffee) mostly to US supermarkets and to restaurants/foodservice. Makes money on brand pricing and heavy cost-cutting. Share is being lost to private label and to healthier brands in the center-of-store categories.

**3. Why the model likes it.** b1 composite 0.528, rank 235 of the live universe (decile 6). Driven by the sentiment family (0.77, SUE 0.81) and cash-flow yield (FCF/price 13.6% trailing), not by quality (0.36). Partly an artefact: trailing FCF is flattered by working capital (see 6) and trailing earnings yield is negative because of impairments.

**8. Bull / bear.**
Bull: (1) Sales outlook raised, Emerging Markets +8.5% organic, US share "improving" per CEO; (2) $700m reinvestment plus productivity could restore earnings growth in 2027-28 and the stock trades at 11.5x; (3) 6.8% yield covered by FCF and leverage near 3x.
Bear: (1) Adjusted operating income -18% in Q2 and guided -16% to -18% for FY; North America volume/mix -3.8 pp; (2) FCF is propped by supplier-terms working capital, and ratings are on negative outlook or review (Moody's Baa2 review for downgrade), raising borrowing cost and dividend pressure; (3) repeated impairments and a paused split mean no structural catalyst.

**9. Kill criteria (exit or do not start).**
1. Adjusted EPS guidance midpoint for FY26 cut below $2.00 at any release.
2. North America organic volume/mix worse than -4.0 pp for two consecutive quarters.
3. Net Leverage (company definition: debt less cash and short-term investments over adjusted EBITDA, consolidated) above 3.5x, or Moody's downgrade below Baa3.
4. Dividend cut below $0.40 per quarter, or declared dividends exceeding free cash flow (consolidated operating cash flow less capex) over any four quarters.
5. FCF conversion below 90% of adjusted net income for a full year (would show working capital reversing).
