# HPQ - HP Inc. (F143, wave 8 standard-depth diligence)

**1. Verdict: WATCH.** A cheap, cash-generative PC and printing company whose "raised" FY26 guide is mostly one-off tariff refunds, with structurally declining print profits, memory-cost inflation, an interim CEO and a guided mid-single-digit PC unit decline in 2027; price implies roughly flat cash flows forever versus my base of about -1.5% a year. Horizon 12-36 months. Overturns nothing in the quick triage (advance=false) but replaces its "trained knowledge" sourcing with filings.

**Condition that would move it to INCLUDE-SMALL:** FY27 non-GAAP EPS guidance (ex tariff refunds) at or above $3.00 and Printing operating margin holding at or above 17%, or a price near $26 (base-case value).

## 2. Business in plain English
HP sells PCs, workstations and peripherals (Personal Systems, about 75% of revenue: $11.8B of $15.7B in Q3 FY26) and printers plus ink/toner supplies (Printing, $3.9B, but an 18.1% operating margin versus 4.6% for PCs, so about 57% of segment profit). Customers are consumers, businesses and governments through a multi-tier channel. Moat is scale, channel reach and the installed base that buys supplies; it is eroding in print (hardware units down 7%, supplies revenue down 3% in Q3) and thin in PCs.

## 8. Bull and bear
Bull: (1) 11% FCF yield and $7.7B buyback capacity shrink the share count about 3% a year; (2) Commercial PS +22% in Q3 and Windows/AI-PC refresh; (3) Autonomy recovery (HP's half of $1.24B) and $0.3B tariff refunds are cash not in the base.
Bear: (1) memory and storage inflation: "higher inflationary pressure in memory and storage costs and supply constraints ... which we anticipate will continue" (10-Q); PS op margin 4.6%; (2) PS units -16% in Q3, industry PC volumes guided down mid-single-digits in CY27; (3) Printing supplies revenue down, hardware units -7%, and the CEO seat is interim.
