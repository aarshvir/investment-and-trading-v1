# WSM — Williams-Sonoma, Inc. — Diligence Dossier (Agent F117, Wave-8 Standard)

## 1. Verdict
**WATCH** (12–36 month horizon). A genuinely excellent operator (18% operating margin, no funded debt, all brands comping positive, FY26 guidance raised), but at $231.90 the stock prices about 19% annual free-cash-flow growth for ten years (my reverse DCF at V1's 10.5% cost of equity; 15% at 9.5%) against my evidence-based base case of 7%. GAAP earnings are also inflated by a one-time tariff refund. Would become INCLUDE-SMALL at roughly $150–170 or after two more quarters proving non-GAAP EPS of ~$10.5+ with stable margins. The quick triage (Q02: advance=false, "excessive") is confirmed on the business facts, although I disagree with V1's scenario margin assumption (section 7).

## 2. Business in plain English
Williams-Sonoma sells furniture, cookware and home goods through Pottery Barn, West Elm, Williams Sonoma, Pottery Barn Kids and Teen, Rejuvenation and others, online, by catalogue, business-to-business and in 508 stores (Aug 2 2026). The company describes itself as a digital-first, design-led home retailer; selling its own-brand products earns higher margins than reselling third-party goods but exposes it to tariffs and housing-market demand. Its position rests on owned brand design, a digital-first model and "The Key Rewards" loyalty and credit-card programme.

## 8. Bull and bear
**Bull:** (1) Every brand delivers positive comps and they are accelerating (+3.2% to +4.8% to +6.2%), with guidance raised in August ("We are raising our fiscal 2026 guidance"); (2) fortress balance sheet, ~$1.1B of underlying FCF, $1.1B buyback authorisation and a 15% dividend increase; (3) tariff refund of ~$198M shows management can recover costs, and FY25 margin held at 18.1% despite tariffs.
**Bear:** (1) Underlying non-GAAP operating income grew only 3% in FQ2'26 with merchandise margin -230 bps from tariffs, and guidance "assumes ... all tariffs currently in place will remain", including new Section 301 tariffs announced July 23; (2) housing-sensitive, high-ticket category with oil prices "elevated"; (3) the stock has re-rated to the 89th percentile of its own 15-year history, while V1's delivered five-year growth is +1.9%.

## 9. Key risks and kill criteria
1. Comparable brand revenue growth below 0% for two consecutive quarters (FQ2'26 +6.2%; FQ1'26 +4.8%).
2. TTM non-GAAP operating margin below 16.0% (consolidated; now 17.8% = ($1,415.7M + $630.2M - $618.8M) / $8,005.2M).
3. Any cut to the low end of the "operating margin, on a non-GAAP basis, between 17.8% to 18.2%" range in an earnings release.
4. Non-GAAP gross margin below 42.0% in any quarter (FQ2'26 45.5%; FQ1'26 43.9%).
5. Trigger: revolver drawn while consolidated liquidity is under five hundred million dollars of cash (reported balance Aug 2 2026: $1,028.9M, no borrowings).
