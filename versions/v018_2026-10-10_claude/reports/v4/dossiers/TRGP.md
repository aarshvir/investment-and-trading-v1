## 1. Verdict

The triage said "no: great franchise, priced for growth" and the primary-source work confirms it, with one nuance: the company has beaten and raised every quarter and the free-cash-flow inflection management describes (large downstream projects completing in the second half of 2027) is real. Condition to upgrade: a price near $230-235 (my base DCF value), or evidence that 2028 free cash flow per share exceeds about $17.

## 2. Business in plain English
Targa collects natural gas and natural-gas liquids (NGLs: ethane, propane, butane) from producers' wells, mostly in the Permian Basin, cleans and separates them in plants, moves them by pipeline to its Mont Belvieu, Texas fractionation hub, splits them into products and exports propane and butane from its Galena Park terminal. It earns per-unit fees (fees were 19% of Q2 revenue, 36% growth) plus a margin on commodities and marketing. Competitive position: owns the integrated chain from wellhead to export dock in the highest-growth US basin, which is hard to replicate.

## 8. Bull and bear
Bull: (1) Record Permian volumes and a dated capital-project pipeline (Train 11 and Delaware Express started in Q2-26; Trains 12 and 13, Speedway, GPMT export expansion and Copperhead and Yeti plants come online 2027-2028). (2) Beat-and-raise record: FY25 $4,957M versus a $4.65-4.85B range; FY26 range raised $300M. (3) Management: "The completion of our key large downstream capital projects in the second half of 2027 will provide us with meaningful operating leverage" (Feb 19 2026 release, acc. 0001193125-26-058040).
Bear: (1) Priced for it already: 26x earnings, 13.5x EBITDA, 3.5x leverage. (2) Q2 beat leaned on marketing and optimization margin; Waha gas price was negative, showing Permian takeaway constraints. (3) Cash taxes about nil today will normalise, and free cash flow after dividends is negative in 2026.
