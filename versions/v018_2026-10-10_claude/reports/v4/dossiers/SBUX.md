# Starbucks Corporation (SBUX) — Diligence Dossier (Agent F92, Wave 5, standard depth)

**1. Verdict: WATCH.** The Niccol turnaround is real and accelerating (four straight quarters of comp growth, two of margin expansion), and the balance sheet has been de-risked via the China stake sale. But at $95.00 **[Corrected 2026-10-09: the 25 Sep 2026 close was $94.86]** the market has already re-rated the stock to a forward multiple that assumes the turnaround is durable for years, not just proven for a few quarters. Horizon: 12–24 months; revisit on FY27 comp/margin guidance or a pullback.

**2. Business.** Global coffeehouse retailer/licensor; ~40k stores, company-operated + licensed, monetized via beverage/food sales, loyalty (Starbucks Rewards) and, historically, China joint operations (partially monetized via the FY26 China transaction). Moat: brand, real-estate density, loyalty-program switching costs; competitive intensity is rising from Luckin (China), independents and QSR coffee entrants.

**3. Why the model likes it / durability.** Triage (Q02) flagged quality 4/growth 4 on Q3 comps of +7.9% (vs. Street 5.7%) and a guidance raise. This is a genuine operational turnaround under CEO Brian Niccol (labor/throughput initiatives, menu simplification, "Back to Starbucks") rather than a one-off — four consecutive quarters of comp acceleration is a real trend, not a single beat. Durability risk: the base was depressed (comps were negative in FY24–early FY25), so part of the growth rate is a bounce off a low bar, and the pace of comp acceleration cannot mathematically continue at this rate indefinitely.

**5. Guidance track record.** FY26 adjusted EPS guidance raised from $2.25–$2.45 to $2.55–$2.65 at Q3; global same-store-sales guide raised from "at least 5%" to "nearly 6%" (US: "more than 6%," up from "at least 5%"). **6. Earnings quality & balance sheet.** Consolidated total debt ~$13.28B (Jun-26 quarter, per 10-Q balance sheet). Management used China Transaction proceeds to repay ~$1.8B of debt, reducing leverage to ~2.9x (company-stated, supporting investment-grade rating); also tendered for ~$1.3B of senior notes in May 2026. Dividend yield ~2.6% (quarterly $0.62/share). Buybacks were paused in the two quarters ended Mar-26 while deleveraging, with a new $6B authorization now in place — a sign capital priorities are shifting back toward shareholder return once leverage normalizes. GAAP ($0.91) vs. non-GAAP ($0.85) EPS gap in Q3 is a positive (GAAP > non-GAAP), unusual and worth checking — likely reflects a one-time gain related to the China Transaction that is excluded from adjusted EPS **[Corrected 2026-10-09: confirmed: Q3 income statement shows a $536.3M net gain resulting from divestiture of certain operations, excluded from non-GAAP]**; treat non-GAAP as the cleaner run-rate figure per company practice.

**8. Bull case:** (i) four consecutive quarters of comp acceleration under a credible, proven turnaround CEO; (ii) balance sheet materially de-risked (leverage 2.9x, debt tenders, China cash unlocked); (iii) guidance raised twice this fiscal year, a genuine positive-revision trend.
**Bear case:** (i) 36.5x forward earnings for a mature QSR/coffee chain leaves no room for the comp deceleration that mean-reversion alone would suggest; (ii) comp gains partly reflect an easy comparison versus a depressed FY24 base, not necessarily a new sustainable growth rate; (iii) intensifying competition (Luckin in China, value-menu wars from McDonald's/Dunkin domestically) could cap pricing power just as the multiple assumes it expands.

**9. Kill criteria (measurable):**
1. Global comparable-store sales growth decelerates below 3% for two consecutive quarters.
2. FY27 adjusted EPS guidance is cut versus the initial FY27 guide (once issued).
3. Consolidated net debt/EBITDA rises back above 3.5x (reversal of the current deleveraging).
4. US traffic (comparable transactions) turns negative for two consecutive quarters.
5. Non-GAAP operating margin fails to expand for two consecutive quarters (reversing the "two consecutive quarters of margin expansion" trend cited in Q3).
