## 1. Verdict: INCLUDE-SMALL — thesis horizon: 12–24 months
Lennox is a high-quality, high-ROE HVAC compounder (Home Comfort Solutions residential/light-commercial plus a fast-growing Building Climate Solutions commercial arm) trading at a below-peer-group multiple. The triage's thesis — "best-in-class capital efficiency, priced cheaper than HVAC peers because of soft residential demand" — is **largely confirmed**, but this diligence pass surfaces one thing the triage's red-flag list did not capture: **FY2026 adjusted-EPS guidance was cut at the Q2 2026 release** (to $23.00–$24.00 from $23.50–$25.00, i.e., the top end down $1.00/−4%, midpoint down ~3%), even as revenue-growth guidance was simultaneously **raised** (to ~8% from 6–7%, driven by a bigger acquisition contribution). **[Corrected 2026-10-08: the revenue guide was raised to ~8% at the Q1 release (29 Apr 2026, acc 0001069202-26-000052); at Q2 it was reaffirmed at ~8% and only the acquisition share rose from 4% to 5%; the EPS cut at Q2 is correct]** That combination — higher guided revenue, lower guided EPS — means the growth on offer is increasingly acquisition-driven and margin-diluting, not pure organic operating leverage, which is a real, if modest, deterioration versus the "clean compounder" framing. On the positive side of the ledger, this pass confirms the triage's own flagged **R454B refrigerant-transition risk is largely already behind the company** (the EPA-mandated transition to lower-GWP refrigerants "became effective January 1, 2025" per the FY2025 10-K, i.e., it is a completed transition being lapped, not a forward risk) and finds **no material litigation** disclosed (only routine claims, per Item 3/Note 5). A reverse DCF implies ~3–5% ten-year FCF growth versus a plausible high-single-digit base case once Home Comfort Solutions stabilizes — "below," i.e., cheap. INCLUDE-SMALL, not INCLUDE: Home Comfort Solutions (the larger segment) is still contracting YoY, guidance was just cut, and the balance sheet is increasingly leaning on commercial paper to fund a string of bolt-on acquisitions (Duro Dyne, Supco, Comfort-Aire, Century, Heat Controller).

## 2. Business in plain English
Lennox designs, manufactures and sells heating, ventilation, air conditioning and refrigeration (HVACR) equipment and parts. **Home Comfort Solutions** sells residential and light-commercial systems through one-step (company-owned) and two-step (independent distributor) channels; **Building Climate Solutions** sells commercial unitary/applied systems, controls and services to national accounts and commercial customers. It earns industry-leading margins (segment margins in the low-to-mid 20s%) through brand strength (Lennox, plus recently acquired parts/service brands), energy-efficiency-regulation compliance, and a very capital-light balance sheet funded by aggressive historical buybacks (hence a headline ROE the model reads as ~72%, which is in large part a capital-structure artefact of a thin equity base, not solely an operating-margin phenomenon).

## 8. Bull case and bear case
**Bull:** (1) Building Climate Solutions (now ~39% of revenue) is compounding at 15–26% organic growth with margin expansion, a genuine structural growth engine largely independent of the residential cycle; (2) reverse DCF implies only ~3–5%/year FCF growth for a decade against a plausible high-single-digit base case once Home Comfort Solutions stabilizes (management's own words: "sequential improvement," two-step channel inventories "expected to normalize by the second quarter" of 2026); (3) the R454B refrigerant-transition cost/complexity risk the triage flagged is already behind the company (effective January 2025), removing a forward overhang.
**Bear:** (1) FY2026 adjusted-EPS guidance was cut in July 2026 (to $23.00–$24.00 from $23.50–$25.00) even as revenue guidance was raised — the incremental growth is increasingly acquisition-funded and margin-diluting, not pure operating leverage; (2) Home Comfort Solutions, still the larger segment (~61% of Q2 2026 revenue), fell 7% YoY in Q2 2026 and 10% in Q1 2026, with new-construction-driven softness management itself calls a "meaningful headwind"; (3) commercial paper (the funding source for recent bolt-on M&A) rose 82% in six months to $412M, and the company continues to run on a very thin equity base (~$1.3bn against a ~$13bn market cap) — a capital structure with limited absorption capacity if end markets disappoint further.

## 9. Key risks & kill criteria
1. FY2026 adjusted EPS guidance ($23.00–$24.00, given 2026-07-29) is **cut again** at the Q3 2026 or Q4 2026 release.
2. Home Comfort Solutions revenue does not show sequential stabilization (i.e., a second consecutive quarter of YoY decline worse than −7%) by Q1 2027.
3. Consolidated net debt/EBITDA rises above **2.5x** as commercial paper continues to fund acquisitions, without a disclosed deleveraging plan.
4. Building Climate Solutions organic growth decelerates below **8%** for two consecutive quarters (versus the 15–26% pace of the last two quarters).
5. Free cash flow for FY2026 comes in below the low end of the company's own guided **$750M–$850M** range.
