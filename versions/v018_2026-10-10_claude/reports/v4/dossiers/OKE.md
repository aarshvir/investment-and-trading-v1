# OKE - ONEOK, Inc. (NYSE: OKE) - F162 diligence dossier (wave 8, STANDARD depth)

**1. Verdict: INCLUDE-SMALL (half weight), thesis horizon 12-36 months.** Reason: a fee-based Permian-to-Gulf midstream system whose price (25 Sep 2026 close $88.63) implies roughly 1% 10-year growth in unlevered free cash flow against a base case of about 3.5%. Named reservation: after the 10 Sep 2026 Apollo deal, a $9bn Class B interest sits structurally ahead of common equity and takes 15% of operating-company cash flow, and 2026 profit includes a Waha-Katy price-spread windfall. The quick triage (not advanced; "Low FCF yield... high leverage (4.3x)") is partly overturned: leverage on my calculation is 3.9x consolidated TTM and falls toward the company's ~3.25x pro forma target; the FCF yield is 5.2% on OCF minus capex, not 1.6%.

**2. Business.** ONEOK moves and processes natural gas liquids (NGLs), natural gas, refined products and crude through about 60,000 miles of pipe. Most earnings are volume- and fee-based (company says NGL earnings are "approximately 90% fee-based"; Q4 2025 release, acc. 0001039684-26-000004). Scale was bought: Magellan, EnLink and Medallion in 2024-25, now Brazos Midland (signed 18 Aug 2026). Moat: integrated wellhead-to-water system, hard to replicate.

**3. Why the model likes it (b1/d4).** Quality and value families (EBIT/EV, earnings yield 6.7%, d4 forward P/E 14.2x). Partly durable (fees, volume growth), partly an artefact: H1 2026 Natural Gas Pipelines EBITDA benefited by "a $169 million increase in optimization and marketing activity due primarily to favorable price differentials between the Waha Hub and Katy" (Q2 2026 release, acc. 0001039684-26-000027), which is spread-driven and not contracted.

**8. Bull / bear.** Bull: (1) Permian dedicated acreage (~600,000 acres, >12-year weighted contract term per company release) feeds NGL and gas systems; (2) deleveraging to ~3.25x frees buybacks or dividend growth; (3) Class B capital account shrinks, accruing upside to common. Bear: (1) 15% of OpCo cash flow plus a 7% cap is an expensive, subordinated claim if growth disappoints; (2) commodity-linked optimisation earnings (Waha-Katy) revert; 2026 guidance assumed WTI "approximately $55 to $60 per barrel" (Q4'25 release), and producer activity could slow; (3) integration of four large deals, more equity (ATM) and Brazos regulatory/execution risk.
