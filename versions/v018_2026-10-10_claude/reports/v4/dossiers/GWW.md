# W.W. Grainger, Inc. (GWW) — Fundamental Diligence Dossier

**Verdict: WATCH** — thesis horizon 12–36 months if valuation resets or growth outperforms base case for longer than 3 years.
One-sentence reason: a best-in-class MRO distributor (46% ROE, three straight quarterly guidance raises, a Q3-2025 profit dip that was a clean one-off UK-exit charge rather than an operating problem) is nonetheless priced at ~25–27x forward adjusted EPS for ~13%/yr of implied 10-year FCF growth versus a ~10%/yr evidence-based base case — a smaller valuation gap than the other two names in this batch, but still "above," not "at or below," base.

## 1. Business in plain English
Grainger distributes maintenance, repair and operating (MRO) supplies — safety equipment, tools, fasteners, motors, cleaning supplies — to businesses and institutions across North America and, through its majority-owned Japanese subsidiary MonotaRO and its Zoro e-commerce brands, in Japan/Europe/North America respectively. It runs two segments: **High-Touch Solutions – N.A.** (large-account, high-service relationships, ~85% of sales) and **Endless Assortment** (Zoro, MonotaRO — a long-tail, e-commerce marketplace model). The moat is scale (broad SKU assortment, fast delivery, deep customer integration into large accounts' procurement systems) that smaller distributors cannot match; it is not a patent or network-effect moat.

## 5. Moat and competition
- **High-Touch Solutions – N.A.:** Q2 2026 sales +11.9% (+11.7% daily constant-currency), gross margin 41.8% (+80bps YoY), driven by volume growth and price increases as tariff costs are passed through to customers — evidence of real pricing power, not just volume.
- **Endless Assortment (MonotaRO + Zoro):** Q2 2026 sales +13.5% reported (+20.6% daily organic constant-currency) — the faster-growing, lower-margin (10.4–10.8% guided operating margin vs. 17.2–17.6% for High-Touch) segment; MonotaRO is majority-owned (consolidated with a noncontrolling interest — see §6 entity scope) and separately listed on the Tokyo Stock Exchange.
- **U.K. exit:** Grainger announced its intention to exit the U.K. market, including a planned divestiture of the Cromwell business, disclosed in Q3 2025 (impairment recognized then; held for sale at 30-Sep-2025) — a rare instance of Grainger retreating from a market, now essentially complete and no longer distorting comparisons as of Q2 2026.
- **Tariffs:** company recognized **$43M of IEEPA tariff refunds** in Q2 2026 that reduced cost of goods sold — tariff policy is a real, quantified swing factor for gross margin in both directions (a headwind in 2025, a modest tailwind via refunds in 2026).
- Competitors include Fastenal, MSC Industrial, Amazon Business (a genuine long-tail/e-commerce competitive threat to the Endless Assortment segment specifically) and numerous regional distributors.

## 8. Bull case
1. Three consecutive guidance raises (initiate → raise → raise) since Feb-2026, backed by verifiable, accelerating organic growth (daily constant-currency sales +13.7% in Q2 2026) across both segments.
2. Best-in-class returns on capital (46% TTM ROE) with a genuine scale/service moat in High-Touch and a fast-growing e-commerce arm (MonotaRO/Zoro, +20.6% organic in Q2 2026) that most MRO peers lack.
3. The Q3-2025 profit "miss" was fully explained and lapped within two quarters (U.K./Cromwell exit, a discrete, disclosed non-cash charge) — a clean example of a business that discloses adverse facts clearly rather than burying them, per the wave-3 mandate to check exactly this.

## 9. Bear case
1. The price implies ~13%/yr sustained FCF growth for a decade — above the company's own historical high-single-digit-plus-buybacks algorithm, even though the gap to base case is the smallest of the three names in this batch.
2. Gross margin in High-Touch Solutions – N.A. has shown tariff-driven price/cost timing volatility (down 50bps in Q3 2025, up 80bps in Q2 2026 partly on one-off IEEPA refunds) — the current tailwind may not repeat.
3. Endless Assortment faces a structurally tougher competitor set (Amazon Business specifically) than High-Touch's relationship-driven large accounts.

## 10. Key risks & kill criteria — thesis-invalidation triggers (what would change this verdict)
1. High-Touch Solutions – N.A. daily organic constant-currency growth falls below 3% for two consecutive quarters.
2. Adjusted operating margin falls below 15% for two consecutive quarters (below the low end of the FY2026 guided range).
3. FY guidance is cut in an upcoming release (breaking the two-consecutive-raise pattern since Feb-2026).
4. Gross margin decline resumes and exceeds −50bps YoY for two consecutive quarters (tariff price/cost timing turning unfavorable again, no offsetting refunds).
5. Endless Assortment (MonotaRO/Zoro) daily organic constant-currency growth decelerates below 8% for two consecutive quarters.
