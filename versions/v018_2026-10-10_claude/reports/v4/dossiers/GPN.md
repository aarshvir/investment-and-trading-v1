# GPN - Global Payments Inc. (diligence agent F166, wave 8)

**1. Verdict: WATCH** (thesis horizon 12-36 months). The shares look cheap on adjusted earnings (about 6.3x the FY26 guide midpoint), but the cash flow that would justify that is not yet visible in the filings, the FY26 guide was just cut, and a $2.45bn income-tax liability is unexplained. Condition to upgrade: two consecutive quarters of operating cash flow minus capex that supports roughly $2bn a year, with the tax payable settled and FY26 EPS inside the cut range.

Triage (Q04/Q06/Q08: "DO NOT ADVANCE", net debt/EBITDA "of 4") partly overturned: leverage on my read is about 2.9x before and about 3.3x after the tax payable (below), not 4x. Non-advance conclusion kept for now.

**2. Business.** Since 9 Jan 2026 (10-Q Note 2) GPN is a pure-play merchant payments company: it bought Worldpay from FIS and GTCR (about $6.0bn cash plus 42.8m shares) and sold Issuer Solutions to FIS (about $7.5bn cash plus FIS's Worldpay stake). Three segments: Enterprise, Platforms, SMB (Q2 adjusted net revenue $838m / $628m / $1,513m). It earns processing fees, software subscriptions (Genius platform) and acquiring spread across 175 countries. Moat is scale and distribution; the threat is price competition and software-led rivals.

**3. Why the model likes it.** b1 ranks it 476 of ~480 live (composite_qvm 0.07); Value family 0.32 (ebit/ev 3.2%, fcf yield 3.6%), Momentum 0.46, Quality 0.02. It is a cheap-multiple artefact: ROE -4%, SUE -0.32, asset growth +31% and net issuance +9% reflect the deal, not operations. Not durable evidence of quality.

**8. Bull / bear.** Bull: (1) pure-play with scale and 42% adjusted operating margin; (2) $600m Worldpay synergies and $650m transformation benefit (10-Q) not yet in numbers; (3) $7.5bn 2025-27 capital return plan; $1.1bn of stock repurchased in H1'26 (about 5% of today's market cap) at an average $73.01. Bear: (1) guide cut within one quarter of first full pure-play quarter; (2) cash conversion unproven, $2.45bn tax payable; (3) $22.4bn debt, interest about $1bn a year, with 4-5% growth leaving little margin for a travel/macro shock.

**9. Kill criteria / upgrade triggers (measurable).**
1. Consolidated net debt (10-Q Note 6 "Total long-term debt" less balance-sheet cash and cash equivalents) divided by adjusted EBITDA above 3.75x at any quarter-end.
2. FY2026 adjusted EPS guidance cut below $13.60 or normalized constant-currency adjusted net revenue growth guided below 4%.
3. Normalized adjusted net revenue growth below 3% for two consecutive quarters.
4. Operating cash flow minus capital expenditures (consolidated cash flow statement) for calendar 2026 below $1.5bn.
5. Worldpay run-rate expense synergy target of $600m by year-end 2028 reduced or delayed.
