# PSKY - Paramount Skydance Corporation (diligence agent F164, standard depth)

## 1. Verdict
**REJECT** for the stated mandate (15-20% drawdown tolerance), thesis horizon 12-36 months. The equity becomes a claim (about 4x to 7x net debt/EBITDA, see section 6) on a $6 billion synergy plan the day the Warner Bros. Discovery (WBD) deal closes; the court barrier fell on 30 Sep 2026 (post-cutoff), so the binary event risk is now replaced by leverage and integration risk. The 25 Sep price ($9.96) needs about 83% of the promised synergies from day one (section 7). Re-underwrite only after the first post-close balance sheet shows net leverage under 5.0x with positive free cash flow.
Triage (Q01_triage.json: "no", 2/2/2, "leveraged WBD buyout") is confirmed; the special-situation framing is retained but my conclusion rests on filings, not the label.

## 2. Business in plain English
Paramount owns CBS, Paramount+ and Pluto TV, its film and television studios, and cable networks including BET, VH1 and Comedy Central (named in the 30 Sep 2026 consent decree). It earns subscription fees from streaming (81.6M Paramount+ subscribers), advertising and affiliate fees from TV Media (shrinking), and film and TV licensing from Studios. Control sits with the Ellison family and RedBird, who "will remain the sole holders of Paramount Class A Common Stock, representing 100% of the voting shares" (10-Q Note on the WBD Merger, acc 0002041610-26-000054). The company signed to buy WBD (HBO, Warner Bros., CNN) on 27 Feb 2026.

## 8. Bull and bear
Bull: (1) the antitrust barrier is gone: States Consent Decree entered 30 Sep 2026 (8-K acc 0001104659-26-112347, post-cutoff) and 65 jurisdictions cleared; (2) streaming scale (Paramount+ revenue +16% in Q2) plus HBO Max in one platform and "$6 billion in synergies" (27 Feb 8-K); (3) the equity buyers pay a $12.00 floor against a $9.96 market price, and existing holders receive warrants (record date 5 Oct 2026, expected issue about 13 Oct 2026).
Bear: (1) leverage: about $77B net debt on about $11B EBITDA and falling WBD revenue (-11%); (2) linear TV is shrinking (TV Media revenue -9%, advertising -14%) and a large part of 2026 EBITDA growth is accounting-driven; (3) a media-quality and governance overhang: Ellison/RedBird hold all voting stock, the consent decree imposes 30+ film releases a year, a $1.5B extra US-production spend, a news-independence board and divestiture triggers (Miramax, six cable channels).

## 9. Key risks and kill criteria
1. Consolidated net debt to LTM adjusted EBITDA above 6.0x at the first post-close quarter end (company target 4.3x synergised).
2. Consolidated free cash flow (OCF less capex) negative in any two consecutive post-close quarters.
3. Run-rate realised synergies below $2.0B twelve months after close (target over $6B).
4. Paramount+ paid subscribers below 80.0M for two consecutive quarters (81.6M at Q2 2026).
5. TV Media revenue decline worse than -12% y/y for two consecutive quarters (Q2 2026: -9%).
