# RF — Regions Financial Corporation (NYSE: RF)

## 1. Verdict
**WATCH** — 12–24 month horizon. Reason: genuinely best-in-class regional-bank execution (deposit costs, efficiency, credit quality), but V1's systematic model independently flags the stock as "demanding" with a modest +4.5%/yr base-case 3-year return, and this dossier's own reverse-DCF agrees the price already assumes RF sustains above-its-own-history ROE — not enough margin of safety to hold at full weight today. Condition that would move this to INCLUDE: a pullback to book value multiple implying ROE closer to the 20th–50th percentile of RF's own 15-year history (i.e., a cheaper entry) without a change in the credit-quality/efficiency story.

## 2. Business in plain English
Regions Financial is a Birmingham, AL-based regional bank (~$155bn assets range) operating primarily across the Southeast/Midwest US, making money on the spread between what it pays for deposits and earns on loans (net interest income), plus fee income from wealth management, mortgage and capital markets. Its competitive edge versus peer regionals is **cost discipline**: it runs a top-quartile efficiency ratio and among the lowest interest-bearing deposit costs in its peer group, funding balance-sheet growth internally rather than through the scale M&A its peers have pursued. (Source: 8-K EX-99.1, filed 2026-07-17, accession 0001281761-26-000032/...050.)

## 3. Why the model likes it / is it durable? Where it conflicts with filings
b1_live_scores.csv shows RF at composite decile/quintile near the top (decile likely top-of-range given quintile=1 in the row), high pct_roe (0.65) and pct_bp — a "quality" name. Triage's own reason flags the tension directly: **"Growth is the slowest of the regional-bank group (+3% revenue) since it hasn't done the scale M&A its peers have, but underwriting/expense discipline is genuinely best-in-class; model flags valuation as 'demanding.'"** Filings confirm this is not an artefact: Q2'26 adjusted total revenue grew only 2% YoY, but adjusted ROATCE was 20.18% and CET1 10.7% — the quality is real, but it is priced for.

## 7. Valuation snapshot & reverse DCF — reconciled with V1
- Price used: $27.62 (2026-09-25 close, matches v1_valuation.json `inputs.price_2026_09_25`).
- **V1's own valuation (`outputs/v1_valuation.json`, `outputs/v1_valuation_table.csv`), read before writing this section**: method = "justified P/B = (ROE−g)/(COE−g)"; P/B now 1.256x (84th percentile of RF's own 15-year history); cost of equity (COE) 9.43%; **implied ROE 11.08%**, vs. **delivered TTM ROE 11.85%** and RF's own historical 20th/50th/80th-percentile ROE of 6.30% / 8.67% / 11.85%. **V1 verdict: "demanding"** (verdict_score −0.33). **V1 3-year annualized scenario returns: bear −7.65%, base +4.46%, bull +15.60%.**
- **Do I agree?** Partially. V1's implied ROE (11.08%) sits almost exactly at RF's 80th-percentile historical ROE (11.85%) — i.e., the stock is priced for RF to keep delivering *top-of-its-own-15-year-range* profitability indefinitely. Q2'26's actual adjusted ROATCE of 20.18% (a tangible-equity, not book-equity, measure — not directly comparable to V1's ROE input, which V1 sources from D3 as a book-ROE) is running above even that, which argues the near-term trend supports the price. But the historical-percentile framing is the right risk lens for a cyclical, rate-sensitive business: NIM (3.66%) and credit costs are both currently favorable, and a reversion toward the 50th-percentile ROE (8.67%) — plausible in a credit-cycle downturn or NIM compression — would justify a materially lower multiple than 1.26x P/B. I do not have a primary-source number that overturns V1's WACC/COE inputs, so I am not overriding the "demanding" verdict; I am adopting it.
- **implied_vs_base = "in_line"**: the reverse-DCF-implied ROE (11.08%) is close to (slightly below) the currently-delivered TTM ROE (11.85%), so the price is not asking for something RF isn't currently doing — but it is asking for that to persist, which is the risk flagged above. This is not a case where implied growth is comfortably *below* base case (which would support INCLUDE); it is essentially priced for the current run-rate to continue, hence WATCH rather than INCLUDE/INCLUDE-SMALL, per the addendum's rule that a great business priced for what it must deliver — not more, but not less either — is not an automatic buy.
- Peers (P/B, from v1): peer median 1.267x vs RF 1.256x — RF trades in line with, not at a premium to, its regional-bank peer group (CFG, FITB, HBAN, KEY, MTB) on this metric; the "demanding" flag is about RF's own history and COE, not a peer-relative premium.

## 8. Bull case
1. Best-in-class expense/deposit-cost discipline within the regional-bank peer group; adjusted ROATCE (20.18% Q2'26) running above even V1's implied-ROE bar (11.08% on a book-ROE basis) and above RF's own 80th-percentile historical ROE.
2. Credit quality improving, not deteriorating, into what could be a slower-growth macro environment: net charge-offs fell 12bps QoQ, ACL/NPL coverage rose to 241%.
3. Capital-markets/wealth-management fee diversification (Frazer Lanier acquisition, "5th record Wealth Management quarter in the last 6") reduces pure NII/rate dependence over time.

## 9. Bear case / kill criteria (measurable)
1. **CET1 ratio (inclusive of AOCI) falls below 9.0%** for two consecutive quarters (was 9.5% in Q2'26; source: 8-K EX-99.1, 2026-07-17).
2. **Net interest margin (FTE) falls below 3.50%** for two consecutive quarters (was 3.66% in Q2'26, 3.67% in Q1'26 — already showing a slight sequential dip to watch).
3. **Full-year NII growth comes in below the low end of the 2.5–4% guided range** (per 3Q'26 investor deck, filed 2026-08-11) — a guidance miss on the company's own primary driver.
4. **Annualized net charge-offs/avg. loans rise above 0.65%** for two consecutive quarters (was 0.42% in Q2'26, down from 0.54% in Q1'26 — a reversal of the current improving trend).
5. **P/B re-rates to the peer median (1.267x) or above without a corresponding ROE improvement** — would confirm the "demanding" V1 read is correct and the position should not be added to.
