# DDOG — Datadog, Inc. (F149, wave 8, STANDARD depth)

**1. Verdict: WATCH.** Excellent, fast-growing platform, but the 25 Sep 2026 close ($268.13) requires roughly 25-27% revenue growth for nine more years even at a 30-35% owner-cash margin, above my bull case (~20%), and the company has just disclosed that its largest customer is cutting usage. Horizon 24-36 months. Changes to INCLUDE-SMALL if: price falls roughly 35-40% (implied growth back at or below ~13-15% CAGR), or two more quarters show revenue re-accelerating ex the largest customer with non-GAAP operating margin holding at or above 23%.

**2. Business in plain English.** Datadog sells a subscription/usage-priced software platform that lets engineering and security teams see what is happening inside their cloud applications (infrastructure, application performance, logs, security, AI-model monitoring). About 4,720 customers pay $100k+ ARR and they are 91% of ARR (10-Q, acc. 0001628280-26-054458). Moat: one integrated platform with 1,000+ integrations and high switching costs; risks are hyperscaler bundling, usage optimisation by big customers, and AI-native customer concentration.

**3. Why the model likes it (b1/d4).** Quality and momentum, not value: gross-profit/assets 0.42 (77th pct), accruals 96th pct (good), 12-1 momentum +66% (75th pct), positive earnings surprise; value family is the 4th percentile (earnings yield 0.2%, FCF yield 1.2% in b1; d4 FCF yield 1.1%, NTM P/E 93.9x). Durable part: growth and gross margin. Artefact: "accruals" and OCF look excellent only because SBC (about $0.75bn in FY25, non-cash) is excluded from operating cash flow. Composite decile 3. Triage (Q11) said do not advance on price; I agree on price and add a fundamental reason the triage missed (largest-customer contraction, below).
