# ED - Consolidated Edison, Inc. (agent F119, standard depth, information cutoff 2026-09-25 close)

**1. Verdict: INCLUDE-SMALL** (half weight; horizon 24-36 months). Reason: a regulated, rate-base-growth utility with a three-year CECONY rate plan already approved and guidance the market price does not require it to beat (price implies about 4.7% EPS growth vs 6-7% guided). Named reservation: free cash flow is structurally negative and management plans about $1.1bn (2026), $1.2bn (2027) and up to $3.3bn (2028-2030) of new common equity, while first-half 2026 adjusted EPS grew only 3.1% so the 2026 guide is second-half loaded. Triage (Q15_triage.json: advance=false, "priced for more than the growth on offer") is overturned on the reverse DCF, but the margin of safety is thin.

**2. Business in plain English (10-K acc. 0001047862-26-000031).** Con Edison delivers electricity, gas and steam to about New York City and Westchester (CECONY, a regulated subsidiary) and to a smaller area of New York and New Jersey (Orange and Rockland, O&R). Regulators set the prices; earnings equal rate base times an allowed return of roughly 9.4-9.6% (RECO 9.6% per 10-Q Note B), so growth comes from investing more (utility plant $70.4bn gross at 30 Jun 2026, consolidated). No competition in its service area; the risk is political/affordability, not economic.

**3. Why the model likes it, and durability (10-Q acc. 0001047862-26-000142).** Low volatility (vol_1y 17%), dividend $3.56/yr (four quarters at $0.89; 3.5% yield), stable adjusted EPS. V1 instead labels it "excessive". The appeal is durable (regulated) but a chunk of the quant "value" is 2025-26 non-recurring items: 1H26 GAAP EPS $3.37 includes a $0.37 after-tax gain on selling the ~6.6% MVP pipeline interest (completed in Q1 2026 for $357.5m aggregate consideration, per 10-Q), so adjusted EPS $3.00 is the right base.
