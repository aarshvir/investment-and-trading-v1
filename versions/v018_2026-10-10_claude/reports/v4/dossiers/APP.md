## 2. Business in plain English

AppLovin runs an AI-driven advertising marketplace (AXON) that helps mobile-app and, increasingly, e-commerce/web advertisers find and pay for the users most likely to spend money, matching advertiser bids to app/publisher ad inventory in real time. It earns a take rate on ad spend transacted through its platform. Since selling its own mobile-games "Apps" business to Tripledot Studios (closed 30-Jun-2025, for $400m cash + ~20% of Tripledot's equity), AppLovin is a pure-play, high-margin ad-tech/data platform rather than an app publisher, competing with Meta, Google, TikTok and The Trade Desk for advertiser budget.

## 8. Bull case / bear case

**Bull:**
1. AXON's AI-matching engine keeps taking share of mobile and (newly) web/CTV ad budgets at ~83–85% Adjusted EBITDA margins with near-zero incremental capital — a rare combination of growth and cash generation.
2. Net debt is trivial (0.1x EBITDA) and the company is aggressively retiring shares with excess FCF (~$2.6bn bought back in FY2025 alone), directly boosting per-share value.
3. The SEC's data-practices probe into AXON is reported to have closed without enforcement action (Aug-2026 press reports) — if that holds, one of the two biggest overhangs (regulatory) is resolved, leaving only private litigation.

**Bear:**
1. Multiple independent short-sellers (Culper, Fuzzy Panda, Muddy Waters, CapitalWatch) have alleged ad fraud, data misuse and — in CapitalWatch's Jan-2026 report — money-laundering-adjacent conduct; even a partial retraction (CapitalWatch, Feb-2026) does not resolve the core ad-practice allegations, which remain open.
2. Two distinct securities class actions are live: the original "Brownback Action" (class period 7-Nov-2024 to 27-Mar-2025; motion to dismiss fully briefed as of Feb-2026, no ruling yet per the 10-Q filed 5-Aug-2026) and a newer one covering 12-Feb-2026 to 5-Aug-2026, filed after a 13-Jul-2026 analyst report and the Q2'26 print (lead-plaintiff deadline 16-Nov-2026, per contemporaneous law-firm press releases — not yet reflected in a company filing).
3. The ~28% single-session reaction to an in-line Q2'26 print shows the stock is priced for perfection; any real deceleration (the Q3'26 Adjusted EBITDA margin guide already stepped down to 83% from 84–85%) could trigger further multiple compression independent of the litigation risk.

## 9. Key risks & kill criteria (measurable signposts that would break this thesis)

1. Net debt/TTM Adjusted EBITDA (currently ≈0.1x) rises above **1.0x** (would signal debt-funded buybacks/M&A changing the risk profile).
2. A U.S. court denies the motion to dismiss the Brownback Action securities class action (fully briefed since Feb-2026), allowing discovery to proceed.
3. Quarterly revenue growth decelerates below **25% YoY** for two consecutive quarters (from the current 53–59% range).
4. The SEC, DOJ or FTC opens or reopens a **formal** investigation/enforcement action (as opposed to the reportedly closed data-practices probe) into AXON's data or ad-fraud practices.
5. Adjusted EBITDA margin guidance for a future quarter is set below **80%** (from 83–85% currently), signalling a structural, not one-quarter, margin reset.
