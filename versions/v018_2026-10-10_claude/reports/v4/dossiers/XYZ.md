## 1. Business in plain English
Block runs two ecosystems: Square (card acceptance, point-of-sale software and lending for sellers) and Cash App (consumer payments, Cash App Card, bitcoin, Afterpay BNPL, Borrow loans, banking-like "Primary Banking Actives"), linked by Afterpay. It earns processing and subscription fees, interest and lending income, and bitcoin trading margin. Competitive position: Square has had rising U.S. GPV growth (+10% in Q2'26, fastest since Q2 2023); Cash App has 59 million monthly transacting actives; the moat is two-sided data and owned hardware/distribution, with its own industrial bank (Square Financial Services).

## 5. Growth drivers and moat
Cash App Borrow and BNPL (consumer-lending origination volume +59% to $18.9B in Q2), Cash App Card and Commerce Enablement volume +17% to $56.5B, Square field sales/ISO channel (U.S. GPV +10%, international +28%), Neighborhoods ($1B annualised GPV enabled, up 220% since March), and AI-driven cost reduction (workforce cut by over 40%; adjusted operating margin 27% vs 22% a year earlier). Moat: network data on both sides, owned hardware and its chartered bank. Weak points: Cash App's regulatory history, bitcoin revenue mix (Bitcoin revenue $1.89B at about 4% gross margin), and consumer-credit exposure.

## 8. Bull and bear
Bull: (1) gross profit growth of 20%+ with adjusted margins rising toward 30% as AI-driven cost cuts hold; (2) Cash App primary-banking actives +17% lifting lending attach; (3) DOJ and class-action overhang settles near the accrual and $4.6B buyback shrinks the count.
Bear: (1) loss provisions grew 99% YoY against 25% gross profit growth; if losses rise to 25% of gross profit, adjusted operating income falls by more than $150M a quarter; (2) DOJ resolution could exceed $526M, and the securities class action is live; (3) the guide implies growth slowing to 16% in H2 while the stock carries a 19x adjusted multiple on earnings that exclude legal costs.

## 9. Key risks and kill criteria

3. Quarterly gross profit growth below 12% YoY for two consecutive quarters (Q3'26 guide: +18%).
4. FY26 Adjusted Diluted EPS guide cut below $3.85 (the May 2026 level) at any release.
5. Cash and cash equivalents (consolidated, balance sheet) below $4.0B before the 1 Nov 2027 convertible maturity without new financing.
