# SOLV — Solventum Corporation (F144, wave 8 standard diligence)

**1. Verdict: WATCH, 12–24 month horizon.** The cheap-looking 12.4x NTM P/E rests on adjusted EPS that flatters cash earnings. The Q2 2026 "raise" is mostly a one-off tariff refund, the quarter was inflated by customers ordering early, the free cash flow guide is only $200-300M, and a litigation accrual was raised with no amount disclosed. The triage said not to advance and cited 4.3x leverage; I confirm the not-now call but correct the leverage and litigation details. What would change it: two clean quarters after the Q3 pull-forward reversal, free cash flow above about 50% of adjusted net income, and a quantified Bair Hugger liability.

**One-line thesis:** A former 3M medical-supplies and software business, cutting costs and splitting off its health-IT arm, whose reported profit is well ahead of the cash it produces.

## 2. Business in plain English
Solventum sells wound-care dressings, surgical and infection-prevention products, dental materials and health-information software to hospitals, dentists and clinics. It was spun out of 3M in April 2024, sold its Purification and Filtration unit on 1 Sep 2025 for about $4B cash (10-Q), and on 5 Aug 2026 announced it intends to separate Health Information Systems (HIS, about $354M a quarter of sales). Moat: installed base and clinical relationships in wound care and infection prevention; growth is low single digit.

## 3. Why the model likes it, and is it durable
b1/d4 flags low multiple (12.4x NTM P/E) and FY26 EPS growth of 16.9% (consensus). That is the artefact: FY26 guided adjusted EPS includes about $0.46 of one-off tariff refund (my estimate: $100M pre-tax at about 20% assumed tax over 173.3M diluted shares). Consensus FY27 growth is only 1.2% (d4), which is what the model's own forward growth shows once the one-off is in the base.

## 6. Earnings quality and balance sheet
- **Consolidated, 30 Jun 2026 (10-Q balance sheet and debt note):** total debt $5,079M (carrying; current $506M + long-term $4,573M; senior notes $5.0B principal, 2027-2064), finance leases $207M, cash $403M (Dec 25: $878M), total equity $4,800M, goodwill $5,626M plus intangibles $2,408M. Net debt $4,676M. Against Yahoo TTM EBITDA of $1,139M (aggregator, GAAP basis) that is 4.1x, the source of the triage's 4.3x. On company adjusted profit the ratio is about 2.3x (my estimate: adjusted pre-tax about $1.56B at an assumed 20% tax on $7.15 EPS plus about $0.29B interest plus D&A; not from a filing), so leverage is moderate on adjusted numbers and high on GAAP.
- Cash conversion: H1 26 operating cash flow $38M and FCF -$129M; Q2 benefited from "timing of tax payments and insurance proceeds". FCF guide $200-300M vs adjusted net income of about $1.24B (7.15 x 173M) is about 20%. Drivers: separation and restructuring (Transform for the Future targets about $500M annual savings over four years), legal, and interest.
- Capital return: $355M of buybacks in H1 26 (4.8M shares; $1B authorization from Nov 2025) while H1 FCF was negative; cash fell $475M. SBC about $161M in 2025 (XBRL quarterly sum), 1.9% of sales. No dividend.
- Litigation (10-Q Note 12): Solventum indemnifies 3M for uninsured Bair Hugger liabilities and manages the defence; MDL bellwethers and eight state cases continue; "During the second quarter of 2026... the Company increased its litigation accrual to management's best estimate of probable loss at this time", no amount and no range given. PFAS: the FY25 10-K says 3M indemnifies certain PFAS claims for products sold through 2025, but "As an independent company, Solventum is generally responsible for all PFAS-related liabilities resulting from its business" after the spin.

## 8. Bull and bear
Bull: (1) Transform for the Future cost programme (about $500M a year when complete); (2) separation of HIS could unlock value and sharpen focus; (3) underlying organic growth of about 3.7% with margin gains.
Bear: (1) Q3 sales will be "negatively impacted" by reversal of $125M of early orders; (2) Bair Hugger accrual raised with no disclosed amount, plus PFAS responsibility; (3) FCF of $200-300M does not support the buyback pace or $5.1B debt.

## 9. Key risks and kill criteria
1. Q3 2026 organic sales growth below -2% (reported about 5 Nov 2026), or FY26 organic growth guide cut below +2.5%.
2. FY26 free cash flow below $200M (guide: $200M-$300M).
3. Consolidated total debt less cash (10-Q debt note and balance sheet) above $5.5B (now $4.68B), or any further increase in the Bair Hugger accrual disclosed in a 10-Q.
4. FY27 adjusted EPS guidance midpoint below $7.00 (would mean under 5% growth ex the tariff refund).
5. H2 2026 adjusted operating margin below 21% (H1 26: 24.2%, including the refund and ERP timing; H1 25: 20.8%).
