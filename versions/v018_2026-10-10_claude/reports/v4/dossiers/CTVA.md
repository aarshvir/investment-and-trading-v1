# CTVA - Corteva (agent F157, as of 2026-09-25 close, price $78.52)

## 1. Verdict
**WATCH** - the business is performing and the combined company is not expensive on cash flow, but the instrument changed on 1 Oct 2026: shareholders of record on 24 Sep 2026 received one Vylor (seed) share per Corteva share, so "CTVA" is now essentially the crop-protection company plus the legacy litigation. The 25 Sep close of $78.52 is the price of seed plus crop protection together; I cannot validate a standalone crop-protection price, capital structure or legacy-liability split without the Form 10 detail and the first post-spin filings. Horizon 12-36 months. Upgrade condition: first post-spin 10-Q shows crop-protection standalone EBITDA margin of at least 18%, net debt/EBITDA under 2x, and the legacy PFAS/chlorpyrifos exposure confirmed as capped. Research only, not personal advice.

## 2. Business in plain English
Corteva sells corn and soybean seed (Pioneer and other brands, plus patent-licensing income) and crop-protection chemicals and biologicals to farmers, through dealers and retailers. Seed is the profit engine: in H1 26 it produced $3.0B of the $3.78B segment operating EBITDA on $7.56B of sales; Crop Protection made $0.78B on $3.73B (20.8% margin). Moat in seed is germplasm and traits; in crop protection it is new molecules and biologicals, but price is under pressure in Latin America. The company has been splitting into two since the 1 Oct 2025 announcement.

## 8. Bull and bear
Bull: (1) three guidance raises, with margin up 190 bps in Q2; (2) seed has pricing power and a growing licensing business, and is the larger share of value; (3) the split could close a conglomerate discount, with net dis-synergy only about $25M in 2026.
Bear: (1) H1 26 FCF was -$3.5B and the stock carries litigation and pension cash demands; (2) Crop Protection price fell 4% and North America CP sales fell 12% in Q2; (3) the post-spin ticker holds the lower-margin business, EIDP legacy suits (PFAS, chlorpyrifos, FTC) and a debt stack I cannot yet verify.

## 9. Key risks and kill criteria
1. Crop-protection segment operating EBITDA margin below 15% (H1 26: 20.8%) in any full-year or trailing-four-quarter period reported after the separation.
2. Consolidated net debt / TTM Op. EBITDA of the post-spin Corteva (crop protection) above 2.5x at any quarter-end (Corteva, Inc. consolidated balance sheet).
3. Full-year Op. EPS guidance cut below the $3.60 low end of the 30 Jul 2026 range, or 2027 guidance implying less than 5% Op. EBITDA growth.
4. Accrued litigation (current, "accrued litigation" in the contingencies note; $468M at 30 Jun 2026) rising above $1.0B, or any adverse AFFF/PFAS ruling that exceeds the January 2021 MOU cost-sharing.
5. Crop-protection price decline worse than -4% year on year for two consecutive quarters.
