## 1. Business in plain English
Cintas rents and launders uniforms, mats and restroom supplies to over one million businesses, sells first aid and safety products and services, and inspects fire extinguishers and sprinklers. Customers sign multi-year route-based contracts; Cintas wins by route density (a truck that already stops at the building), which produces 50%+ gross margin in rental. Uniform Rental and Facility Services is about 76% of revenue (Q1 FY27: $2,294.7M of $3,014.0M), First Aid and Safety $388.5M, All Other $330.7M. Main competitors are UniFirst, Vestis and regional launderers; UniFirst is a direct uniform-rental competitor, which is why the FTC issued a second request on the merger.

## 2. Why the model likes it / durability
Quant context (d4, 25 Sep 2026 close, price $199.91): ROE 41%, low leverage, steady double-digit EPS growth. It is a quality and consistency name, not value (forward P/E about 32x on Yahoo forward EPS $6.16, which is a later-year number; 36.4x on the FY27 guide midpoint). Durable: yes, economics are contractual and improving (gross margin 51.5% in Q1 FY27, +120 bps). Artefact risks: Yahoo FCF ($1,623M) differs from the company definition (TTM $2,034M); balance sheet figures below predate the UniFirst financing and heavy buybacks.

Pro forma leverage stays well inside the covenant; the risk is antitrust (route-based uniform rental overlap), integration and a forced divestiture, not solvency.

## 8. Bull and bear
Bull: (1) Eight to nine percent organic growth driven by volume, cross-selling into First Aid and Safety (segment revenue +14% in FY26) and new accounts; (2) gross margin and operating margin at all-time highs with technology and capacity investment; (3) UniFirst adds density and a large cost-synergy pool if cleared.
Bear: (1) 36x earnings and 40x FCF leave no room; a multiple back to 28x costs about 20%; (2) FTC could block or force divestitures and the $350M fee would be payable by Cintas in specified circumstances; (3) buybacks are running above FCF ahead of a $2.85B bridge, and labour, energy and healthcare costs are a margin risk.

## 9. Kill criteria (measurable)
1. Organic revenue growth below 6% for two consecutive quarters (8.9% in Q1 FY27).
2. Fiscal 2027 adjusted EPS guide cut below $5.36 (the original low end), or adjusted operating margin below 22.5% for two quarters (23.6% now).
3. Consolidated debt/EBITDA (company definition, including standby letters of credit) above 2.5x after UniFirst closes (about 0.7x before the deal, covenant 3.5x).
4. FTC sues to block the UniFirst merger or the deal is terminated; or required divestitures exceed 25% of UniFirst revenue.
5. TTM FCF below $1.9B (TTM $2,034M).
