**2. Business.** Tyson processes and sells beef, pork and chicken, plus branded prepared foods (Tyson, Jimmy Dean, Hillshire Farm, Ball Park) and an international arm. Scale buyer of grain and cattle, scale seller to grocers and food service; moat is cost and distribution scale plus brands, not pricing power.
