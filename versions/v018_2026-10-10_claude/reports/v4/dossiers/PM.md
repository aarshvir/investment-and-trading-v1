# PM — Philip Morris International Inc.

**1. Verdict: INCLUDE-SMALL** (reservation: capex-driven near-term FCF compression + structurally negative
equity/high leverage). Marlboro-international owner whose smoke-free transformation (Zyn nicotine pouches, IQOS
heated tobacco) is a genuine, evidenced structural shift, not a one-off. Horizon: 24–36 months.

**2. Business in plain English.** PM sells cigarettes (Marlboro and other brands) outside the US, plus — its
growth engine — smoke-free nicotine products: IQOS heated-tobacco devices/consumables and Zyn nicotine pouches
(largest US oral nicotine brand). It profits from a mix shift toward higher-margin smoke-free volume as combustible
cigarette volumes decline secularly. Moat = brand strength (Marlboro), first-mover regulatory/scale position in
heated tobacco, and now US nicotine-pouch leadership via the 2022 Swedish Match acquisition.

**3. Why the model likes it (b1).** Composite decile 3/quintile 2 — mid-table overall. Quality pctile 0.53 (best
family) driven by strong ROE, OCF/assets 0.87 pctile; leverage pctile is weak (0.07, i.e. genuinely high leverage —
consistent with negative book equity, see §6) — **this is a real risk, not a model artefact.** Momentum (0.64) and
earnings-momentum (0.27, weaker) reflect the market has partly priced the smoke-free story already.

**9. Kill criteria:** (1) Adjusted EPS guidance is cut (vs the $8.35–$8.50 FY26 range) at any subsequent release.

company disclosure). (3) Net debt/EBITDA (consolidated) rises above 3.0x. (4) FCF yield falls below 2.5% for two
consecutive quarters. (5) A regulatory reversal (FDA marketing-denial order) hits a Zyn variant representing >10%
of pouch volume.
