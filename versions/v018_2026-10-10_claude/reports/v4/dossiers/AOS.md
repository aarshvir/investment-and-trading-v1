## 1. Verdict
**INCLUDE-SMALL** — thesis horizon 24–36 months. Named reservation: China (Rest of World segment) is still
deteriorating (sales −19% YoY in Q2 2026, −28% local currency in China specifically) and management has cut the
top end of full-year EPS guidance twice in two consecutive quarters — a real, filing-confirmed negative trend,
not just a one-off markdown as the original triage framed it. The North American core (water heaters + fast-
growing boilers, plus the newly acquired Leonard Valve) remains healthy and margin-generative even after
absorbing restructuring charges. At 14.4–14.7x NTM P/E for a ~27% trailing ROE, capital-light duopoly business
with a rising buyback, the valuation gives real downside cushion, but the reservation (unresolved China
overhang, two-in-a-row guidance trims) argues for a half-weight position rather than full conviction.

## 2. Business in plain English
A. O. Smith makes and sells residential and commercial water heaters, boilers and water-treatment products. In
North America it holds a duopoly-like leading position (alongside Rheem) built on brand, distributor
relationships and a large installed base needing replacement (most water heaters are sold as break/replace, not
discretionary purchases, which smooths demand). Its Rest of World segment is overwhelmingly China, where it
sells water heaters and, increasingly, faces a structurally weaker consumer-appliance market.

## 8. Bull case / Bear case
**Bull (3):**
1. NA segment organic sales +3% and boiler volumes +21% in Q2 2026 show the core replacement-driven water-
   heater/boiler franchise is executing well even in a soft residential demand backdrop; adjusted NA margin held
   at 24.4% despite input-cost pressure.
2. FY2026 buyback target raised to $300m (from an unstated lower prior level) even as EPS guidance was cut — a
   direct signal management views the ~15x forward multiple as attractive relative to intrinsic value. **[Corrected 2026-10-06: the prior FY2026 target was disclosed: $200M (Q1 2026 release, acc 0000091142-26-000082); raised 50% to $300M, see Correction section DV04]**
3. Leonard Valve (Jan-2026, bolt-on) extends AOS into adjacent commercial-valve markets and is already
   contributing to NA sales growth; a template for further capital-light bolt-on M&A funded by AOS's strong FCF.

**Bear (3):**
1. China (Rest of World) sales fell 19% YoY (28% local currency) in Q2 2026 with segment margin collapsing from
   10.5% to 5.2% — two consecutive quarters of guidance cuts confirm this is not yet bottoming, and management's
   own language ("continue our strategic assessment of the business") signals unresolved strategic uncertainty,
   potentially including a future divestiture or further write-down.
2. Debt jumped roughly 4x since December 2025 (to $637.5m, 25.7% leverage) to fund the Leonard Valve deal —
   still a low absolute leverage level for this business, but the trend direction (rising, not falling) is worth
   monitoring if further bolt-on M&A is layered on before China stabilizes.
3. GAAP diluted EPS has now missed the prior-year comparable in both of the last two quarters (−11% Q1, −15% Q2)
   and the FY2026 guidance range itself has been trimmed twice; a third cut at the Q3 2026 release (due
   ~27-Oct-2026) would be a clear deterioration signal.
