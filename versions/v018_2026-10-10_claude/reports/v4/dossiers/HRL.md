**2. Business in plain English.** Hormel makes branded packaged meats and snacks (SPAM, Applegate, Hormel Black Label, Planters, Skippy, Jennie-O turkey), sells to grocers (Retail), restaurants and institutions (Foodservice, 12 straight quarters of organic growth) and exports (International). It earns a thin 8-10% adjusted operating margin and is exposed to hog, turkey and grain costs. It has raised its dividend for 60 years (Q4 FY25 release, acc. 0000048465-25-000056).

**3. Why the model likes it.** b1/d4: NTM P/E 12.7x, FCF yield, dividend yield 5.9%, very low beta (0.3). Durable part: staple demand and Foodservice. Artefact risk: the cheap multiple reflects years of earnings below the 2021-22 level and portfolio write-downs; GAAP trailing P/E is 31.8x on $0.62 EPS because of impairments.
