# Flex Ltd. (NASDAQ: FLEX) — Diligence Dossier

## 1. Verdict
**WATCH** (12–36 month horizon). Overturns the triage's implicit INCLUDE lean. Flex's own filings show that the AI-datacenter power/cooling business the triage cited as the growth driver — JetCool liquid cooling, Anord Mardix and Crown Technical Systems power distribution — is the Cloud and Power Infrastructure ("CPI") segment Flex has publicly committed to spinning off into an independent company ("SpinCo") in Q1 calendar 2027, retaining ≤19.9% of SpinCo post-spin. The remaining "RemainCo" that will carry the FLEX ticker afterward is weighted back toward lower-margin, more cyclical EMS (Agility/Reliability segments: automotive, industrial, consumer, communications). Shareholders receive SpinCo shares pro rata (tax-free spin), so total look-through value isn't necessarily destroyed, but anyone buying FLEX today for its AI-cooling exposure is buying an asset that will legally detach within roughly two quarters of this report.

## 2. Business in plain English
Flex is a contract electronics manufacturer (EMS): it designs, builds and manages products for other companies' brands across data-center/cloud infrastructure, automotive, industrial, health-tech and consumer devices, earning thin manufacturing margins on high revenue volumes plus higher-margin fees for value-added engineering and, increasingly, power/cooling systems it now designs itself for AI data centers.

## 3. Why the model likes it — durable or artefact?
The triage cited "20.6% revenue growth and a real margin-mix shift toward higher-value datacenter power/cooling" at 19.5x NTM P/E for ~40% implied Street upside. The revenue growth is real and accelerating (see §4). The margin-mix shift is real too — but it lives almost entirely inside CPI, the segment being spun off. Once CPI leaves, RemainCo's growth/margin mix reverts toward its historical EMS profile unless the core Reliability/Agility businesses (automotive, industrial) reaccelerate independently — which the Q1 FY27 release says they are ("continued execution... from joining the S&P 500 to expanding our role in AI infrastructure"), but the bulk of the AI upside itself is walking out the door. **This is a partial artefact**: the factor score is scoring a business mix that will not exist in its current form for long.

## 6. Earnings quality & balance sheet
- **FCF conversion:** FY26 FCF/adjusted net income = $1,060M/$1,248M ≈ 85%; Q1 FY27 FCF/adjusted net income = $41M/$374M ≈ 11% (depressed by capex for the EPP integration and working-capital build — accounts payable +$1.1B, receivables +$0.36B q/q). **[Corrected 2026-10-10: these balance changes include acquired EPP balances; the cash-flow working-capital line was -$149M]**
- **GAAP-to-adjusted gap:** Q1 FY27 non-GAAP adjustments of $142M (SBC $51M, "legal and other" $67M — of which $53M is spin-off/portfolio-optimization costs plus $14M acquisition costs — and intangible amortization $23M) turned a $392M GAAP operating income into $534M adjusted. The spin costs are real cash costs, not purely non-cash.
- **Leverage:** consolidated long-term debt jumped from $3,751M (Mar-26) to $5,219M (Jun-26) to fund the EPP acquisition ($1,134M cash out in investing activities); cash rose to $2,840M on $2,830M of new borrowings. Net debt ≈ $2,379M as of Jun-26 vs. ≈$1,362M as of Mar-26 — leverage nearly doubled in one quarter, entity: **Flex Ltd. consolidated balance sheet, Schedule III of the 8-K**.
- **Share count:** diluted shares fell from 389M (Q4 FY25) to 374M (Q1 FY27) on buybacks (FY26 buybacks $944M), a tailwind to EPS growth independent of operating performance.
- **Ukraine exposure:** an August 21, 2025 missile strike on Flex's Mukachevo, Ukraine facility caused $51M of FY26 asset impairments/write-downs (per the Q4 FY26 release) — a live geopolitical tail risk to the Reliability segment's European footprint.

## 8. Bull case / Bear case
**Bull:** (1) FY27 guidance raised twice in successive quarters on real order strength; (2) shareholders keep economic exposure to the AI-cooling story via SpinCo shares even after the spin; (3) buybacks and S&P 500 inclusion support the multiple.
**Bear:** (1) the specific factor-model driver (datacenter power/cooling mix) is leaving the FLEX ticker within about two quarters of this report, and RemainCo's guidance economics after the spin are not yet disclosed; (2) leverage roughly doubled in one quarter to fund the EPP deal, adding balance-sheet risk right before a corporate separation; (3) FCF conversion collapsed to 11% in the most recent quarter and core EMS margins remain structurally thin (GAAP operating margin ~5%).

## 9. Key risks & kill criteria
1. RemainCo (post-spin) FY28 adjusted operating margin guidance, once disclosed, comes in below 6.0% (vs. 7.0-7.2% consolidated FY27 guide) — signals the AI mix benefit left with SpinCo as feared.
2. Consolidated net debt/EBITDA (EBITDA proxy: adjusted operating income + D&A) exceeds 2.0x at any quarter-end before the spin closes.
3. Free cash flow conversion (FCF/adjusted net income) stays below 40% for two consecutive quarters.
4. FY27 guidance is cut (not raised) at any of the next two quarterly releases.
5. The CPI spin-off is delayed beyond Q2 calendar 2027 or restructured to retain >19.9% Flex ownership, signalling execution trouble.
