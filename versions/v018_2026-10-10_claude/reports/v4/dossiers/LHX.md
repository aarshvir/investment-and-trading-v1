# LHX - L3Harris Technologies, Inc. (NYSE: LHX) - standard-depth diligence (agent F140)

**1. Verdict: INCLUDE-SMALL. Horizon 24-36 months.** The triage "no advance" (average quality, modest growth) does not hold up on primary sources: Q2 2026 revenue +8%, record backlog, guidance raised in three of the last four releases, and a 25 Sep 2026 price that implies only about 1.8% a year of free-cash-flow growth for ten years against my base of about 6.5%. The triage also missed the biggest adverse fact: on 17 Aug 2026 the Board ended CEO Christopher Kubasik's employment after an investigation into conduct "not consistent with the values of the Company" (8-K, accession 0000202058-26-000066). Named reservation: until the new CEO's first guidance and the full Board explanation are in, half weight only.

**2. Business in plain English.** L3Harris builds the electronics, radios, sensors, space payloads, missile propulsion and mission aircraft systems that the US Department of War (DoW) and allies buy. Three segments (Q2 release revenue guide table: "$11,700", "$8,000", "$4,100" in $ millions): Space & Mission Systems about $11.7B revenue, Communications & Spectrum Dominance about $8.0B (25-27% margins, the profit engine), Missile Solutions about $4.1B (Aerojet Rocketdyne rocket motors, where demand is surging). Revenue is mostly multi-year government contracts; backlog was $42.0B at 3 Jul 2026 and about 40% converts to revenue within 12 months (10-Q Note O, accession 0000202058-26-000058). Moat: sole-source positions, classified programs, installed base of tactical radios, and rocket-motor capacity that the DoW is funding directly.

**9. Key risks and kill criteria.**
1. Full-year 2026 free cash flow guidance cut below $2.8B (currently "$3.0B", Q2 2026 release), or H2 2026 free cash flow below $2.0B.
2. Segment operating margin below 15.5% in two consecutive quarters (16.0% in Q2 2026; guide "low 16%").
3. Book-to-bill below 1.0x in two consecutive quarters (1.2x in Q2 2026; backlog $42.0B).
4. Any Board or SEC/DOJ disclosure that the CEO investigation touched financial reporting, government-contract compliance or a restatement, or the CFO or a segment president leaves within 12 months.
5. Missile Solutions Qualified IPO not completed by 31 Dec 2027 (non-IPO redemption terms then apply to the $1.0B preferred), or consolidated total long-term debt (carrying value, including current portion) above $12.0B at any quarter-end ($10.999B at 3 Jul 2026).
