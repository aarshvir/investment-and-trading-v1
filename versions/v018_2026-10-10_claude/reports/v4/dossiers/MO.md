# Altria Group, Inc. (MO) - Fundamental Diligence Dossier (F128, standard depth)

**Verdict: WATCH**, 12-36 month horizon. Reason: a very profitable US cigarette franchise whose price (68.82 at 25 Sep 2026 close) already equals what a realistic cash-flow path is worth; the market implies about +1.0% a year of free-cash-flow growth and my base case is +0.8%, so there is no margin of safety against a business losing about 4-5% of cigarette volume a year and carrying open-ended regulatory and litigation risk. Change condition: a price near 60 (implied growth about -1%, 7.4% dividend yield) or smoke-free profit turning positive (oral tobacco operating income is currently falling, -23.5% in Q2 2026). Triage (Q03, not advanced, "yield trap") is broadly confirmed; I soften its tone: cash conversion is excellent, the issue is price versus growth, not quality.

## 1. Business in plain English
Altria sells Marlboro and other cigarettes in the US (PM USA), moist smokeless tobacco (Copenhagen, Skoal) and on! nicotine pouches (USSTC, Helix), plus the NJOY vapour brand. It earns money by raising prices faster than volumes fall: Smokeable adjusted operating income margin was 64.8% in Q2 2026. Moat is brand, distribution and regulatory barriers; threats are falling smoking rates, discount-brand switching (discount share of cigarettes 33.8%, +2.6 points year on year) and illicit disposable vapes. It also owns about 8.1% of Anheuser-Busch InBev (equity method) and 42.2% of Cronos.

## 2. Why the model likes it / durability
b1 shows a high-yield, low-beta, value-and-quality profile (Yahoo beta 0.49, payout 0.89, trailing P/E 14.5x on GAAP EPS 4.75, forward P/E 11.7x on 5.86). Durable in the sense that cash flow is stable; the multiple is cheap because the volume decline is permanent. Artefacts: Yahoo revenue (20.44B) is revenue net of excise taxes, not reported net revenues (23.28B for 2025), and GAAP EPS is depressed by 2025 goodwill impairments and swings with ABI and litigation items, so adjusted EPS is the usable series.

## 7. Bull and bear
Bull: (1) 65% smokeable margins and pricing power keep adjusted EPS growing 3-5% a year while volume falls; (2) the ABI stake is worth $13.2B against a $8.6B carrying value, and oral nicotine (on! PLUS expanding to 120,000 stores, more flavours in Q4) could stabilise oral profit; (3) cost programme targets "cumulative savings of at least $600 million by the end of 2029" (Q4 2025 release).
Bear: (1) trade-adjusted domestic cigarette volume fell an estimated 4.5% in Q2 2026 (industry 5%) while Marlboro retail share fell to 39.5% (-1.5 points year on year), and discount share is rising; (2) oral tobacco profit is shrinking (Q2 -23.5%) and NJOY ACE "does not return to the marketplace in 2026" (guide assumption iv); (3) payout is about 78% of guided EPS with capex stepping up, leaving little for buybacks, and tail risks (FDA, litigation, Canada JUUL class) are open-ended.

## 8. Kill criteria (thesis-invalidation triggers)
1. Full-year 2026 adjusted diluted EPS guidance cut below the current $5.61 low end, or reported FY2026 adjusted EPS below $5.56.
2. Trade-adjusted smokeable domestic cigarette shipment volume decline worse than 8% in two consecutive quarters (4.5% in Q2 2026).
3. Smokeable adjusted operating income margin below 62.0% in two consecutive quarters (64.8% in Q2 2026; 62.4% total adjusted OCI margin for 2025).
4. Dividend not increased at the next annual raise, or declared dividend per share above 90% of trailing adjusted EPS (about 78% now on guide mid-point).
5. A final FDA rule banning menthol cigarettes, or a US tobacco-and-health judgment above $1 billion against PM USA or Altria that is not stayed on appeal.

## 10. Red-flag scan
- Auditor, restatement, going-concern: none seen in the Q2 2026 10-Q (0000764180-26-000094); I did not read the auditor's report on the 10-K.
- Litigation (10-Q Note 12, as of 27 Jul 2026): accrued liability for tobacco and health and certain other litigation $156M at 30 Jun 2026; 20 opt-out MDL cases remain; Altria is defendant in four JUUL e-vapor class actions in Canada and in July 2026 a British Columbia court "certified a nationwide class" of JUUL purchasers ("Altria intends to appeal"); PM USA is defendant in nine Canadian health-care-cost actions and six smoking-and-health class actions, but Canadian tobacco claims are covered by a CAD 32.5B global plan under which "neither Altria nor PM USA is responsible for any payments" (indemnity from PMI).
- Regulation: ITC exclusion order on NJOY ACE (January 2025) and FDA processes; the 10-Q says illicit disposable vapes are being enforced "more gradually than initially expected".
- Impairments: 2025 goodwill impairment $873M on e-vapor; 10-Q says no further impairment indicators in H1 2026 but the fair-value headroom is not quantified in what I read.
- Governance: 8-K of 28 Aug 2026 (0000764180-26-000098, Item 5.02) elected a new director, Steven W. Presley, board now 11; routine. Form 3 (2 Sep) and Form 4 (11 Sep) filings exist; I did not open them, so no insider-selling conclusion.
