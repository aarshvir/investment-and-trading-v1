# LYV - Live Nation Entertainment (Communication Services / Movies & Entertainment)
**Verdict: WATCH** - record demand and strong Ticketmaster and sponsorship economics, but a lost antitrust jury trial with possible Ticketmaster divestiture, and a price that already assumes compounding. Horizon 12-36 months. Revisit when the remedies order is known and FCF-adjusted yield reaches about 5% (about $115 a share on current numbers).

## 1. Business in plain English
Live Nation promotes concerts, owns and operates venues (Concerts), sells tickets through Ticketmaster (Ticketing) and sells brand sponsorships (Sponsorship & Advertising). Scale across artists, venues and ticketing is the moat, which is also what the states attack.

## 7. Bull and bear
Bull: (1) strongest ticket sales on record (143M sold through mid-July, 14M ahead); (2) international and venue build-out with 20%+ IRRs claimed; (3) sponsorship AOI +13% and record deferred revenue.
Bear: (1) jury liability on all counts, per-ticket damages not sized, remedies may include Ticketmaster divestiture; (2) near-zero equity, $3.0B current debt, converts in the money; (3) about 20x AOI and low free cash flow after growth capex.

## 8. Risks and kill criteria
1. Court enters equitable remedies in the Litigating States case requiring divestiture of Ticketmaster or sale of Live Nation-owned amphitheaters (docket order in the SDNY antitrust case, United States v. Live Nation).
2. Additional litigation accrual or judgment beyond the $450 million recorded in H1 2026 (10-Q Note 6, Corporate expenses) takes aggregate charges above $900 million in any subsequent filing, including any per-ticket damages calculation trebled under the Clayton Act.
3. Full-year 2026 consolidated adjusted operating income (non-GAAP, release reconciliation) grows less than 10% from 2025, contradicting 'on track for double-digit adjusted operating income growth this year' (Q2 2026 release).
4. Free cash flow - adjusted (company non-GAAP, release reconciliation) for full-year 2026 is below $1.3 billion, against $1,298.2 million in 2025, meaning growth capex is not converting.
5. Event-related deferred revenue at quarter-end (Q2 2026 record $6.4 billion, consolidated) grows less than 10% year over year at 30 Sep 2026 or 31 Dec 2026, signalling demand or sell-through has turned.
