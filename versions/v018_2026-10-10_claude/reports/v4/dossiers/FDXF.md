Base 3-year return ~+5.9% a year, so no margin of safety yet; entry where implied = base is about **$92**. Triage rejected it on "6.2x net debt/EBITDA"; that number does not reproduce (debt-only net leverage is ~2.8x on adjusted EBITDA), so the objection is the price and the spin-year uncertainty, not insolvency risk.

**2. Business in plain English.** Truck-based shipping of freight too big for parcels but too small for a full truck: ~86k shipments a day across 365+ locations in the US, Canada and Mexico (Priority and Economy services), ~$8.8B of revenue. It earns money from price per shipment and network density; fuel surcharges pass fuel costs through. Moat: biggest network and brand, but its adjusted operating margin (~12-14%) is believed to trail the best LTL peer (Old Dominion), a gap not quantified from filings in this pass, which is both the opportunity and the risk.
