## 2. Business in plain English
Agilent sells lab instruments (chromatography, mass spectrometry), the consumables and service contracts that keep them running (CrossLab, about 40% of revenue, 34.3% segment operating margin in Q3 FY26), and diagnostics products (Biocare pathology added 24 Jun 2026). Customers are pharma, biopharma, chemical and environmental labs. The installed base and recurring service annuity are the moat; instruments are cyclical.

## 8. Bull and bear
Bull: (1) three straight raises and LDG core +10% in Q3 FY26 show a genuine instrument upcycle; (2) 130+ bps of FY26 non-GAAP margin expansion from the Ignite programme; (3) Biocare adds recurring pathology revenue to LDG. Bear: (1) 25-28x earnings leaves no room if pharma or China capital spending softens; (2) the latest raise includes a non-recurring refund and about 18.5% GAAP tax rate; (3) GAAP EPS growth (8%) trails non-GAAP (18%) because of $95M nine-month "transformational initiatives" costs that recur every year.

## 9. Key risks and kill criteria
1. Core (organic) revenue growth below 3% for two consecutive quarters (FY26 Q3 was 7.3%; FY26 guide is 5.8% to 6.0%).
2. Consolidated net debt (carrying debt less cash and equivalents, condensed consolidated balance sheet) above 2.5x TTM EBITDA (about 1.1x at 31 Jul 2026).
3. Non-GAAP operating margin falling below 25% in any quarter once the one-off tariff refund benefit (about 110 bps in Q3 FY26) is stripped out (Q3 FY26 ex-refund: about 27.2%).
4. Full-year FY27 non-GAAP EPS guidance initiated below $6.60, which would imply growth under 6% on the FY26 guide midpoint of $6.195.
5. Biocare (acquired 24 Jun 2026, $950 million net) impairment or restructuring charge above $100 million within 24 months of closing.
