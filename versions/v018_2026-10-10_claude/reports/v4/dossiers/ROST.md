# ROST - Ross Stores (Nasdaq) - diligence dossier F118 (STANDARD depth)

**1. Verdict: WATCH** (horizon 24-36 months). A best-in-class off-price retailer with net cash and a 42.6% ROE, but at $236.12 the stock trades near 29x FY26 earnings excluding a one-off tariff refund; the price implies faster cash-flow growth than my base case after a record-margin first half. Would upgrade near $200 (about 24x ex-refund FY26 EPS) or after two quarters proving operating margin above 13% ex one-offs with comps above 4%.

**2. Business.** Ross Dress for Less and dd's DISCOUNTS sell brand-name apparel, footwear and home goods at 20-60% below department-store prices; it buys opportunistically from brands and retailers with excess inventory and rents small-box stores (about 1,909 Ross and 364 dd's at Nov 2025). Moat: buying scale and low-cost store model; growth from 115 new stores planned in 2026 and traffic-led comps.
