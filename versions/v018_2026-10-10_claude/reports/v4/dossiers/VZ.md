# Verizon Communications Inc. (VZ) - Fundamental Diligence Dossier (F128, standard depth)

**Verdict: INCLUDE-SMALL** (half weight), 12-36 month horizon. Reason: at 47.08 (25 Sep 2026 close) the price implies free cash flow shrinking about 2% a year for a decade even after I strip out timing help, while a company that has raised guidance two quarters in a row is guiding to growth. Named reservation: leverage and cash-flow quality. Consolidated debt is $165.2B after the Frontier deal, and part of the 2026 free-cash-flow jump is cash-tax deferral and lower device financing that will not repeat. Triage (Q01, not advanced: quality 3, growth 3, "restructuring-driven") is overturned on price: the growth bar is low, and the turnaround has delivered two raised guides, but I hold it at half weight until leverage falls.

## 1. Business in plain English
Verizon is one of the three US national wireless carriers. Consumers and businesses pay monthly for mobile service and, increasingly, home broadband over fixed wireless (5G) and its own fibre (now 31 states after buying Frontier on 20 Jan 2026). Customers are sticky but switching and promotions are intense; wireless service revenue is guided "approximately flat" in 2026 while mobility-plus-broadband service revenue grows 2.5% to 3.0%. Moat is network quality, spectrum ($158.2B of licences on the balance sheet) and scale; threats are price competition, cable mobile resellers, and a heavy debt load.

## 2. Why the model likes it / durability

Drivers are value (FCF yield, earnings yield) and low beta (Yahoo 0.24), not growth. Durable as long as cash flow holds; the discount reflects leverage, flat wireless revenue and a turnaround with a new CEO (Dan Schulman). Artefacts: Yahoo FCF (17.5B) is below the company definition (TTM about 21.5B, my calculation from 10-Q and 10-K), and Yahoo total debt (193.6B) includes operating lease liabilities.

## 7. Bull and bear
Bull: (1) mobility and broadband service revenue is guided to "approach 3.0 percent in third-quarter 2026 and approximately 4.0 percent in fourth-quarter 2026" with 184,000 postpaid phone net adds and 348,000 broadband net adds in Q2; (2) record adjusted EBITDA margin (40.1%) and FCF +16% in H1 with buybacks of up to $4.5B; (3) 17.1 million fixed wireless and fibre connections give a convergence bundle.
Bear: (1) consolidated debt $165.2B, total leverage about 3.0-3.2x and $21.8B due within a year, so a rate or credit shock hits; (2) FCF quality: tax deferral and low device-upgrade working capital flatter 2026 and vendor financing and spectrum sit outside FCF; (3) wireless service revenue is "approximately flat" and equipment revenue is falling about 20%, so growth depends on volume adds in a promotional market, and the January 2026 network outage already cost 80 basis points of wireless service revenue growth in Q1.
