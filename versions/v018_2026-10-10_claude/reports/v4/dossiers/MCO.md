# Moody's Corporation (MCO) — Diligence Dossier

**Agent:** F54 | **As of:** 2026-09-25 close ($468.70) | **Depth:** Standard

**Note on naming:** Moody's Corporation (ticker MCO) has two operating segments that it labels "MIS"
(Moody's Investors Service — credit ratings) and "**MA**" (Moody's Analytics — data/research/software). This
dossier always writes "MA segment" for Moody's Analytics to avoid confusion with Mastercard's ticker MA,
which is a separate company covered in a separate dossier.

## 1. Verdict
**INCLUDE-SMALL** (24–36 month horizon), reservation: the price already assumes MCO keeps delivering
mid-teens adjusted EPS growth and margin expansion every quarter — my reverse DCF implied growth (≈12%/yr)
is essentially **in line with**, not below, a realistic base case, so there is limited valuation cushion if
rated-debt issuance (structurally procyclical) turns down. Near-duopoly ratings franchise (with S&P Global)
plus a fast-growing analytics/software arm, with an excellent and improving guidance track record, but a
thin (and recently shrinking) equity base funded mostly by debt against a buyback program.

## 2. Business in plain English
Moody's has two businesses under one roof: **MIS**, which rates bonds and loans for issuers (governments,
corporates, structured finance) and earns a fee — largely a toll on global debt-capital-markets issuance
volume, in a global duopoly with S&P Global; and the **MA segment**, which sells data, analytics, research,
risk-management software and now AI-enabled decision tools to banks, insurers and corporates on a
subscription/ARR basis. MIS is highly cyclical with issuance volumes but very high-margin; the MA segment is
recurring-revenue and growing faster, diversifying MCO away from bond-market cyclicality over time.

## 3. Why the model likes it — durable or artefact?
Triage (Q05) called out 76.9% ROE and a "near-regulatory moat," with the 25.5x multiple looking fair for 20%
EPS growth. Confirmed and refined: `b1_live_scores.csv` (2026-09-25) shows gross-profit/assets in the 99th
percentile and OCF/assets in the 99th percentile — genuinely best-in-class capital efficiency, not an
accounting artefact, because MIS requires almost no incremental capital to rate an additional bond. The
"76.9% ROE" figure is itself partly an artefact of a small, buyback-shrunk equity base (Total Moody's
shareholders' equity fell from $4.054bn to $3.025bn in just six months — see §6) rather than pure operating
economics; this dossier relies on operating cash flow, adjusted operating margin and the guidance track
record (§5) rather than ROE as the primary quality evidence.

## 8. Bull case / Bear case
**Bull:** (1) the MA segment (Moody's Analytics) grew ARR +9% YoY in 1Q26 and margins expanded 250bps,
diversifying MCO's earnings away from bond-issuance cyclicality over time; (2) MIS delivered record quarterly
revenue ($1.26bn, 2Q26, +25% YoY) on record global rated issuance, showing the ratings franchise still has
significant operating leverage in a strong issuance environment; (3) four consecutive guidance
raises/narrowings-upward (§5) show real forecasting discipline and execution, not promotional guidance.
**Bear:** (1) MIS revenue is a leveraged bet on global debt-issuance volumes, which are macro- and rate-cycle
dependent — a credit-market slowdown (the historical pattern in every prior MCO down-cycle) would hit
transactional MIS revenue (34% YoY growth in 2Q26, meaning a high base to lap) hard and fast, with limited
warning; (2) the equity base is thin and shrinking ($3.0bn against $6.9bn of debt) — MCO relies on continued
market access to refinance debt as it comes due, a real (if currently low-probability) risk if credit markets
seize up precisely when MIS revenue would also be falling; (3) the reverse DCF shows little valuation
cushion — a growth disappointment would likely be met with a multiple de-rating, not just slower growth.

## 9. Key risks & kill criteria (measurable)
1. FY2026 adjusted diluted EPS guidance is cut below the $16.50–$17.00 range reaffirmed/narrowed as of the
   2026-07-22 release.
2. Consolidated revenue growth falls below the guided high-single-digit range (i.e. below 7% YoY) for two
   consecutive quarters.
3. MIS transactional revenue declines YoY for two consecutive quarters (a leading indicator of an
   issuance-volume downturn).
4. Consolidated net debt/TTM EBITDA exceeds 3.0x (from ≈1.35x currently).
5. Total Moody's shareholders' equity turns negative on the consolidated balance sheet (from $3,025M
   currently), which would signal buybacks have outpaced retained earnings by a materially wider margin than
   today.
