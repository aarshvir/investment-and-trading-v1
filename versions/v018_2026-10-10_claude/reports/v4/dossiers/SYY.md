# SYY — Sysco Corporation (F144, wave 8 standard diligence)

**1. Verdict: INCLUDE-SMALL (half weight), 12–36 month horizon.** Sysco is a steady, cash-generative category leader that is mid-way through a very large, debt-funded purchase of Jetro Restaurant Depot. The standalone business is priced for less growth than it is delivering. The named reservation is the pending deal: FTC second request, roughly 5x pro forma leverage, and buybacks paused. The triage said "not advance" on a fair 15x P/E. I overturn it partly: the price is reasonable, but the real issue is the deal, which the triage did not mention.

**One-line thesis:** America's largest restaurant-food distributor is buying the cash-and-carry leader with mostly borrowed money, and the stock is cheap enough to pay for that if regulators approve.

## 2. Business in plain English
Sysco sells and delivers food and supplies to restaurants, hospitals, schools, hotels and stadiums (about 75,000 employees; US Foodservice is the largest of three reportable segments). It earns a thin gross margin (18.5% FY26) on about $85B of sales by being the biggest and densest route network. Its moat is scale and logistics, not pricing power. On 30 Mar 2026 it agreed to buy Jetro Restaurant Depot (JRD), a 166-store cash-and-carry chain with about $16B of sales (8-K 0000950142-26-000899, Item 7.01).

## 3. Why the model likes it, and is it durable
b1/d4 show a low-beta (0.63), 5%+ FCF-yield staple with 15.2x NTM P/E. That is durable. The caveat is that Yahoo's "freeCashflow" ($1.91B) and debt ($15.05B) in d4 include leases; the consolidated debt on the company's table is $13.5B (see section 6).

- FY26 initial range (quoted in later releases): adjusted EPS "$4.50-$4.60". Q1 release not opened.
- Q2 (27 Jan 2026, acc 0000096021-26-000006): "we now expect our full year adjusted EPS to be at the high end of our previously provided guidance range of $4.50-$4.60." Direction: raised to top of range.
- 30 Mar 2026 (Jetro 8-K, acc 0000950142-26-000899, Ex. 99.1): "Sysco reiterating its outlook for sales growth of 3% to 5% and adjusted EPS to be at the high end of" the range. Maintained.
- Q3 (28 Apr 2026, acc 0000096021-26-000019): "support our confidence in delivering full-year adjusted EPS at the high end of our $4.50 to $4.60 guidance range". Maintained.
- Q4 (4 Aug 2026, acc 0000096021-26-000026): actual $4.61, above the top of the range; sales +3.9% was in the lower half of 3%-5%. FY27 new: "introducing guidance on a 53-week basis that includes sales growth of 6%-7% and adjusted EPS growth of 9%-11%". On $4.61 that is $5.02-5.12. The release does not say whether Jetro is included; 6%-7% sales growth cannot include a business that adds about 19% to sales, so I treat it as standalone (my inference). The 53rd week is worth about 2 points of the growth (my estimate, not stated).

Net debt about $32.3B over management's $6.4B pro forma adjusted EBITDA is about 5.0x (my arithmetic) vs the stated 2.75x target; management says "reduce net leverage by at least 1.0x in the first 24 months" and buybacks are paused.

## 8. Bull and bear
Bull: (1) local volume growth turning positive and accelerating (+2.6% Q4); (2) about $100M AI and cost-out savings in FY27; (3) JRD margin and synergy upside with 125+ new-store runway.
Bear: (1) 5x leverage at about 6% coupons on the new notes, with GAAP EPS diluted to $2.83 pro forma; (2) FTC second request received 27 May 2026 could force remedies or a block; (3) the interim CFO (Brandon Sewell, Q4 release) and 53-week FY27 flatter growth.

## 9. Key risks and kill criteria
1. FTC suit to block, or merger agreement terminated, before the target close of fiscal Q3 2027 (by 27 Mar 2027).
2. Consolidated net debt/adjusted EBITDA (earnings-release Net Debt table) above 4.5x at fiscal year-end June 2027, or not down at least 0.5x within 12 months of close.
3. US Foodservice local case volume growth below 1.0% in two consecutive quarters (Q4 FY26: +2.6%).
4. FY27 adjusted EPS growth guidance cut below the 9% low end, or actual FY27 adjusted EPS below $4.98 (FY26 $4.61 x 1.08).
5. JRD segment sales down year on year for two consecutive quarters after close (2025: +3.1%).

## 10. Catalysts and calendar
Next earnings 3 Nov 2026 (Yahoo estimate, unconfirmed). FTC compliance with the second request, then 30-day waiting period; ex-dividend dates; remaining financing (about $21B planned vs about $16-17B raised so far, my sum of the above).
