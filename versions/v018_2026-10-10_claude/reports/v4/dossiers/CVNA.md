# CVNA - Carvana Co. (CIK 1690820) - diligence dossier (agent F169, wave 8)

**1. Verdict: WATCH** (12-36 months). The operating story is genuinely strong (10-quarter run of record units and profits), but at $65.06 (25 Sep 2026; post 5-for-1 split, ~1.127B fully-converted shares) the stock already discounts about 20.9% a year FCF growth for a decade versus my 19.5% base (itself near management's own 3M-unit goal). Base 3-year return +7.8% a year, below the ~10% hurdle; entry where implied = base is about **$58**. Triage's "do not advance" is only partly right: its "FY guidance cut" is wrong (see data conflicts); the real issues are valuation and earnings quality.

**2. Business in plain English.** Online used-car retailer: buys cars from consumers and auctions (it now owns ADESA wholesale sites), reconditions them in its own plants, delivers nationwide, and arranges loans which it sells to Ally and other buyers for a gain. Q2'26: 197,325 retail units (+37.7% y/y), ~2% of used-car sales. Moat: selection from owned reconditioning capacity (~1.5M units/yr built out), logistics density and brand; scale advantage in SG&A per unit. Weak points: reliance on loan-sale gains, a related party (DriveTime) for service contracts, and a controlling family.

**3. Why the model likes/dislikes it.** b1 gives no composite (only 2 of 4 families): quality family 0.07 (gross profit/assets 38th pct, accruals 3rd pct, asset growth 3rd pct), momentum 0.54 (12-1 return -2.6%). No value read (no trailing FCF/earnings yield in b1). The weak quality percentiles are real: asset growth +55% and accruals reflect inventory/receivable build, not an artefact.
