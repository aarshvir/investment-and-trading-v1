## 2. Business in plain English
Emerson makes the automation software, instruments, valves and control systems that run factories, refineries, power plants and liquid-natural-gas facilities (its Automation Solutions segment), plus a smaller climate-technologies/tools business. Since acquiring majority control of AspenTech (industrial software), Emerson has been repositioning toward higher-margin software and recurring-revenue automation content sold to process industries (chemicals, energy, life sciences) and discrete manufacturers. It makes money on equipment sales plus a growing tail of software licenses/maintenance and services.

## 8. Bull case / Bear case
**Bull:** (1) AspenTech software integration lifts consolidated margins and multiple as the mix shifts toward recurring software revenue; (2) a global automation capex upcycle (reshoring, LNG buildout, life-sciences capacity) sustains mid-to-high-single-digit organic growth; (3) deleveraging continues (debt already down YoY) freeing capital for buybacks.
**Bull risk cuts both ways** — the wide $104–$205 analyst target range signals genuine disagreement on the AspenTech-adjusted earnings power.
**Bear:** (1) Industrial capex slows in a global growth scare, hitting order books with a lag; (2) the GAAP/adjusted EPS gap turns out to be structural (ongoing restructuring, not one-off), meaning the market has been paying for earnings quality that won't materialize; (3) AspenTech integration costs or write-downs recur, further depressing GAAP net income and testing investor patience with the "adjusted" narrative.

## 9. Key risks & measurable kill criteria

2. Consolidated net debt/EBITDA rises above 2.5x (currently ≈1.9x).
3. The trailing/NTM P/E gap widens further (i.e., GAAP EPS deteriorates further) without an accompanying explicit one-off/restructuring disclosure — a sign the "adjusted" story is not holding.
4. Guidance for FY2026 (next given at Q4 FY2026 earnings, ~Nov 2026) is cut from the current run-rate implied by consensus (FY1 EPS $7.26). **[Corrected 2026-10-08: $7.26 is NTM consensus; FY2026 adjusted EPS guide is ~$6.55 and was raised on Aug 4 2026]**
