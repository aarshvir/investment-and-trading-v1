# WST — West Pharmaceutical Services, Inc.

## 1. Verdict

**WATCH** — thesis horizon 12–36 months. A genuinely high-quality, fast-growing supplier to injectable drugs: sales grew 13.8% in Q2 2026 (12.7% organic), 2026 guidance ("$8.85 to $9.05" adjusted EPS) has been raised at each release, the company is net cash and buying back stock. But at $370.44 the market price demands about 17% a year of free-cash-flow growth for ten years, against my evidence-based base case of 11% and a bull of 14%. Even the bull case sits under the implied rate, and my base three-year return is about 3% a year. It becomes INCLUDE-SMALL at roughly $310 or lower (base return of 9% a year), or if FCF compounds faster than base. Triage (Q07) said not to advance on price; the primary-source work confirms that, and adds a CEO handover (effective 31 Aug 2026), a May 2026 cyberattack and a mislabelled cash-flow figure in the Q2 release.

## 2. Business in plain English

West makes the rubber stoppers, plungers, seals, syringe components and self-injection devices that sit inside vials and prefilled syringes for injectable medicines, and sells them to drug makers (biologics, GLP-1 and generics). It earns money per component or device, with premium "high-value" products (HVP) carrying better prices and margins; 49% of Q2 2026 sales were HVP Components and 15% HVP Delivery Devices. The moat is regulatory qualification: once a drug is approved with West components, switching is slow and costly. Concentration is the trade-off: the ten largest customers were 47.6% of 2025 sales and one was 15.8% ($485.9m) (10-K, accession 0000105770-26-000010).

## 8. Bull and bear

**Bull:** (1) Organic growth 15.3% in Q1 and 12.7% in Q2 2026 with HVP Delivery Devices +29%. (2) Four straight beat-and-raises ("$8.85 to $9.05"); FY26 EPS guide low end up 12.7% since February ($7.85 to $8.85). (3) Net cash (consolidated), 37.7% gross margin and $454m of H1 buybacks.
**Bear:** (1) Valuation: 41x earnings, 1.7% FCF yield; a slip to 30x on base earnings returns about 3% a year. (2) Concentration: top customer 15.8% of 2025 sales; GLP-1 demand, oral alternatives and dosing changes are named in the 10-K risk factors. (3) Operational risk: May 2026 cyberattack; CEO handover; Q3 organic guide ("7.0% to 8.9% organic") decelerates from 12.7%.

## 9. Key risks and kill criteria (measurable thesis-invalidation triggers)

1. Organic net sales growth below 6.0% in two consecutive quarters (Q2 2026: 12.7%; Q3 guide "7.0% to 8.9%").
2. HVP Components organic growth below 8% in two consecutive quarters (Q2 2026: +18.4%).
3. FY2026 adjusted-EPS guidance cut below the "$8.85" low end, or disclosure of loss or a cut above 25% in orders from the top customer (15.8% of 2025 sales).
4. TTM FCF (operating cash flow less capex) below 60% of TTM GAAP net income at the Q4 2026 release (77% today).
5. A new material cybersecurity incident, or litigation accrual above $50m.
