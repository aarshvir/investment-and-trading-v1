# Boston Scientific Corporation (NYSE: BSX) — Diligence Dossier

**Agent:** F24 | **As of:** 2026-09-25 close ($43.92) | **Depth:** Standard

## 1. Verdict

**WATCH** — 12-month horizon before re-underwriting. This overturns the triage's INCLUDE-leaning framing ("best-in-class... broad-based double-digit growth," 13x NTM P/E vs "~38% street upside"). The 13x multiple is real, but it reflects two 2026 guidance cuts driven by genuine competitive share loss (electrophysiology, Watchman), not merely sentiment, and the business is now mid-recovery from an August 2026 cyberattack whose Q3/FY financial impact the company itself says it cannot yet quantify. Valuation alone does not resolve an open operational question; the Oct 28, 2026 print (Q3 + reset guidance) is the next real evidence point.

## 2. Business

Boston Scientific makes medical devices across cardiology (drug-eluting stents, Watchman left-atrial-appendage closure, Farapulse pulsed-field ablation for AFib), urology, endoscopy, neuromodulation and peripheral interventions, sold to hospitals worldwide. It competes chiefly with Medtronic, Abbott and Johnson & Johnson. Historically a share-taking double-digit organic grower via Watchman and Farapulse.

## 3. Why the model liked it, and is it durable

b1 factors ranked BSX on quality (gross profit/assets, ROCE) and momentum-adjacent growth built when Watchman (+~30% in 2025) and Farapulse were compounding at 20%+. **Not durable as scored**: both drivers decelerated sharply starting Q1 2026 (source #6, #7 below) — this is the kind of quant/fundamental conflict the mandate asks to surface. The triage's "broad-based double-digit growth" line is stale by roughly two quarters.

## 8. Bull case
1. 13x forward earnings is a genuine historical low for a device franchise that still holds #1/#2 positions in Watchman and PFA.
2. FY2025 beat every raised guidance step (adj. EPS $3.06 vs initial $2.80–2.87 guide) — a real execution track record pre-dates 2026.
3. Silk Road Medical (TCAR, stroke prevention) adds a new growth vector at a reasonable ~$1.26bn price if it closes as planned. **[Corrected 2026-10-08: the deal closed on 17 Sep 2024, so this is an existing, not pending, growth vector and not an incremental 2026 catalyst.]**

## 9. Bear case
1. Two consecutive guidance cuts in 2026 were driven by **competitors taking share** in Farapulse/PFA and a Watchman volume collapse (30%→4.3% growth) — a moat question, not noise.
2. The August 2026 cyberattack disrupted global manufacturing/shipping and the company cannot yet size the Q3/FY hit; revised guidance isn't due until Oct 28.
3. An active securities-fraud class action (D. Mass., filed 2026-03-05) alleges management misrepresented EP competitive positioning ahead of the Feb 2026 correction — an unresolved legal overhang.

## 10. Kill criteria (measurable — the specific, observable conditions that would invalidate this thesis)
1. FY2026 adjusted EPS (reported ~Feb 2027) comes in **below $3.00** — a down year versus FY2025's $3.06.
2. Watchman US procedure volume growth is **negative** for two consecutive quarters.
3. Electrophysiology/Farapulse organic growth stays **below 5%** for two consecutive quarters.
4. Net debt/EBITDA rises **above 3.0x** (from ~2.4x today).
5. The BSX securities-fraud class action is certified and/or the company discloses a material litigation reserve or settlement.
