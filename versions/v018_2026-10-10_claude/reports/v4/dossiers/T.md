# AT&T Inc. (T) - Fundamental Diligence Dossier (F127, standard depth)

**Verdict: INCLUDE-SMALL** (half weight), 12-36 month horizon. Named reservation: consolidated leverage steps up to about 3.0x net debt/adjusted EBITDA when the pending $23B EchoStar spectrum deal closes, and 2026 free cash flow guidance needs a back-half-weighted year. The price implies shrinking cash flow; a flat-to-modestly-growing business with a 4.4% dividend and about $10B of 2026 buybacks clears that bar, but the balance sheet leaves no room for error.

## 1. Business in plain English
AT&T sells mobile service (one of the three national wireless carriers), fibre and fixed-wireless home internet, and business connectivity. Customers pay monthly subscriptions, so revenue is sticky; the model is to bundle fibre with wireless (42.5% of households with its advanced home internet also chose AT&T wireless, per the Q2 2026 release). Moat is scale, spectrum and owned fibre, but it is one of three national carriers in a market with price competition, and a large legacy copper business is shrinking by design (Legacy service revenue guided to fall 20%+ in 2026).

## 7. Bull and bear
Bull: (1) record fibre plus fixed-wireless net adds (367k fibre, 279k fixed wireless in Q2 2026) drive convergence; (2) FCF guide rises to 21B+ by 2028 against a 174B market cap; (3) share count falls about 4% a year.
Bear: (1) leverage at about 3.1x after EchoStar, with 9.3B of debt due within a year, limits flexibility if rates stay high; (2) H2 FCF requirement of 10.8B+ could be missed, and capex is 23-24B a year; (3) legacy copper decline and wireless price promotion compress service revenue growth to 1-2%.

## 8. Kill criteria
1. Net debt/adjusted EBITDA (consolidated, company definition) above 3.3x at any quarter-end after the EchoStar close, or no target-range path stated.
2. Full-year 2026 free cash flow guide cut below $18 billion, or reported FY2026 FCF below $17 billion.
3. Adjusted EPS 2026 guide cut below $2.25, or two consecutive quarters of adjusted EPS below $0.52.
4. Postpaid phone churn above 1.00% for two consecutive quarters (0.86% in Q2 2026).
5. EchoStar transaction not closed by 31 March 2027, or terminated.
