## 2. Business in plain English
CSX runs a ~20,000-employee Class I freight railroad covering the eastern US (merchandise ~62% of Q2 revenue, intermodal ~16%, coal ~13%, trucking and other the rest). It earns money by hauling carloads and containers at a price per unit plus fuel surcharges; moat is the fixed rail network and limited duplication east of the Mississippi. Competitor Norfolk Southern is, per the 10-K, party to a pending merger with Union Pacific needing Surface Transportation Board (STB) approval, which is the main strategic overhang.

## 3. Why the model likes it, and is it durable
b1/V1 shortlisted it on quality and momentum; triage called momentum "merger-speculation driven". Filings show part of the 2026 improvement is real (volume +6%, headcount -1,392 YoY, non-fuel expense -$39m) and part is optical: Q2 revenue +10% includes fuel surcharge revenue of $410m vs $222m, offset by fuel expense +$177m (locomotive fuel price +74%), so margin percentage is flattered while dollars are neutral. Cost savings and 2025 comps (Helene rebuild, network disruption) are partly one-time.

## 8. Bull / bear
Bull: (1) intermodal +9% volume and +26% revenue in Q2 on domestic wins and a tight truck market; (2) headcount -6% YoY with op margin 38.3% shows the cost reset is structural; (3) any STB-driven industry consolidation could reprice eastern rail scarcity.
Bear: (1) price already embeds ~9.5% growth vs 5.2% delivered; (2) Q2 fuel lag was -$54m and the surcharge windfall reverses if diesel falls; (3) merger path is unresolved: Q2 MD&A cites "advisory expenses related to potential industry consolidation" and the UNP/NS outcome could strand CSX's positioning.

## 9. Kill criteria
1. GAAP operating margin below 34.0% for two consecutive quarters (Q2'26 38.3%, H1'26 37.2%; FY25 32.1%).
2. Consolidated net debt (current maturities + long-term debt less cash and short-term investments) / TTM (operating income + D&A) above 3.2x (now ~2.6x).
3. Total carload volume down year over year for two consecutive quarters (Q2'26 +6%, H1'26 +4%).
4. TTM operating cash flow less property additions below $2.0bn (now ~$2.8bn) or 2026 capex guidance raised above $2.6bn.
5. CSX signs a definitive agreement to acquire or merge with another Class I railroad at consideration above $10bn, or an STB decision materially adverse to CSX's interchange position.
