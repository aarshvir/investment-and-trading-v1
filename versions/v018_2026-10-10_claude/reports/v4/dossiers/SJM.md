**2. Business.** Makes Folgers, Dunkin' and Cafe Bustelo coffee, Jif, Uncrustables, Smucker's jams, Milk-Bone and Meow Mix pet food, and Hostess snack cakes, sold mostly through US grocers and mass retailers. Profit is concentrated in coffee (Q1 FY27 U.S. Retail Coffee segment profit $300.0m on sales $807.8m); Uncrustables is the volume growth engine and Sweet Baked Snacks is the drag (Q1 FY27 sales -7%, segment margin 12.6%).

Durable: coffee pricing, Uncrustables. Not durable: refunds.

## Risks and bear case
**8. Bull / bear.** Bull: (1) Uncrustables and pet growth plus ~$0.4-0.5bn/yr deleveraging lift EPS mid-single digits; (2) coffee margin recovery and a second guidance raise; (3) rerating toward 13-14x. Bear: (1) FY27 net sales guided down 1-2% with "neutral volume/mix"; (2) ~$0.60 of FY27 EPS is non-recurring refunds, so FY28 faces a tough compare; (3) Sweet Baked Snacks goodwill is already fully written off, further write-downs are possible and 3.3x leverage limits flexibility; coffee costs are cyclical.
