## 2. Business in plain English
DexCom makes continuous glucose monitors (CGM) — the G7 sensor/transmitter/app system, plus the Stelo over-the-counter sensor for non-insulin users. It sells mainly to people with diabetes (Type 1 and insulin-using Type 2) through pharmacy and durable-medical-equipment channels, reimbursed by payers/Medicare, with a razor/razor-blade model (durable transmitter + recurring disposable sensors). Competitive position: #2 global CGM share behind Abbott's FreeStyle Libre franchise, ahead of Medtronic; Abbott's Lingo/Libre Rio and newer entrants are pushing into DexCom's OTC/non-insulin white space.

## 3. Why the model likes it / durability
b1 factor scores flag DexCom on quality (high ROE, high gross margin, low leverage) and growth (double-digit revenue growth, EPS momentum). This is durable operating performance, not an artefact: Q2 2026 (10-Q filed with SEC, accession 0001093557-26-000073 covers Q1; Q2 10-Q filed under CIK 0001093557, period 2026-06-30) revenue grew 13.1% YoY to $1.31bn (consolidated, GAAP), adjusted gross margin 64.1% (+400bps YoY), and the company raised full-year guidance and repurchased ~$600m of stock in the quarter — evidence of real cash generation, not a one-off (Source 1, Source 2).

## 8. Bull case / Bear case
**Bull:** (1) Stelo/OTC non-insulin expansion is a large new addressable market DexCom is capturing early; (2) international revenue growing faster than US (19% vs 11% YoY in Q2 2026, Source 1) with room to add reimbursed markets; (3) margin expansion (gross margin +400bps YoY) shows manufacturing scale is still building, supporting double-digit EPS growth even at slower revenue growth.
**Bear:** (1) Abbott's Lingo/Libre Rio and other OTC entrants directly target DexCom's newest growth leg with a lower-cost brand; (2) a securities class action (filed 2026, alleging undisclosed G6/G7 design changes affected reliability, covering the purchase period Jan 2024–Sep 2025) is an unresolved legal overhang (Source 4); (3) at 28.9x NTM P/E vs a 17.5x peer median, any deceleration toward the 10.9% consensus growth rate would compress the multiple materially.

## 9. Key risks & kill criteria (measurable)
1. Quarterly YoY revenue growth < 8% for two consecutive quarters (deceleration below guided range).
2. Net new US CGM patient adds or US revenue growth turns negative for a quarter (share loss to Abbott).
3. Gross margin falls >200bps YoY for two consecutive quarters (competitive pricing pressure).
4. Adverse ruling, certification, or material settlement in the pending securities class action exceeding $200m.
5. FDA safety communication or recall affecting G7/Stelo sensor accuracy.
