# COST - Costco Wholesale Corp (consolidated; FY ended 30 Aug 2026) - Dossier F154, wave 8

**1. Verdict: WATCH** (12-36 months). Best-in-class membership retailer with steady 6-7% underlying comps, but the 25 Sep 2026 close ($922.77, one day after the FY26 print; market cap about $409B) implies about 18% a year of free-cash-flow growth for five years at an 8% discount rate (14.0% at 7%) vs my base of 9%. Condition that would change it: price near $650-700, or growth that accelerates beyond high single digits. Triage (Q02, advance=false on price) is **confirmed**; V1 row: none (v1_verdict null).

**2. Business in plain English.** A members-only warehouse club: 939 warehouses (647 U.S./Puerto Rico), 84.1M paid memberships, 42.3M Executive members. It sells a narrow range of goods at very thin markups (gross margin about 11% of net sales) and earns most of its profit from annual fees (membership fees $5,907M in FY26 against operating income of $11,685M). Renewal rate is 89.8% worldwide and 92.3% in the U.S./Canada (Q4 supplemental). Source: 8-K EX-99.1/99.2 filed 2026-09-24, accession 0000909832-26-000084.

**9. Kill criteria (measurable; entity = consolidated Costco, EX-99.1/99.2 tables).**
1. Adjusted total-company comparable sales (ex gas and FX) below 4.0% for two consecutive quarters (Q4 FY26: 6.7%).
2. U.S./Canada membership renewal rate below 91.5% (Q4 FY26: 92.3%) or worldwide below 89.0% (Q4: 89.8%).
3. Paid-membership growth below 2.0% year over year (Q4 FY26: +3.8%).
4. Reported gross margin (supplemental) below 10.75% in any quarter (Q4 FY26: 11.02%).
5. Quarterly membership income growth below 4.0% (Q4 FY26: +7.3%).
