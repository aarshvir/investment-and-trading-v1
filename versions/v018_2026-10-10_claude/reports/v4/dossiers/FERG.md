**2. Business in plain English.** Ferguson distributes plumbing, HVAC, waterworks, fire/fab, industrial PVF and appliances through ~1,700 North American locations to contractors (roughly half residential, half non-residential revenue; US is 95% of sales). It earns a ~31% gross margin on product resale plus value-added services; scale in sourcing and branch density against fragmented local rivals is the moat. Fiscal year changed to calendar in 2025 (five-month transition period Aug–Dec 2025).

**3. Why the model likes it.** b1 rank 274; quality (GP/A, ROE 0.30) and low leverage score high; value and momentum mid (EP 0.043). Durable: quality is structural; the value component is thin.
