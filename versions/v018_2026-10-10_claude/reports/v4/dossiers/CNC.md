# CNC - Centene Corporation (NYSE: CNC) - Standard-depth diligence (agent F122)

**1. Verdict: INCLUDE-SMALL (half weight)** - A Medicaid/Marketplace/Medicare-PDP insurer that is visibly recovering (three guidance raises in 2026, Marketplace repriced, leverage falling) and is priced for little growth; the named reservation is earnings visibility: guidance is a "greater than" floor, 2025 guidance was withdrawn, Medicaid still runs a 93.9% benefit ratio, and 2027 policy changes (Medicaid work rules, California transition) are not yet in numbers. Horizon 24-36 months.

**Triage check (Q08: advance=false, "business, not just the stock, is impaired").** Partly overturned. The history is real (guidance withdrawn 1 Jul 2025, $6.7bn goodwill impairment Q3'25, 2025 adjusted EPS $2.08 vs $7.17 in 2024). But the filings show the recovery is in the reported numbers: Q1 and Q2 2026 adjusted EPS $3.37 and $2.51; Marketplace HBR fell to the high-70s; and the company has raised the 2026 floor from $3.00 to $3.40 to $4.80. We agree with the triage that Medicaid is not yet fixed.

**2. Business.** Centene is the largest US Medicaid managed-care insurer (12.1m Medicaid members), plus Marketplace/ACA plans (3.5m, down from 5.9m a year ago after repricing ~95% of membership), Medicare Advantage and the largest standalone Part D (PDP) book (8.8m). It is paid a premium per member by states and the federal government and keeps the difference after medical costs, so the health benefits ratio (HBR) is everything: one point of HBR on ~$175bn of premium is ~$1.7bn pre-tax. Competitive position is scale in government programmes with thin, regulated margins.

**3. Why the model likes it.** b1 composite 0.81 (decile 9, live rank 93 of 503): value family 0.74 (FCF-yield percentile 0.99), momentum 0.94; quality family only 0.22 (ROE percentile 0.05). The value score is largely an artefact: TTM free cash flow of ~$9bn (Yahoo/D3) on a ~$30bn market cap reflects float, risk-adjustment and pass-through payment timing, while TTM GAAP EPS is about -$10 and e/p is negative (-0.17). Company text on Q2 OCF: "primarily driven by net earnings and the timing of pass through, premium and other payments, partially offset by net improvement in 2025 risk adjustment transfer position." Adjusted earnings, not FCF, are the right yardstick.

**8. Bull / bear.** Bull: (1) Marketplace re-priced to a 79.2% Commercial HBR with smaller, healthier membership; (2) Medicaid HBR trend improving (94.9% to 93.9% YoY) with states matching rates to acuity; (3) PDP growth (8.8m) and a cheaper cost base (voluntary separation programme; adjusted SG&A ratio guide 6.9%-7.5%), leverage falling (debt/capital 46.5% to 41.6%).
