# FICO - Fair Isaac Corporation - Diligence Dossier (Agent F126, Wave-8 Standard)

## 1. Verdict
**WATCH** (12-36 month horizon). Wonderful economics, but at the 25 Sep 2026 close ($863.09) the price implies 5.7% 10-year free-cash-flow growth against my 3.8% base, the balance sheet is leveraged to buy back stock, and nearly all of 2026's growth came from one price increase in mortgage scores that regulators and competitors are now attacking. The triage called the moat "structurally impaired" on a Sep 4 FHFA headline; primary sources show the change is narrower (Section 11) but a larger FHFA step came after the cutoff (Sep 29). What would change the verdict: a price at or below about $750 (my base-case value per share) with the leverage covenant headroom intact, or disclosure that mortgage score volumes are holding under the direct-license model.

## 2. Business in plain English
FICO sells two things. Scores (68% of Q3 FY26 revenue): the FICO credit score, sold to lenders (B2B, mainly per-score fees on mortgage, auto and card applications via the credit bureaus) and to consumers (B2C). Software (32%): fraud, decisioning and analytics software, with a platform push (platform ARR +62%, non-platform ARR -17%). It earns a royalty-like fee each time a lender pulls a score, with a 53% operating margin on almost no capital. Competitive position: near-monopoly in U.S. mortgage scoring, challenged by VantageScore (owned by the three credit bureaus), which is cheaper and now accepted by Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac.

## 3. Why the model likes it - durable or artefact?
b1 composite 0.731 (rank 134): profitability (pct_gp_a 0.99, pct_ocf_a 0.99), buybacks (pct_net_issuance 0.98) and earnings surprise (SUE 0.87); penalised on leverage (0.01) and book-to-price (0.01, negative book equity of -$4.1bn). Durable part: extreme margins and cash conversion. Artefacts: (a) the profit surge is a price step (mortgage origination score unit price), not volume; (b) net issuance score reflects a debt-funded ASR; (c) negative equity makes ROE and book metrics meaningless (ROE NaN).

## 8. Bull and bear
Bull: (1) per-score pricing power proved: Scores revenue +41% in Q3 with a 54% operating margin; (2) software platform ARR +62%, net retention 148% for platform; (3) shrinking share count (21.6m) means each percent of FCF growth is amplified per share.
Bear: (1) 98% of growth is price; VantageScore 4.0 is priced at $4 or below for mortgage (secondary sources) and all approved lenders may use it since Sep 9 2026; (2) debt/EBITDA about 4.4x (GAAP) against a covenant stepping down to 4.0x on Dec 31 2026 while the term loan starts amortising; (3) a live Sherman Act Section 2 antitrust claim against FICO plus regulatory pressure on scoring fees.

## 9. Key risks and kill criteria
1. Scores segment revenue below $400m in any fiscal quarter (Q3 FY26: $458.9m).
2. Consolidated total debt (10-Q balance sheet, current maturities plus long-term debt) divided by trailing-twelve-month GAAP EBITDA above 4.5x, or any covenant amendment or waiver disclosure.
3. FY27 initial non-GAAP EPS guidance below the FY26 guide of $42.43, or FY27 guidance midpoint implying revenue growth under 0%.
4. Software ARR growth below 5% year over year (June 30 2026: +10%) or platform net retention below 120% (now 148%).
5. A regulator or the GSEs setting a mortgage score price, a cap or an effective default to VantageScore that cuts FICO mortgage origination royalty revenue by more than 25% on a run-rate basis.
