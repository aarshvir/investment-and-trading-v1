1. Legacy MS revenue (Tecfidera, Tysabri, Spinraza) **[Corrected 2026-10-06: Spinraza is not MS; Q2 2026 Spinraza was $401.9M, +2% YoY]** continues its structural decline and could outrun the pace of new

   bites earlier than modeled.
2. The Apellis deal added ~$1.8bn of new term-loan debt on top of existing notes; if integration costs run longer than
