# RVTY - Revvity, Inc. - Diligence Dossier (F148, Wave 8)

## 1. Verdict
**WATCH** (12-36 month horizon). A solid cash generator with improving Diagnostics, but the stock ($151.11) trades at 28x guided adjusted EPS with 4-5% organic growth, and the latest guidance raise is almost entirely a tariff refund. Would change on: price below about $125, or organic growth sustained at 5%+ with Life Sciences turning positive. Triage (`Q07_triage.json`, advance=false) is **confirmed**, though its 72.6x P/E flag is a GAAP and amortization artefact. No V1 row (`v1_verdict` = null).

## 2. Business in plain English
Revvity sells instruments, reagents, software and services to drug discoverers (Life Sciences) and to diagnostic labs and newborn-screening programmes (Diagnostics). Most revenue is recurring consumables and services. It is exiting its China Immunodiagnostics unit (about 6% of 2025 revenue). Moat: installed base, regulated diagnostic assays, scale in newborn screening.
