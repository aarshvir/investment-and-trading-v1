# DGX - Quest Diagnostics Incorporated (clinical laboratory testing)

**1. Verdict: INCLUDE-SMALL (half weight)** - a high-quality, share-gaining lab franchise growing revenue ~10% with guidance raised three times in a row, but priced at ~19.6x forward earnings for growth the price roughly already assumes; named reservation: Medicare lab-fee (PAMA) reform risk and dilutive low-margin volume from new hospital/dialysis contracts. Horizon 24-36 months. V1 row absent (v1_verdict = null). Triage said "advance: false, price already captures growth" - **partly adopted**: the valuation read is right (implied growth in line with base, not below), but triage missed the strength of execution, so it is investable at half weight rather than rejected.

**2. Business.** America's largest independent clinical lab: doctors, hospitals, health plans and consumers send blood, urine and pathology samples (about 90% of insured lives are accessible per the 10-Q, acc. 0001022079-26-000070) and Quest charges per test. Scale in routine testing, courier/patient-service-center density and a growing advanced-diagnostics menu (Alzheimer's blood tests, Haystack MRD cancer test) are the moat; main rival Labcorp.

**3. Why the model likes it.** b1 quality/momentum families; Yahoo revenue growth +10.2%. Durable: volume growth from Corewell Health (Michigan lab joint venture/Co-Lab Solutions) and Fresenius dialysis-centre testing - the 10-Q says about 8 points of H1 organic volume growth came from these two, with the rest of the book growing ~4%. Partly cyclical/one-time: ramp effects lap in 2027.

## Risks and bear case
**8. Bull / bear.** Bull: (1) Corewell/Fresenius ramps plus Advanced Diagnostics (AD-Detect, Haystack MRD) sustain 6%+ organic growth; (2) automation ("Invigorate") offsets wage inflation and margin recovers; (3) PAMA reform (the RESULTS Act cited in the 10-K acc. 0001022079-26-000015 and the 10-Q) stabilises Medicare rates. Bear: (1) revenue per requisition is falling as mix shifts to routine tests, compressing margin (adjusted -40 bp in Q2); (2) 2027 laps the ramp and the 10-Q (acc. 0001022079-26-000070) itself notes Congress "has repeatedly acted to delay" the Medicare cuts (through 2026) without fixing the structure; I did not verify 2027 status; (3) 19.6x leaves no room for a growth miss and several government inquiries are open.


## Thesis invalidation triggers
**9. Kill criteria.**
1. Organic requisition volume excluding Corewell and Fresenius below +2% for two consecutive quarters (currently ~+4%).
2. Adjusted operating margin below 15.0% in any quarter of a full year where revenue is guided up (H1 2026: 16.0%).
3. Full-year adjusted EPS guidance midpoint cut below $10.83 (the prior range top), i.e. erasing the last raise.
4. Consolidated net debt/EBITDA above 3.0x.
5. An enacted Medicare CLFS cut effective 2027 that the company estimates at more than $150m annual revenue.

**10. Catalysts.** Q3 2026 results ~22 Oct 2026 (Yahoo estimate, unconfirmed); ex-dividend ~6 Oct 2026 (Yahoo); PAMA/appropriations decisions around year-end; Haystack MRD and AD-Detect launches.

**11. Red-flag scan.** From the 10-Q legal note: AMCA data-security incident reported 3 Jun 2019, with a group of state attorney-general offices still investigating; subpoenas from the U.S. Attorney for the District of New Jersey about the Company's New York Giants relationship; New York AG inquiry on ordering/billing of test panels to Medicaid; Texas AG Civil Investigative Demand on Medicaid billing; qui tam/False Claims Act suits; 401(k) fiduciary class actions. The 10-Q states the Company "does not believe that material losses" are probable for these as of 30 Jun 2026. No restatement, going-concern or auditor change seen. Form 4 pattern and short-interest NOT examined.
