# IR - Ingersoll Rand (Industrials / Industrial Machinery)
**Verdict: WATCH** - a high-quality compounder with excellent cash conversion, but about 22x adjusted earnings for low organic growth. Horizon 12-36 months. Revisit near $62 (about 17x FY2026E) or on organic growth above 4% for two quarters.

## 1. Business in plain English
Ingersoll Rand makes compressors, vacuum pumps, blowers and power tools (Industrial Technologies & Services, about 79% of Q2'26 revenue) and precision pumps and fluid handling for life sciences and aerospace (Precision & Science Technologies). It sells equipment plus recurring parts and service, and buys niche businesses, run under its "IRX" operating system.
