# META — Meta Platforms, Inc.

## 1. Verdict
**WATCH.** Horizon 12–36 months. Meta's ad engine is genuinely benefiting from AI (Advantage+ at a $20bn+ annual run-rate) and the FTC's monopoly case ended in Meta's favor at trial, but Q2 2026 delivered the company's **first year-over-year profit decline of the AI era**, operating margin compressed from 43% to 31%, free cash flow fell to $784m for the quarter, and the model's own factor scores are the weakest of the four names (bottom quintile). At the 25-Sep-2026 close ($751.66), a 10-year reverse DCF implies ~17.5% constant revenue growth versus an evidence-based base case of ~11.2% — the largest gap of the four mega-caps reviewed.

## 2. Business in plain English
Meta sells advertising across Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp and Messenger, using AI to target ads and (increasingly) to automate ad creation and campaign optimization for advertisers (the "Advantage+" suite). It also runs Reality Labs, a large, persistently loss-making bet on VR/AR hardware and the metaverse, and develops the open-weight Llama model family, which it does not directly charge for but uses to build the same ad-serving AI.

## 8. Bull case
1. Meta **won the FTC's monopoly trial outright** in November 2025 (Judge Boasberg found no monopoly in social networking given TikTok competition) — the single largest structural risk (forced divestiture of Instagram/WhatsApp) is resolved in Meta's favor at the trial-court level, even though the FTC has now appealed (Jan-2026).
2. Advantage+ (AI-automated ad buying/creative) is at a **$20bn+ annualized run-rate** — the clearest, largest, currently-monetizing AI product disclosed by any of the four companies in this program, not a future promise.
3. Core ad metrics (impressions +14%, price/ad +12%, both YoY) show the auction is still healthy and growing on both volume and price — a two-lever growth engine, not reliant on one variable.

## Bear case
1. Q2 2026 was the **first YoY profit decline of the AI era** for Meta — operating margin fell 12 points (43%→31%) in one year, and the model's own factor scores (bottom quintile) are flagging this in real time, not lagging it.
2. Reality Labs remains a **~$18bn/year structural loss** with no disclosed path to profitability, and this capital is not going toward the core ad business's own AI infrastructure.
3. Despite winning the FTC trial, Meta was **fined €200m (April 2025) for its "pay or consent" advertising model under the EU DMA**, and the European Commission has **opened a further non-compliance investigation into that same pay-or-consent model** — an unresolved, recurring regulatory cost center specific to Meta's ad-targeting business model, distinct from and in addition to the FTC appeal.

## 9. Key risks & kill criteria (thesis-invalidation triggers — signposts to watch)
1. Operating margin remains below **35% for two consecutive quarters** (confirms the Q2 2026 compression is structural, not one-off legal/severance noise).
2. Quarterly free cash flow remains below **$5bn for two consecutive quarters** (confirms the capex-to-cash-flow ratio is not easing as capacity comes online).
3. 2026 AI capex guidance is raised a **third time** beyond $145bn.
4. The EU Commission issues a further fine or mandated product change on the "pay or consent" model (beyond the existing €200m April-2025 fine).
5. Reality Labs operating loss exceeds **$20bn on a trailing-12-month basis** without a disclosed monetization roadmap.
