# SMCI — Super Micro Computer, Inc. (agent F152, wave 8, STANDARD depth)

Information cutoff: 2026-09-25 close ($43.26). Research only; not personal investment advice.

## 1. Verdict
**WATCH** (thesis horizon 12-36 months). Triage said "no"; I confirm it, for new reasons. The AI-server demand is real (FY26 sales $39.1B, FY27 guide $65-72B) and Q4 margins jumped, but the stock is priced for more than my base case delivers (implied 10-yr earnings growth 13.2% vs my base 9.2%), internal control is still ineffective, the company is under SDNY/SEC/BIS investigation after a co-founder's indictment, and FY26 free cash flow was about -$7.0B. Condition that would change the view: material weakness remediated, investigations closed without a company charge, two straight quarters of positive free cash flow, and a price at or below about $38 (my base-case value per share).

## 2. Business in plain English
Supermicro designs and assembles servers, storage and, increasingly, whole AI data-centre racks (its "DCBBS" building blocks), selling mainly to cloud/AI infrastructure customers and enterprises. It earns a thin hardware margin (FY26 gross margin 10.8%) on very large, lumpy orders that are mostly GPUs bought from suppliers. One customer was 28.1% of FY26 net sales (10-K, "Concentration of Customer Risk"). Position: fast-follower to Nvidia platforms, competing with Dell and HPE on price and speed; little switching cost.

## 3. Why the model likes / dislikes it (b1, d4)
b1 composite rank 368 (decile 3, not top-30). Value family is high (NTM P/E 9.5, earnings-yield percentile 0.91) and surprise family is high (beat 5 of last 8), but quality and momentum are weak: FCF-yield percentile 0.006, gross-profit/assets percentile 0.11, 12-1 momentum percentile 0.12. The low P/E is partly an artefact: Q4 FY26 margin (17.5%) is 2x the FY26 average, and FCF is deeply negative, so "cheap on earnings" does not hold on cash.

## 8. Bull and bear
Bull: (1) record backlog, "generated more than $60 billion in new orders" in FY26 and FY27 guide of $65-72B (+66% to +84%); (2) Q4 margins of 17.5% if mix (enterprise, DCBBS) persists; (3) $7.5B cash after the equity raise removes near-term funding risk.
Bear: (1) investigation tail: SDNY indictment, SEC, BIS subpoenas could end in penalties or an export-privilege denial; (2) cash burn: -$7.0B FCF, $34.2B purchase commitments, 28.1% customer concentration; gross margin was between 6.3% and 11.8% in each of the six quarters before Q4 FY26; (3) dilution and a 7% preferred coupon ahead of common holders; ICFR still ineffective.
