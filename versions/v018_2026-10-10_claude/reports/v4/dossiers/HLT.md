# HLT - Hilton Worldwide Holdings (NYSE: HLT) - wave 8 / F158

**Verdict: WATCH** - a best-in-class asset-light hotel franchisor that is executing and raising guidance, but the 25 Sep 2026 close ($313.96, market cap about $70.7B, 35x FY26 adjusted EPS guidance, 3% FCF yield) implies about 10% a year FCF growth for 10 years against my 8% base, while buybacks are funded partly with new debt. Would become INCLUDE-SMALL at roughly $250 (about 28x FY26 adjusted EPS) or on evidence that fee growth can sustain above 8%. Horizon 12-36 months.

**Thesis in plain English:** Hilton collects fees from the owners of the hotels it brands and manages and adds about 6% more rooms each year; it is a very good toll-collector that the market already prices as one.

## 1. Business
Franchise and management fees on hotels owned by others (Q2'26 release, 8-K acc. 0001585689-26-000042): pipeline 541,300 rooms in 3,853 hotels (up 6% y/y), 24,100 rooms opened in Q2 and net unit growth 6.1%; comparable system RevPAR +3.9% (currency neutral). Management and franchise fee revenue +6.4% in Q2 and +8.3% in H1.

## 2. Why the model likes it / durability
b1: asset-light (ebit_ev 3.5%, fcfp 2.9%); high leverage score (0.79) and negative equity. The strength is structural (fees, unit growth), but a stockholders' deficit of $6.3B (consolidated, 30 Jun 2026, XBRL StockholdersEquity) means the equity base is the product of buybacks, not losses. Triage (Q02_triage.json) quoted 31.1x NTM P/E and 4.2x leverage and rejected it on price: on the filings, price is 35.1x FY26 adjusted EPS guidance midpoint ($8.95) and leverage 3.2x (see 5); conclusion unchanged.

## 7. Bull / Bear
Bull: (1) 6.1% net unit growth and a record 541,300-room pipeline, almost half under construction; (2) EBITDA guidance raised twice, adjusted EPS guide $8.89-$9.01, 4% fewer shares y/y; (3) fee-based and capital-light, FCF $2.0B on $80M capex.
Bear: (1) 35x earnings for an 8% grower leaves no margin for a RevPAR downturn; (2) $3.5B of capital return versus about $2.0B of FCF is debt-funded and the equity is already negative $6.3B; (3) GAAP net income guidance has been cut twice while EBITDA rose.

## 8. Kill criteria / what changes the verdict
1. Move to INCLUDE-SMALL only at 28x FY26 adjusted EPS or below (about $250), or if FY27 consensus adjusted EPS rises above $10.50.
2. Exit/avoid if net unit growth falls below 4.5% for two consecutive quarters (Q2'26: 6.1%).
3. Exit if system-wide comparable RevPAR is negative for two consecutive quarters.
4. Exit if net debt/TTM Adjusted EBITDA exceeds 3.8x (now 3.2x, consolidated).
5. Exit if FY adjusted EBITDA guidance is cut below $4,040M.
