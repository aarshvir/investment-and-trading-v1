**2. Business.** Rockwell sells industrial automation and digitalization hardware/software (PLCs, drives, software like FactoryTalk/Plex, sensing) to discrete and process manufacturers globally, monetized via product sales plus a growing recurring-software/services mix. Moat: large installed base, high switching costs in control systems, #1/#2 share in NA industrial automation.

**3. Why the model likes it / durability.** Triage (Q09) flagged quality 4, growth 4, on the back of orders recovering across e-commerce, data-center, semis and energy end markets. Some of it is cyclical normalization off a 2023–24 destocking trough, which is a real but bounded tailwind, not a permanent step-change.
