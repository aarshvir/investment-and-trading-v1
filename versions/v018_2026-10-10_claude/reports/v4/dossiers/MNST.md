# MNST — Monster Beverage Corporation — Diligence Dossier (Agent F88, wave 5)

**Verdict: WATCH** (12–36 month horizon). Genuinely excellent business (net cash, ~20% revenue growth, global share
gains) but priced for continued mid-teens-plus growth for a decade; that is more than category-maturity and the
loss-making alcohol segment support as a base case. A great business priced for more than it can deliver is a WATCH.

## Business in plain English
Monster makes and markets energy drinks (Monster Energy, Reign) sold through bottler/distributor networks worldwide,
and has expanded into flavored malt beverages/hard seltzers ("Alcohol Brands"). It earns high gross margins on brand
strength and category leadership (global #2 behind/with Red Bull) but does not own manufacturing/distribution —
capital-light model, no long-term debt.

## Why the model likes it / durability
b1 snapshot (25 Sep 2026): quality decile 1, but momentum family score is negative and mid-cap staples exposure —
the growth signal here is real revenue/EPS acceleration, not an accounting artefact. Durability risk: energy-drink
category growth normalizing globally and the alcohol segment remaining a drag (see below).

## Bull case
1. International sales +34.6% YoY and now ~46% of revenue — a long runway still under-penetrated vs. the US.
2. Net-cash balance sheet with no debt gives full flexibility for buybacks, bolt-on M&A or weathering a downturn.
3. Q4 2026 pricing actions plus a Dec 2026 investor day could re-set the growth/margin narrative positively.

## Bear case
1. 34–41x earnings already prices in growth well above what a maturing, more-penetrated US energy category can
   deliver long-run; any deceleration below high-teens is a multiple-compression risk.
2. FCF conversion falling YoY (0.75x→0.65x) despite revenue acceleration is a genuine earnings-quality flag, not
   yet explained by a one-off.
3. Alcohol Brands remain loss-making with company-acknowledged marketing/advertising litigation exposure
   (class-action risk alleging improper targeting of underage consumers in some states) — a real, if contained,
   legal tail risk on top of a strategically unproven segment.

## Key risks & kill criteria (measurable)
1. Consolidated net sales growth decelerates below 12% YoY for two consecutive quarters.
2. FCF conversion (FCF/NI) stays below 0.70x for two more consecutive quarters without a stated one-off explanation.
3. Alcohol Brands segment operating losses widen YoY for two consecutive quarters.
4. Gross margin compresses more than 150bps YoY in any quarter on aluminum/tariff cost pass-through failure.
5. Any adverse ruling or settlement in the alcohol-marketing class actions exceeding $50M.

## Correction (verification DV28, 2026-10-09)

1. **Stock split and EPS basis (Last-quarters table, Valuation).** Wrong/missing: the table shows diluted EPS of $0.59 (Q2 2026) and $0.58 (Q1 2026) beside a $43.11 price with no mention of the stock split. The 10-Q and release (filed before the split) report pre-split EPS of $0.59 (Q2 2025: $0.50; adjusted $0.60 vs $0.52) on ~988M diluted shares. On the post-split basis of the $43.11 price, Q2 2026 EPS is ~$0.30 and shares outstanding are ~1.96 billion. The quoted 34x NTM and 41x TTM multiples are consistent with the post-split price. Q1 2025 EPS is $0.45 (H1 $0.95 less Q2 $0.50), not $0.46. Verdict unchanged.
2. **FCF conversion (Earnings quality, Bear case 2, kill criterion 2).** Wrong text: "fell to ~0.65x (from ~0.75x in Q2 2025)". Correct, from the 10-Q cash flow statements (Q2 = six months less Q1): Q2 2026 free cash flow $460.8M (operating cash flow $509.2M less capex $48.4M) = 0.79x net income of $584.5M; Q2 2025 $434.6M = 0.89x net income of $488.8M. H1 2026 is 0.90x and TTM is ~1.0x (FY2025 free cash flow $1,966M). The direction (down) is right but the level is ~0.14x higher than stated, so kill criterion 2 (below 0.70x) is further from triggering than the dossier implies. Verdict unchanged.

This confirms "mid-teens-or-higher" and implied_vs_base = above; verdict WATCH unchanged.
