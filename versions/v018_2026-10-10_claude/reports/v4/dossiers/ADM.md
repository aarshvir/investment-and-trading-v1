**2. Business in plain English.** ADM buys crops from farmers (soybeans, corn, wheat), crushes, mills and refines them into meal, oil, ethanol, sweeteners and flavours, and ships grain globally. It has three segments: Ag Services and Oilseeds (AS&O), Carbohydrate Solutions and Nutrition. Earnings are thin processing margins (Q2 2026 gross profit $1,935m on revenue $22,681m, 8.5%, before $1,026m of SG&A), not pricing power, and the 22.5% Wilmar stake adds equity income.

**3. Durable part: Nutrition (Flavors) growth and cost actions. Cyclical/policy part: crush margins tied to the finalised 2026-27 renewable volume obligations (RVO) and "elevated global energy prices" (Q2 release).
