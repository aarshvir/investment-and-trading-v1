## 1. Verdict: INCLUDE-SMALL — thesis horizon: 12–24 months
Henry Schein is the larger half of the US dental-distribution duopoly (with Patterson) plus a sizeable medical-distribution arm, generating stable ~31% gross margins on a low-single-digit-margin, high-recurring-revenue distribution model. This diligence pass **confirms** the triage's read: FY2026 guidance has been **raised twice** (Feb → reaffirmed May → raised Aug 2026, all primary-source-verified below) **[Corrected 2026-10-08: guidance was raised once, in Aug 2026; May reaffirmed]** on real internal sales-growth acceleration and margin execution from a new "value-creation" cost program (targeting >$200M of annual operating-income improvement, $125M run-rate by year-end 2026). It also **confirms the triage's two named red flags are effectively closed**: the FY2025 10-K's Note 17 (Legal Proceedings/Commitments and Contingencies) discloses only an immaterial opioid-supply-chain MDL (<0.4% of 2025 net sales are opioids) — **no dental-antitrust matter is disclosed at all** in the current 10-K — and the Q4 2025 earnings release shows the **2023 cyber incident's insurance recovery process concluded in Q1 2025** ($20M received, "the remaining insurance recovery"). What this pass adds beyond the triage: **consolidated net debt/EBITDA is materially higher than a first glance at long-term debt suggests once short-term borrowings ($1,024M at Q2 2026) are added** — total debt $3,462M against TTM EBITDA ~$1,007M is **~3.3x**, matching the triage's own 3.4x figure and confirming it as the single most important balance-sheet fact, not a data conflict. A reverse DCF (below) implies the market is pricing in only ~3.8% ten-year FCF growth, below what a mid-to-high-single-digit base case (guided growth + the value-creation margin program) would support — i.e., statistically cheap. INCLUDE-SMALL, not INCLUDE: the leverage is real (above where this dossier would want to hold at full weight) and the new CEO (in the role since 2026-03-02) has only delivered two quarters.

## 2. Business in plain English
Henry Schein distributes consumables, equipment, technology and value-added services to independent and group dental and medical practices worldwide. It buys from manufacturers and resells through its own sales/logistics network, earning a distribution margin plus higher-margin technology/software and specialty products revenue. It competes on breadth of catalog, next-day delivery, practice-management software, and financing/services bundled around the core distribution relationship; in US dental distribution it is one of two dominant national players (with Patterson Companies).

## 8. Bull case and bear case
**Bull:** (1) FY2026 guidance raised twice running on real internal-growth acceleration, not just easy comps; **[Corrected 2026-10-08: one raise (Aug 2026), one reaffirmation (May)]** (2) the value-creation program ($200M+ targeted operating-income improvement, $125M run-rate by YE2026) is a self-funded margin lever largely independent of end-market growth; (3) reverse DCF implies only ~3.8%/year FCF growth versus a base case of mid-to-high-single-digit growth once the program matures.
**Bear:** (1) consolidated net debt/EBITDA ≈ 3.3x is elevated for a low-margin distributor and has been rising via short-term borrowings, not just term debt, while the company keeps buying back stock; (2) the new CEO (7 months in seat) inherits a promised $200M+ savings program that is not yet fully proven out; (3) distribution is a scale/GPO-pricing-pressure business — the ~31% gross margin has been essentially flat for two years, so the growth thesis rests entirely on execution of the new cost program plus mid-single-digit volume growth, both unproven beyond two quarters.

## 9. Key risks & kill criteria
1. Consolidated net debt/EBITDA (total debt incl. short-term borrowings ÷ TTM EBITDA) rises above **4.0x** without a specific, disclosed deleveraging plan.
2. FY2026 guidance (non-GAAP EPS $5.29–$5.39, sales growth 4.5–5.5%, given 2026-08-04) is **cut** at the Q3 2026 or Q4 2026 release.
3. Consolidated gross margin falls below **30.5%** for two consecutive quarters (versus the ~31–32% range held since 2024).
4. The value-creation program's disclosed run-rate savings fall short of the **$125M by year-end 2026** target by more than 20% when next reported.
5. A new, material legal proceeding (antitrust, regulatory, or a fresh cyber incident) is disclosed in a subsequent 10-Q/10-K Legal Proceedings note.
