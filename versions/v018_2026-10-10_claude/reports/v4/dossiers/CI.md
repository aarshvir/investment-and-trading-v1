# CI - The Cigna Group (NYSE: CI) - standard-depth diligence (agent F145)

**1. Verdict: INCLUDE-SMALL** (half weight; thesis horizon 12-36 months). The market price of $272.01 discounts a shrinking cash-flow stream (about -2% a year for ten years) for a company that has raised its 2026 adjusted EPS floor twice this year and whose medical-cost ratio is inside its guided range. Named reservation: Evernorth's pharmacy benefit (PBM) profit is falling 27-28% a year under contract renewals and the move to a "rebate-free" model, and federal PBM rules take effect in 2028; if that compression spreads into Specialty and Care Services the cheap multiple is deserved. Research only; not personal advice.

**Triage reconciliation.** Quick triage (CI row: "Insurer/PBM (Evernorth), cheap on trailing multiples"; "Sector MLR pressure; PBM regulatory scrutiny"; model-gated not advanced). Verified: cheapness is real (8.9x FY2026 guide, 8.1x NTM per d4), MLR pressure did not show up at Cigna Healthcare (Q2-26 medical care ratio 84.5% vs 2026 range 83.7-84.7%), PBM pressure did (PBS pre-tax -27%). Triage partly overturned: sector MLR fear is not visible in Cigna's numbers; PBM regulation is the genuine risk.

**2. Business in plain English.** Cigna earns money two ways. Evernorth (about 85% of adjusted revenue) runs Express Scripts (pharmacy benefit management: negotiating drug prices and processing claims for employers and health plans), a home-delivery and specialty pharmacy, and care services; it makes thin margins (2.6-3.1% pre-tax) on very large revenue. Cigna Healthcare sells employer-funded medical plans, stop-loss, and international health cover at 10-13% pre-tax margins. Scale in PBM and specialty drugs is the moat; client concentration and politics are the weakness.

The value signal is partly durable (cash generation is real: OCF $9.6bn in 2025) but the earnings yield is cheap because the market expects PBM earnings to fall; it is a priced-in risk, not an accounting artefact. Note b1 has no growth data for CI (NaN for asset growth and accruals, as a financial).
