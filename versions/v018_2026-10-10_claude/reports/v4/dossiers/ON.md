# ON — ON Semiconductor Corporation (onsemi)

## 1. Verdict
**WATCH** — a real cyclical recovery is underway (three straight quarters of sequential beat-and-raise, AI data-center revenue guided to more than double in 2026), and valuation is not obviously stretched, but a large dilutive all-stock acquisition, a fresh (Jul 2026) securities-fraud investigation, and a pattern of large annual restructuring charges make this too uncertain to size as a full position today. Horizon: 12–36 months (spans the Synaptics deal's expected mid-2027 close).

## 2. Business in plain English
onsemi designs and manufactures power semiconductors and image sensors — mainly silicon-carbide (SiC) power devices for electric vehicles, plus analog/power chips for industrial, and a growing AI-data-center power business (competing to power Nvidia/AWS-class server racks). It sells directly and through distribution to automotive OEMs/Tier-1s, industrial customers and hyperscalers, and owns its own fabs (an unusually vertically integrated model for its size).

## 3. Why the model likes it / is it durable?
Earnings-momentum and valuation factors likely favor it because EPS is recovering sharply off a 2025 trough (GAAP EPS troughed at −$1.15 in Q1 2025). This looks like a genuine cyclical inflection reinforced by a structural AI tailwind: AI data-center revenue is guided to "more than double" in 2026, and management has beaten and raised revenue guidance for three consecutive quarters. The risk to durability: (a) automotive/industrial — still the bulk of revenue — remains soft, and (b) Chinese SiC entrants (Geely/Zeekr/Xiaomi-linked suppliers) are taking share in China even as onsemi grows there, which could compress long-run SiC pricing/margins company-wide.

## 8. Bull case / Bear case
**Bull:** (1) AI data-center power revenue, guided to more than double in 2026 off Nvidia MGX/AWS design wins, becomes a durable new growth leg; (2) China SiC revenue +60–70% YoY in 2026 shows share gains even amid a soft China auto market; (3) Synaptics adds a genuine Edge-AI/HMI franchise and enlarges TAM to a stated $243B by 2030.
**Bear:** (1) automotive/industrial — still the majority of revenue — has not clearly troughed; (2) Chinese SiC entrants could compress pricing company-wide over time; (3) the Synaptics deal is all-stock, dilutive, and unrelated to the core power/SiC business — integration risk with a ~9–12 month closing runway; (4) a fresh (opened Jul 2026) securities-fraud investigation alleges onsemi misrepresented long-term service agreements as "committed/locked in" and overstated demand visibility — unresolved.

## 9. Key risks & kill criteria (measurable)
1. Non-GAAP gross margin falls below 36% for two consecutive quarters.
2. AI data-center revenue fails to at least double YoY for full-year 2026 as guided.
3. Sequential revenue guidance is cut (breaking the 3-quarter beat-and-raise streak).
4. Synaptics deal is terminated, re-priced materially, or an SEC enforcement action/restatement/settlement >$100M is disclosed tied to the fraud investigation.
5. Net debt/EBITDA rises above 2.0x post-Synaptics financing.
