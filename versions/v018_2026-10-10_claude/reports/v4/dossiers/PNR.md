## 1. Verdict: WATCH — thesis horizon: 12–24 months (re-review at Q4 2026 earnings, when Taco closes and the securities litigation's next procedural step is due)
Pentair is a genuinely well-run, high-margin water-treatment/pool-equipment/flow-control business (50 consecutive years of dividend increases) trading at a statistically cheap ~10.4x NTM P/E after a severe, company-confirmed Q2 2026 miss (Pool-segment channel destock). The triage's core question — "cyclical, not structural" — is **largely confirmed by primary sources**: Flow (+5% reported, core −1%) and Water Solutions (core −3%, but segment income +17%) held up fine; only Pool (sales −42%) collapsed, and management attributes 100% of it to channel-inventory correction, not share loss. However, this diligence pass surfaced two material items the triage did not flag: (1) a **securities-fraud class action** (filed against Pentair and named executives, alleging the Pool channel problem and related disclosures go back to March 2025, i.e., that the "sudden Q2 2026 surprise" framing may understate what management knew and when) that is unresolved and squarely challenges the "purely cyclical, no disclosure issue" narrative management is using; and (2) a **$1.425bn, largely debt-financed acquisition (Taco Group Holdings)** signed the same day as the Q2 miss, which will materially raise leverage into Q4 2026 close, on top of a CFO who resigned after ~4 months (same week as the pre-announcement) and was only replaced with a permanent hire (effective Nov 1, 2026) in September. None of this makes the valuation wrong — the reverse DCF below shows the market is pricing in essentially zero real growth, which looks too pessimistic if the Pool thesis is right — but the combination of unresolved litigation about disclosure integrity and a leverage-raising acquisition closing imminently means this is **not a name to underwrite at full conviction today**. WATCH, not INCLUDE: revisit once the Taco pro forma balance sheet is filed and the securities case reaches a motion-to-dismiss ruling or is otherwise clarified.

## 2. Business in plain English
Pentair makes and sells water-related equipment across three segments: **Flow** (pumps and water-treatment systems for residential, commercial, agricultural and industrial customers), **Water Solutions** (filtration, water-quality and, since a January 2026 segment reorganization, legacy residential/irrigation flow products), and **Pool** (pumps, filters, heaters, automation and cleaning equipment for pool builders, dealers and consumers). It earns high operating margins (consolidated ROS ~18–20% GAAP, ~25% adjusted) by selling through distributors, pool builders and OEM/retail channels, competing on brand, energy-efficiency/regulatory compliance (e.g., variable-speed pump mandates) and channel relationships built over decades.

**Reverse DCF (this dossier's own two-stage model, 10-year explicit FCF growth at rate g + Gordon-growth terminal value at 2.5%, WACC 8.5%):** solving for the g that reconciles today's EV to this FCF base gives **g ≈ +0.9%/year** — the market is pricing in essentially **flat real FCF for a decade**. Sensitivity: at WACC 8.0%, implied g ≈ −0.2%; at WACC 9.0%, implied g ≈ +1.9%. **This dossier's base case is that this implied growth is too pessimistic** if management's own 2027 Pool-recovery guidance and the still-healthy Flow/Water Solutions trajectory play out, and the Taco deal (once closed) adds a genuine new growth leg in Water Solutions — i.e., **implied growth is below this dossier's base case ("below")**. The offsetting consideration (why this is WATCH, not INCLUDE, despite the cheap reverse-DCF read) is that the securities litigation specifically disputes whether the "temporary channel reset" framing is accurate, and if it is not, the depressed FCF base itself — not just the multiple — could be wrong.

## 8. Bull case and bear case
**Bull (3 points):**
1. Flow (+5% reported, core −1%) and Water Solutions (core −3%, segment income +17%, ROS +560bps) are executing well through the Pool downturn — the underlying non-Pool business is healthy, per the company's own segment disclosures.
2. Reverse DCF implies the market is pricing in ~0–1% FCF growth for a decade against a 50-year dividend-growth track record and management's explicit guide to "significant sales and EPS growth in 2027" once the Pool inventory reset completes.
3. Free cash flow held up far better than GAAP/adjusted EPS through the miss ($553M in Q2 2026 alone, only −7% YoY vs. a 17% sales decline), and the balance sheet (pre-Taco) carries low leverage (~1.6x net debt/EBITDA).

**Bear (3 points):**
1. A securities-fraud class action (filed against Pentair and named executives, expanded class period March 2025–July 2026) alleges channel-stuffing-related disclosure issues predating the Q2 2026 "surprise," directly challenging the "purely cyclical, newly-discovered" narrative management and the triage both rely on; unresolved as of 2026-09-25.
2. FY2026 guidance has been cut twice in five months (Feb → Apr → Jul), each time attributed to Pool, alongside a CFO resignation the same week as the pre-announcement — a pattern of deteriorating forecast reliability and management-team instability that raises execution risk into the Taco integration.
3. The $1.425bn Taco acquisition, financed with a $1.4bn bridge facility, will materially raise leverage at Q4 2026 close (this dossier's own estimate: high-2x/low-3x net debt/EBITDA pro forma, from ~1.6x today), just as the Pool segment's own recovery timeline remains unproven.

## 9. Key risks & kill criteria (measurable)
1. **The securities-fraud class action is not dismissed and/or a subsequent restatement or SEC/DOJ inquiry is disclosed** — any of these would invalidate the "cyclical, not disclosure-related" reading this WATCH verdict relies on.
2. **Pro forma net debt/EBITDA (consolidated, post-Taco close) exceeds 3.5x** at or after the Q4 2026/Q1 2027 10-K/10-Q, without a credible, dated deleveraging path.
3. **Q3 2026 or Q4 2026 results miss the company's own just-issued guidance range** (Q3: GAAP EPS $0.97–$1.00; sales −4% to −6%) — a third consecutive guidance miss/cut would break the "one-time channel reset" thesis.
4. **Pool segment organic/core sales do not show sequential stabilization by Q2 2027** (i.e., the "recovery in 2027" management itself has guided to does not materialize on schedule).
5. **A second unplanned CFO or CEO departure** before the newly-hired permanent CFO (effective 2026-11-01) completes at least one full guidance cycle.
