## 1. Business in plain English
American Water is the largest US investor-owned water and wastewater utility. Regulated state subsidiaries (New Jersey, Pennsylvania, California, Missouri, Illinois and others) sell water service and earn a regulator-approved return on rate base; growth comes from capital investment (2026 plan $3.7B), acquisitions of small systems and rate cases. Little commodity exposure; weather and regulatory lag are the main swing factors.

## 8. Bull and bear
Bull: (1) 7-9% EPS/dividend growth compounding on $3.7B annual capital programme; (2) Essential merger closes by Q1 2027, adding scale and Pennsylvania synergies; (3) rate-case outcomes (Pennsylvania 9.55% ROE) and acquisitions sustain the growth despite affordability politics; bond-proxy re-rating if yields fall.
Bear: (1) rising rates: the 2033 issue at 5.55% shows debt cost rising; the stock's 21.6x multiple compresses; (2) merger execution: non-unanimous Pennsylvania settlement, remaining approvals (Illinois, New Jersey, North Carolina, Texas pending per third-party reporting not opened in a filing), integration; (3) ~$1B negative FCF and equity issuance keep per-share growth below the 7-9% target, while Q1 2026 adjusted EPS fell 1 cent year on year.

## 9. Key risks and kill criteria (measurable)

3. Long-term EPS/dividend growth target lowered below 7% (current 7-9%).
