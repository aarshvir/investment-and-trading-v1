# AKAM — Akamai Technologies, Inc. — Diligence Dossier (Agent F153, Wave-8 Standard)

## 1. Verdict
**WATCH** (thesis horizon 24-48 months). A real strategic step happened days before the 25 Sep 2026 cutoff: an $11.6B, seven-year commitment from Anthropic that could lift revenue about 35-40% at full run-rate, but it needs about $5.5B of capex, $1.7B of it pre-purchased in 2026, comes with a warrant for about 5% of the stock, and lands on a business whose stock-based-compensation-adjusted free cash flow is already near zero. At $113.94 the price implies more than my base case delivers. The quick triage (Q11: advance=false, "elevated leverage ... 5.5x") is overturned on the leverage point (net debt is about 1.7x on filings; see section 11) but confirmed on the conclusion. What would change it: evidence that Anthropic service starts on schedule and first payments are received, 2027 capex guided back toward 20% of revenue, and SBC-adjusted FCF turning clearly positive; or a price near $85-90.

## 2. Business in plain English
Akamai started as a content-delivery network (CDN) and now sells three things: security (web-application firewalls, DDoS defence, zero-trust segmentation), delivery of websites and video, and a growing cloud-computing business (Cloud Infrastructure Services, "CIS", the former Linode). Security is 55% of revenue and growing about 10%; delivery is shrinking about 5-6% a year; CIS is growing about 39% and is where the AI-infrastructure demand sits. Competitors are Cloudflare, Fastly and the hyperscalers; Akamai's edge is its distributed network, but its pivot is capital-hungry and margin-dilutive.

## 8. Bull and bear
**Bull:** (1) Security is a durable 10% grower (Q2 +10%, segmentation and API security growing 30%+ in Q4'25); (2) CIS contracted backlog now exceeds $14B ($2.8B + $11.6B) with the Anthropic option of up to $9B more, a visible AI-infrastructure revenue stream; (3) zero- and low-coupon convertibles ($3.5B at 0%) give cheap funding and the balance sheet has $4.6B of cash and securities.
**Bear:** (1) Non-GAAP operating margin fell from 30% to 25% in a year and GAAP operating income halved; capex and memory costs are rising ("significant increase in server and memory costs"); (2) concentration and counterparty risk: one customer would be about 37% of revenue, with termination rights for outage and a warrant giving it about 5% of the equity; (3) SBC-adjusted FCF is about $120M and 2026 capex is rising by $1.7B, so equity value depends on the Anthropic IRR and renewal after year seven.

## 9. Key risks and kill criteria
1. Anthropic Project Plan 2 or 3 terminated, or no revenue recognised under Project Plan 3 by the quarter ending 2027-06-30 (10-Q).
2. Non-GAAP operating margin below 22% for two consecutive quarters (Q2'26: 25%; the Aug 6 2026 release's Q3 row reads "Non-GAAP operating margin* 24 % 26 %").
3. Consolidated net debt (par converts less cash and marketable securities) above 3.0x TTM Adjusted EBITDA at any quarter-end (about 1.7x at Jun 30 2026), or 2026 purchases of property and equipment plus capitalised software above $3.2B.
4. Security revenue growth below 7% year on year for two consecutive quarters (Q2'26: +10%).
5. SBC above 14% of revenue for two consecutive quarters (Q2'26: 13.3%).
