# Cognizant Technology Solutions (CTSH) - Fundamental Diligence Dossier (F127, standard depth)

**Verdict: INCLUDE-SMALL** (half weight), 12-36 month horizon. Named reservation: AI-driven deflation of IT-services work and a slowing order book (Q2 2026 bookings -6% YoY; top of 2026 revenue guide cut). The price (about 10x 2026 guided adjusted EPS, net cash-neutral balance sheet, about 10% FCF yield) implies a shrinking cash flow; a business growing low-to-mid single digits with steady margins only has to hold still to clear that.

## 1. Business in plain English
Cognizant builds, runs and modernises software and data systems for large companies, with Financial Services described in the Q2 2026 release as its largest and most mature segment (up 12% YoY) and a heavy health-insurance/payer client base per the triage card. It is paid mostly by time-and-materials and fixed-fee contracts, with a large offshore (India) workforce of 356,700. The moat is client relationships and scale in regulated industries, not technology; it competes with Accenture, Infosys, TCS and increasingly with AI tools that shrink the hours clients buy.

## 7. Bull and bear
Bull: (1) Financial Services is growing double digits for two consecutive quarters; (2) margin expansion of 20-40 bp a year with Project Leap savings; (3) 5% a year share count shrink at a low multiple.
Bear: (1) AI coding and agentic tools cut billable hours; the CFO cited a "complex environment" in the Q2 2026 release; (2) trailing bookings growth slowed from 11% (Q1 2026) to 5% (Q2 2026) and Q2 bookings fell 6%, which precedes revenue; (3) India tax dispute (ITD asserted about 33B rupees, $349M at 30 Jun 2026, on the 2016 share-repurchase transaction) and management spending beyond FCF on M&A.

## 8. Kill criteria
1. Constant-currency revenue growth below 3.0% for two consecutive quarters (4.1% in Q2 2026).
2. Trailing-12-month bookings growth negative (5% in Q2 2026) or book-to-bill below 1.1x.
3. Full-year 2026 adjusted diluted EPS guide cut below $5.63, the Q1 2026 low end.
4. Adjusted operating margin below 15.5% for any full year, or Project Leap costs above $320 million.
5. Consolidated net debt above $2.0B, or an adverse India Supreme Court/High Court ruling requiring cash payment above $349M.
