# IFF - International Flavors & Fragrances Inc. (agent F155, wave 8, STANDARD depth)

Information cutoff: 2026-09-25 close ($85.69, d4/b1 px_actual; 255.1M shares, market cap about $21.9B). Research only; not personal investment advice.

## 1. Verdict
**WATCH** (thesis horizon 12-36 months). The triage said "no" and that holds, but with a sharper reason: after the pending sale of Food Ingredients, the remaining three businesses are growing 2-4% in sales and 4-8% in EBITDA (company guidance), yet the price already assumes roughly 10% a year of free-cash-flow-per-share growth for ten years, and trailing cash conversion has been poor (FY25 free cash flow $256M on adjusted EPS of $4.20). Condition to upgrade: a price below about $60-65 (my base-case DCF is about $58-60 per share), or evidence that continuing-operations free cash flow exceeds $800M a year after the sale closes.

## 2. Business in plain English
IFF makes flavour, fragrance, food-and-health ingredients and biotech enzymes sold to consumer-goods and food companies; revenue comes from custom formulations and ingredient supply under multi-year customer relationships. It is one of four global fragrance and flavour leaders (competitive position strong, but it carries a heavy goodwill and debt load from the 2021 DuPont Nutrition and Biosciences merger). In 2026 it is selling Food Ingredients (to CVC Capital Partners) and its soy crush, concentrates and lecithin business (to Bunge), leaving Taste, Health and Biosciences, and Scent.

## 3. Why the model likes it (b1, d4)
b1 composite decile 4 (rank 392; not top-30). Support comes from valuation percentiles that look cheap on EBITDA and book value, and recent surprise. Weaknesses: negative trailing GAAP margin and ROE because of the $1,153M goodwill impairment in H1-25 (10-Q cash flow statement, six months ended 2025-06-30) and negative FY25 EPS of $(1.46). The value reading is partly an accounting artefact; the continuing business is profitable but not cheap.

## 8. Bull and bear
Bull: (1) Mix shift to Scent, Taste and Health and Biosciences, with Q2-26 currency-neutral sales +6% and EBITDA +6%. (2) Sale proceeds cut leverage toward the stated 2.0x-2.5x and fund a $2.5B buyback (about 11% of market cap). (3) Stranded-cost removal (~$100M, about 6% of EBITDA) and productivity lift margins beyond the 20.9% reported.
Bear: (1) Priced for about 10% free-cash-flow growth on a business guiding 2-4% sales. (2) Cash conversion has been weak, restructuring and legal costs recur, and Food Ingredients goes out at about 8x EBITDA ($4.3B on about $520M) while the stock trades at about 13-15x, so selling it does not make the rest cheaper. (3) Goodwill is 58% of equity and a prior impairment shows the risk.

## 9. Key risks and kill criteria (each measurable)
1. Company-stated net debt to credit-adjusted EBITDA (release debt-covenant table) above 3.25x at any quarter-end (2026-06-30: 2.5x; covenant maximum 3.75x).
2. The Food Ingredients sale is terminated, or has not closed by the May 28 2027 outside date (8-K acc. 0001193125-26-249963).
3. Continuing-operations adjusted operating EBITDA guidance cut below $1.53B, or comparable currency-neutral sales growth below 2% for two consecutive quarters (Q2-26: +6%).
4. Free cash flow (operating cash flow less capex, 10-Q) below $500M for the 12 months to the 2027-06-30 quarter (H1-26: $378M).
5. A fine or settlement above $250M in the European Commission, UK CMA, Swiss, Mexican, Singapore or India fragrance investigations (none quantified in the 10-Q; provisions recognised but undisclosed).
