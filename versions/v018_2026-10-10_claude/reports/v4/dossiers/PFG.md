## 1. Verdict
**INCLUDE-SMALL** (half weight), horizon 18–36 months. Triage said "not advanced: steady, average-quality insurer". I agree the quality is average, but I overturn the "no" on price: at about 12x 2026E operating EPS the market already assumes only ~4% a year of free-cash growth for 10 years (section 7), against a business guiding to 9–12% EPS growth and running ahead of it in H1. Named reservation: earnings and AUM are sensitive to equity markets, and Q2 2026 AUM net cash flow was -$11.1B.

## 2. Business in plain English
Principal sells retirement plans (401(k) recordkeeping and income products) to small and mid-sized US employers, runs a global asset manager (US investment management plus pension funds in Latin America and Asia), and sells group benefits (dental, disability, group life) and individual life insurance. It earns fees on $808B of AUM ($1.9T assets under administration), underwriting margin on group benefits, and spread on general-account investments. Moat: entrenched small/mid-business retirement distribution and record-keeping scale; weakness: fee-based earnings move with markets.

## 3. Why the model likes it
b1 (2026-09-25) composite decile and momentum are strong with a cheap forward multiple; quality is the weak family. The durable part is real: segment margins are expanding (RIS 41.5% vs 40.9%, Specialty Benefits 18.2% vs 15.2% in Q2 2026 vs Q2 2025, release) and 2Q26 operating EPS ex-significant variances grew 17%. The artefact risk is the favourable variable investment income and encaje (Chilean pension reserve) returns; the company itself labels these "significant variances" and removes them in the ex-SV figure, which I use as the base.

## 8. Bull and bear
**Bull:** (1) Margins expanding across segments with "15-17% non-GAAP ROE" targeted and H1 EPS +15%. (2) Steady capital return: $0.8–1.1B buybacks plus 40% dividend ratio against a cheap multiple. (3) Market-linked fee growth: AUA +9%, RIS transfer deposits +30%.
**Bear:** (1) Asset-market sensitivity: equity drawdown hits AUM, fees and encaje at once; Q2 net outflow -$11.1B. (2) Credit: $13.8B commercial mortgages with problem loans rising. (3) GAAP earnings noisy (net income -1% while operating +12%), so underlying quality is harder to audit.

## 9. Key risks and kill criteria
1. Non-GAAP operating EPS ex-significant variances below $2.17 (the 1Q26 level) in any two consecutive quarters.
2. AUM net cash flow worse than -$10B in two consecutive quarters (2Q26: -$11.1B).
3. Delinquent problem commercial mortgage loans (consolidated carrying amount) above $500M (now $218.7M at 2026-06-30).
4. Principal Life Insurance Company statutory RBC ratio below 350% (406% at 2025-12-31), or excess and available capital below $1.0B (now $1.6B).
5. Full-year 2026 outlook of "9-12% annual non-GAAP operating earnings per diluted share (EPS) growth" or "15-17% non-GAAP ROE" withdrawn or cut.
