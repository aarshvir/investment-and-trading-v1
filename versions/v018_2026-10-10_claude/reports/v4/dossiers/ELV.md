**Agent:** F146 (wave 8 standard diligence). **Price basis:** 2026-09-25 close $396.09 (d4 snapshot, Yahoo; cross-check only); ~216.9m shares -> market cap ~$85.9bn. Research only, not personal advice.

## 1. Verdict
**INCLUDE-SMALL** (half weight), horizon 12–36 months. The 25 Sep price implies only ~3.1% 10-year cash-earnings growth against my base of ~6.5%, because the market still prices ELV as if the 2025–26 medical-cost reset (adjusted net income $7,694m in 2024, ~$33.0 a share derived, -> $30.29 in 2025 -> guided at least $27.00 in 2026) never recovers. Named reservation: ELV's profit now hinges on government-program medical cost trend (Medicaid, ACA, Medicare Advantage) and on an unresolved DOJ False Claims Act suit, and guidance is written as "at least", so a cost-trend relapse would break the case. The quick triage ("neither quality nor growth clears the bar", advance=false) is overturned on price-versus-delivery, not on quality: the business is lower-quality than a year ago, but priced for less.

## 2. Business in plain English
Elevance is a Blue Cross Blue Shield licensee (Anthem brand; the 10-K says its licences give it "exclusive, and in certain areas, non-exclusive" use of the Blue names in assigned territories) and sells health insurance to ~44.9m medical members (employers, individuals/ACA, Medicare Advantage, Medicaid) and, through Carelon, pharmacy benefit management (CarelonRx) and care services (Carelon Services) sold inside and outside its own plans. Operating revenue was $197.6bn in 2025. Its edge is Blue-brand territory exclusivity plus a vertically integrated pharmacy/care arm; its weakness is that premiums are set a year ahead while medical costs are not.
