# TXT — Textron Inc. (F142, wave 8, STANDARD depth)

**1. Verdict: INCLUDE-SMALL (half weight), thesis horizon 24–36 months.** Reason: the 25 Sep 2026 close ($76.59) implies only ~7% 10-year FCF growth against an evidence-based base case of ~9%, but the reservation is named: a live, unresolved US Army funding gap on the MV-75 programme (up to ~$120m catch-up charge and ~$350m cash, per the 10-Q), weak H1 cash conversion, and an unpriced Industrial separation. The Q08 triage (advance = false, "margins and growth middling") is partly overturned on price, not on quality: the business is a 2/5–3/5 quality conglomerate that is cheap, not a compounder.

**Most recent period incorporated: Q2 FY2026 (quarter ended 4 Jul 2026; 10-Q filed 2026-07-28, acc. 0000217346-26-000036); checked for events to 2026-09-25 (no TXT 8-K after 2026-07-28 in EDGAR submissions).**

**2. Business in plain English.** Textron builds Cessna/Beechcraft business jets and turboprops (Textron Aviation, ~40% of revenue), Bell helicopters including the US Army's MV-75 Cheyenne tiltrotor (Bell), defence systems and armoured vehicles (Textron Systems) and, today, auto fuel systems (Kautex) and golf carts/turf equipment (Textron Specialized Vehicles) in the Industrial segment, which it is now trying to sell or spin. It earns money on aircraft deliveries, aftermarket parts/service and US government programmes. Competitive position: strong brands in light/mid-size jets and commercial helicopters; Bell is one of two US military rotorcraft primes.

**3. Why the model likes it.** b1 live rank 264, decile 5: value family (EP 0.078, FCF yield 0.068, EBIT/EV 0.116 -> high value percentiles) plus momentum; quality and sentiment mid-range (3 of 4 families). Durability: value is real (11.7x adjusted EPS) but the adjusted EPS excludes a LIFO inventory provision ($0.87/share in the 2026 outlook table), a recurring cost, so GAAP P/E is ~13.9x on the $5.49 mid-point.
