# RDDT — Reddit, Inc. — F20 diligence dossier

## 1. Verdict
**INCLUDE-SMALL** (half weight) — thesis horizon 12–36 months. Statistically one of the cheapest names in the wave-1 batch on a trailing free-cash-flow basis for its growth rate, but the reservation is specific and real: Reddit's revenue quality depends on a Google relationship (referral traffic *and* a $60m/yr AI data-licensing deal) that is unresolved, litigated, and could move against Reddit in either direction (Google could reduce referral traffic via AI Overviews, or Reddit could itself walk away from the licensing renewal). Combined with only ~2.5 years of public reporting history and a recent cluster of large insider sales, size the position for that uncertainty rather than at full conviction.

## 2. Business in plain English
Reddit is a network of topic communities ("subreddits") where users post and discuss content; it monetizes primarily through advertising (feed and search ads, increasingly self-serve/automated via "Reddit Max") and a smaller, faster-growing "Other revenue" line from licensing its user-generated content and search data to AI companies (Google, OpenAI). Its moat is a 20-year archive of authentic human discussion that is scarce and valuable as AI-training/grounding data, plus a large, logged-in-adjacent audience (130.3m daily actives, +18% YoY) that advertisers are only now starting to buy at scale. Competitive position: a distinct, defensible niche (community + search-indexed long-tail content) rather than a direct Meta/TikTok feed competitor.

## 3. Why the model likes it — durable or artefact?
b1_live_scores.csv: composite percentile 18th (decile 2, live_rank 407 — the model's factor composite actually ranks RDDT modestly, driven down by weak Value-family percentiles, unsurprising for a richly-priced grower); the triage advance was driven by growth (5/5) and price-vs-growth (4/5), not the systematic quant score. **This name reached full diligence through the quality-growth-lane / triage route, not the top-70 quant gate.** The growth is real and getting *more* durable each quarter (8 straight quarters of accelerating-then-decelerating-but-still-60%+ revenue growth, GAAP operating profitability achieved in Q4-2024 and expanding every quarter since), not a one-off — but the "Other revenue" (AI licensing) piece the market is most excited about is growing slower (+24% per Q2-26 call commentary) than the core ad business, which is the opposite of the popular narrative.

## 8. Bull case
1. Ad-tooling ramp (Reddit Max, automated bidding) is early; Active Advertisers +70% YoY and Max revenue +150% sequentially in Q2-26 suggest a long runway of monetization-per-user catch-up versus Meta/Google/Pinterest.
2. Reddit's decade-plus corpus of authentic human discussion is a scarce, differentiated training/grounding asset for AI systems seeking non-synthetic data — multiple licensing counterparties beyond Google (reportedly OpenAI) diversify this revenue stream over time.
3. Near-zero capex intensity (TTM capex 0.3% of revenue) means incremental revenue drops through to FCF at a very high rate — operating leverage is structurally favorable versus most social-media peers who must fund large infrastructure.

## 9. Bear case
1. Reddit's own June-2025 securities class action (N.D. Cal.) alleges the company misrepresented the risk that Google's AI Overviews would divert referral traffic — a risk that is real, litigated, and unresolved, and Reddit itself is reportedly threatening to cut Google's AI-training access as the ~$60m/yr licensing deal comes up for renewal (a lose-lose: either Reddit loses that revenue and goodwill, or backs down and confirms it has little leverage).
2. "Other revenue" (the AI-licensing line the bull case rests on) is growing *slower* (+24%) than core advertising — the market narrative and the underlying mix may be misaligned.
3. CEO Steve Huffman (~$3.56m) and COO Jennifer Wong (~$7.75m) both sold stock on the same day (15-Jul-2026), shortly before the Google-deal uncertainty hit the stock; combined with a public-trading history too short (2.5 years) to have been tested through a full economic cycle, this argues for a smaller position size regardless of the valuation cushion.

## 10. Key risks & kill criteria (measurable)
1. Core advertising revenue growth (ex "Other"/data-licensing) falls below 25% YoY for two consecutive quarters.
2. Daily active users (DAU) growth falls below 10% YoY for two consecutive quarters.
3. Adjusted EBITDA margin contracts YoY (not just sequentially) for two consecutive quarters.
4. The Google AI-training/data-licensing relationship terminates (non-renewal or Reddit-initiated cutoff) without a replacement licensing deal of comparable (~$60m+/yr) size within two quarters.
5. The N.D. Cal. securities class action results in a certified class and/or a settlement or judgment exceeding $150m.
