## Business in plain English
Nasdaq operates the Nasdaq stock exchange and sells market data, listings services, and — since the 2024 Adenza
acquisition — risk/regulatory-compliance software to banks and asset managers. It is shifting from a trading-volume-
driven exchange toward a recurring-revenue "market/regtech software" business, which the model likes for margin and
revenue-quality reasons.

## Bull case
1. Adenza integration is ahead of its own schedule (EPS-accretive 6 months early, $160M+ synergies actioned) —
   execution risk that worried the initial deal skeptics is resolving favorably.
2. 97% FCF conversion funds simultaneous deleveraging and >$500M/quarter shareholder returns — a rare combination.
3. Margin expansion (op. margin +2pp, EBITDA margin +2pp YoY in Q2 2026) shows the software mix shift is
   structurally improving profitability, not just cutting costs.

## Bear case
1. Gross leverage (2.6x) is still a full turn or more above where a pure-play exchange traded historically —
   any EBITDA growth slowdown (market-data/trading-volume cyclicality) slows the deleveraging path.
2. Regulatory/litigation risk is structurally elevated for an exchange operator (10-K explicitly lists "adverse
   changes in litigation or regulatory areas... or increased regulatory oversight" as a principal risk factor) —
   new rules (e.g., expanded listing-denial discretion, night-session risk disclosures) add compliance overhead.
3. At 24x forward earnings the stock is priced for continued double-digit growth with no margin for a
   market-activity downturn (lower trading volumes/IPO issuance would hit the legacy exchange segment directly).

## Key risks & kill criteria (measurable)
1. Consolidated gross leverage ratio fails to fall below 2.3x within the next four quarters (vs. 2.6x at Q2 2026).
2. FCF conversion falls below 85% of net income for two consecutive quarters.
3. Adenza-related expense synergies actioned stall below $160M (i.e., no further progress) for two consecutive quarters.
4. Quarterly net revenue growth decelerates below 8% YoY for two consecutive quarters.
5. A material adverse regulatory or litigation ruling (>$100M) tied to Nasdaq's exchange/listing operations.
