### Strongest bear case
The market has de-rated BR ~35% in 12 months through a beat-and-raise year, so it is pricing something the dossier underweights: (a) franchise-erosion fear - agentic AI, tokenization (BR is itself building tokenized-asset voting, which could disintermediate the legacy street-name chain it monetises) and a standing regulatory option to open proxy plumbing to competition (no new proposal found this window); (b) quality of growth is decelerating - Q4 FY26 adjusted EPS grew only +8% (FY +12%), adjusted operating margin was flat (26.9% vs 27.0%), event-driven revenue fell 10%, ICS recurring growth includes ~1pt of acquisitions, and the FY27 EPS range (8-12%) leans on record buybacks and balance-sheet use (CQG ~$173m, $500m 2036 notes) rather than organic margin expansion; (c) FY26 GAAP is flattered by a $227m non-cash digital-asset gain (adjusted is the clean series); (d) distribution revenue ($606m in Q4, postage-driven) inflates the top line at near-zero margin, so only 'recurring' matters and it is not accelerating on a multi-year view (FY24 6.6%, FY25 5.9%, FY26 8%). That FCF/share compounds (dossier base 8-10%/yr) rather than stalls - i.e. adjusted EPS growth of 13-15% converts to cash after separation costs, NCI buy-outs, opioid payments and the loss of Medical-Surgical cash flow.
