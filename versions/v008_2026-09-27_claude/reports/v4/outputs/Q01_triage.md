## Notable non-mechanical calls
- **APP / CVNA**: both face serious short-seller allegations, but scored differently — APP's most serious claim was retracted (no restatement, denies vigorously) and growth/valuation are exceptional, so it's advanced for forensic diligence; CVNA has *documented* (not just alleged) related-party accounting and a fresh guidance cut, so it's passed.
- **GM**: inherited v1_verdict "excessive" is rejected here — likely based on the high trailing P/E (36.9x), but two 2026 guidance raises and a 5.6x NTM P/E tell a different story. Full diligence should reconcile this.
- **ROST**: v1_verdict "excessive" is confirmed — strong business, just fully priced.
- **ECHO, PSKY, WBD**: all three are event-driven special situations (spectrum monetization, contested mega-merger, merger-arb target) that don't map cleanly onto a quality/growth framework; flagged as not-advanced here but worth a separate special-situations track.
