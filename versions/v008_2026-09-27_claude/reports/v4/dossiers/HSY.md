## 1. Verdict: INCLUDE-SMALL — thesis horizon: 12–24 months
Hershey is one half of the US chocolate/candy duopoly (with Mars), a genuinely durable, high-brand-equity business that suffered a historic margin shock in FY2025 from record cocoa prices (FY2025 operating profit fell 50.3% YoY, adjusted EPS −32.7%). This diligence pass **confirms the triage's cocoa-relief thesis with primary-source evidence that is running ahead of the triage's own framing**: FY2026 guidance was not just reaffirmed but **raised** at the Q2 2026 release (net sales growth 4.5–5% from 4–5%; organic 3–3.5% from 2.5–3.5%; adjusted EPS growth 32.5–35% from 30–35%), and H1 2026 results already show adjusted EPS +28.4% YoY with Q2 alone +57.0%. The company itself frames this as sustainable ("well positioned to navigate dynamic markets and deliver on our full-year financial commitments," CEO Kirk Tanner, 2026-07-30). The one item this pass surfaces that the triage did not flag: **trailing-twelve-month free cash flow (~$2,228M) is running above both FY2024 ($1,926M) and FY2025 ($1,823M) actuals** — a genuinely strong number, but this dossier treats it as likely inflated by a temporary working-capital/cocoa-cost-timing tailwind rather than a new steady-state, and uses a normalized FY2024–25 average (~$1,874M) for the reverse DCF below, which still implies a **cheap** valuation. INCLUDE-SMALL, not INCLUDE: (1) the core 2026 EPS-growth narrative is largely a cost-relief snapback off a depressed base, not yet proven as a multi-year structural re-rate, and (2) consolidated debt has grown materially (total debt $5,611M vs. $2,3xx–3,2xxM historically, per the leverage build below) alongside continued buybacks and dividends held flat through the crisis year — a genuine, named reservation, not a valuation disagreement.

## 2. Business in plain English
Hershey manufactures and markets chocolate and confectionery (Hershey's, Reese's, Kit Kat U.S. license) plus a growing salty-snacks portfolio (SkinnyPop, Pirate's Booty, and the 2025 LesserEvil acquisition), sold through US grocery, mass, club, drug and convenience channels and internationally. It earns high gross margins (mid-40s% normally) by owning iconic brands with pricing power, though margins are directly exposed to cocoa and sugar commodity costs, which is exactly what hit FY2025.

## 7. Valuation snapshot and reverse DCF (no V1 row exists for HSY — this dossier's own build)
At the 2026-09-25 close of $166.37 (mktcap ≈ $33,746M per `d4_live_snapshot`; EV ≈ **$38,566M** using net debt $4,820M), NTM adjusted P/E ≈ $166.37 / $8.36 (FY2026 guide midpoint) ≈ **19.9x**.
**Reverse DCF** (10-year FCF growth at rate *g* + terminal growth 2.5%, WACC 7.0%), using the **normalized** FCF base (~$1,874M, not the flattered TTM $2,228M): implied **g ≈ +1.25%/year**. Sensitivity: WACC 6.5% → g ≈ −0.15%; WACC 7.5% → g ≈ +2.55%. Using the raw TTM FCF instead would imply a *negative* growth rate (g ≈ −0.9% at 7.0% WACC) — i.e., the raw number would make HSY look even cheaper, which is exactly why this dossier prefers the more conservative normalized base. **This dossier's base case** is mid-single-digit long-run FCF growth (~4–6%) once the cocoa-cost tailwind fully normalizes into 2027–28 (organic sales growth of 3–3.5% plus a return to historical ~24–27% adjusted operating margins), which is **above** the implied ~1–3% — hence **"below"** (fair to cheap). Cross-check: NTM P/E of 19.9x is below Hershey's own pre-2023 historical range (often 22–26x), consistent with the market not yet having fully re-rated the recovery.

## 8. Bull case and bear case
**Bull:** (1) FY2026 guidance raised on every metric at the Q2 release, with adjusted EPS growth already running at the high end (+28.4% H1); (2) the reverse DCF implies only ~1–3%/year normalized FCF growth for a decade, below a plausible mid-single-digit base case, even before crediting further multiple re-rating toward Hershey's historical 22–26x range; (3) FCF proved resilient through the FY2025 shock (FCF fell only ~5% while GAAP EPS fell 60%), evidencing balance-sheet and working-capital discipline.
**Bull risk offsets / Bear:** (1) the entire 2026 growth story is substantially a cocoa-cost-relief snapback off an artificially depressed FY2025 base — durability of the 32–35% adjusted-EPS-growth guide beyond 2026 (once the base normalizes) is unproven; (2) cocoa is a volatile, weather- and geopolitically-sensitive commodity — a renewed price spike would directly reverse the current tailwind; (3) net debt has risen to ~$4.8bn (net debt/EBITDA ~1.8x, up from a lower base) funding buybacks, dividends (held flat, not grown, through the crisis) and the LesserEvil acquisition — capital allocation is levered into the recovery, not building excess cushion.

## 9. Key risks & kill criteria (any of these would break this thesis; INCLUDE-SMALL invalidation triggers)
1. FY2026 guidance (adjusted EPS growth 32.5–35%, net sales growth 4.5–5%, given 2026-07-30) is **cut** at the Q3 2026 release.
2. Cocoa or other key commodity costs re-accelerate materially, evidenced by adjusted gross margin declining for two consecutive quarters from the H1 2026 level.
3. Net debt/EBITDA (consolidated) rises above **2.5x** without a specific, disclosed acquisition or buyback rationale.
4. The common-stock dividend ($1.370/quarter, $5.480/yr) is cut or suspended.
5. Organic constant-currency net sales growth falls below **2%** for two consecutive quarters (versus the 3–3.5% FY2026 guided floor).
