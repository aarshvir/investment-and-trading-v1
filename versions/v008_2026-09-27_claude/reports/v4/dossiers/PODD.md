# PODD — Insulet Corporation

## 1. Verdict
**WATCH** — a high-quality, fast-growing insulin-patch-pump franchise (Omnipod) with reasonable valuation, but an unresolved securities-fraud investigation tied to a concealed device defect, plus a confirmed deceleration in the core U.S. market, are genuine, unresolved red flags that argue against sizing this now. Horizon: 12–24 months (pending litigation clarity and 2–3 quarters confirming the U.S. retention issue is contained).

## 2. Business in plain English
Insulet makes the Omnipod, a tubeless, wearable insulin-delivery patch pump for people with diabetes (both Type 1 and, increasingly, Type 2), sold in the U.S. and internationally, plus a small legacy drug-delivery/device-manufacturing business for third parties. It makes money on a razor/razor-blade model: pump controllers plus recurring pod (cartridge) sales, direct to consumers via pharmacy/DME channels and increasingly through pharmacy benefit coverage.

## 3. Why the model likes it / is it durable?
Growth and quality factors like it: revenue has grown 20%+ in constant currency for ten consecutive years through 2025, ROE ~26%, with expanding margins. The Aug 2026 guidance cut (see §5) is a genuine red flag on durability — management explicitly attributed it to weaker-than-expected retention/utilization among newly onboarded U.S. Type 2 diabetes customers in their first 90 days, not competition, pricing or GLP-1 drug substitution. International Omnipod growth (+59.4% YoY in Q1 2026, guidance since raised twice) is offsetting this and is the stronger, more durable growth leg right now.

## 5. Guidance track record (last 3 releases — verbatim from company releases)
- **18 Feb 2026 (Q4/FY2025):** initial FY2026 guide — Total Omnipod growth 21%–23%; International Omnipod 24%–26%; adjusted operating margin +~100 bps; adjusted EPS growth >25%.
- **6 May 2026 (Q1 2026):** **RAISED** — Total Omnipod 22%–24%; International Omnipod 26%–28%; U.S. Omnipod maintained 20%–22%; adj. operating margin and adj. EPS guidance maintained. Company explicitly stated it was "raising our total company revenue growth outlook."
- **5 Aug 2026 (Q2 2026):** **MIXED** — Total Omnipod/total revenue growth cut to 20%–22% (cc); **U.S. Omnipod cut to 17%–19%** (from 20%–22%) explicitly due to "utilization/attrition trends below prior expectations, especially Type 2"; International Omnipod **raised again** to 30%–32%; **adjusted EPS guidance raised** to "at least 30%" (from >25%), with ~100 bps of margin expansion still guided. So: the weaker (U.S.) segment guide was cut, but the bottom-line (adjusted EPS) guide was raised — a genuinely mixed signal that deserves scrutiny (see §6).
Sources: Insulet 8-K Ex-99.1 releases, 18 Feb 2026, 6 May 2026, 5 Aug 2026 (SEC EDGAR).

## 6. Earnings quality & balance sheet
- **FCF conversion:** Q2 2026 OCF (derived, H1 2026 YTD $202.2M − Q1 2026 $113.8M) = $88.4M; capex $32.5M → FCF $55.9M vs GAAP NI $95.0M = **~59% conversion**, weaker than the prior three quarters (Q3'25 ~$100M FCF, Q4'25 ~$183M, Q1'26 ~$89.5M). This coincides with the two 2026 medical-device corrections and likely reflects warranty/inventory costs and Costa Rica plant ramp working-capital drag — worth monitoring, not yet alarming.
- **SBC:** ~2.8% of revenue (Q1 2026: $21.3M / $761.7M).
- **Balance sheet (consolidated, 30 Jun 2026):** cash $534.9M; debt = Term Loan B $475.0M (due 2031) + Senior Unsecured Notes $450.0M at 6.5% (due 2033) + smaller equipment/Costa Rica plant financings = $929.5M non-current + $18.9M current = $948.4M gross debt → **net debt ≈ $413.5M** vs equity $1,422.2M. The company **repurchased/eliminated $419.9M of its former Convertible Senior Notes in H1 2025**, replacing convertible with fixed-rate secured/unsecured debt — a de-risking of the capital structure (removes dilution overhang) at the cost of a higher fixed coupon.
- **Buybacks:** Board approved an additional $350M repurchase authorization (Feb 2026); completed $300M accelerated share repurchase by 31 Mar 2026 — a notably aggressive capital-return stance for a company still investing in growth capex.

## 8. Bull case / Bear case
**Bull:** (1) Ten straight years of 20%+ constant-currency growth show a structurally durable franchise, now diversifying into Type 2 and international; (2) International Omnipod growth (+59% YoY) is accelerating and guidance keeps being raised there; (3) management is defending profitability (adjusted EPS guide raised to ≥30% even as the revenue guide was trimmed), suggesting real cost/mix discipline.
**Bear:** (1) The U.S. Omnipod guidance cut (20–22% → 17–19%) is a genuine, company-confirmed execution miss in the core, highest-margin market — first 90-day Type 2 retention is a real unsolved problem, not yet fixed; (2) two voluntary Medical Device Corrections in 2026 (see §11) covering ~8.5% of 2025 global pod production, with a large gap between company-reported (29) and FDA-reported (476) serious injuries, is a real quality-control and potential disclosure problem; (3) FCF conversion has weakened for two of the last three quarters.

## 9. Key risks & kill criteria (measurable)
1. U.S. Omnipod revenue growth falls below 15% YoY for two consecutive quarters (current guide is already 17–19%).
2. A third voluntary Medical Device Correction/recall affecting >2% of the installed base within 12 months of the 26 May 2026 MDC.
3. An SEC enforcement action, formal subpoena, or securities-fraud settlement/judgment is disclosed (currently only law-firm investigations, not confirmed regulatory action).
4. FCF/NI conversion stays below 70% for two consecutive quarters.
5. Non-GAAP gross margin falls below 71% for two consecutive quarters (device-correction/warranty costs eating into the structural margin).

## 11. Red-flag scan
- **Voluntary Medical Device Corrections:** (a) 12 Mar 2026 — a manufacturing defect (tear in internal tubing) could cause insulin to release inside the Pod instead of being infused, causing under/over-delivery; stock fell ~6.9% (from $236.07 to $219.84) on disclosure. (b) 26 May 2026 — a second, separate MDC for specific lots of Omnipod 5, DASH and Eros Pods (under-delivery), covering ~7 million pods (~8.5% of 2025 global production).
- **Adverse-event disclosure gap:** FDA posted (29 Apr 2026) that Insulet had reported **476 serious injuries** associated with the correction, versus the **29 confirmed serious adverse events** the company had initially cited — a material discrepancy.
- **Securities-fraud investigations (law-firm led, not adjudicated):** multiple firms (Levi & Korsinsky and others) are investigating whether Insulet and executives knew of the tubing defect before the 12 Mar 2026 disclosure, specifically scrutinizing the 18 Feb 2026 Q4-2025 earnings call where the CEO described Omnipod as demonstrating "reliability, consistency and broad appeal." **Treat this as an unresolved allegation, not a proven fact** — but it is a genuine, material overhang that the original triage did not surface.
- Insider selling: not checked in the time available for this dossier — flag for follow-up.
