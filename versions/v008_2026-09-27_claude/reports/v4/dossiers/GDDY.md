# GoDaddy Inc. (NASDAQ: GDDY) — Diligence Dossier (Agent F67)

## 1. Verdict
**INCLUDE** — durable, high-margin domain/hosting franchise buying back stock aggressively at a price that implies shrinking free cash flow, which the primary-source evidence does not support. Thesis horizon: 12–36 months.

## 2. Business in plain English
GoDaddy sells domain names, web hosting, security and productivity tools, and payments/commerce software mainly to small businesses and individuals worldwide. It makes money from subscriptions (Core: domains, hosting) and from Applications & Commerce (A&C: websites, payments, productivity, and the domain "aftermarket" resale business). High switching costs (domains are sticky; moving a live website/email is painful) and a trusted brand give it durable renewal rates and pricing power.

## 3. Why the model likes it — durable or artefact?
b1 factor scores (composite ~87th percentile, live_rank 115): strong on quality (net margin 17.8%, ROE >4 — inflated by near-zero book equity from buybacks, see §6), value (FCF yield 9.8%), and momentum is negative (12-1m return −28.5%, i.e., recent price weakness against a growing business) — a value/quality signal, not a momentum one. This looks durable: revenue and FCF have grown every quarter for at least two years (§4) and guidance execution has been steady (§5), not a one-off. The extreme ROE figure is a leverage/buyback artefact, not evidence of unusual returns on new capital, and should be discounted.

- **SBC:** Q1 2026 $75.3m vs Q1 2026 revenue $1,266.9m ≈ **5.9% of revenue** (Q1 2025: $80.4m/$1,194.3m ≈ 6.7%) — SBC is declining as a share of revenue.

- **Stockholders' equity is near zero:** $237.3m at 2026-03-31 → **$6.7m at 2026-06-30** (10-Q balance sheet), driven by cumulative share repurchases exceeding retained earnings + APIC. This is a mechanical consequence of returning nearly all FCF to shareholders via buybacks funded partly by debt, not a solvency problem (net debt/EBITDA is moderate and FCF is strongly positive) — but it does mean GDDY has essentially no equity cushion against a multi-year earnings shock.
- **Buybacks:** $824.4m repurchased in H1 2026 (10-Q cash-flow statement); diluted shares outstanding fell from 142.0m (9mo-2025 weighted avg) to 131.0m (Q2-26 weighted avg), and shares outstanding on the 10-Q cover fell from 135.1m (2025-10-24) to 126.6m (2026-07-24) — **~6.3% share-count reduction in 9 months**.

## 8. Bull case / bear case
**Bull:** (1) Dominant, sticky domain registrar with a renewing subscriber base and rising A&C attach; (2) NEBITDA margin >33% and rising, funding ~6–8%/year buybacks that compound per-share FCF growth well above revenue growth; (3) three straight quarters of unchanged-midpoint guidance shows underwriting discipline, reducing the odds of a negative surprise.

**Bear:** (1) Core (domains/hosting) growth is structurally low-single-digit and AI website-builder/commerce competitors could erode A&C's faster growth over time; (2) a lost Express Mobile patent appeal could cost up to $170m (§9) and, more importantly, an adverse claim-construction ruling could force product changes; (3) near-zero stockholders' equity and buyback-funded capital return leave no cushion if FCF were to fall for a sustained period — the balance sheet is not a source of optionality in a downturn.

## 9. Key risks & kill criteria
1. **Core segment organic revenue growth <2% for two consecutive quarters** (loss of the pricing/renewal engine).
2. **Net debt/EBITDA exceeds 3.0x** (currently ~1.9x) — leverage discipline breaking down.
3. **FY2026 free-cash-flow guidance is formally cut below the reaffirmed ~$1.8bn** (would break the 3-release execute-to-plan record).
4. **Adverse final ruling or settlement in Express Mobile, Inc. v. GoDaddy above the disclosed $170m reasonably-possible range**, or an injunction affecting core product features (Note 11, 10-Q Q2-26).
5. **NEBITDA margin guidance cut below the >33% full-year target.**

## 11. Red-flag scan
- **Litigation (material, dated, from the filing itself):** On **2025-11-07**, a Delaware federal jury found GDDY infringed two Express Mobile, Inc. web-technology patents. On **2026-05-14** the District Court upheld the infringement verdict and rejection of GDDY's invalidity defenses, granted pre/post-judgment interest, but reversed the willfulness finding and denied enhanced damages. GDDY filed a notice of appeal on **2026-06-15**. No liability is accrued (loss not deemed probable); reasonably possible loss exposure is **$0–$170.0m, excluding interest** (10-Q Q2-26, Note 11, filed 2026-07-31). This is the only material legal proceeding disclosed as changed from the 2025 10-K.
- **Auditor/material weakness/restatement/going-concern:** none found in the sections reviewed (Legal Proceedings, Note 11, cover-page controls statement).
- **Insider selling pattern:** not individually reviewed line-by-line within the time box; 23 Form 4 filings since 2026-06-01 were counted from the SEC submissions feed but not classified sale-vs-award — disclosed as a limitation, not evidence either way.
