# CBOE — Cboe Global Markets, Inc. (Agent F83, wave 5)

## 1. Verdict
**INCLUDE-SMALL** (half weight). Reservation: 2027 consensus EPS growth decelerates sharply to ~5.5% from ~25% in 2026 (Zacks aggregator cross-check, not primary) — the growth re-acceleration this thesis needs is not yet visible in forward estimates, and the stock has already sold off ~28% from recent highs, which we treat as the market pricing that deceleration in, not as an anomaly to buy blind. Horizon: 12–24 months.

## 2. Business in plain English
Cboe operates the largest U.S. listed-options exchange (SPX and VIX products are effectively proprietary, single-listed franchises), plus cash equities, global FX, and a growing market-data/analytics business (Data Vantage). It earns transaction fees tied to trading volume plus recurring data/access fees — a high-margin, operationally levered model where incremental volume drops through at high flow-through.

## 3. Why the model likes it / durability
Q05 triage: "27% margins, 26% ROE, 23% revenue growth on a fair 17x forward multiple; options volume franchise is hard to replicate." Confirmed directionally: Q2 2026 net revenue $732m (+25% YoY), adjusted diluted EPS $3.56 (+45% YoY), broad-based double-digit growth across Derivatives (+30%), Data Vantage (+15%) and Global FX (+17%) [1]. The SPX/VIX franchise moat is durable (regulatory/listing exclusivity); the growth *rate* is less durable — much of 2026's YoY comparison benefits from elevated volatility/volume that is not guaranteed to repeat, which is exactly what the 2027 consensus deceleration (25%→5.5%) implies.

## 8. Bull case
1. SPX/VIX single-listed franchise is a structural, hard-to-replicate moat; Data Vantage (recurring data fees) is growing faster than transaction revenue, improving revenue quality.
2. FY2026 guidance raised twice in 2026 (organic net revenue, Data Vantage growth) — management is executing ahead of its own plan.
3. Recent ~28% pullback from highs (per aggregator) may have already re-based expectations ahead of the 2027 growth deceleration that consensus already shows.

## 9. Bear case
1. 2027 consensus EPS growth of only ~5.5% (vs ~25% in 2026) suggests 2026's growth was largely a volatility/volume cycle, not a new run-rate — the core risk to the thesis.
2. High-margin transaction-fee model is inherently volume-cyclical; a low-volatility regime compresses Derivatives net revenue quickly.
3. Regulatory/competitive risk to options-exchange fee structures (payment-for-order-flow-style scrutiny, new derivatives venues) not independently assessed this pass (data gap).

## Kill criteria (measurable)
1. Organic net-revenue growth guidance cut versus the raised mid-to-high-teens FY2026 range.
2. Two consecutive quarters of Derivatives segment net-revenue growth below 10% YoY.
3. Consolidated leverage ratio approaches the covenant ceiling (3.50x, or 4.00–4.25x under the step-up) — any move above ~2.5x warrants review.
4. Data Vantage organic growth falls back below "low double digits" for two consecutive quarters (i.e., loses the 2026 upgrade to "low teens").
5. Forward P/E re-rates above 25x without a corresponding upgrade to multi-year (not single-year) growth guidance — a valuation-discipline trigger given the INCLUDE-SMALL sizing.
