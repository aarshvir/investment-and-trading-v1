## 1. Verdict
**INCLUDE-SMALL (half weight).** Thesis horizon 12–24 months. Cheap (9.9x NTM P/E), high-quality (44% TTM ROE, zero funded debt), and management just *raised* FY2027 guidance — but the brand-momentum evidence that should drive a re-rating is deteriorating in real time (HOKA revenue growth 18.5%→14.5%→7.7% over the last three reported quarters; operating margin fell 190bps YoY in the latest quarter). This is a genuine two-sided call, not a table-pounding buy — size it accordingly and watch the kill criteria below closely at the next print (~Oct 22–23, 2026).

## 2. Business in plain English
Deckers designs, markets and distributes footwear and apparel under five brands — HOKA (performance running, the growth engine), UGG (heritage lifestyle boots/slippers, the cash cow), Teva, Sanuk and Koolaburra (small, declining "other"). It sells through wholesale (department stores, specialty and national retail chains, ~59% of FY26 sales) and direct-to-consumer (owned stores + e-commerce, ~41%). Manufacturing is 100% outsourced to third-party contractors, predominantly in Vietnam (Deckers has cut China production to an estimated 4–5% of the total), which is the source of its tariff exposure.

Whether V is "artefact" or "opportunity" is precisely the open question this dossier cannot resolve with certainty — see bull/bear below.

## 6. Earnings quality & balance sheet
FCF conversion is strong and clean: TTM (through Q1 FY27) OCF $1,193.7m vs TTM capex $75.9m → **TTM FCF ≈$1,117.8m**, FCF/NI ≈110% (TTM NI $1,014.8m) — cash generation exceeds accounting profit, a good sign. SBC is small (≈0.8% of revenue). **No GAAP-vs-adjusted gap exists** (GAAP-only reporter). Balance sheet: **zero funded debt**, $1.60bn cash, only operating-lease liabilities ($398.6m LT + $73.4m ST as of Q1 FY27). On 2026-08-27 the company amended and extended its unsecured revolver to $500m/5-year maturity (2031) with Citi/HSBC/Fifth Third — routine treasury management, undrawn, not a leverage event. Inventory rose to $807.6m at Q1 FY27 (vs $849.4m a year earlier, actually down YoY — no build-up red flag). Buybacks are aggressive: $1.075bn repurchased in FY26 alone, authorization increased to ~$5bn total; share count down from ~152.4m (FY25) to ~136.7m (Q1 FY27), a genuine and material per-share tailwind. **Tariffs:** the two press releases reviewed directly disclose no specific dollar tariff bridge (only that inventory "includes the impact of incremental tariffs"). Financial press (not independently verified against a primary transcript this session — flagged Tier-4/secondary) reported the company guided to a ~$150m FY26 tariff cost hit in mid-2025, later revised up to ~$185m, mitigated via price increases — this is a meaningful, real cost headwind that the press releases understate relative to TPR/RL's explicit bps disclosure, and is a disclosure-transparency gap worth pressing management on.

## 8. Bull case / Bear case
**Bull:** (1) 8-for-8 quarterly EPS beats with a still-positive average surprise of +25%, a debt-free balance sheet, and a $5bn buyback runway systematically shrinking the share count. (2) Management raised guidance at every one of the last three releases even while absorbing tariff cost inflation — the "beat and raise" machine hasn't actually broken, only slowed. (3) International revenue (+25.5% Q4, +8.4% Q1FY27) and DTC (+13.0% Q1FY27) are still growing well above the group average, showing real runway outside the maturing US wholesale base.
**Bear:** (1) HOKA's growth has nearly collapsed sequentially (18.5%→14.5%→7.7%) in a category (performance running) that On, Nike, Asics and Brooks are all now contesting hard — this could be genuine category maturation, not noise. (2) Operating margin fell 190bps YoY in the most recent quarter as SG&A outgrew gross profit — the first sign that scale leverage is inverting. (3) Tariff cost disclosure is thin and reportedly moved materially higher intra-year ($150m→$185m per secondary sources); if true and not fully offset by pricing, FY27 guidance (GM ≈56.5%, down from 56.4% Q1 actual but below FY26's 57.7%) already implicitly assumes structural margin give-back.

## 9. Key risks & kill criteria
1. HOKA quarterly YoY growth falls below 5% for two consecutive quarters (from 7.7% in Q1 FY27) — would confirm structural, not cyclical, deceleration.
2. GAAP operating margin below 15% for two consecutive quarters (currently 15.2% Q1 FY27, down from 17.1%).
3. FY2027 guidance cut at any point (would break the "raised every time" streak that is the main remaining bull pillar).
4. Domestic (US) revenue growth turns negative for two consecutive quarters (was +2.7% to +3.2% in the last two prints — already anemic).
5. Evidence that tariff mitigation (pricing) is triggering wholesale sell-through weakness or channel pushback (watch wholesale growth, currently still positive at +2.2–7.1%).
