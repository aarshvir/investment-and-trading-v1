# PTC Inc. (NASDAQ: PTC) — Diligence Dossier
Agent F98 | As of 2026-09-25 close (px $138.12) | Fiscal year ends Sept 30

## 1. Verdict
**INCLUDE** — durable, high-margin PLM/SaaS franchise; the FY26 GAAP "revenue decline" that triggered triage is a divestiture accounting artifact, not organic decay; ARR growth is healthy and guidance was raised twice this year. Thesis horizon: 12–36 months.

## 2. Business in plain English
PTC sells product-lifecycle-management (PLM), CAD and industrial IoT/AI software — Windchill (PLM), Creo/Onshape (CAD), Codebeamer (ALM) and ServiceMax (field service) — to industrial manufacturers (aerospace, medtech, auto, electronics) who need a single digital record of a product from design through service. Revenue is subscription-based (ARR), high-switching-cost (multi-year implementations embedded in engineering workflows), sold direct and via a growing partner channel (Rockwell, Microsoft).

## 7. Valuation snapshot
No PTC row exists in `v4/outputs/v1_valuation_table.csv` — **v1_verdict = null**, so there is no systematic model to reconcile against; this section is this agent's own reverse-DCF sense only.
- NTM (FY26 guide) non-GAAP EPS midpoint ≈ $8.15 → P/E ≈ 138.12 / 8.15 ≈ **16.9x**.
- FY26 FCF guide midpoint $850M; PTC market cap ≈ $14.99B (b1 mktcap field, as_of 2026-09-25) → **FCF yield ≈ 5.7%**. (Triage's cited 6.9% FCF yield likely used a trailing or gross-of-buyback-year FCF base; direction — mid-single-digit FCF yield — agrees.)
- **Reverse DCF (Gordon-growth sense):** implied perpetual growth ≈ WACC − FCF yield. Using a software-sector WACC of ~9%, implied growth ≈ 9% − 5.7% ≈ **3.3%/yr**. PTC's own guided ARR growth (constant currency, ex-divested) is 9–9.5% for FY26, and management describes the go-to-market realignment as improving durability, not decaying it. **Base case (this agent): ARR/FCF growth decelerating from ~9% toward high-single-digits (7–8%) over the next 3 years** as the divested-business comp laps and SaaS mix normalizes — still comfortably above the ~3.3% the price implies. Verdict discipline: since implied growth (3.3%) is below base case (7–8%), INCLUDE is consistent with the addendum's rule.

## 8. Bull case / Bear case
**Bull:**
1. ARR reacceleration to 9%+ cc growth, beating guidance twice in the last two quarters, with management raising the full-year range each time.
2. Divestiture of lower-margin/slower-growth IoT (Kepware/ThingWorx) is a genuine portfolio upgrade — proceeds funded both debt paydown flexibility and buybacks, and the remaining PLM/CAD/ALM core is the highest-margin, highest-switching-cost part of the business.
3. AI/"Intelligent Product Lifecycle" positioning (per CEO commentary in the Q3'26 release) is a plausible new upsell vector on top of an already-embedded install base, not a speculative pivot.

**Bear:**
1. GAAP EPS for FY26 is inflated by a non-recurring $463M divestiture gain; if the market is pricing FY26 GAAP EPS naively, a "beat" narrative could reverse once FY27 comps normalize without the gain.
2. New debt draw ($1.17B → $1.4B LT debt in one quarter) to fund buybacks after already receiving divestiture cash is a capital-allocation choice to monitor — priority should be growth investment/deleveraging, not just EPS support via buybacks.
3. Revenue growth guide for FY26 (as reported, including lapping the divested businesses) is actually **negative to flat (-2% to 0%)** — the ARR/organic story is good, but headline revenue optics will look weak for several more quarters and could pressure the stock on GAAP-revenue-focused screens (exactly the mechanism that triggered the original triage flag).

## 9. Key risks & kill criteria (measurable)
1. Constant-currency ARR growth (ex-divested businesses) falls below **6%** YoY for two consecutive quarters.
2. Consolidated net debt (LT debt less cash, on the quarterly balance sheet) rises above **$2.0B** without a corresponding step-up in FY run-rate FCF guidance.
3. FY guidance is cut (not just reaffirmed/raised) in any quarterly release.
4. Non-GAAP operating margin (currently guided ~41%) compresses below **36%** for two consecutive quarters.
5. Any 8-K disclosure of a material cybersecurity incident, restatement, or auditor change (none identified as of this review).
