# BIIB — Biogen Inc. — Diligence Dossier (Agent F108, Wave 6)

## 1. Verdict
**INCLUDE-SMALL** (half weight). Horizon: 18–36 months.
One-sentence reason: Leqembi (Alzheimer's) and the newly acquired Apellis geographic-atrophy franchise are real, growing
revenue streams that should let earnings snap back sharply once $5.6bn of debt-funded M&A integration costs roll off, but
the balance sheet is now meaningfully more levered and H1 2026 GAAP earnings collapsed, so this is not a full-weight
position until the post-merger run-rate is proven for 2–3 quarters.

## 2. Business in plain English
Biogen is a neuroscience/immunology biotech. Its legacy multiple-sclerosis (MS) franchise (Tecfidera, Tysabri, Vumerity,
Spinraza) is in secular volume/price decline as it faces generics and newer competitors. Growth now comes from Leqembi
(lecanemab, Alzheimer's, co-marketed with Eisai) and, since May 2026, from Apellis Pharmaceuticals' pegcetacoplan
franchise (Syfovre for geographic atrophy, Empaveli), acquired for ~$5.6bn. It also earns royalties (Spinraza/Zurzuvae
economics) and sells rare-disease drugs. Customers are neurologists, ophthalmologists and health systems; reimbursement
runs through Medicare Part B/D and commercial payers.

## 3. Why the model likes it / durability
Q06 triage flagged +194% forward EPS growth (Leqembi ramp + restructuring) and a +55% trailing run, with the Street
seeing only ~5% further upside — hence "full diligence" was ordered. That EPS-growth read is now partly an artefact:
much of the 2026 forward-EPS jump is a base-effect bounce off the H1 2026 M&A-cost trough (see §4), not pure operating
leverage. The durable piece is Leqembi's volume ramp (+74% YoY global in-market sales in Q1 2026 per company release)
and pegcetacoplan's growing GA market share — both real, IP-protected franchises with limited near-term generic risk.

## 8. Bull case / Bear case
**Bull (3 points):**
1. Leqembi keeps compounding off a low base (+74% YoY Q1 2026) as diagnosis rates and infusion capacity scale.
2. Apellis's Syfovre/Empaveli franchise in geographic atrophy is a genuine growth market with limited near-term
   competitive entry; cost synergies from the combined commercial organization show up in 2027 margins.
3. Valuation is not demanding on a normalized-earnings basis (see §7); a return to the ~$600m/quarter net income run
   rate seen in 2025 Q2 would roughly halve the trailing P/E.

**Bear (3 points):**
1. Legacy MS revenue (Tecfidera, Tysabri, Spinraza) continues its structural decline and could outrun the pace of new
   franchise ramp, especially if pricing pressure (IRA-related Medicare negotiation exposure for large-revenue drugs)
   bites earlier than modeled.
2. The Apellis deal added ~$1.8bn of new term-loan debt on top of existing notes; if integration costs run longer than
   guided or a near-term refinancing hits a worse rate environment, EPS recovery could be delayed further.
3. GAAP earnings have now missed sharply for two of the last four quarters (Q4 2025 loss, Q2 2026 EPS of $0.66 vs
   $4.33 a year earlier) — even allowing for one-off deal costs, this is a meaningfully more volatile earnings profile
   than the triage's "cost discipline improving margins" framing suggested.

## 9. Key risks & measurable kill criteria
1. GAAP diluted EPS below $1.50 for **two consecutive quarters** *excluding* clearly-labeled, auditor-confirmed
   one-off deal/restructuring charges (i.e., the underlying run-rate, not the headline, fails to recover).
2. Gross debt (notes payable + term loan, consolidated Biogen Inc.) remains above $7.5bn **and** trailing-12-month
   operating cash flow falls below $2.0bn as of any quarter-end after Q4 2026.
3. Leqembi global in-market sales growth decelerates below 30% YoY for two consecutive quarters.
4. A negative FDA/EMA safety signal (ARIA-related label change, boxed warning, or REMS tightening) on Leqembi.
5. Legacy MS franchise revenue (Tecfidera + Tysabri + Vumerity + Spinraza, combined) declines more than 15% YoY in
   any quarter, outpacing modeled offsets.

## 11. Red-flag scan
- **M&A/financing**: $5.3–5.6bn Apellis acquisition closed May 14, 2026, funded partly by a new $2.0bn unsecured term
  loan (8-K, 2026-05-14, Ex-10.2) plus balance-sheet cash — a real leverage step-up, consolidated-entity basis.
- **Litigation**: a shareholder derivative suit against Apellis's former leadership over alleged concealment of
  pegcetacoplan (Syfovre) safety risks was dismissed for lack of standing once the acquisition closed and Apellis
  shareholders no longer held Apellis stock (Bloomberg Law, dated to the 2026 deal-close period) — this closes an
  overhang but does not resolve the underlying safety-risk allegations, which should be tracked for any new suit filed
  directly against Biogen as successor.
- **Restructuring**: the company disclosed a "comprehensive review" of the former Apellis pipeline and is trimming
  positions within the combined research organization (company statements, 2026) — headcount/pipeline-cut risk to
  monitor.
- No auditor change, material weakness, restatement, or going-concern language was identified in the sources reviewed
  this pass; this is not an exhaustive 10-K risk-factor read and should not be taken as a clean bill of health beyond
  what was checked.
