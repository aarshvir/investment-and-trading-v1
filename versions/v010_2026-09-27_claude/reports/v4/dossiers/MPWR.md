# MPWR — Monolithic Power Systems, Inc. (diligence agent F29, standard depth)

## 1. Verdict
**WATCH** (12–36 month thesis horizon). MPWR is executing exceptionally well operationally (record, accelerating revenue and margins on AI data-center power delivery), but the stock is priced for that acceleration to continue for a decade, the company has an **unremediated material weakness**, a **live, partially-surviving securities-fraud suit** centred on the exact customer-concentration risk the triage flagged, and V4's own quant model ranks it in the **bottom quintile** of the entire coverage universe. This is not a "no v1 row so skip" situation — the combination of valuation, litigation and governance overhang means it does not clear the bar for inclusion today.

## 2. Business in plain English
Monolithic Power Systems designs high-performance, mixed-signal power-management and voltage-regulation semiconductors — chips that convert and regulate electrical power for GPUs, servers, storage, industrial and automotive systems. It is fabless (it designs chips and outsources manufacturing). Its fastest-growing segment is voltage-regulator modules and power stages for AI-GPU/data-center racks, where its largest disclosed customer relationship is with Nvidia-class AI accelerator platforms.

## 8. Bull case / Bear case
**Bull:** (1) Genuine, filing-verified acceleration: revenue growth has gone from +22% YoY (Q3'24) to +47.6% YoY (Q2'26), with Q3'26 guided to +55–57% YoY — an unusual acceleration, not deceleration, this deep into an AI-capex cycle. (2) Full-year enterprise/data-center growth guidance raised from 85% to 130% on the July 2026 call — management is signalling the ramp is still under-modelled by its own prior guide. (3) No funded debt, ~$1.4B net cash, and FCF/NI ≈73% once the tax-distorted quarter rolls out of the trailing window — the balance sheet can self-fund growth.
**Bear:** (1) A securities class action (*Waterford Twp. Gen. Emps. Ret. Sys. v. Monolithic Power Systems, Inc., et al.*, No. 25-cv-220, W.D. Wash., filed 2026-02-04) alleges the company hid critical defects in power modules used by its largest customer (Nvidia) and that Nvidia cancelled roughly half its outstanding orders (per a November 2024 Edgewater Research report cited in coverage) — a Washington federal judge **largely denied** MPWR's motion to dismiss on 2026-05-07, rejecting a "fraud-by-hindsight" defence; consolidated derivative suits (*Miller v. Hsing, et al.*, No. 25-cv-527) proceed alongside it. This is precisely the customer-concentration risk the triage flagged, now the subject of active litigation, not just a red flag on a page. (2) A financial restatement (disclosed 2026-02-26) found the company had not properly accounted for deferred income taxes tied to a one-time foreign tax incentive, cutting FY2024 income-tax benefit/net deferred taxes by $194.6M, and the company's internal-controls material weakness is **not yet remediated** as of the FY2025 10-K. (3) b1's systematic quant score ranks MPWR in the bottom quintile of the entire ~500-name coverage universe on quality and value, and the reverse DCF above requires roughly 10-year growth a third again as fast as its own already-strong 2-year historical CAGR.

## 9. Key risks & kill criteria
1. Any quarter where YoY revenue growth decelerates below **30%** (roughly two-thirds of the current run-rate), signalling the AI-rack ramp is normalising.
2. An adverse ruling, settlement disclosure, or SEC enforcement action in the Waterford Twp. securities action or the Miller derivative suit that confirms material, un-remediated disclosure failures.
3. The material weakness remains unremediated for **two more consecutive 10-K/10-Q filings** without a disclosed remediation timeline.
4. Nvidia (or the single largest disclosed AI-platform customer) is confirmed, via a 10-K customer-concentration disclosure or company statement, to have reduced or in-sourced power-delivery content.
5. Gross margin falls below **53%** (below the two-year band of 55.1–55.4%) for a full quarter, indicating pricing or mix pressure.

## 11. Red-flag scan
- **Restatement (material):** Audit Committee determined 2026-02-26 that the FY2024 10-K (filed 2025-03-03) and all 2025 10-Qs "should no longer be relied upon" due to an unintentional error in deferred-income-tax accounting for a foreign tax incentive; restated FY2024 income-tax benefit/net deferred taxes reduced by $194.6M (FY2025 10-K, Explanatory Note, sec.gov). **Material weakness disclosed and not yet remediated.**
- **Securities litigation (live):** *Waterford Twp. Gen. Emps. Ret. Sys. v. Monolithic Power Systems, Inc., et al.*, No. 25-cv-220 (W.D. Wash.), filed 2026-02-04, alleging Section 10(b)/20(a) violations tied to alleged concealment of Nvidia-related power-module defects and order cancellations; motion to dismiss **largely denied 2026-05-07**. Consolidated derivative suits: *Miller v. Hsing, et al.*, No. 25-cv-527 (W.D. Wash.).
- **Law-firm investigations:** Kahn Swick & Foti and others have announced investigations into the board's conduct around the restatement and the Nvidia allegations (businesswire, 2026-06; Simply Wall St, 2026) — these are plaintiff-side solicitations, not adjudicated findings, but corroborate that the governance question is active, not resolved.
- **Insider selling:** third-party coverage (Simply Wall St) flags "fresh insider share sales" alongside the restatement and investigations; I could not independently pull specific Form 4 dollar amounts in the time available for this pass — flagged as an open item.
