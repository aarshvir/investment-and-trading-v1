HIG closed $122.36 above both the old $120 review level and approximately $120.62 modeled 12% ceiling; the 23 September NICO commutation creates a $393m after-tax GAAP gain with **no core-earnings contribution**. USB at $57.76 was 1.92× June tangible book; with analyst-assumed 6% TBV/share growth and unchanged 1.92× exit, its after-withholding/cost 3-year illustration is 8.25%, falling to 2.16% at 1.60×. These are good businesses/candidates but not compelling 6%/5%/4% fresh allocations at those prices.

The replacements have an explicit cost. With *assumed* 2% EPS growth and constant dividends, a flat multiple returns ~3.84% per year after a 30% dividend-withholding and 25bp each-side dealing convention; 18× exit is negative. This is a deliberate 5% defensive allocation, not a high-return claim.

## Decision risk and selling discipline

The earlier **15–20% temporary-loss tolerance remains an important warning**, but it cannot be imposed as a guaranteed drawdown bound on a fully invested unhedged stock portfolio. A repeat of the observed target's GFC path would mark $50,000 down about **$22,040**, or $100,000 down about **$44,080** at the trough; a future event could be worse. If that 15–20% number was a hard, non-negotiable maximum, the two instructions cannot both be met by long-only stocks. I am following the latest stocks-only instruction without disguising the risk through a Treasury or cash allocation.

Weekly review is a **decision checkpoint**, not daily monitoring or a mechanical stop. Reconcile all actual holdings first: a current position above target can call for `HOLD` or `REDUCE`, and a broken thesis can call for `SELL`, but cost basis, taxes and portfolio exposure are needed before a personal liquidation instruction. Review new SEC/issuer filings, earnings and guidance, business economics, normalized owner cash with SBC/reinvestment/regulated-capital needs, capital allocation, next catalyst, dated comparable prices and correlated sector stress. For PG, PEP and PEG, the binding next test is cash after capex versus dividends; for MSFT/NVDA, AI capex/supply and cash conversion; for PTC and MCK, verify the reason for October 1 intraday moves before execution. Do not count a buyback as both cash returned and free share shrinkage. Re-evaluate all weights if the overall basket falls 10%, 15% or 20%; those are review thresholds, **not guaranteed stops**. A proven thesis break or persistent balance-sheet deterioration warrants reduction/replacement even if price has not fallen. Compare an excluded name only when updated primary cash economics and entry value beat the weakest funded target, not to fill a quota.

No personal tax residence, actual holdings, trading fees, FX, executable quote, or near-term spending requirement was supplied. The published model applies an illustrative 30% dividend withholding and 25bp each-side costs only to the stated company scenario sensitivities, not a full personal tax projection. Fractional shares or whole-share rounding can leave small settlement cash temporarily; that does not create a strategic cash target. No return probability, calibrated portfolio CAGR, alpha or maximum-loss promise is asserted. The repaired v013 causal share/covariance methods are retained, but the old v011 factor backtest has **not** been fully rerun causally and must not set weights.
