## Decision rules and prospective learning

Before any implementation, reconcile actual holdings and near-term cash liabilities; confirm tax residence, eligible fund/share class, live price and currency, all dealing/FX fees, current fund holdings, and total equity exposure. No brokerage account was accessed. The index target is **25% total equity**, not 25% in addition to existing equity. Individual stock limits remain 2% of total capital, direct sector 5%, known issuer look-through planning 3.5% if direct names later return. A weekly review is a decision checkpoint, not daily monitoring or a stop order. If a real holding breaks its operating thesis, exceeds the cap, or the account has a cash need, reassess HOLD/REDUCE/SELL with the actual tax/cost effects. The prior weekly policy's discretionary reviews at 8%, 12% and 15% drawdowns still apply, with no guaranteed floor.

At **30 days**, append realized adjusted total returns, earnings/event surprises, actual fund costs and every unfilled alert to a *new* ledger; do not change this snapshot. At **90 days**, compare AMP/PAYX and candidate cash/EPS/valuation forecasts with filings, include calibration of misses and a fresh source/currency audit, then consider direct-stock replacement only if after-cost downside-adjusted economics beat the index and reserve. At **12 months**, measure time-weighted results only from supplied dated actual holdings; separately compare locked hypothetical portfolios with SPY and Treasury benchmarks, including dividends, tax assumptions, costs and decision turnover. Rebuild the full factor backtest from causally available raw data before using its ranks for real allocation. A future empirically estimated frontier must show covariance and expected-return estimation error, taxes, constraints and stability; the scenario map here is not that frontier.

**What could make this recommendation wrong:** a large index drawdown beyond the constructed stresses; near-term cash need or tax treatment that makes the chosen fund unsuitable; reserve reinvestment rates below the illustrative 3.5%; AMP/PAYX rerating and cash recovery that outpace the index after our pause; or evidence that another company has a high-confidence, cash-backed edge that this sweep has not captured. Those are observable research triggers, not arbitrary AI conviction scores. The requested Druckenmiller-style regime composite was not calculated because its five dated upstream signals were not all available; an invented score cannot override the user's risk budget. The finance-skills source/sector conventions were used for the bounded company audit, while its broader full-document requirement was **not** claimed for all 90 names.
